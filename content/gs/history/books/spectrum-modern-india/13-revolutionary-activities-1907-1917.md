## Gist
*Book pages 282–293.*

Revolutionary activity grew as a **by-product of militant nationalism**. Its **first phase** was a fallout of the **Swadeshi and Boycott Movement** and lasted **till 1917**; the second phase followed the Non-Cooperation Movement. Young nationalists, disillusioned with leaders (even the Extremists) who failed to find new forms of struggle, and facing repression, concluded the British must be **expelled by force**.

Rejecting mass revolution or subverting the Army as impractical, they followed the **Russian nihilists or Irish nationalists**: **individual heroic actions** — assassinating unpopular officials and traitors, **swadeshi dacoities** to raise funds and, in the First World War, military conspiracies with help from Britain's enemies.

**Bengal:** The first groups were organised in **1902** in **Midnapore (Jnanendranath Basu)** and Calcutta — the **Anushilan Samiti founded by Promotha Mitter**. In **April 1906** an inner circle (**Barindra Kumar Ghosh, Bhupendranath Dutta**) started the weekly **Yugantar**. In **1908**, **Prafulla Chaki and Khudiram Bose** threw a bomb at a carriage in **Muzaffarpur** (Bihar) supposed to carry judge **Kingsford**; he was not in it and **two British ladies were killed**. **Chaki shot himself; Khudiram was hanged**. The **Alipore conspiracy case** (Manicktolla/Muraripukur conspiracy) followed: **C.R. Das defended Aurobindo, who was acquitted**; Barindra Ghosh and **Ullaskar Dutt** got death sentences commuted to life. In **December 1912** **Rashbehari Bose and Sachin Sanyal** bombed **Viceroy Hardinge** at **Chandni Chowk, Delhi** (Delhi Conspiracy trial). **Bagha Jatin (Jatindranath Mukherjee)** led the **Jugantar**, linking Calcutta with **Bengal, Bihar and Orissa**, and planned the **'German Plot'/'Zimmerman Plan'**; he died at **Balasore in September 1915**.

**Maharashtra:** **Vasudev Balwant Phadke's Ramosi Peasant Force (1879)**; the **Chapekar brothers killed Rand and Lt. Ayerst (1897)**; **Savarkar's Mitra Mela (1899)** merged with **Abhinav Bharat (1904)**; **Anant Lakshman Kanhere killed A.M.T. Jackson, Collector of Nasik (1909)**.

**Punjab:** **Lajpat Rai (Punjabee)** and **Ajit Singh** (Bhagat Singh's uncle; **Anjuman-i-Mohisban-i-Watan**, journal **Bharat Mata**). In **May 1907** the government banned political meetings and **deported Lajpat Rai and Ajit Singh**.

**Abroad:** **Shyamji Krishnavarma's India House (London, 1905)**; **Madanlal Dhingra killed Curzon-Wyllie (1909)**; **Madam Bhikaji Cama** (Paris, Geneva; **Bande Mataram**); Virendranath Chattopadhyaya in **Berlin**. The **Ghadr Party (1913)**, headquartered at **San Francisco**, was led by Lala Hardayal, Kartar Singh Saraba, Barkatullah and others. The **Komagata Maru** (370 passengers) returned to Calcutta in **September 1914**; **22 died at Budge Budge**. A Ghadr revolt fixed for **21 February 1915** failed through treachery; the **Defence of India Act (March 1915)** was passed mainly to smash the Ghadr. The **Berlin Committee (1915)** and a **Kabul mission** (Mahendra Pratap, Barkatullah, Obaidullah Sindhi) followed; the **Singapore mutiny (15 February 1915)** was crushed.

Activity **declined** after the war: prisoners were released, **Montagu's August 1917 statement** created conciliation, and **Gandhi** offered non-violent non-cooperation.

## Key facts
- First phase of revolutionary activity: fallout of the **Swadeshi and Boycott Movement**, continued **till 1917**.
- Revolutionaries followed the methods of **Russian nihilists or Irish nationalists** — individual heroic actions, not mass struggle.
- First revolutionary groups (**1902**): **Midnapore (Jnanendranath Basu)** and Calcutta's **Anushilan Samiti (Promotha Mitter)**.
- Weekly **Yugantar** started **April 1906** by **Barindra Kumar Ghosh and Bhupendranath Dutta**.
- **1907**: abortive attempt on **Sir Fuller**, first Lt. Governor of Eastern Bengal and Assam.
- **Muzaffarpur, 1908**: **Prafulla Chaki and Khudiram Bose** bombed a carriage meant for judge **Kingsford**; **two British ladies killed**; Chaki shot himself, **Khudiram hanged**.
- **Alipore conspiracy case** = **Manicktolla bomb conspiracy = Muraripukur conspiracy**; **Chittaranjan Das** defended **Aurobindo** (acquitted).
- **Barindra Ghosh** (head) and **Ullaskar Dutt** (bomb maker): death sentences **commuted to life**.
- Approver **Narendra Gosain** shot in jail by **Satyendranath Bose and Kanailal Dutta**.
- **Barrah dacoity (1908)** — **Dacca Anushilan under Pulin Das**.
- **December 1912**: bomb on **Viceroy Hardinge** at **Chandni Chowk, Delhi** by **Rashbehari Bose and Sachin Sanyal**; Hardinge injured.
- **Delhi Conspiracy trial**: **Basant Kumar Biswas, Amir Chand, Avadh Behari** executed; Rashbehari Bose evaded arrest.
- **Bagha Jatin** linked Calcutta with **Bengal, Bihar and Orissa**; died at **Balasore, September 1915**; call: **"We shall die to awaken the nation"**.
- Revolutionary papers: **Sandhya and Yugantar** (Bengal), **Kal** (Maharashtra).
- **Ramosi Peasant Force (1879)** — **Vasudev Balwant Phadke**.
- **Chapekar brothers (Damodar and Balkrishna)** killed Poona Plague Commissioner **Rand** and **Lt. Ayerst** in **1897**.
- **Mitra Mela (1899)** → merged with **Abhinav Bharat (1904)**, modelled on Mazzini's 'Young Italy'.
- **A.M.T. Jackson**, Collector of Nasik, killed in **1909** by **Anant Lakshman Kanhere**; Savarkar got **transportation for life**.
- **Ajit Singh** (Bhagat Singh's uncle) — **Anjuman-i-Mohisban-i-Watan**, Lahore; journal **Bharat Mata**.
- **May 1907**: ban on political meetings; **Lajpat Rai and Ajit Singh deported**.
- **Rashbehari Bose escaped to Japan (1915)**; later important in founding the **INA**.
- **India House / Indian Home Rule Society** — **Shyamji Krishnavarma, London, 1905**; journal **The Indian Sociologist**.
- **Madanlal Dhingra** assassinated **Curzon-Wyllie** in **1909**.
- **Savarkar** extradited **1910**, transported for life in the **Nasik conspiracy case**.
- **Ghadr Party** established **1913**, HQ **San Francisco**; earlier **Swadesh Sevak Home (Vancouver)** and **United India House (Seattle)**.
- **Komagata Maru**: **370 passengers**, Singapore to Vancouver; anchored at Calcutta **September 1914**; **22 died at Budge Budge**.
- Ghadr revolt date: **21 February 1915** at **Ferozepur, Lahore and Rawalpindi**; foiled by treachery; **45 hanged**.
- Punjab political dacoities (**Jan-Feb 1915**) had new social content: raiders targeted **moneylenders and debt records**.
- After the failed revolt, **Rashbehari Bose fled to Japan** while **Sachin Sanyal was transported for life**.
- **Defence of India Act, March 1915** — passed primarily to smash the **Ghadr movement**.
- **Berlin Committee for Indian Independence (1915)** — **Virendranath Chattopadhyay, Bhupendranath Dutta, Lala Hardayal**, under the 'Zimmerman Plan'.
- **Kabul mission**: **Raja Mahendra Pratap Singh, Barkatullah, Obaidullah Sindhi** — 'provisional Indian government' with crown prince **Amanullah**.
- **Singapore mutiny, 15 February 1915**: **5th Light Infantry and 36th Sikh battalion**; **37 executed, 41 transported for life**.
