## Gist
*Book pages 635–652.*

The years from independence to **Nehru's death (May 1964)** are called the **'Nehruvian Era'**. The Congress won in **1952, 1957 and 1962**; **Rajendra Prasad** (Bihar) was elected President by the electoral college of the first Parliament.

**Language.** Gandhi favoured **Hindustani**. The Constituent Assembly's Language Committee made **Hindi in Devanagari the 'official' (not 'national') language**, with English for **15 years**. The **Official Languages Act, 1963** made English an **'associate additional official language'**; the **1967 amendment** gave a bilingual solution.

**Linguistic states.** The Congress had organised itself into **21 linguistic units** at **Nagpur (1920)**. The **Dhar Commission (June 1948)** and the **JVP Committee (December 1948 — Nehru, Patel, Pattabhi Sitaramayya)** both opposed linguistic states. After **Potti Sriramulu** died fasting for **56 days**, **Andhra** was formed on **October 1, 1953**. The **SRC (August 1953; Fazl Ali, K.M. Panikkar, H.N. Kunzru)** reported in **October 1955**; the **States Reorganisation Act (November 1956)** created **14 states and 6 centrally administered territories**. Bombay was split into **Maharashtra and Gujarat (May 1960)**; **Nagaland** was conceded in **1960** and inaugurated in **1963**; **PEPSU** merged with Punjab (1956).

**Parties.** Socialists left the Congress (**Nashik, March 1948**) and merged with the **KMPP** into the **Praja Socialist Party (September 1952; Kripalani chairman, Ashoka Mehta general secretary)**. **Jayaprakash Narayan** turned to **Bhoodan (1954)** and **'partyless democracy'** after 1957. The **CPI** accepted the parliamentary path at **Amritsar (1958)** and split into **CPI and CPM in 1964**. The **Bharatiya Jana Sangh (October 21, 1951)** was led by **S.P. Mookerjee**. The **Swatantra Party (August 1959; Rajagopalachari, Masani, Ranga, Munshi)** wanted a laissez-faire state and won **18 seats in 1962**, becoming the main opposition in **Bihar**, Rajasthan, Gujarat and Orissa. The **Jharkhand Party** was a regional party of **undivided Bihar**. In **1957** Kerala elected **E.M.S. Namboodiripad's** communist government; after agitation over its **Education Bill** it was dismissed in **July 1959** — the first elected government dismissed in independent India.

**Planning and science.** The **Planning Commission** (extra-constitutional, **March 1950**) was chaired by the PM; the **NDC** was set up on **August 6, 1952**. The **First Plan** followed the **Harrod-Domar model**; the **Second**, by **P.C. Mahalanobis**, stressed heavy industry. India chose a **mixed economy**. The **National Physical Laboratory (January 1947)**, first **IIT at Kharagpur (1952)**, **Atomic Energy Commission (August 1948, Homi Bhabha)**, **Trombay reactor (August 1956, Asia's first)**, **INCOSPAR and Thumba (1962)** and the **Scientific Policy Resolution (March 1958)** marked the period.

**Society.** Literacy was **16.6 per cent (1951)**; universities grew from **18 to 54**. The **Radhakrishnan Commission (1949)** led to the **UGC (1953)**; **NCERT** came in **1961**. The **Anti-Untouchability Law (1955)** and the **Hindu Code Bill**, passed as **four separate Acts**, followed.

**Foreign relations.** Pakistan rejected Kashmir's accession (**October 26, 1947**); ceasefire **January 1, 1949**; India withdrew its plebiscite offer in **1955**. The **Indus Waters Treaty** was signed at **Karachi on September 19, 1960**. China occupied Tibet (1950); the **Dalai Lama** took asylum (1959); China attacked in **October 1962** and withdrew unilaterally in **November**. Treaties were signed with **Bhutan (August 1949)** and **Nepal (July 1950)**.

## Key facts
- **Rajendra Prasad** elected President by the electoral college of the first Parliament.
- Language Committee: **Hindi in Devanagari** as **'official'** language; English for **15 years**.
- **Official Languages Act, 1963** — English as **'associate additional official language'**; **1967 amendment** — bilingual Centre-state communication.
- Congress **Nagpur session (1920)** — India divided into **21 linguistic units** for its organisation.
- **Linguistic Provinces Commission (June 1948)** — **Justice S.K. Dhar**; opposed linguistic provinces.
- **JVP Committee (December 1948)** — **Jawaharlal Nehru, Vallabhbhai Patel, Pattabhi Sitaramayya**.
- **Potti Sriramulu** died after a **56-day** fast; **Andhra** formed **October 1, 1953**, from Madras state.
- **SRC (August 1953)** — **Fazl Ali, K.M. Panikkar, Hridaynath Kunzru**; report **October 1955**.
- **States Reorganisation Act, November 1956** — **14 states, 6 centrally administered territories**.
- Bombay split into **Maharashtra and Gujarat** (agreed **May 1960**); **Nagaland** conceded 1960, inaugurated **1963**.
- Socialists' break with the Congress announced at **Nashik, March 28, 1948**.
- **Praja Socialist Party (September 1952)** — chairman **J.B. Kripalani**, general secretary **Ashoka Mehta**.
- **Jayaprakash Narayan** — **Bhoodan (1954)**; left active politics after 1957; **'partyless democracy'**.
- CPI: **Amritsar (1958)** — socialism through peaceful, parliamentary means; split into **CPI and CPM (1964)**.
- **Bharatiya Jana Sangh** founded **October 21, 1951**; **S.P. Mookerjee** had resigned over the **Liaquat-Nehru Pact (April 1951)**.
- **Swatantra Party (August 1959)** — **C. Rajagopalachari**, Minoo Masani, N.G. Ranga, K.M. Munshi; **18 Lok Sabha seats (1962)**.
- In 1962 Swatantra was the main opposition in **Bihar, Rajasthan, Gujarat and Orissa**.
- **Jharkhand Party** — regional party of **undivided Bihar**.
- **Hindu Mahasabha** — founded **1915 at Haridwar** by **Madan Mohan Malaviya**.
- **Kerala, 1957** — **E.M.S. Namboodiripad**; government dismissed, President's Rule **July 1959**.
- **Planning Commission** — **March 1950**, by resolution; **NDC** — **August 6, 1952**.
- **First Plan (1951–56)** — **Harrod-Domar model**; **Second Plan** — **P.C. Mahalanobis**, heavy industry.
- **National Physical Laboratory (January 1947)** — India's first national laboratory.
- First IIT at **Kharagpur (1952)**; **Atomic Energy Commission (August 1948)** — **Homi J. Bhabha**.
- **Trombay** reactor critical **August 1956** — India's and Asia's first.
- **INCOSPAR** and **TERLS (Thumba)** — **1962**; **Scientific Policy Resolution** — **March 1958**.
- Literacy **16.6 per cent (1951)**; universities **18 (1947) → 54 (1964)**.
- **Radhakrishnan Commission (1949)** → **UGC (1953)**; **Mudaliar Commission (1952)**; **NCERT (September 1961)**.
- **Anti-Untouchability Law, 1955**; **Hindu Code Bill** passed as **four separate Acts**.
- Ceasefire in Kashmir **January 1, 1949**; plebiscite offer withdrawn **1955**.
- **Indus Waters Treaty** — **Karachi, September 19, 1960**; interim agreement **April 17, 1959**.
- China occupied **Longju** (1959); **Sino-Indian War, October 1962**; **Colombo** mediation **December 1962**.
- Treaties: **Bhutan (August 1949)**, **Nepal (July 1950)**.
- ⚠ May be outdated: **14 states and 6 territories** (1956) — India now has many more states and union territories.
- ⚠ May be outdated: **Planning Commission** and **NDC** — the Planning Commission was replaced by NITI Aayog in 2015.
- ⚠ May be outdated: IITs described as **five** institutes — many more IITs exist now.
- ⚠ May be outdated: **Anti-Untouchability Law, 1955** — renamed the Protection of Civil Rights Act in 1976.
- ⚠ May be outdated: **Indus Waters Treaty** in force — India put the treaty in abeyance in 2025.
