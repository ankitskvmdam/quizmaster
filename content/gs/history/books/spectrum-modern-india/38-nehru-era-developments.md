## The Nehruvian Era

- **Nehruvian Era:** The years from independence to Nehru's death (May 1964) are called the 'Nehruvian Era'.
- **Elections:** The Congress won the elections of 1952, 1957 and 1962.
- **President:** Rajendra Prasad, who was from Bihar, was elected President. The electoral college of the first Parliament elected him.

## Language

- Gandhi favoured Hindustani.
- **Language Committee:** The Language Committee of the Constituent Assembly made Hindi in the Devanagari script the 'official' language. It did not call Hindi the 'national' language. English was to continue for 15 years.
- **Official Languages Act, 1963:** This Act made English an 'associate additional official language'.
- **1967 amendment:** The amendment gave a bilingual solution. Communication between the Centre and the states was to be bilingual.

## Linguistic states

In 1920, at its Nagpur session, the Congress had divided India into 21 linguistic units for its own organisation.

### New states

1. **1 October 1953:** Andhra was formed out of Madras state. This came after Potti Sriramulu died on a fast of 56 days.
2. **1956:** PEPSU (Patiala and East Punjab States Union) was merged with Punjab.
3. **November 1956:** The States Reorganisation Act was passed. It created 14 states and 6 centrally administered territories.
4. **May 1960:** It was agreed to split Bombay into Maharashtra and Gujarat.
5. **1960:** Nagaland was conceded.
6. **1963:** Nagaland was inaugurated.

⚠ May be outdated: 14 states and 6 territories (1956). India now has many more states and union territories.

## Political parties

### Socialists

1. **28 March 1948:** The socialists announced their break with the Congress at Nashik.
2. **September 1952:** The socialists merged with the Kisan Mazdoor Praja Party (KMPP). The new party was the Praja Socialist Party. J.B. Kripalani was its chairman and Ashoka Mehta its general secretary.
3. **1954:** Jayaprakash Narayan turned to Bhoodan (the land-gift movement).
4. **After 1957:** Jayaprakash Narayan left active politics. He spoke of a 'partyless democracy'.

### Communists

1. **1957:** Kerala elected a communist government under E.M.S. Namboodiripad.
2. **1958:** At Amritsar the Communist Party of India (CPI) accepted the parliamentary path. It would seek socialism through peaceful, parliamentary means.
3. **July 1959:** The Kerala government was dismissed after an agitation over its Education Bill. President's Rule was imposed. It was the first elected government to be dismissed in independent India.
4. **1964:** The party split into the CPI and the CPM (Communist Party of India (Marxist)).

### Bharatiya Jana Sangh

- **21 October 1951:** The Bharatiya Jana Sangh was founded. S.P. Mookerjee led it.
- S.P. Mookerjee had resigned over the Liaquat-Nehru Pact (April 1951).

### Swatantra Party

1. **August 1959:** The Swatantra Party was founded. Its leaders were C. Rajagopalachari, Minoo Masani, N.G. Ranga and K.M. Munshi.
2. The party wanted a laissez-faire state, that is, one that leaves the economy alone.
3. **1962:** It won 18 Lok Sabha seats. It became the main opposition in Bihar, Rajasthan, Gujarat and Orissa.

### Other parties

- **Jharkhand Party:** It was a regional party of undivided Bihar.
- **Hindu Mahasabha:** Madan Mohan Malaviya founded it in 1915 at Haridwar.

## Planning and the economy

1. **March 1950:** The Planning Commission was set up by a resolution. It was an extra-constitutional body, that is, not set up by the Constitution. The Prime Minister was its chairman.
2. **1951–56:** The First Plan followed the Harrod-Domar model.
3. **6 August 1952:** The National Development Council (NDC) was set up.
4. **Second Plan:** P.C. Mahalanobis drew it up. It stressed heavy industry.

India chose a mixed economy, with both public and private sectors.

⚠ May be outdated: the Planning Commission and the NDC. The Planning Commission was replaced by NITI Aayog in 2015.

## Science and technology

1. **January 1947:** The National Physical Laboratory was set up. It was India's first national laboratory.
2. **August 1948:** The Atomic Energy Commission was set up under Homi J. Bhabha.
3. **1952:** The first Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) was set up at Kharagpur.
4. **August 1956:** The reactor at Trombay went critical. It was the first reactor in India and in Asia.
5. **March 1958:** The Scientific Policy Resolution was adopted.
6. **1962:** INCOSPAR (Indian National Committee for Space Research) was set up. TERLS, the rocket launching station at Thumba, came in the same year.

⚠ May be outdated: the book describes the IITs as five institutes. Many more IITs exist now.

- **Literacy:** It was 16.6 per cent in 1951.
- **Universities:** There were 18 universities in 1947. By 1964 there were 54.

- **1955:** The Anti-Untouchability Law was passed.

## Foreign relations

### Pakistan

1. **26 October 1947:** This is the date of Kashmir's accession. Pakistan rejected the accession.
2. **1 January 1949:** A ceasefire came into force in Kashmir.
3. **1955:** India withdrew its offer of a plebiscite (a direct vote of the people).
4. **17 April 1959:** An interim agreement on the Indus waters was reached.
5. **19 September 1960:** The Indus Waters Treaty was signed at Karachi.

⚠ May be outdated: the Indus Waters Treaty as being in force. India put the treaty in abeyance in 2025.

### China

1. **1950:** China occupied Tibet.
2. **1959:** The Dalai Lama took asylum. In the same year China occupied Longju.
3. **October 1962:** China attacked India. This was the Sino-Indian War. China withdrew unilaterally in November.
4. **December 1962:** The Colombo mediation took place.

### Bhutan and Nepal

- **August 1949:** India signed a treaty with Bhutan.
- **July 1950:** India signed a treaty with Nepal.
