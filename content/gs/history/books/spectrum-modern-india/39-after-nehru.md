## Gist
*Book pages 653–802.* This is the book's longest chapter (150 pages), so the gist is in two parts.

### 1964-1984: Shastri, Indira Gandhi, Janata

After Nehru's death (May 1964) **Gulzarilal Nanda** was interim PM (again in January 1966). The **Syndicate** (formed **1963**, incl. Congress president **K. Kamaraj**) guided the succession; **Lal Bahadur Shastri** (born **2 October 1904**; 'Shastri' title from **Kashi Vidyapeeth, 1928**) became PM on **9 June 1964**. As home minister (**1961**) he formed the committee that led to the **Central Vigilance Commission**. He was the first PM to have a secretary (**L.K. Jha**), the seed of the PMO. Crises: **anti-Hindi agitation in Madras (1965)**, food shortages and the war with Pakistan. Slogan **'Jai Jawan Jai Kisan'**; **C. Subramaniam** laid the base of the **Green Revolution**; **APC and FCI (January 1965)**; **NDDB at Anand (1965)** with **Verghese Kurien**. Pakistan probed the **Rann of Kachchh (April 1965)**, then launched **Operation Grandslam** in Kashmir; ceasefire **23 September**. The **Tashkent Declaration** (**10 January 1966**, Soviet mediator **Kosygin**) had no no-war pact; Shastri died the next morning.

**Indira Gandhi** became the **third PM** and first woman PM on **19 January 1966**. She **devalued the rupee (June 1966, 36.5%)**; annual plans ran **1966-69**. After the **1967** polls (thin majority; SVD governments in states; **DMK** under **C.N. Annadurai** in Madras) the party split in **1969**: she backed **V.V. Giri** against official candidate **N. Sanjiva Reddy**, was expelled by **S. Nijalingappa**, and led **Congress (R) 'Requisitionists'** against **Congress (O)**. She nationalised **14 banks (July 1969)** by ordinance, passed the **MRTP Act (1969)**, and won the **1971** election on **'Garibi Hatao'** against the Grand Alliance's 'Indira Hatao'. Privy purses ended with the **26th Amendment (1971)**; **RAW (1968)** under **R.N. Kao**; **P.N. Haksar** first principal secretary.

**1971 war**: Pakistan attacked on **3 December**; **Operation Trident** hit Karachi; India recognised Bangladesh on **6 December**; **Niazi** surrendered to **Jagjit Singh Aurora** at **Ramna Race Course, Dacca, 16 December**. The **Indo-Soviet Treaty** was signed in **August 1971**; the **Simla Agreement (2 July 1972)** gave 'renunciation of force' instead of a no-war pact. **Sikkim** became the **22nd state (May 1975, 36th Amendment)**. **Pokhran I, 'Smiling Buddha'**, on **18 May 1974**.

**Bihar and the Emergency**: after Gujarat's **Nav Nirman** movement, **student protests in Bihar** were taken up by **Jayaprakash Narayan**, who called for **'Total Revolution'** and demanded the **Bihar Congress government resign and the assembly be dissolved**. On **12 June 1975** Justice **Sinha** (Allahabad HC), on **Raj Narain's** petition, voided Indira's 1971 election; Emergency was proclaimed under **Article 352(1)** on **25-26 June 1975** by President **Fakhruddin Ali Ahmed**. Leaders were held under **MISA**; **Sanjay Gandhi** drove sterilisation and demolitions; the **42nd Amendment (1976)** was a 'mini constitution'. Emergency ended **21 March 1977**.

**Janata (1977-80)**: Jan Sangh, Congress (O), BLD and Socialists won **330 of 542**; **Morarji Desai** became the first non-Congress PM (**23 March 1977**). The **Shah Commission** probed excesses; Indira's **Belchi (Bihar) visit on an elephant** revived her. **43rd (1977)** and **44th (1978)** Amendments undid the Emergency; the **Mandal Commission** (January 1979) was headed by **B.P. Mandal, ex-CM of Bihar**. **Charan Singh** never faced Parliament.

**Indira's second phase (1980-84)**: **IRDP (2 October 1980)**, **NABARD (1982)**, **Operation Meghdoot (April 1984)** in Siachen, **NAM summit at Delhi (1983)**, **Rakesh Sharma** in space (**April 1984**). In Punjab, **Operation Blue Star** (June 1984) killed **Bhindranwale**; Indira was shot by **Beant Singh and Satwant Singh** on **31 October 1984**.

### 1984-2019: Rajiv Gandhi to the NDA

**Rajiv Gandhi (Oct 1984–Dec 1989)** began with the **anti-Sikh riots** and the **Bhopal gas tragedy (Dec 3, 1984; methyl isocyanate, Union Carbide)**. The Congress won **over 400 seats** in **December 1984**. He signed accords with **Longowal (Punjab, July 1985)** and the **AASU (Assam, Aug 15, 1985)**, and **Mizoram became the 23rd state (Feb 1987)** under **Laldenga**. Reforms included the **anti-defection law (Jan 1985)**, the **Environment Protection Act (1986)**, **technology missions** (**Sam Pitroda**) and the **NPE 1986** (**Navodaya Vidyalayas**). The negatives were the **Shah Bano** reversal (**Muslim Women Act 1986**), the opening of the **Babri Mosque locks (1986)** and **Bofors**. Abroad: **Operation Cactus (Maldives)**, a **China visit (Dec 1988)** and the **Indo-Sri Lanka Accord (July 29, 1987)**, which sent the **IPKF**. The **LTTE** later **assassinated Rajiv at Sriperumbudur (May 21, 1991)**.

**V.P. Singh (Dec 1989–Nov 1990)** led the **National Front**, with outside support from the BJP and the Left. On **Aug 7, 1990** he accepted the **Mandal report**: **27% reservation for OBCs**. In **November 1992** the Supreme Court upheld it **7–3**, with a **50% cap** and **no caste quota in promotions**. **Advani's Rath Yatra** (from **Somnath**) was stopped in **Bihar by CM Lalu Prasad Yadav**, who had Advani arrested. The BJP withdrew support. **Chandra Shekhar (Nov 1990–June 1991)**, with only **64 MPs**, **pledged gold** in the balance-of-payments crisis.

**P.V. Narasimha Rao (1991–96)**, who won the **Nandyal** by-election, made **Manmohan Singh** (then **UGC Chairman**) his finance minister. Their **New Economic Policy 1991** devalued the rupee and brought the **SEBI Act 1992** and the **NSE**. The **73rd Amendment** added **Part IX and the Eleventh Schedule (29 subjects)**; the **74th** added **Part IX-A**. Rao launched the **Look East policy**. The **Babri Masjid was demolished on Dec 6, 1992**; the **Liberhan Commission** reported in **June 2009** and named **68 people culpable**.

**1996–99:** the BJP won **161 seats** in 1996 but **Vajpayee resigned after 13 days**. **Deve Gowda** and then **I.K. Gujral** led the **United Front**. Gujral is remembered for the **Gujral Doctrine** (five principles, **no reciprocity** asked of neighbours). During his term the **Fodder Scam** led **Lalu** to form the **RJD (July 1997)**; the **Jain Commission** report brought Gujral down. The **NDA (March 1998)** carried out **Pokhran II, Operation Shakti (May 1998; May 11 = National Technology Day)**, the **Lahore Declaration (Feb 1999)** and the **Kargil war (Operation Vijay; IAF 'Safed Sagar'; over by July 26, 1999)**. It lost a confidence vote **by one vote (April 17, 1999)**.

**NDA (1999–2004)** brought the **Golden Quadrilateral**, **PMGSY**, the **FRBM Act 2003**, the **86th Amendment (2002)** and **POTA**. It also faced the **Parliament attack (Dec 2001)** and **Godhra (Feb 2002)**. It was the **first non-Congress government to almost complete its term**. **UPA (2004–14)**, with **Manmohan Singh** as PM and **Sonia Gandhi** chairing the **NAC**, passed **NREGA (2006)**, the **RTI Act (2005)**, the **Indo-US nuclear deal (Oct 2008)**, the **RTE Act (2009)** and the **Lokpal Act (assent Jan 1, 2014)**, amid the **2G and coal scams**.

**NDA from 2014:** the **BJP won 282 seats** and the **Congress 44**. The government brought **NITI Aayog (Jan 1, 2015)**, the **JAM trinity**, **demonetisation (Nov 8, 2016)**, **GST (101st Amendment, 2016)** and **PM-KISAN (2019)**. On security there were the **Uri attack and surgical strikes (Sept 2016)**, **Pulwama (Feb 14, 2019)** and **Balakot**. In **2019** the **NDA won 353 seats (BJP 303)**.

## Key facts
### 1964-1984: Shastri, Indira Gandhi, Janata

- **Gulzarilal Nanda** was interim PM twice — after Nehru's death (**1964**) and after Shastri's death (**1966**).
- **Syndicate** — Congress group formed in **1963**, incl. party president **K. Kamaraj**; guided both successions.
- **Lal Bahadur Shastri**: born **2 October 1904**; title 'Shastri' from **Kashi Vidyapeeth, Varanasi (1928)**; sworn in as second PM on **9 June 1964**.
- As UP minister Shastri appointed **women bus conductors** and ordered **water jets instead of lathis**; resigned as railways minister in **1956** over a rail accident.
- As home minister (**1961**) Shastri formed the **Committee on Prevention of Corruption** headed by **K. Santhanam**, whose recommendations led to the **Central Vigilance Commission**.
- Shastri was the first PM to have a **secretary to the PM (L.K. Jha)** — foundation of the PMO.
- Shastri's three crises: **anti-Hindi agitation in Madras**, **food shortages**, **second war with Pakistan**.
- **'Jai Jawan Jai Kisan'** — Shastri's slogan during the 1965 war.
- Green Revolution base under Shastri with **C. Subramaniam**; **Dr B.P. Pal** — first scientist to head **ICAR**; **M.S. Swaminathan** (IARI) flagged **Norman Borlaug's** wheat; **250 tonnes** of wheat seed imported in **1965**.
- **Subramaniam-Freeman accord (Rome, November 1965)** — India to end food grain imports by **1971**.
- Green Revolution had a **threefold thrust: technological, economic and organisational**; Shastri also set in motion the **White Revolution** — a national campaign to raise milk production and supply.
- **Agricultural Prices Commission** and **Food Corporation of India** — **January 1965**.
- **NDDB** set up at **Anand (1965)**; Shastri had opened Amul's cattle feed factory at **Kanjari (October 1964)**; **Verghese Kurien**.
- US President **Lyndon B. Johnson** threatened to stop wheat under **PL-480**; Shastri urged people to **skip a meal** a week.
- Shastri inaugurated the **plutonium reprocessing plant at Trombay (1965)**; **SNEPP** set up at Homi Bhabha's initiative.
- **Bandaranaike-Shastri accord (1964)**: repatriation of **6,00,000 Indian Tamils** to India and **citizenship for 3,75,000 Tamils in Ceylon**, by **1981**; later considered lapsed; Shastri visited **Rangoon (December 1965)** — General **Ne Win**.
- 1965 war: clashes in **Rann of Kachchh (April 1965)**, ceasefire via **Britain**; **Operation Grandslam** (August) in Kashmir; defence minister **Y.B. Chavan**; UN ceasefire **23 September 1965**.
- **Tashkent Declaration** — **10 January 1966**, Ayub Khan and Shastri, mediated by **Alexei Kosygin**; withdrawal by **25 February 1966** to positions held before **5 August 1965**; no no-war pact.
- **Indira Gandhi** — born **19 November 1917, Allahabad**; at twelve led a children's group, the **Vanar Sena**; sons **Rajiv and Sanjay**; became **third PM (first woman PM) on 19 January 1966**.
- **1967** polls: thin Congress majority; **Morarji Desai** deputy PM and finance minister.
- **1969 presidential election**: official candidate **N. Sanjiva Reddy** lost to **V.V. Giri** (August 1969) after **Zakir Hussain's** death.
- **November 1969**: **S. Nijalingappa** expelled Indira; **Congress (R) = Requisitionists**; **Congress (O) = Organisation**, headed by **Ram Subhag Singh**.
- **1971** election: **'Garibi Hatao'** (Congress R) vs **'Indira Hatao'** (Grand Alliance of Swatantra, Congress-O, SSP); first time national polls were **delinked** from state polls.
- **14 banks nationalised (July 1969)** by ordinance; **6 more in 1980**.
- **Privy purses** (Article **291**) abolished by the **26th Amendment (1971)**, inserting **Article 363-A**; the 1969 bill failed in the Rajya Sabha **by one vote**.
- **MRTP Act (1969)** — after abolition of the managing agency system.
- **Oil companies nationalised (1973)** after they refused fuel to Indian forces in the 1971 war.
- **Rupee devalued June 1966 by 36.5%**; **annual plans 1966-69**; **Fourth Plan 1969-74**; **Fifth Plan 1974-79** (ended 1978).
- **RAW (1968)** — head **R.N. Kao**; **P.N. Haksar** — first **principal secretary** to a PM.
- **24th Amendment (1971)** — Parliament may amend any provision (Golaknath); **25th (1972)** — directive principles; **38th (1975)** — emergency satisfaction non-justiciable; **39th (1975)** — PM's election disputes to a special court.
- **A.N. Ray** made Chief Justice (**1973**) superseding three senior judges.
- **42nd Amendment (1976)** — 'mini constitution': **'socialist secular'** and **'integrity'** in Preamble, **fundamental duties**, Lok Sabha term **six years**, seats frozen at **1971 census**, President's rule without approval **one year**.
- **DMK** formed the first non-Congress government in Madras in **1967** under **C.N. Annadurai**.
- **CP(ML)** formed **1969**, led by **Charu Mazumdar**; **Naxalbari** rising **1967** under **Kanu Sanyal**.
- **Shiv Sena** founded **1966** by **Bal Thackeray**.
- **1966**: Punjab reorganised — **Haryana** formed; **Chandigarh** a shared union territory; **Darshan Singh Pherumal** fasted unto death.
- **MNF** under **Laldenga** revolted (**1966**); IAF strikes on **Aizawl**; **Mizoram UT January 1972**, state **1987**.
- **Nagaland** a state in **1963**; **Shillong Accord November 1975**.
- **Meghalaya, Manipur, Tripura** statehood **1972**; NEFA became UT **Arunachal Pradesh**.
- **Sikkim** — protectorate **1950**; **22nd state, May 1975**, by the **36th Amendment**.
- **Official Languages Act amended 1967** — de facto bilingualism.
- **1971 war**: Pakistan attacked **3 December 1971**; **Sam Manekshaw**; **Operation Trident** on Karachi by **Vice Admiral S.N. Kohli** (Western Naval Command); **Vice Admiral Nilakanta Krishnan** (Eastern) blockade; **INS Vikrant**.
- India recognised Bangladesh **6 December 1971**; US sent **Task Force 74 / USS Enterprise**.
- Surrender **16 December 1971** at **Ramna Race Course, Dacca** — **Jagjit Singh Aurora** and **A.A.K. Niazi**.
- **Indo-Soviet Treaty of Peace, Friendship and Cooperation — August 1971** (Swaran Singh, Gromyko).
- **Simla Agreement** — summit June 1972, signed **2 July 1972**; **'renunciation of force'** instead of no-war pact; LoC from the ceasefire of **17 December 1971**; withdrawal within **30 days**.
- **Katchatheevu** ceded to Sri Lanka in **1974**.
- **Pokhran I, 'Smiling Buddha'** — **18 May 1974**; **Raja Ramanna** (BARC director), **Homi Sethna** (AEC chairman); plutonium from Canada's **CIRUS** reactor; led to the **Nuclear Suppliers Group**.
- **Nav Nirman movement, Gujarat (December 1973-March 1974)** — CM **Chimanbhai Patel** resigned.
- **JP movement (Bihar)** began as **student protests**; JP called for **'Total Revolution'**, demanded the **Bihar Congress government resign and assembly be dissolved**; **non-left** opposition backed him.
- **All-India railway strike — May 1974**.
- **12 June 1975** — Justice **Sinha** (Allahabad HC) voided Indira's 1971 election on **Raj Narain's** petition; **V.R. Krishna Iyer** gave a conditional stay on **24 June**.
- Emergency under **Article 352(1)**, **25-26 June 1975**, President **Fakhruddin Ali Ahmed**; ended **21 March 1977**.
- Arrests under **MISA**; banned: **Anand Marg, RSS, Jamaat-i-Islami, CPI(ML)**; **CPI supported** the Emergency, **CPM opposed**.
- **Dev Kant Baruah** — "India is Indira, and Indira is India" (1976); **Guwahati AICC (1976)** — Sanjay Gandhi's debut.
- **Janata Party** = **Jan Sangh, Congress (O), BLD (Charan Singh), Socialist Party**; **CFD** (**Jagjivan Ram, Nandini Satpathy, H.N. Bahuguna**) allied and later merged.
- Janata won **330 of 542** seats (March 1977); symbol **'chakra-haldhar'**.
- **Morarji Desai** — first non-Congress PM, chosen by **Kripalani and JP**, sworn in **23 March 1977**.
- **N. Sanjiva Reddy** elected President **June 1977** after **Fakhruddin Ali Ahmed's** death (February 1977).
- **Shah Commission** (Justice **J.C. Shah**) probed Emergency excesses.
- **Belchi (Bihar), May 1977** — Harijans burnt; Indira reached the village, last stretch on an **elephant**.
- **Congress (I)** formed **January 1978**; Indira won from **Chikmagalur**.
- Desai government resigned **July 1979**; **Charan Singh** never faced Parliament (Indira withdrew support).
- **43rd Amendment (1977)** and **44th Amendment (1978)** (law minister **Shanti Bhushan**): 'armed rebellion' replaced 'internal disturbances'; **written advice of cabinet**; **Articles 20 and 21** never suspended; **right to property (Article 31)** removed from Fundamental Rights.
- Janata **Sixth Plan (1978-83)** — **'rolling plan'**; **Food for Work (April 1977)**; **George Fernandes** expelled **Coca-Cola and IBM**; **Madhu Dandavate** cushioned second-class sleepers.
- **Jimmy Carter** — first US president to visit India after Eisenhower; **Vajpayee** visited **Beijing (1979)**.
- **Mandal Commission** (Second Backward Classes Commission) — **January 1979**, chairman **Bindeshwari Prasad Mandal**, former **CM of Bihar**.
- 1980: Indira won **Rae Bareilly** and **Medak**, kept **Medak**; **Sanjay** died **June 1980**; **Rajiv** elected from **Amethi**.
- **New Sixth Plan 1980-85**; **IRDP — 2 October 1980**; **NREP** October 1980; **NABARD (1982)**, **Shivaraman Committee**; **Operation Forward (1982)**.
- Environment: **Wildlife Protection Act 1972**, **Project Tiger 1973**, **Water Act 1974**, **Forest (Conservation) Act 1980**, **Air Act 1981**.
- **Siachen**: undemarcated beyond **NJ9842**; **Operation Meghdoot (April 1984)**; India secured **Sia La and Bilfond La**, Pakistan kept **Gyong La**.
- **Rakesh Sharma** — **Soyuz T-11**, **2 April 1984**, to **Salyut 7**; "Sare jahan se achcha".
- **NAM summit, Delhi, 1983** — Indira its chairperson.
- **Jharkhand** statehood movement (then part of **Bihar**); **NSCN** set up by **Muivah**; **Telugu Desam (1982)** — **N.T. Rama Rao**.
- Punjab: **Anandpur Resolution (1973)**; **Bhindranwale** — head of **Damdami Taksal**; **Khalistan** declared **June 1980** (president **Jagjit Singh Chauhan**, London); Akali MLAs resigned on **Republic Day 1983**.
- **Operation Blue Star (June 1984)** — recommended by Army Chief **A.S. Vaidya**; troops under **Maj. Gen. K.S. Brar**, directed by **Gen. K. Sundarji**; **Bhindranwale** and **Shubeg Singh** killed.
- Indira assassinated **31 October 1984** by **Beant Singh and Satwant Singh**; **Rajiv Gandhi** sworn in by President **Zail Singh**.
- ⚠ May be outdated: **42nd Amendment froze seats at the 1971 census till the first census after 2000** — the freeze was later extended (to the first census after 2026).
- ⚠ May be outdated: **MRTP Act (1969)** and **FERA** — MRTP was repealed and replaced by the Competition Act, and FERA by FEMA.
- ⚠ May be outdated: **Five-year plans / Planning Commission** — the Planning Commission was replaced by NITI Aayog (2015) and five-year plans ended in 2017.
- ⚠ May be outdated: **Indira Gandhi declared J&K a constituent of India (1975)** and the National Conference/state set-up — J&K's special status (Article 370) was ended and the state reorganised into union territories in 2019.

### 1984-2019: Rajiv Gandhi to the NDA

- **Bhopal gas tragedy** — **December 3, 1984**, **methyl isocyanate (MIC)** gas from the **Union Carbide** plant; UCC paid **USD 470 million** as a full and final settlement.
- **Bhopal Gas Leak Disaster Act (March 1985)** made the **government the sole representative** of the victims.
- **December 1984** election: Congress (I) won **more than 400 seats**; Rajiv Gandhi was the **youngest** PM.
- **Rajiv–Longowal accord: July 1985** (Longowal shot dead in August); **Operation Black Thunder: May 1988** (police under **J.F. Ribeiro and K.P.S. Gill**).
- **Assam Accord** with the **AASU**: **August 15, 1985**; the AASU became the **Asom Gana Parishad** and won the December elections.
- **Mizoram** became the **23rd state in February 1987**, with **Laldenga (Mizo National Front)** as chief minister.
- **GNLF** (Darjeeling) under **Subhash Ghisingh** accepted an **autonomous district council**; Tripura **TNV** signed an MoU in **August 1988**.
- **Rigged 1987 J&K elections** (against the **Muslim United Front**) marked the **beginning of militancy** in Kashmir.
- **Anti-defection law** — **January 1985**; **Environment Protection Act** — **1986**; **Public Liability Insurance Act** — **1991**.
- **NREP and RLEGP** were merged into the **Jawahar Rozgar Yojana** in **April 1989**.
- **Six technology missions** (drinking water, milk, edible oil, immunisation, literacy, telecom); **Sam Pitroda** chaired the **Telecom Commission**; **MTNL** was set up.
- **NPE 1986** (the first NPE was **1968**) brought **Operation Blackboard**, **IGNOU** and **Navodaya Vidyalayas** in every district.
- **Shah Bano (1985)**: the SC applied **Section 125 CrPC**; the **Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Divorce) Act, 1986** reversed it and limited maintenance to the **iddat** period; **Arif Mohammed Khan resigned** in protest.
- **Faizabad district judge** ordered the **Babri Mosque locks** opened on **February 1, 1986**.
- **Bofors deal (March 1986)**: **400 155mm howitzers**, **USD 285 million (about Rs 1500 crore)**; a **Swedish radio** report in **April 1987** alleged bribes; **Chitra Subramaniam** of **The Hindu** investigated; **Ottavio Quattrocchi** (**Snamprogetti**) was named.
- Farmers' bodies: **Shetkari Sanghatana — Sharad Joshi (Maharashtra)**; **Bharatiya Kisan Union — Mahendra Singh Tikait (Punjab, Haryana)**.
- **Operation Flowers are Blooming — Seychelles (1986)**; **Operation Cactus — Maldives (1988)**; **Operation Rajiv — Quaid Post, Siachen (1987)**; **Operation Poomalai (Eagle Mission 4) — Jaffna air-drop, June 4, 1987**.
- **December 1988**: Rajiv Gandhi became the **first PM since Nehru (1954)** to visit **China**, where he met **Deng Xiaoping**; a **Joint Working Group** was set up on the boundary.
- **Indo-Sri Lanka Accord**: **Rajiv Gandhi and J.R. Jayawardene**, Colombo, **July 29, 1987**; it sent the **IPKF**.
- **Rajiv Gandhi** was assassinated by an **LTTE suicide bomber** at **Sriperumbudur (Tamil Nadu)** on **May 21, 1991**.
- **Janata Dal** was formed from **Jan Morcha (V.P. Singh)**, the Janata Party, Lok Dal and Congress (S); its coalition with regional parties was the **National Front**.
- **1989** was the **first election in which no single party won a majority**; **V.P. Singh** was sworn in on **December 2, 1989** and **recalled the IPKF**.
- **JKLF** kidnapped the daughter of Home Minister **Mufti Mohammad Sayeed** (December 1989); **Jagmohan** became governor; the **Kashmiri Pandits** fled the valley.
- **Mandal Commission** (officially the **Backward Classes Commission**) reported in **December 1980**; **V.P. Singh** announced **27% OBC reservation** on **August 7, 1990**.
- **Supreme Court (November 1992)**: **seven judges upheld** the order and **three dissented**; it set a **50% ceiling** and **no caste criterion in promotions**.
- **Rao government order (September 1991)**: within the 27%, **preference to poorer OBCs**.
- **Rise of Lalu Yadav (Bihar)** and **Mulayam Singh Yadav (UP)** as OBC leaders was part of V.P. Singh's Mandal calculation.
- **L.K. Advani's Ram Rath Yatra** began at **Somnath (September 1990)**; **Bihar CM Lalu Prasad Yadav** had Advani **arrested in Bihar**; **Mulayam Singh's** UP government arrested the kar sevaks.
- **Chandra Shekhar and Devi Lal** formed the **Samajwadi Janata Party**; Chandra Shekhar was sworn in on **November 10, 1990** with **64 MPs**.
- **Gold was pledged (March 1991)** under Chandra Shekhar (finance minister **Yashwant Sinha**) to meet the **balance-of-payments crisis**.
- **1991 election**: Congress won **244 seats**; **Narasimha Rao** entered the Lok Sabha through a by-election from **Nandyal (Andhra Pradesh)**.
- **Manmohan Singh** was **Chairman of the UGC** when Rao made him finance minister; **Rao himself held the industries portfolio**.
- **1991 reforms**: rupee devalued, **Industrial Policy 1991**, **MRTP Act** modified, **Controller of Capital Issues abolished**, **SEBI Act 1992**, **NSE** started.
- **73rd Amendment**: **Part IX** and the **Eleventh Schedule (29 subjects)**; **74th Amendment**: **Part IX-A** (municipalities).
- **Look East policy** (towards **ASEAN**) was launched by **Rao**; Rao visited **China in 1993**.
- **Babri Masjid demolished on December 6, 1992**; **Kalyan Singh** was UP CM; **Liberhan Commission** (appointed ten days later) reported in **June 2009** and named **68 people culpable**.
- **JMM bribery case**: in the **July 1993** no-confidence vote, JMM and **Janata Dal (Ajit Singh)** members voted against the motion; **Rao and Buta Singh** were convicted, then **cleared in 2002**.
- **Kanshi Ram** founded **BAMCEF**, then the **BSP**; **Mayawati** became the **first Dalit woman CM** of UP in **June 1995** (and again **2007–2012**).
- **1996**: BJP won **161 seats** (187 with allies **Samata Party, Shiv Sena and Haryana Vikas Party**); **Vajpayee resigned after 13 days**.
- **United Front**: **Deve Gowda** (Ganga water accord with Bangladesh), then **I.K. Gujral (sworn in April 21, 1997)**.
- **Gujral Doctrine**: five principles; with Nepal, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Maldives and Sri Lanka, India **does not ask for reciprocity**.
- **Fodder Scam**: the **Governor of Bihar** allowed a CBI case against CM **Lalu Prasad Yadav**, who formed the **Rashtriya Janata Dal (July 1997)**; the RJD **kept supporting** the United Front.
- **Jain Commission** (on the Rajiv Gandhi assassination conspiracy) criticised the **DMK**; the Congress withdrew support from Gujral on **November 28, 1997**.
- **NDA**: Vajpayee sworn in **March 1998**; lost the confidence vote **by one vote on April 17, 1999** after the **AIADMK** withdrew (**Giridhar Gamang's** vote).
- **Pokhran II (Operation Shakti)**: **five** tests in **May 1998**; **A.P.J. Abdul Kalam** (DRDO) and **R. Chidambaram** (DAE); **May 11 = National Technology Day**; the US applied the **Glenn Amendment** for the first time; Pakistan answered with **Chagai I and II**.
- **Delhi–Lahore bus** and the **Lahore Declaration** (**Vajpayee and Nawaz Sharif**): **February 1999**.
- **Kargil**: incursion planned under **General Pervez Musharraf**; Army's **Operation Vijay**, IAF's **Safed Sagar**; **Tiger Hill (Drass)** overlooks the **Srinagar–Leh highway**; war over by **July 26, 1999**.
- **Vajpayee sworn in a third time on October 13, 1999**; **JD(U)** and **DMK** were new NDA constituents.
- **NDA (1999–2004)**: **Golden Quadrilateral**, **PMGSY**, **VSNL privatised**, **Disinvestment Commission upgraded to a ministry**, **FRBM Act 2003**, **Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan**, **86th Amendment 2002 (right to education)**.
- **IC 814** hijack (**December 1999**, **Masood Azhar** released); **Agra summit** with **Musharraf (July 2001)**; **Parliament attack (December 2001)** by **LeT and JeM**, followed by **POTA**.
- **Godhra**: **Sabarmati Express** fire in **February 2002**; **Narendra Modi** was Gujarat CM.
- The NDA was the **first non-Congress government to last almost its full term**; it lost in **2004** after the **'India Shining'** campaign.
- **UPA**: **Manmohan Singh** (a Rajya Sabha member) sworn in **May 22, 2004**; **Sonia Gandhi** chaired the **NAC**; the **Common Minimum Programme** was seen as **left-of-centre**.
- **NREGA (2006)**: **100 days** of paid work for rural households, with **one-third of the work for women**; **NRHM (2005)**; **RTI Act (2005)**: reply **within thirty days**; **VAT (2005)**.
- **Indo-US civil nuclear agreement** signed in **October 2008**; the UPA won a **trust vote in July 2008** after the **Left withdrew support**; **Nathula Pass reopened in 2006**.
- **Pratibha Patil** became the **first woman President (2007)**.
- **26/11 Mumbai attack (2008)**: **Ajmal Amir Kasab** was captured and **hanged in 2012**; the **NIA** was set up in **December 2008**.
- In **2009**, Manmohan Singh became the **first PM since Nehru (1962)** re-elected after completing a full term.
- **Telangana**: **KCR's fast (November 2009)**; **Srikrishna Commission** report (**December 2010**) rejected by the TRS; **Telangana Bill cleared February 2014**; the state came into existence in **June 2014**.
- **West Bengal (May 2011)**: the **Left Front** (in power since **1977**) lost to **Mamata Banerjee's TMC**.
- **RTE Act 2009** (giving effect to **Article 21-A**, in force from **April 1, 2010**): **25%** of seats in non-minority private schools for weaker sections.
- **Women's reservation bill (33%)** was shelved because allies, **especially the RJD**, refused to support it.
- **Justice J.S. Verma Committee** (after the **December 2012** Delhi gang rape): did **not recommend the death penalty**, wanted the **marital rape exception removed**, and called **'failure of governance'** the root cause; **Criminal Law (Amendment) Act, March 2013**.
- **Mangalyaan (Mars Orbiter Mission)** launched in **November 2013**; India was the **first to reach Mars orbit on its first attempt (September 24, 2014)**.
- **Anna Hazare** (from **Ralegaon Siddhi**) fasted in **April 2011**; **Arvind Kejriwal** formed the **AAP on November 26, 2012**.
- **Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act 2013**: received **assent on January 1, 2014** (President **Pranab Mukherjee**); the **PM is included**; criticised as **not a constitutional body** and for **excluding the judiciary**.
- **2014 election**: BJP **282 seats** (vote share about **19% → 31%**), Congress **44** (its worst); **all SAARC heads** attended the swearing-in on **May 26, 2014**.
- **Digital India**: **August 2014**; **UPI** and **BHIM** developed.
- **Planning Commission scrapped in August 2014**; **NITI Aayog** announced on **January 1, 2015** (by **executive order**; it does **not allocate funds**).
- **JAM** = **Jan Dhan, Aadhaar, Mobile**; **PM Jan Dhan Yojana** launched **August 24, 2014**; **Aadhaar Act, March 2016** (Aadhaar was launched by the UPA in **January 2009**).
- **Swachh Bharat Mission** — **October 2, 2014**; **Make in India** — **September 2014**; **Ayushman Bharat** — **2018**; **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao** and **Sukanya Samriddhi** — **2015**.
- **Laws of 2016–18**: **IBC 2016**; **Benami Transactions (Prohibition) Amendment Act 2016** (1–7 years' jail); **RERA 2016**; **Fugitive Economic Offenders Act (April 2018)**; **HELP 2016** (uniform hydrocarbon licence).
- **Demonetisation**: **November 8, 2016** (Rs 500 and Rs 1,000 notes); **PMGKY (December 2016)** charged a **50%** fine on undisclosed income.
- **GST**: **Constitution (101st Amendment) Act, 2016**; the **GST Council** is made up of the central and state finance ministers; **five petroleum products and alcohol** were left out.
- **PM-KISAN (2019)**: **Rs 6,000 a year** to farmers with **less than five acres**.
- **J&K**: the **PDP–BJP government** (March 2015) was the **first time the BJP was in government in the state**; **Mehbooba Mufti** became the **first woman CM (April 2016)**; the BJP pulled out in **June 2018**.
- **Burhan Wani** was killed in **July 2016**; **Pathankot** attack **January 2016**; **Uri** attack **September 18, 2016**, followed by **surgical strikes on September 29, 2016**.
- **Pulwama (February 14, 2019)**: **40 CRPF personnel** killed (**JeM**); IAF **Mirage** jets struck **Balakot** on **February 26**; **Wing Commander Abhinandan Varthaman** was captured and released two days later.
- **Doklam stand-off (2017)**: at the junction of **India, Bhutan and China**, close to the **Siliguri Corridor**.
- **Sushma Swaraj** was the **first woman to hold independent charge of External Affairs (2014)**; **LEMOA (2016)** and **COMCASA (2018)** were signed with the US; **Look East** became **Act East**.
- India joined the **MTCR (2016)**, **Wassenaar Arrangement (2017)** and the **Australia Group**; its **NSG** bid was pending because **China opposed** it; India co-founded the **International Solar Alliance** with **France**.
- **Section 377** (the book writes 'Article 377'): homosexuality was **decriminalised by the SC in 2018** (the **Naz Foundation** case began in **2001**; the Delhi HC ruled in **2009**; the SC reversed it in **2013**); the **right to privacy** was upheld in **August 2017**.
- **2019 election**: NDA **353**, BJP **303**, Congress **52**; **Ram Nath Kovind** administered the oath on **May 30, 2019**; **S. Jaishankar** became foreign minister and **Amit Shah** home minister; 'Sabka Vishwas' was added to 'Sabka Saath, Sabka Vikas'.
