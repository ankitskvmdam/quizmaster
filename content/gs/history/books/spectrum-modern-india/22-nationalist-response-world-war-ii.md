## Background

1. After civil disobedience, Gandhi felt the Congress had to set its house in order before any new struggle.
2. Subhash Chandra Bose wanted action. He was the president of the Bengal Provincial Congress Committee (PCC).

## Bose and the Congress (1938–39)

1. **February 1938:** The Haripura session was held in Gujarat. Subhash Bose was unanimously elected president. He spoke of planning.
2. Later he set up a National Planning Committee.
3. As Congress president (1938), Bose insisted on a separate planning commission for women. It was chaired by Rani Lakshmi Bhai Rajawade.
4. **January 1939:** Bose stood again in the 1939 presidential election. He defeated Gandhi's candidate Pattabhi Sitaramayya by 1580 votes to 1377. Gandhi said, "Pattabhi's defeat is my defeat."
5. **March 1939:** The Tripuri session was held in the Central Provinces, near Jabalpur. Bose proposed a six-month ultimatum to Britain.
6. At Tripuri, Govind Ballabh Pant moved a resolution. It reaffirmed faith in Gandhian policies. It asked Bose to nominate the working committee "in accordance with the wishes of Gandhiji".
7. Tripuri also approved a Congress Medical Mission to China.
8. **April 1939:** Bose resigned. Rajendra Prasad became Congress president.
9. **May 1939:** Bose formed the Forward Bloc at Makur, Unnao.
10. **August 1939:** Bose was removed as Bengal PCC president. He was debarred from elective Congress office for three years.

## Gandhi and Bose: how their ideas differed

| Point | Gandhi | Bose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Method | Non-violence | Force |
| Emphasis | Means | Ends |
| Ideal | Ramrajya and village republics | 'Samyavada' and an initially authoritarian state |
| Economy | Trusteeship | State-led industrialisation |

### Gandhi's ideas

- **Hind Swaraj (1909):** Gandhi's political ideas are tied to this work.
- **Village panchayat:** He spoke of a village panchayat of five persons.
- **Duties:** He put more emphasis on duties than rights.
- **Theory of trusteeship:** Capitalists were to act as trustees of wealth.
- **Nai Talim:** This means basic education. Gandhi conceived it in 1937. He wanted free, compulsory education for ages 7 to 14.

### Bose's ideas

- **Samyavada:** This was Bose's synthesis of Socialism and Fascism. It meant Socialism plus Fascist discipline. He stated it as early as 1930.
- **1943:** Bose formed the Rani of Jhansi Regiment. It was commanded by Captain Lakshmi Swaminathan (Sahgal).
- **21 October 1943:** The Provisional Government of Azad Hind was proclaimed.

### What each said of the other

- **1942:** Gandhi called Bose the "Prince among the Patriots".
- **1944:** Bose called Gandhi "The Father of Our Nation". He said this in a radio broadcast from Rangoon.

## The war and the Congress (1939–40)

1. **1 September 1939:** Germany attacked Poland and the war broke out. The Government of India declared India's support without consulting Indians.
2. The Congress offered conditional support. It set two conditions: a constituent assembly after the war, and a responsible government at the Centre immediately. Viceroy Linlithgow rejected the offer.
3. The Congress Working Committee (CWC) met at Wardha. Gandhi wanted unconditional support. Bose and the socialists Acharya Narendra Dev and Jayaprakash Narayan wanted a struggle. Nehru's middle position was adopted.
4. **17 October 1939:** Linlithgow's statement came. It was negative.
5. **23 October 1939:** The CWC asked the Congress ministries to resign.
6. Secretary of State Zetland branded the Congress a purely Hindu organisation.
7. **January 1940:** Linlithgow spoke of "Dominion status of Westminster variety, after the war".
8. **March 1940:** The Ramgarh session was held with Maulana Abul Kalam Azad as president. It demanded nothing short of complete independence.
9. **March 1940:** The Muslim League passed the Pakistan Resolution at Lahore. It is also called the Lahore Resolution.

## August Offer (August 1940)

The August Offer was the first recognition of the Indians' inherent right to frame their constitution.

### What it offered

- Dominion status.
- An executive council with an Indian majority.
- A constituent assembly after the war, made up "mainly" of Indians.
- No constitution without the minorities' consent.

### Response

- The Congress rejected the offer.
- Nehru said, "Dominion status concept is dead as a doornail."

### Viceroy's council (July 1941)

- Indians got 8 out of 12 seats in the Viceroy's executive council. This was an Indian majority for the first time.
- A National Defence Council was set up. It was advisory.

## Individual satyagraha (October 1940)

1. **October 1940:** Individual satyagraha began. The first satyagrahi was Vinoba Bhave. The second was Nehru.
2. It was also called the 'Delhi Chalo Movement'.
3. **May 1941:** By this month 25,000 people had been convicted.
4. Around this time Gandhi named Nehru as his successor. Rajmohan Gandhi explains this choice in The Good Boatman.

## Cripps Mission (March 1942)

Stafford Cripps led the mission. He was a left-wing Labourite and a member of the War Cabinet.

### What it offered

- Dominion status, with the right to leave the Commonwealth.
- A constituent assembly after the war.
- A provincial right to secede. Secession needed a 60 per cent vote of the legislature. Otherwise there would be a plebiscite (a direct vote) of adult males.
- Defence stayed in British hands.

### Response

- Everyone objected to the scheme.
- The Congress negotiators with Cripps were Nehru and Maulana Azad.
- Gandhi called the Cripps scheme "a post-dated cheque".
- Cripps was undermined by Churchill, Amery, Linlithgow and Ward.
- The talks broke down over the viceroy's veto.
