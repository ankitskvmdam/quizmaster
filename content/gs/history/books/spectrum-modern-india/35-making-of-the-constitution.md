## Background: roots in the national movement

- The Indian Constitution is described as the longest in the world.
- It was framed between December 1946 and December 1949. But its roots lie in the national movement.
- One view is that it merely culminated the British reforms of 1861, 1892, 1909, 1919 and 1935. Mridula Mukherjee rejects this view in the book India Since Independence.

## Early milestones, 1895 to 1928

1. **1895:** The Constitution of India Bill was prepared. It is also called the Home Rule Bill. Annie Besant believed it was inspired by Tilak.
2. **1922:** Gandhi wrote the article 'Independence' in Young India.
3. **February 1924:** Motilal Nehru moved the 'National Demand' resolution in the Central Legislative Assembly.
4. **December 1925:** The Commonwealth of India Bill had its first reading in the House of Commons. It was defeated. The names linked with the Bill are Annie Besant, Tej Bahadur Sapru and V.S. Srinivasa Shastri.
5. **November 1927:** The all-white Simon Commission came.
6. **August 1928:** The Nehru Report came out.

The Nehru Report had five main points:

- dominion status (self-rule within the British Empire);
- sovereignty of the people;
- fundamental rights;
- residuary powers (powers not assigned to centre or provinces) with the centre;
- joint electorates (all communities voting together).

## The demand for a constituent assembly, 1933 to 1946

1. M.N. Roy was the first to suggest a constituent assembly.
2. **1933:** Jawaharlal Nehru enunciated the idea. He was the first national leader to do so.
3. **1934:** The Congress adopted the demand as its official policy.
4. **1936:** The Lucknow session rejected any imposed constitution.
5. **1937:** The Congress provinces and Sind passed resolutions. These were drafted under Acharya Kripalani.
6. **September 1937:** S. Satyamurti moved the constituent-assembly resolution in the Central Legislative Assembly.
7. **1938:** The Haripura session repeated the demand.
8. **1942:** The Cripps Proposals conceded the demand for the first time. Indians were to frame the constitution through a constituent assembly.
9. **15 March 1946:** The Cabinet Mission set up the Constituent Assembly.

## The Constituent Assembly

### How it was formed

- **Election:** The members were indirectly elected by the provincial assemblies.
- **Ratio:** There was one member per million of the population.
- **Method:** The method was proportional representation with single transferable vote.
- **Franchise:** The franchise (the right to vote) of 1935 kept out over 70 per cent of adults.
- **Parties:** The Congress won 208 seats. The Muslim League won 73 seats.

### First steps, December 1946 to July 1947

1. **9 December 1946:** The Assembly met for the first time. It met in the Constitution Hall, now the Central Hall of Parliament House.
2. Dr Sachchidananda Sinha was the provisional president. He was the oldest member and came from Bihar. J.B. Kripalani proposed his name.
3. **11 December 1946:** Dr Rajendra Prasad, also from Bihar, was elected President of the Constituent Assembly.
4. **13 December 1946:** Nehru moved the Objectives Resolution.
5. **26 June 1947:** A separate assembly for Pakistan was announced.
6. **18 July 1947:** The Indian Independence Act, 1947 came into force. It made the Assembly fully sovereign. Section 8 gave it full legislative power.
7. **22 July 1947:** The National Flag was adopted.

⚠ May be outdated: the Constitution Hall is called the Central Hall of Parliament House. Parliament moved to a new building in 2023. The old building has since been renamed.

### Drafting and adoption, October 1947 to January 1950

1. **October 1947:** B.N. Rau, the Constitutional Adviser, prepared a draft. It had 240 clauses and 13 schedules.
2. The Drafting Committee worked under its chairman, Dr B.R. Ambedkar. N. Madhava Rao replaced B.L. Mitter on it. T.T. Krishnamachari replaced D.P. Khaitan, who died in 1948.
3. **21 February 1948:** The committee's second draft was placed. It had 315 Articles and 9 Schedules.
4. About 7000 amendments were proposed. Of these, 2500 were discussed.
5. The Preamble was adopted last.
6. **26 November 1949:** The Constitution was adopted. The work had taken 2 years, 11 months and 18 days.
7. **24 January 1950:** This was the last day of the Assembly. 284 members signed the Constitution. The National Anthem and the National Song were adopted. Prasad was elected the first President of India.
8. **26 January 1950:** The Constitution came into effect.

### How decisions were taken

- Decisions were taken by consensus.
- Granville Austin said that Nehru, Patel, Prasad and Azad formed a 'virtual oligarchy' (rule by a few).
