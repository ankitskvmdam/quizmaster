## Gist
*Book pages 610–618.*

The Indian Constitution came into effect on **January 26, 1950** and is described as the **longest in the world**. It was framed between **December 1946 and December 1949**, but its roots lie in the national movement. **Mridula Mukherjee** (*India Since Independence*) rejects the view that it merely culminated British reforms of 1861, 1892, 1909, 1919 and 1935.

Early milestones: the **Constitution of India Bill (Home Rule Bill), 1895**, which **Annie Besant** believed was inspired by **Tilak**; **Gandhi's 1922 article 'Independence' in Young India**; **Motilal Nehru's 'National Demand' resolution (February 1924)** in the Central Legislative Assembly; the **Commonwealth of India Bill** (Besant, Tej Bahadur Sapru, V.S. Srinivasa Shastri), first read in the House of Commons in **December 1925** and defeated; the all-white **Simon Commission (November 1927)**; and the **Nehru Report (August 1928)** — dominion status, sovereignty of the people, fundamental rights, **residuary powers with the centre** and **joint electorates**.

**M.N. Roy** first suggested a constituent assembly, but **Jawaharlal Nehru** was the first national leader to enunciate it (**1933**); the Congress adopted the demand in **1934**. The **Lucknow session (1936)** rejected any imposed constitution; in **1937** Congress provinces and **Sind** passed resolutions drafted under **Acharya Kripalani**, and **S. Satyamurti** moved one in the Central Assembly; **Haripura (1938)** repeated it. The **Cripps Proposals (1942)** first conceded a constituent assembly; the **Cabinet Mission (March 15, 1946)** set it up.

Members were **indirectly elected** by provincial assemblies, **one per million** population, by **proportional representation with single transferable vote**. Strength: **389** — **296** from British India (292 from 11 Governors' Provinces + 4 Chief Commissioners' Provinces: Delhi, Ajmer-Merwara, Coorg, British Baluchistan) and **93** from princely states. Congress won **208** seats, the Muslim League **73**. The 1935 franchise kept out **over 70 per cent** of adults.

The Assembly first met on **December 9, 1946**; **Dr Sachchidananda Sinha** (oldest member, from **Bihar**) was **provisional president**, and **Dr Rajendra Prasad** (Bihar) was elected **President on December 11**. **Nehru moved the Objectives Resolution on December 13, 1946**. A separate Pakistan assembly was announced on **June 26, 1947**; the **Indian Independence Act** (in force **July 18, 1947**) made the Assembly fully sovereign (**Section 8**: full legislative power).

**B.N. Rau** (constitutional adviser) drafted **240 clauses and 13 schedules** (Oct 1947); the **Drafting Committee under Dr B.R. Ambedkar** produced a second draft of **315 Articles and 9 Schedules**, placed on **February 21, 1948**. About **7000** amendments were proposed, **2500** discussed; the **Preamble was adopted last**. The Constitution was adopted on **November 26, 1949** after **2 years, 11 months and 18 days**. On **January 24, 1950** (last day) **284 members signed**, the National Anthem and Song were adopted and **Prasad was elected first President of India**; the flag had been adopted on **July 22, 1947**. **Granville Austin** called Nehru, Patel, Prasad and Azad a **'virtual oligarchy'**; decisions were by **consensus**.

## Key facts
- Constitution came into effect on **January 26, 1950**; adopted on **November 26, 1949**.
- **Constitution of India Bill / Home Rule Bill (1895)** — Annie Besant believed it was inspired by **Tilak**.
- **National Demand** resolution moved by **Motilal Nehru, February 1924**, in the Central Legislative Assembly.
- **Commonwealth of India Bill** — Besant, Sapru, Srinivasa Shastri; first reading in the Commons **December 1925**; defeated.
- **Nehru Report (August 1928)** — dominion status, **residuary powers with the centre**, **joint electorates**.
- Constituent assembly idea first suggested by **M.N. Roy**; first enunciated by a national leader, **Nehru, in 1933**; Congress official policy **1934**.
- **S. Satyamurti** moved the constituent-assembly resolution in the Central Legislative Assembly (**September 1937**).
- **Cripps Proposals (1942)** conceded framing of the constitution by Indians through a constituent assembly.
- Assembly strength **389**: **296** British India + **93** princely states; **one member per million** population.
- Elected by **proportional representation with single transferable vote**; Congress **208**, Muslim League **73** seats.
- First meeting **December 9, 1946** in the Constitution Hall (now **Central Hall of Parliament House**).
- **Dr Sachchidananda Sinha** — **provisional president** (oldest member); proposed by **J.B. Kripalani**.
- **Dr Rajendra Prasad** — elected **President of the Constituent Assembly on December 11, 1946**.
- **Objectives Resolution** — moved by **Nehru, December 13, 1946**.
- Indian Independence Act, 1947 in force **July 18, 1947**; **Section 8** gave the Assembly full legislative power.
- Constitutional Adviser **B.N. Rau** — draft of **240 clauses, 13 schedules** (October 1947).
- Drafting Committee chairman **Dr B.R. Ambedkar**; **N. Madhava Rao** replaced B.L. Mitter; **T.T. Krishnamachari** replaced D.P. Khaitan (died 1948).
- Second draft: **315 Articles, 9 Schedules**, placed **February 21, 1948**.
- About **7000** amendments proposed, **2500** discussed; **Preamble adopted last**; took **2 years, 11 months, 18 days**.
- **284** members signed on **January 24, 1950**, the Assembly's last day.
- National Flag adopted **July 22, 1947**; National Anthem and Song **January 24, 1950**.
- **Granville Austin** — Nehru, Patel, Prasad, Azad formed a **'virtual oligarchy'**.
- ⚠ May be outdated: the Constitution Hall is called the **Central Hall of Parliament House** — Parliament moved to a new building in 2023 and the old building has since been renamed.
