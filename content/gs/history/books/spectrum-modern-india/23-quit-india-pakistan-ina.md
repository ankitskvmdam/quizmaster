## Gist
*Book pages 447–461.*

After the Cripps Mission failed, the **CWC at Wardha (14 July 1942)** accepted the idea of a struggle. Reasons included Britain's unchanged attitude, rising prices, British reverses in South-East Asia, the racist evacuation there (**'Black Road' for Indians, 'White Road' for Europeans**) and the need to prepare the masses for a Japanese invasion. The **Quit India resolution**, **proposed by Nehru and seconded by Patel**, was ratified at **Gowalia Tank, Bombay, on 8 August 1942**. Gandhi, named leader, gave the mantra **'Do or Die'**.

Early on **9 August 1942** all top leaders were arrested. Congress bodies were banned under the **Criminal Law Amendment Act, 1908**. **Aruna Asaf Ali** presided over the session and hoisted the flag. Violence against symbols of authority was **most intense in eastern UP and Bihar**. Underground leaders included **Rammanohar Lohia, Jayaprakash Narayan, Aruna Asaf Ali, Usha Mehta** (who ran an **underground radio in Bombay**), **Biju Patnaik** and **Sucheta Kripalani**. Parallel governments came up at **Ballia** (August 1942, **Chittu Pandey**), **Tamluk, Midnapore** (**Jatiya Sarkar**, December 1942 to September 1944) and **Satara** (**Prati Sarkar**, mid-1943 to 1945, **Y.B. Chavan, Nana Patil**). Communists, the **Muslim League** and the **Hindu Mahasabha** stayed out. About **10,000** people were killed. The **main storm centres** were **eastern UP, Bihar, Midnapore, Maharashtra and Karnataka**.

Gandhi's **fast of February 1943** was directed against the violence of the State; **three members of the viceroy's executive council resigned**. **Pakistan Day** was observed on **23 March 1943**. The **Bengal famine of 1943** killed **1.5–3 million** people. It was largely man-made: food went to the army, Burma rice imports stopped, and supplies were mismanaged.

The **C. Rajagopalachari formula (1944)**, backed by Gandhi, proposed a **post-war plebiscite of the entire population** of Muslim-majority areas. Jinnah wanted **only Muslims** to vote and rejected a common centre, and **Vir Savarkar** condemned it. The **Desai–Liaqat proposal** (Bhulabhai Desai and Liaqat Ali Khan) offered **parity** between Congress and League nominees and **20% seats for minorities**. The **Wavell Plan** was discussed at the **Shimla Conference (June 1945)**. It proposed an all-Indian council except the governor-general and commander-in-chief, with **equal caste Hindu and Muslim representation**. The League demanded to nominate all Muslim members, and Wavell's announcement that talks had broken down gave the League a **virtual veto**.

**Subhash Bose** (fourth in the ICS; resigned **1921**; guru **C.R. Das**; Calcutta mayor **1923**) held an **Anti-Compromise Conference at Ramgarh (March 1940)**. He escaped and reached **Peshawar on 26 January 1941 as 'Ziauddin'**, then met Hitler as **'Orlando Mazzotta'**. The **INA** was first the idea of **Mohan Singh** (first division **September 1942**, **16,300** men). **Rashbehari Bose** handed the **Indian Independence League** and the INA to Subhash in **July 1943**. The **Provisional Government of Free India** was formed at **Singapore on 21 October 1943**, and Andaman and Nicobar were renamed **Shahid Dweep and Swaraj Dweep**. **Colonel Malik** hoisted the INA flag at **Moirang, Manipur (14 April 1944)**. Japan surrendered on **15 August 1945**, and Bose reportedly died in an air crash at **Taipei on 18 August 1945**.

## Key facts
- **CWC at Wardha, 14 July 1942** — accepted the idea of a struggle.
- Quit India resolution **proposed by Nehru, seconded by Sardar Patel**; ratified at **Gowalia Tank, Bombay, 8 August 1942**.
- Gandhi's mantra: **'Do or Die'**; Sumit Sarkar saw it as Gandhi's **militant mood**.
- Leaders arrested on **9 August 1942**; Congress bodies declared unlawful under the **Criminal Law Amendment Act, 1908**; meetings banned under **rule 56 of the Defence of India Rules**.
- **Aruna Asaf Ali** presided over the Congress session on 9 August 1942 and hoisted the flag.
- Public violence was **most intense in eastern UP and Bihar**; storm centres: **eastern UP, Bihar, Midnapore, Maharashtra, Karnataka**.
- **Usha Mehta** started an **underground radio in Bombay**.
- Underground leaders: **Rammanohar Lohia, Jayaprakash Narayan, Aruna Asaf Ali, Usha Mehta, Biju Patnaik, Achyut Patwardhan, Sucheta Kripalani**.
- Parallel governments: **Ballia — Chittu Pandey (August 1942, a week)**; **Tamluk, Midnapore — Jatiya Sarkar (Dec 1942–Sept 1944)**; **Satara — Prati Sarkar (mid-1943–1945)** under **Y.B. Chavan, Nana Patil**.
- The **Muslim League opposed** the movement; the **Hindu Mahasabha boycotted** it; there was a **complete absence of anti-zamindar violence**.
- About **10,000** people killed in repression; **martial law was not applied**.
- Gandhi's fast — **February 1943**; **three members** of the viceroy's executive council resigned.
- **Pakistan Day — 23 March 1943**.
- **Famine of 1943** — **1.5 to 3 million** died; worst areas included **Tamluk-Contai-Diamond Harbour, Dacca, Faridpur, Tippera, Noakhali**.
- **C. Rajagopalachari formula (1944)** — plebiscite of the **entire population** of Muslim-majority areas after the war; **Gandhi supported** it; condemned by Hindu leaders under **Vir Savarkar**.
- **Desai–Liaqat Pact** — **Bhulabhai Desai** and **Liaqat Ali Khan**; equal Congress–League nominees, **20% seats for minorities**.
- **Wavell Plan — Shimla Conference, June 1945**; Wavell wanted **Khizr Hyat Khan** as Muslim representative from Western Punjab.
- Bose: **4th in ICS**, resigned **1921**; guru **Chittaranjan Das**; **mayor of Calcutta, 1923**.
- **Anti-Compromise Conference, Ramgarh (March 1940)** — Forward Bloc and **Kisan Sabha**; struggle from **6 April** (National Week).
- Bose reached **Peshawar on 26 January 1941 as 'Ziauddin'** (helped by **Bhagat Ram**); met Hitler as **'Orlando Mazzotta'**.
- **Freedom Army (Mukti Sena)** office at **Dresden**; slogan **'Jai Hind'** from the **Free India Centre, Germany**.
- INA idea originally of **Mohan Singh**; first division **September 1942, 16,300 men**; Japanese wanted only **2,000**.
- **Rashbehari Bose** founded the **Indian Independence League (Tokyo, 1942)** and handed it and the INA to Subhash in **July 1943**; Subhash became Supreme Commander on **25 August**.
- **Provisional Government of Free India — Singapore, 21 October 1943**; **H.C. Chatterjee** (Finance), **M.A. Aiyar** (Broadcasting), **Lakshmi Swaminathan** (Women).
- "Give me blood, I will give you freedom" — given in **Malaya**.
- **6 November 1943** — Andaman and Nicobar renamed **Shahid Dweep and Swaraj Dweep**.
- INA HQ moved to **Rangoon, January 1944**; war cry **"Chalo Delhi!"**.
- **6 July 1944** — Bose called Gandhi **'Father of Nation'** on **Azad Hind Radio**.
- INA on Indian soil **18 March 1944**; **Colonel Malik** hoisted the INA flag at **Moirang, Manipur, 14 April 1944**; **Shah Nawaz's** battalion fought in the **Imphal** campaign.
- Bose reportedly died in an air crash at **Taipei, 18 August 1945**.
