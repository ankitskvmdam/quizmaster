## Gist
*Book pages 462–490.*

The last two years of British rule had **two strands**: tortuous negotiations among the government, Congress and League, ending in freedom and partition amid communal violence; and sporadic, militant mass action — the **INA release movement, RIN revolt, Tebhaga, Worli, Punjab Kisan Morchas, Punnapra-Vayalar** and **Telangana**.

Congress leaders were released in **June 1945**. In **July 1945** the **Labour Party** formed the government with **Clement Attlee** as PM and **Pethick Lawrence** as Secretary of State. Elections were announced in **August 1945**; in **September 1945** a constituent assembly was promised. Reasons: the **USA and USSR** favoured Indian freedom, a war-weary Britain, fear of a 1942-type revolt, and the last elections dating from **1934 (Centre) and 1937 (provinces)**.

The **1945-46 election campaign** glorified 1942 martyrs. The first **INA trial at the Red Fort (November 1945)** put a Hindu (**Prem Kumar Sehgal**), a Muslim (**Shah Nawaz Khan**) and a Sikh (**Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon**) in the dock. Congress (**Bombay session, September 1945**) backed the INA; **Bhulabhai Desai, Tej Bahadur Sapru, K.N. Katju, Nehru and Asaf Ali** defended them. **INA Day** was **12 November 1945**.

Three upsurges followed: **21 November 1945** (Calcutta, INA trials), **11 February 1946** (Calcutta, seven-year sentence on **Rashid Ali**) and **18 February 1946** — the strike of about **1100 RIN ratings of HMIS Talwar** in **Bombay**. **Patel and Jinnah** got the ratings to surrender on **23 February**. Congress did not back the upsurges because of their **tactics and timing**.

**Results:** Congress won **91% of non-Muslim votes** and **57 of 102** Central Assembly seats; the **League** won **86.6% of Muslim votes**, all **30 reserved seats**, and majorities in **Bengal and Sindh**. Punjab got a **Unionist-Congress-Akali** coalition under **Khizr Hayat Khan**.

The **Cabinet Mission** (**Pethick Lawrence** chairman, **Stafford Cripps**, **A.V. Alexander**) reached Delhi on **24 March 1946**. Its plan (May 1946) rejected full Pakistan and grouped provinces into **Section A** (Madras, Bombay, CP, UP, **Bihar**, Orissa), **B** (Punjab, NWFP, Sindh) and **C** (Bengal, Assam), with a **389-member** constituent assembly. The League accepted on **6 June**, Congress on **24 June 1946**; after **Nehru's 10 July** statement the League withdrew (**29 July**) and called for **"direct action" from 16 August**. Riots hit Calcutta, Bombay, Noakhali, **Bihar** and Garhmukteshwar.

Nehru's **Interim Government** was sworn in on **2 September 1946**; the League joined on **26 October 1946** and obstructed it. The Constituent Assembly met on **9 December 1946** and passed Nehru's **Objectives Resolution**.

The chapter traces **communalism** through three stages (**communal nationalism, liberal, extreme**) and the **two-nation theory**: the **1906 Shimla deputation** under the **Agha Khan**, the **Muslim League (1906)**, separate electorates (**1909**), the **Punjab Hindu Sabha (1909)**, the **Hindu Mahasabha (1915)**, the **RSS (1925)**, the **Communal Award (1932)** and the **Pakistan Resolution (Lahore, 24 March 1940)**.

## Key facts
- Labour government formed in Britain in **July 1945** — PM **Clement Attlee**, Secretary of State for India **Pethick Lawrence**.
- Last elections before 1945-46: **1934 for the Centre**, **1937 for the provinces**.
- First INA trial: **Red Fort, Delhi, November 1945** — **Prem Kumar Sehgal, Shah Nawaz Khan, Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon**.
- First post-War Congress session (**Bombay, September 1945**) passed a resolution supporting the INA cause.
- INA defence: **Bhulabhai Desai, Tej Bahadur Sapru, Kailash Nath Katju, Jawaharlal Nehru, Asaf Ali**.
- **INA Day: November 12, 1945**; **INA week: November 5–11**.
- Upsurges: **Nov 21, 1945** Calcutta (INA trials); **Feb 11, 1946** Calcutta (seven-year sentence on **Rashid Ali**); **Feb 18, 1946** Bombay (**RIN ratings**).
- About **1100 ratings of HMIS Talwar** struck; rebels hoisted the **tricolour, crescent and hammer-and-sickle** flags.
- Royal Indian Air Force sympathy strikes: **Bombay, Poona, Calcutta, Jessore, Ambala**.
- **Patel and Jinnah** persuaded the ratings to surrender on **February 23, 1946**; a **Maratha battalion** rounded them up.
- Gandhi: if the ratings mutinied for freedom they were **"doubly wrong"**; Congress did not back the upsurges due to **tactics and timing**.
- Congress: **91% non-Muslim votes**, **57 of 102** Central Assembly seats; no majority in **Bengal, Sindh, Punjab**.
- League: **86.6% Muslim votes**, all **30 reserved seats**, majority in **Bengal and Sindh**.
- Punjab: **Unionist-Congress-Akali** coalition under **Khizr Hayat Khan**.
- Franchise: under **10%** for provinces, under **1%** for the Central Assembly.
- Cabinet Mission: **Pethick Lawrence (chairman)**, **Stafford Cripps (President, Board of Trade)**, **A.V. Alexander (First Lord of Admiralty)**; reached Delhi **March 24, 1946**.
- Mission rejected full Pakistan: non-Muslims would be **38% in the North-West** and **48% in the North-East**.
- Sections: **A** — Madras, Bombay, CP, UP, **Bihar**, Orissa; **B** — Punjab, NWFP, Sindh; **C** — Bengal, Assam.
- Constituent assembly of **389**: **292** provinces, **4** chief commissioner's provinces, **93** princely states.
- A province could leave a group **after the first general elections**, and seek reconsideration **after 10 years**.
- **Wavell's 'Breakdown Plan' (May 1946)**: British withdrawal to the Muslim provinces of the North-West and North-East.
- League accepted the plan on **June 6**, Congress on **June 24, 1946**; League withdrew on **July 29, 1946** and called for **"direct action" from August 16**.
- Worst-hit riot areas: **Calcutta, Bombay, Noakhali, Bihar, Garhmukteshwar (UP)**.
- Interim Government under Nehru sworn in **September 2, 1946**; League joined **October 26, 1946**.
- Interim ministers: **Patel** — Home, I&B; **Baldev Singh** — Defence; **Rajendra Prasad** — Agriculture and Food; **Jagjivan Ram** — Labour; **Liaquat Ali Khan** — Finance; **Jogendra Nath Mandal (League)** — Law.
- Constituent Assembly first met **December 9, 1946**; **Objectives Resolution** drafted by **Nehru**.
- Communalism's stages: **communal nationalism → liberal communalism → extreme communalism**.
- **1887**: **Syed Ahmed Khan** and **Raja Shiv Prasad of Bhinga** propped up as an anti-Congress front.
- **1906 Shimla deputation** to **Lord Minto** led by the **Agha Khan**; Muslim League founded by **Agha Khan, Nawab Salimullah of Dacca, Mohsin-ul-Mulk, Waqar-ul-Mulk**.
- **Punjab Hindu Sabha (1909)** — **U.N. Mukherjee and Lal Chand**; **Hindu Mahasabha** first session **April 1915** under the **Maharaja of Kasim Bazar**; **RSS 1925**.
- 1920s: Arya Samaj **Shuddhi and Sangathan**; Muslim **Tabligh and Tanzeem**.
- Separate Muslim nation idea: **Rahmat Ali** (early 1930s), developed by **Iqbal**.
- **Pakistan Resolution**: Lahore session, **March 24, 1940**.
