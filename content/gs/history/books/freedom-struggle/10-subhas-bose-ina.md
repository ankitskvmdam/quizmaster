## Gist
**Subhas Chandra Bose** was born at **Cuttack** in **1897**. He passed the Indian Civil Service examination but resigned in **1921** to join the national movement, and worked in Bengal under his political guru **C.R. Das**. He became Congress president at **Haripura (1938)** and won again at **Tripuri (1939)** against Gandhi's candidate. But he could not work with the Gandhian leadership, resigned, and founded the **Forward Bloc** in **1939**.

Bose believed that Britain's difficulty in the Second World War was India's opportunity, and that India should take the help of Britain's enemies. The government put him under house arrest in Calcutta. In **January 1941** he escaped in disguise, travelled through **Peshawar and Kabul**, and reached **Germany**. There he set up the **Free India Centre**, raised the **Free India Legion** from Indian prisoners of war, and was first called **"Netaji"**.

Meanwhile Japan had overrun South-East Asia. In **1942** **Captain Mohan Singh** formed the first **Indian National Army (INA, Azad Hind Fauj)** from Indian prisoners of war taken by Japan, and **Rash Behari Bose** led the **Indian Independence League**. This first INA broke down. In **1943** Subhas Bose came from Germany by submarine, and in **July 1943** at **Singapore** Rash Behari Bose handed over the leadership to him. He gave the army the battle cry **"Chalo Delhi"**.

On **21 October 1943** Bose proclaimed the **Provisional Government of Free India** at **Singapore**. It was recognised by **nine** countries, including Japan. Japan handed over the **Andaman and Nicobar Islands**, which Bose renamed **Shaheed and Swaraj**. In **1944** the INA fought beside the Japanese on the **Imphal–Kohima** front and hoisted its flag at **Moirang** in Manipur. But the campaign failed, Japan surrendered in **August 1945**, and Bose is said to have died in an air crash at **Taipei** on **18 August 1945**.

The INA lost the war but won the country. When the British tried its officers at the **Red Fort** in **1945–46**, the whole nation rose in their support, and **Bhulabhai Desai** led their defence. The government had to set the officers free. The trials, and the naval revolt at Bombay that followed, showed the British that they could no longer rely on Indian soldiers.

## Key facts
- **Subhas Chandra Bose** was born on **23 January 1897** at **Cuttack** in Orissa; his birthday is observed as **Parakram Diwas**.
- Bose stood **fourth** in the Indian Civil Service examination of 1920 and **resigned in 1921** to join the national movement.
- His political guru was **Chittaranjan Das**.
- Bose was Congress president at **Haripura (1938)**; at **Tripuri (1939)** he defeated **Pattabhi Sitaramayya**, whom Gandhi had backed.
- Bose resigned as Congress president in **April 1939**, and **Rajendra Prasad** took his place.
- Bose founded the **Forward Bloc** in **May 1939**, at first as a group within the Congress.
- In **March 1940** Bose and **Swami Sahajanand Saraswati** held the **Anti-Compromise Conference** at **Ramgarh** (then in Bihar), at the same time as the Congress session there.
- Bose was arrested in **July 1940** and later kept under house arrest in his house in **Calcutta**.
- He escaped from Calcutta in **January 1941** disguised as a Pathan under the name **Ziauddin**; his nephew **Sisir Bose** drove him to **Gomoh** (then in Bihar), where he boarded a train.
- He went by way of **Peshawar and Kabul** to **Berlin**, using an Italian passport in the name of **Orlando Mazzotta**.
- In **Germany** he set up the **Free India Centre** in Berlin and broadcast over **Azad Hind Radio**.
- Bose raised the **Free India Legion**, also called the **Indian Legion** or **Tiger Legion**, in **Germany** from Indian prisoners of war.
- The honorific **"Netaji"** was first given to Bose in **Germany**; the greeting **"Jai Hind"** was also adopted at the Free India Centre.
- The first **Indian National Army (Azad Hind Fauj)** was formed in **1942** by **Captain Mohan Singh** from Indian prisoners of war taken by Japan in Malaya and Singapore.
- **Rash Behari Bose**, who had lived in Japan since **1915**, led the **Indian Independence League**; its conferences were held at **Tokyo** and **Bangkok** in **1942**.
- Mohan Singh fell out with the Japanese and was arrested in **December 1942**, and the first INA broke up.
- Bose left Germany by **submarine** in **February 1943** and reached **Singapore** in **July 1943**.
- At **Singapore** in **July 1943** **Rash Behari Bose** handed over the leadership of the Indian Independence League and the INA to Subhas Bose.
- **Subhas Chandra Bose** took command of the INA and gave the battle cry **"Chalo Delhi"** (Dilli Chalo); he became its Supreme Commander in **August 1943**.
- Bose proclaimed the **Provisional Government of Free India (Arzi Hukumat-e-Azad Hind)** at **Singapore** on **21 October 1943**.
- The Provisional Government was recognised by **9** countries, including **Japan, Germany and Italy**; it declared war on **Britain and the United States**.
- Japan handed over the **Andaman and Nicobar Islands** to the Provisional Government; Bose hoisted the flag at **Port Blair** in **December 1943**.
- Bose renamed the Andaman Islands **Shaheed Dweep** and the Nicobar Islands **Swaraj Dweep**.
- The women's regiment of the INA was the **Rani of Jhansi Regiment**, commanded by **Captain Lakshmi Swaminathan** (later Lakshmi Sahgal).
- INA brigades were named after **Gandhi, Nehru, Azad and Subhas**.
- The motto of the INA was **"Ittehad, Etemad, Qurbani"** (unity, faith, sacrifice), and its marching song was **"Kadam Kadam Badhaye Ja"**.
- **"Give me blood and I will give you freedom"** (Tum mujhe khoon do, main tumhe azadi dunga) was Bose's call to Indians in South-East Asia.
- The headquarters of the INA and the Provisional Government moved to **Rangoon** in **January 1944**.
- In a broadcast from **Azad Hind Radio** in **July 1944**, Bose addressed Gandhi as the **"Father of the Nation"** and asked for his blessings.
- The INA fought with the Japanese on the **Imphal–Kohima** front in **1944**; **Colonel Shaukat Malik** hoisted the INA flag at **Moirang** in **Manipur** on **14 April 1944**.
- The campaign failed and the INA retreated; **Japan surrendered** in **August 1945**.
- Bose is reported to have died in an air crash at **Taipei (Taihoku)** in **Formosa (Taiwan)** on **18 August 1945**.
- Bose wrote **The Indian Struggle** and an unfinished autobiography, **An Indian Pilgrim**.
- **INA trials**: held at the **Red Fort, Delhi**, from **November 1945**; the first trial was of **Shah Nawaz Khan, Prem Kumar Sehgal and Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon**.
- **Bhulabhai Desai** led the defence in the INA trials; **Tej Bahadur Sapru, Kailash Nath Katju, Jawaharlal Nehru and Asaf Ali** were also on the defence committee.
- The three officers were sentenced to transportation for life, but the Commander-in-Chief **Claude Auchinleck** remitted the sentences.
- **INA Day** was observed on **12 November 1945**; the Viceroy at the time of the trials was **Lord Wavell**.
- **Royal Indian Navy revolt**: the ratings of **HMIS Talwar** at **Bombay** went on strike on **18 February 1946**; **Vallabhbhai Patel and Jinnah** persuaded them to surrender.

## What the exams ask
- Who founded it: the Forward Bloc, the Free India Legion, the first INA (Mohan Singh).
- Where it happened: "Netaji" (Germany), the Provisional Government (Singapore), the INA flag (Moirang), the trials (Red Fort).
- Slogans of Bose: "Chalo Delhi", "Jai Hind", "Give me blood and I will give you freedom".
- Numbers and names: nine countries recognised the Provisional Government; Shaheed and Swaraj islands.
- The INA trials: the three officers tried and the lawyer who defended them (Bhulabhai Desai).
