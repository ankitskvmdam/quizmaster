This section cuts across all the others. Exams often ask not about an event but about a person: the title he was known by, the paper he edited, the book he wrote, the party he founded or the slogan he gave. These questions mostly come as "match the following" or "which pair is correct", so the facts have to be learnt as pairs.

Titles, papers, books and founders already given in the earlier sections are not repeated here in detail.

## Songs and poems

- **"Vande Mataram":** This song is in Bankim Chandra Chatterjee's novel Anandamath.
- **"Sare Jahan Se Achchha":** Muhammad Iqbal wrote it.
- **"Sarfaroshi ki Tamanna":** Bismil Azimabadi of Patna wrote this poem. Ram Prasad Bismil made it famous.

## What the exams ask

- Match the leader with the title (Deshbandhu, Punjab Kesari, Andhra Kesari, Bihar Kesari, Frontier Gandhi).
- Match the newspaper or journal with its editor (Al-Hilal, Comrade, Bengalee, Indian Opinion, Bahishkrit Bharat).
- Who wrote the book (Unhappy India, India Wins Freedom, India Divided, Poverty and Un-British Rule in India).
- Match the organisation with its founder (Khaksars, Independent Labour Party, Forward Bloc, Liberal Federation).
- "Which of the following pairs is correct?", mixing a title, a session and a founder in one question.
- To which state a lesser-known freedom fighter belonged (Ambabai of Karnataka, Matangini Hazra of Bengal).

> **Remember**

> - **Deshbandhu:** C.R. Das.
> - **Punjab Kesari:** Lala Lajpat Rai.

> - **1918:** The Searchlight began at Patna.
> - **1928:** Lala Lajpat Rai wrote Unhappy India in reply to Mother India.
> - **1931:** Allama Inayatullah Mashriqi founded the Khaksar movement at Lahore.
> - **1936:** B.R. Ambedkar founded the Independent Labour Party.
