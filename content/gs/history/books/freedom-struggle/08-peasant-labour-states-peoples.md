## Gist
The freedom struggle was not fought by the Congress alone. Alongside it, peasants fought landlords and high revenue, factory workers formed trade unions, and the people of the princely states fought their own rulers. These movements had their own leaders and organisations, and from the 1920s they moved closer to the national movement.

Peasants were the first to organise. In **Awadh**, **Baba Ramchandra**, a sanyasi who had once been an indentured labourer in **Fiji**, roused the tenants against the taluqdars in **1920–21**. In Gujarat, **Vallabhbhai Patel** led the **Bardoli Satyagraha** of **1928** against a rise in land revenue and came out of it as "Sardar". In Bihar, **Swami Vidyanand** led the cultivators against the Darbhanga Raj in **1919–20**, and **Swami Sahajanand Saraswati** formed the **Bihar Provincial Kisan Sabha** in **1929**. In **1936** the **All India Kisan Sabha** was founded at **Lucknow** with Sahajanand as president.

Workers followed. The **All India Trade Union Congress (AITUC)** was founded in **1920** at Bombay with **Lala Lajpat Rai** as president. Communists became strong in the unions in the late 1920s, and the government hit back with the **Meerut Conspiracy Case (1929)**. Inside the Congress, young leaders who wanted the party to stand for peasants and workers formed the **Congress Socialist Party** in **1934**.

About one-third of India was ruled by princes under British protection. There the people formed **Praja Mandals** to ask for civil rights and responsible government, and in **1927** these came together in the **All India States' People's Conference**. The Congress kept away from the states at first, but from **1938–39** it backed these struggles openly, and **Jawaharlal Nehru** became president of the States' People's Conference in **1939**.

Near independence the peasant struggles became sharper, as in the **Tebhaga** movement of Bengal and the **Telangana** struggle in Hyderabad. Exams ask who led which movement, where it took place and when each organisation was founded.

## Key facts
- **Pabna (Bengal), 1873**: peasants formed **agrarian leagues** against zamindars who were raising rents.
- **Deccan Riots, 1875**: peasants of **Poona and Ahmednagar** rose against **moneylenders**.
- **Tana Bhagat Movement (1914)**: started by **Jatra Bhagat** among the **Oraons** of Chotanagpur (then in Bihar); the Tana Bhagats later followed Gandhi and refused to pay rent.
- **U.P. Kisan Sabha**: set up in **February 1918** by **Gauri Shankar Mishra and Indra Narayan Dwivedi**, with the support of **Madan Mohan Malaviya**.
- **Baba Ramchandra** led the peasant movement of **Awadh** (Pratapgarh, Rae Bareli, Faizabad, Sultanpur) against the **taluqdars** in **1920–21**.
- Baba Ramchandra had been an **indentured labourer in Fiji**; he went from village to village reciting verses from the **Ramayana of Tulsidas**.
- The **Awadh Kisan Sabha** was formed in **October 1920** at **Pratapgarh**; **Jawaharlal Nehru** was linked with it.
- **Eka Movement (1921–22)**: in **Hardoi, Bahraich and Sitapur** of U.P., led by **Madari Pasi**.
- **Moplah (Mappila) Rebellion, 1921**: Muslim tenants of **Malabar** rose against their landlords and the government.
- **Bijolia Movement**: a long peasant struggle in the princely state of **Mewar** (Rajasthan), led by **Vijay Singh Pathik**.
- **Swami Vidyanand** led the cultivators of north Bihar against the **Darbhanga Raj** in **1919–20**, around the time of the Non-Cooperation Movement.
- **Bardoli Satyagraha (1928)**: in **Bardoli taluka, Surat district, Gujarat**, against an increase in **land revenue**; led by **Vallabhbhai Patel**.
- The **women of Bardoli** gave Patel the title **"Sardar"**; an official inquiry later found the revenue increase unjustified and cut it down.
- **Swami Sahajanand Saraswati** formed the **Bihar Provincial Kisan Sabha** in **1929**; his ashram was at **Bihta** near Patna.
- Sahajanand's co-workers in the Bihar kisan movement: **Karyanand Sharma, Yadunandan Sharma, Rahul Sankrityayan and Panchanan Sharma**.
- Bihar peasants fought for **bakasht land** in the late 1930s; **Karyanand Sharma** led the struggle at **Barahiya Tal (Munger)** and **Yadunandan Sharma** at **Reora (Gaya)**.
- **N.G. Ranga** organised the peasants of **Andhra**.
- The **All India Kisan Sabha** was founded at **Lucknow** in **April 1936**; **Swami Sahajanand Saraswati** was its first president and **N.G. Ranga** its first general secretary.
- The Kisan Sabha met alongside the Congress at **Faizpur** in **December 1936**; the Congress adopted an agrarian programme there.
- **Tebhaga Movement (1946–47)**: sharecroppers (**bargadars**) of **Bengal** demanded **two-thirds** of the crop instead of half; the Bengal Provincial Kisan Sabha led it.
- **Telangana Movement (1946–51)**: peasants of **Hyderabad State** rose under communist leadership against the **Nizam**, the landlords and forced labour (**vetti**).
- **Punnapra-Vayalar (1946)**: a rising of workers in the princely state of **Travancore** against the Dewan **C.P. Ramaswami Iyer**.
- **Bombay Mill Hands' Association (1890)**: founded by **N.M. Lokhande**; it is regarded as the first labour association in India.
- **Madras Labour Union (1918)**: founded by **B.P. Wadia**; it is regarded as the first modern trade union in India.
- The **All India Trade Union Congress (AITUC)** was founded on **31 October 1920** at **Bombay**; **Lala Lajpat Rai** was its first president and **Dewan Chaman Lal** its first general secretary.
- The **Trade Union Act, 1926** gave trade unions legal status and allowed them to be registered.
- The **Communist Party of India** was organised on Indian soil at **Kanpur** in **1925**.
- **Kanpur Bolshevik Conspiracy Case (1924)**: **S.A. Dange, Muzaffar Ahmad, Shaukat Usmani and Nalini Gupta** were jailed.
- **Meerut Conspiracy Case (March 1929)**: the government arrested leading communists and trade unionists, among them **S.A. Dange and Muzaffar Ahmad**, and the Englishmen **Philip Spratt and Ben Bradley**.
- The **Girni Kamgar Union** led the great strike of the **Bombay textile mills** in **1928**.
- The AITUC split at its **Nagpur session (1929)**, over which **Jawaharlal Nehru** presided; the moderates led by **N.M. Joshi** walked out.
- The **Indian National Trade Union Congress (INTUC)** was founded in **1947** under Congress leaders such as **Vallabhbhai Patel**.
- The **Congress Socialist Party (CSP)** was founded in **1934** by **Jayaprakash Narayan, Acharya Narendra Dev, Minoo Masani, Achyut Patwardhan** and others; it worked inside the Congress.
- The first all-India conference of Congress socialists was held at **Patna** in **May 1934** with **Acharya Narendra Dev** in the chair; **Jayaprakash Narayan** became general secretary of the CSP.
- **Praja Mandals** were the people's organisations in the **princely states**; the **Praja Mandal Movement** was the movement parallel to the freedom struggle in states such as Kashmir, Hyderabad and Travancore.
- The **All India States' People's Conference** was founded in **1927** at **Bombay**; its organisers included **Balwantrai Mehta, Manikyalal Kothari and G.R. Abhyankar**.
- The **Butler Committee (1927)** was appointed to examine the relations between the princely states and the paramount power.
- At **Nagpur (1920)** the Congress asked the princes to grant responsible government, but for many years it did not take up struggles in the states in its own name.
- At **Haripura (1938)** the Congress said that its goal of Purna Swaraj covered the states too; at **Tripuri (1939)** it decided to take a direct part in the states' movements.
- **Jawaharlal Nehru** was elected president of the All India States' People's Conference at its **Ludhiana session in 1939**.
- **Rajkot Satyagraha (1938–39)**: **Vallabhbhai Patel** guided it, and **Gandhi** went on a fast at Rajkot in **1939**.
- In **Kashmir**, **Sheikh Abdullah** led the **National Conference** against the Maharaja; in **Hyderabad**, **Swami Ramanand Tirtha** led the State Congress against the Nizam.

## What the exams ask
- Who led which peasant movement: Awadh (Baba Ramchandra), Bardoli (Vallabhbhai Patel), Eka (Madari Pasi), Bihar (Swami Vidyanand, Swami Sahajanand).
- Founding years and founders: AITUC 1920, Bihar Provincial Kisan Sabha 1929, Congress Socialist Party 1934, All India Kisan Sabha 1936.
- The first president of a body: AITUC (Lala Lajpat Rai), All India Kisan Sabha (Sahajanand Saraswati).
- A leader identified from a description, as with Baba Ramchandra.
- The name of the movement in the princely states (Praja Mandal Movement).
