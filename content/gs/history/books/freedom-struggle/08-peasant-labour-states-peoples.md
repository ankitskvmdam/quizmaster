## At a glance: the main organisations

The freedom struggle was not fought by the Congress alone. Peasants fought landlords and high revenue. Factory workers formed trade unions, and the people of the princely states fought their own rulers.

These movements had their own leaders and organisations. From the 1920s they moved closer to the national movement.

## Peasant movements

### Organisations

Peasants were the first to organise.

1. **February 1918:** Gauri Shankar Mishra and Indra Narayan Dwivedi set up the U.P. Kisan Sabha. Madan Mohan Malaviya supported them.
2. **October 1920:** The Awadh Kisan Sabha was formed at Pratapgarh. Jawaharlal Nehru was linked with it.
3. **April 1936:** The All India Kisan Sabha was founded at Lucknow. Swami Sahajanand Saraswati was its first president. N.G. Ranga was its first general secretary.
4. **December 1936:** The Kisan Sabha met alongside the Congress at Faizpur. There the Congress adopted an agrarian programme.

N.G. Ranga organised the peasants of Andhra.

### Struggles

| Movement | Year | Where | What happened |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pabna | 1873 | Bengal | Zamindars were raising rents. Peasants formed agrarian leagues against them. |
| Deccan Riots | 1875 | Poona and Ahmednagar | Peasants rose against moneylenders. |
| Tana Bhagat Movement | 1914 | Chotanagpur (then in Bihar) | Jatra Bhagat started it among the Oraons. |
| Awadh | 1920–21 | Pratapgarh, Rae Bareli, Faizabad, Sultanpur | Baba Ramchandra led the tenants against the taluqdars. |
| Moplah (Mappila) Rebellion | 1921 | Malabar | Muslim tenants rose against their landlords and the government. |
| Eka Movement | 1921–22 | Hardoi, Bahraich and Sitapur (U.P.) | Madari Pasi led it. |
| Bardoli Satyagraha | 1928 | Bardoli taluka, Surat district, Gujarat | Vallabhbhai Patel led it against an increase in land revenue. |
| Tebhaga Movement | 1946–47 | Bengal | Sharecroppers (bargadars) demanded two-thirds of the crop instead of half. |
| Telangana Movement | 1946–51 | Hyderabad State | Peasants rose under communist leadership. |
| Bijolia Movement | — | Mewar (Rajasthan), a princely state | Vijay Singh Pathik led this long peasant struggle. |

More on some of these struggles:

- **Tana Bhagats:** They later followed Gandhi and refused to pay rent.
- **Baba Ramchandra:** He was a sanyasi who had once been an indentured labourer in Fiji. He went from village to village reciting verses from the Ramayana of Tulsidas.
- **Bardoli:** The women of Bardoli gave Patel the title "Sardar". An official inquiry later found the revenue increase unjustified and cut it down.
- **Tebhaga:** The Bengal Provincial Kisan Sabha led it.
- **Telangana:** The peasants rose against the Nizam, the landlords and forced labour (vetti).

Near independence the peasant struggles became sharper, as Tebhaga and Telangana show.

## The kisan movement in Bihar

1. **1919–20:** Swami Vidyanand led the cultivators of north Bihar against the Darbhanga Raj. This was around the time of the Non-Cooperation Movement.
2. **1929:** Swami Sahajanand Saraswati formed the Bihar Provincial Kisan Sabha. His ashram was at Bihta near Patna.
3. **Late 1930s:** Bihar peasants fought for bakasht land. Karyanand Sharma led the struggle at Barahiya Tal (Munger). Yadunandan Sharma led it at Reora (Gaya).

Sahajanand's co-workers in the Bihar kisan movement were Karyanand Sharma, Yadunandan Sharma, Rahul Sankrityayan and Panchanan Sharma.

## Workers' movements

### Organisations

1. **1890:** N.M. Lokhande founded the Bombay Mill Hands' Association. It is regarded as the first labour association in India.
2. **1918:** B.P. Wadia founded the Madras Labour Union. It is regarded as the first modern trade union in India.
3. **31 October 1920:** The All India Trade Union Congress (AITUC) was founded at Bombay. Lala Lajpat Rai was its first president. Dewan Chaman Lal was its first general secretary.
4. **1925:** The Communist Party of India was organised on Indian soil at Kanpur.
5. **1926:** The Trade Union Act, 1926 gave trade unions legal status. It allowed them to be registered.
6. **1929:** The AITUC split at its Nagpur session, over which Jawaharlal Nehru presided. The moderates led by N.M. Joshi walked out.
7. **1947:** The Indian National Trade Union Congress (INTUC) was founded under Congress leaders such as Vallabhbhai Patel.

### Struggles

1. **1924:** In the Kanpur Bolshevik Conspiracy Case, S.A. Dange, Muzaffar Ahmad, Shaukat Usmani and Nalini Gupta were jailed.
2. **1928:** The Girni Kamgar Union led the great strike of the Bombay textile mills.
3. **March 1929:** Communists had become strong in the unions in the late 1920s. The government hit back with the Meerut Conspiracy Case. It arrested leading communists and trade unionists, among them S.A. Dange and Muzaffar Ahmad and the Englishmen Philip Spratt and Ben Bradley.

## The Congress Socialist Party (1934)

Young leaders inside the Congress wanted the party to stand for peasants and workers. So they formed the Congress Socialist Party.

- **Founders:** Jayaprakash Narayan, Acharya Narendra Dev, Minoo Masani, Achyut Patwardhan and others.
- **May 1934:** The first all-India conference of Congress socialists was held at Patna, with Acharya Narendra Dev in the chair. Jayaprakash Narayan became general secretary of the CSP.
- **Inside the Congress:** The CSP worked within the Congress.

## Movements in the princely states

About one-third of India was ruled by princes under British protection. There the people formed Praja Mandals, their own organisations. They asked for civil rights and responsible government.

### Organisations

1. **Praja Mandals:** These were the people's organisations in the princely states. The movement parallel to the freedom struggle in states such as Kashmir, Hyderabad and Travancore is called the Praja Mandal Movement.
2. **1927:** The Praja Mandals came together in the All India States' People's Conference, founded at Bombay. Its organisers included Balwantrai Mehta, Manikyalal Kothari and G.R. Abhyankar.
3. **1939:** Jawaharlal Nehru was elected president of the All India States' People's Conference at its Ludhiana session.

### Struggles

1. **1938–39:** Vallabhbhai Patel guided the Rajkot Satyagraha. Gandhi went on a fast at Rajkot in 1939.
2. **1946:** In Punnapra-Vayalar, workers rose in the princely state of Travancore against the Dewan, C.P. Ramaswami Iyer.
3. **Kashmir:** Sheikh Abdullah led the National Conference against the Maharaja.
4. **Hyderabad:** Swami Ramanand Tirtha led the State Congress against the Nizam.

### The Congress and the states

1. **1920:** At Nagpur the Congress asked the princes to grant responsible government. But for many years it did not take up struggles in the states in its own name.
2. **1927:** The Butler Committee was appointed to examine the relations between the princely states and the paramount power.
3. **1938:** At Haripura the Congress said that its goal of Purna Swaraj covered the states too.
4. **1939:** At Tripuri the Congress decided to take a direct part in the states' movements.

So from 1938–39 the Congress openly backed the struggles in the states.
