## Why some nationalists chose revolution

1. Some young nationalists lost faith in petitions. Later they also lost faith in slow mass movements.
2. They chose to strike at unpopular British officials. They also chose to raise money and arms by force.
3. They hoped that acts of courage and sacrifice would wake the country up.

This revolutionary movement came in two waves.

## The first wave in India (1897–1915)

The first wave followed the Swadeshi Movement. Secret societies grew up, such as the Anushilan Samiti in Bengal and Abhinav Bharat in Maharashtra.

1. **1897:** The Chapekar brothers, Damodar and Balkrishna, killed the plague commissioner Rand and Lieutenant Ayerst at Poona.
2. **1902:** Pramathanath Mitra founded the Anushilan Samiti at Calcutta. Its Dacca branch was led by Pulin Behari Das.
3. **1904:** V.D. Savarkar founded Abhinav Bharat, a secret society, at Nasik. It grew out of the Mitra Mela (1899).
4. **1906:** Barindra Kumar Ghosh and Bhupendranath Datta started the weekly Yugantar.
5. **30 April 1908:** Khudiram Bose and Prafulla Chaki threw a bomb at Muzaffarpur in Bihar. They believed the carriage carried the judge Kingsford, but two English women were killed. This is the Muzaffarpur bomb case. Prafulla Chaki shot himself to avoid arrest.
6. **11 August 1908:** Khudiram Bose was hanged in Muzaffarpur jail.
7. **1908:** In the Alipore Conspiracy Case, Chittaranjan Das defended Aurobindo Ghosh, and Aurobindo was acquitted. His brother Barindra Kumar Ghosh was transported for life.
8. **23 December 1912:** A bomb was thrown at Viceroy Lord Hardinge at Chandni Chowk, during his state entry into the new capital, Delhi. Rash Behari Bose planned it with Sachindranath Sanyal. This is the Delhi Conspiracy Case.
9. **1915:** Jatindranath Mukherjee (Bagha Jatin) died after a fight with the police at Balasore.

## Revolutionaries abroad (1905–1915)

1. **1905:** Shyamji Krishna Varma founded India House in London. With it he started the Indian Home Rule Society and the journal The Indian Sociologist.
2. **1907:** Madam Bhikaji Cama unfurled an Indian flag at the International Socialist Congress at Stuttgart (Germany). She worked from Paris and brought out the journal Bande Mataram.
3. **1909:** Madan Lal Dhingra shot Curzon Wyllie in London.
4. **Before the Ghadar Party:** On the west coast of North America, the Swadesh Sevak Home at Vancouver (G.D. Kumar) and the United India House at Seattle (Tarak Nath Das) prepared the ground for the Ghadar movement.
5. **1911:** Lala Har Dayal came to California. He taught Indian philosophy at a university there before he organised the Ghadar movement.
6. **1913:** Punjabi immigrants founded the Ghadar Party at San Francisco, USA. Its headquarters was the Yugantar Ashram. Sohan Singh Bhakna was its president, and Lala Har Dayal was its moving spirit.
7. **1 November 1913:** The weekly paper Ghadar began. The party took its name from it.
8. **1914:** The Komagata Maru, a ship chartered by Gurdit Singh, carried Indian immigrants. It was turned back from Vancouver. The police fired on its passengers at Budge Budge near Calcutta.
9. **21 February 1915:** The Ghadar army revolt was fixed for this day, but it was betrayed and crushed. Kartar Singh Sarabha was hanged. Rash Behari Bose escaped to Japan.
10. **1915:** Raja Mahendra Pratap set up a provisional government of India at Kabul, with Barkatullah as prime minister.

## The second wave (1924–1934)

The second wave came after the Non-Cooperation Movement was called off.

1. **1924:** Ram Prasad Bismil, Sachindranath Sanyal and Jogesh Chandra Chatterjee founded the Hindustan Republican Association (HRA) at Kanpur.
2. **9 August 1925:** HRA men looted government cash from a train at Kakori near Lucknow. Ram Prasad Bismil and Ashfaqulla Khan planned this Kakori train robbery.
3. **1926:** Bhagat Singh founded the Naujawan Bharat Sabha in Punjab.
4. **December 1927:** Four men were hanged in the Kakori case: Ram Prasad Bismil, Ashfaqulla Khan, Roshan Singh and Rajendra Lahiri.
5. **1928:** The HRA was renamed the Hindustan Socialist Republican Association (HSRA) at Feroz Shah Kotla, Delhi, under Chandrashekhar Azad.
6. **17 December 1928:** Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Azad shot the police officer Saunders at Lahore. They did it to avenge the death of Lala Lajpat Rai.
7. **8 April 1929:** Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt threw bombs in the Central Legislative Assembly. They were protesting against the Public Safety Bill and the Trade Disputes Bill. They later used their trial to spread their ideas.
8. **1929:** Jatin Das died in Lahore jail after a long hunger strike for better treatment of political prisoners.
9. **18 April 1930:** Surya Sen ("Master Da") led the Chittagong Armoury Raid under the banner of the Indian Republican Army. Many young women also took part. His associates were Ganesh Ghosh, Lokenath Bal, Ambika Chakrabarti, Anant Singh, Ardhendu Dastidar, Pritilata Waddedar and Kalpana Dutt.
10. **27 February 1931:** Chandrashekhar Azad died in a police encounter at Alfred Park, Allahabad.
11. **23 March 1931:** Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev and Rajguru were hanged at Lahore, in the Lahore Conspiracy Case.
12. **1932:** Pritilata Waddedar led the attack on the European Club at Pahartali. In the same year Bina Das fired at the Governor of Bengal at a convocation.
13. **1934:** Surya Sen was hanged.
14. **1934:** Baikuntha Shukla of Bihar was hanged in Gaya jail. He had shot the approver (an accused who turned government witness) Phanindranath Ghosh, whose evidence had helped to hang Bhagat Singh.

The government crushed these groups by the mid-1930s. But their courage and their slogans stayed in public memory.

## Writings and slogans

- **Bandi Jiwan:** Sachindranath Sanyal wrote it.
- **"Sarfaroshi Ki Tamanna":** Bismil Azimabadi of Patna wrote this song. Ram Prasad Bismil made it famous but did not write it.
- **"Inquilab Zindabad":** Hasrat Mohani coined this slogan. Bhagat Singh made it popular.
- **"Why I Am an Atheist":** Bhagat Singh wrote it.

## The last famous act (1940)

- **13 March 1940:** Udham Singh shot Michael O'Dwyer at Caxton Hall, London. O'Dwyer had been Lieutenant-Governor of Punjab at the time of Jallianwala Bagh. Udham Singh was hanged the same year.

## What the exams ask

- Who is linked with an act: Chittagong (Surya Sen), Kakori (Bismil, Ashfaqulla), the Assembly bomb (Bhagat Singh, Batukeshwar Dutt).
- Where it happened or was founded: Ghadar Party (San Francisco, USA), India House (London), Madam Cama's flag (Stuttgart), Khudiram's hanging (Muzaffarpur).
- The year: India House 1905, HRA 1924, Kakori 1925.
- Match the organisation with its headquarters or founder. For match questions, note also that the Azad Hind Fauj (INA) under Subhas Bose had its headquarters at Rangoon from 1944.
- Bihar links: Muzaffarpur 1908, Bismil Azimabadi, Baikuntha Shukla.
