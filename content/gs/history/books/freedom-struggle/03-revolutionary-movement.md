## Gist
Some young nationalists lost faith in petitions and, later, in slow mass movements. They chose to strike at unpopular British officials and to raise money and arms by force. They hoped that acts of courage and sacrifice would wake the country up. This revolutionary movement came in two waves.

The first wave followed the Swadeshi Movement. Secret societies such as the **Anushilan Samiti** in Bengal and **Abhinav Bharat** in Maharashtra grew up. In **1908** **Khudiram Bose and Prafulla Chaki** threw a bomb at **Muzaffarpur** in Bihar, meant for the judge **Kingsford**; Khudiram was hanged in Muzaffarpur jail. In **1912** a bomb was thrown at Viceroy **Lord Hardinge** as he entered Delhi in state.

Revolutionaries also worked abroad. **Shyamji Krishna Varma** opened **India House** in London in **1905**, and **Madam Bhikaji Cama** unfurled an Indian flag at **Stuttgart** in **1907**. On the west coast of North America, Punjabi immigrants led by **Lala Har Dayal** and **Sohan Singh Bhakna** founded the **Ghadar Party** at **San Francisco** in **1913**. Their plan for an army revolt in **February 1915** was betrayed and crushed.

The second wave came after the Non-Cooperation Movement was called off. The **Hindustan Republican Association (HRA)**, founded in **1924**, carried out the **Kakori train robbery** in **1925**. In **1928** it became the **Hindustan Socialist Republican Association (HSRA)** under **Chandrashekhar Azad**. **Bhagat Singh** and his comrades killed the police officer **Saunders**, threw bombs in the Central Legislative Assembly in **1929**, and used their trial to spread their ideas. Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev and Rajguru were hanged on **23 March 1931**.

In Bengal, **Surya Sen** led the **Chittagong Armoury Raid** in **1930**, in which many young women also took part. The government crushed these groups by the mid-1930s, but their courage and slogans such as **"Inquilab Zindabad"** stayed in public memory. The last famous act was in **1940**, when **Udham Singh** shot **Michael O'Dwyer** in London.

## Key facts
- **Chapekar brothers (Damodar and Balkrishna)** killed the plague commissioner **Rand** and **Lt Ayerst** at **Poona** in **1897**.
- **Anushilan Samiti**: founded at **Calcutta** in **1902** by **Pramathanath Mitra**; the Dacca branch was led by **Pulin Behari Das**.
- **Abhinav Bharat (1904)**: secret society of **V.D. Savarkar** at Nasik; it grew out of the **Mitra Mela (1899)**.
- The weekly **Yugantar** was started in **1906** by **Barindra Kumar Ghosh and Bhupendranath Datta**.
- **Muzaffarpur bomb case (30 April 1908)**: **Khudiram Bose and Prafulla Chaki** threw a bomb at a carriage they believed carried the judge **Kingsford**; two English women were killed.
- **Prafulla Chaki** shot himself to avoid arrest; **Khudiram Bose** was hanged in **Muzaffarpur jail** on **11 August 1908**.
- **Alipore Conspiracy Case (1908)**: **Aurobindo Ghosh** was defended by **Chittaranjan Das** and acquitted; his brother **Barindra Ghosh** was transported for life.
- **India House** in **London**: founded in **1905** by **Shyamji Krishna Varma**, along with the Indian Home Rule Society and the journal **The Indian Sociologist**.
- **Madan Lal Dhingra** shot **Curzon Wyllie** in London in **1909**.
- **Madam Bhikaji Cama** unfurled an Indian flag at the International Socialist Congress at **Stuttgart (Germany)** in **1907**; she worked from Paris and brought out the journal **Bande Mataram**.
- A bomb was thrown at Viceroy **Lord Hardinge** on **23 December 1912** during his state entry into the new capital, Delhi, at **Chandni Chowk**; it was planned by **Rash Behari Bose** with **Sachindranath Sanyal** (Delhi Conspiracy Case).
- **Swadesh Sevak Home** was at **Vancouver** (G.D. Kumar) and **United India House** at **Seattle** (Tarak Nath Das); both prepared the ground for the Ghadar movement.
- **Ghadar Party**: founded in **1913** at **San Francisco, USA**; its headquarters was the **Yugantar Ashram**. **Sohan Singh Bhakna** was its president and **Lala Har Dayal** its moving spirit.
- **Lala Har Dayal** came to California in **1911** and taught Indian philosophy at a university there before he organised the Ghadar movement.
- The weekly paper **Ghadar** began on **1 November 1913**; the party took its name from it.
- **Komagata Maru (1914)**: a ship chartered by **Gurdit Singh** carrying Indian immigrants was turned back from **Vancouver**; police fired on the passengers at **Budge Budge** near Calcutta.
- The Ghadar rising was fixed for **21 February 1915** but was betrayed; **Kartar Singh Sarabha** was hanged, and **Rash Behari Bose** escaped to **Japan**.
- **Raja Mahendra Pratap** set up a provisional government of India at **Kabul** in **1915**, with **Barkatullah** as prime minister.
- **Jatindranath Mukherjee (Bagha Jatin)** died after a fight with the police at **Balasore** in **1915**.
- **Hindustan Republican Association (HRA)**: founded in **1924** at **Kanpur** by **Ram Prasad Bismil, Sachindranath Sanyal and Jogesh Chandra Chatterjee**.
- **Kakori train robbery (9 August 1925)**: HRA men looted government cash from a train at **Kakori near Lucknow**; it was planned by **Ram Prasad Bismil and Ashfaqulla Khan**.
- Hanged in the Kakori case (**December 1927**): **Ram Prasad Bismil, Ashfaqulla Khan, Roshan Singh and Rajendra Lahiri**.
- **Sachindranath Sanyal** wrote **Bandi Jiwan**.
- The song **"Sarfaroshi Ki Tamanna"** was written by **Bismil Azimabadi of Patna**; **Ram Prasad Bismil** made it famous but did not write it.
- **Naujawan Bharat Sabha** was founded by **Bhagat Singh** in **1926** in Punjab.
- **HSRA**: the HRA was renamed the Hindustan Socialist Republican Association in **1928** at **Feroz Shah Kotla, Delhi**, under **Chandrashekhar Azad**.
- **Saunders**, a police officer, was shot at **Lahore** on **17 December 1928** by **Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Azad** to avenge the death of **Lala Lajpat Rai**.
- **8 April 1929**: **Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt** threw bombs in the **Central Legislative Assembly** in protest against the **Public Safety Bill and the Trade Disputes Bill**.
- **Jatin Das** died in **Lahore jail** in **1929** after a long hunger strike for better treatment of political prisoners.
- **Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev and Rajguru** were hanged at **Lahore** on **23 March 1931** (Lahore Conspiracy Case).
- **Chandrashekhar Azad** died in a police encounter at **Alfred Park, Allahabad**, on **27 February 1931**.
- The slogan **"Inquilab Zindabad"** was coined by **Hasrat Mohani** and made popular by Bhagat Singh; Bhagat Singh wrote **"Why I Am an Atheist"**.
- **Chittagong Armoury Raid (18 April 1930)**: led by **Surya Sen ("Master Da")** under the banner of the **Indian Republican Army**; he was hanged in **1934**.
- Surya Sen's associates: **Ganesh Ghosh, Lokenath Bal, Ambika Chakrabarti, Anant Singh, Ardhendu Dastidar, Pritilata Waddedar and Kalpana Dutt**.
- **Pritilata Waddedar** led the attack on the European Club at Pahartali (**1932**); **Bina Das** fired at the Governor of Bengal at a convocation (**1932**).
- **Baikuntha Shukla** of Bihar shot the approver **Phanindranath Ghosh**, whose evidence had helped to hang Bhagat Singh; he was hanged in **Gaya jail** in **1934**.
- **Udham Singh** shot **Michael O'Dwyer**, Lieutenant-Governor of Punjab at the time of Jallianwala Bagh, at **Caxton Hall, London**, on **13 March 1940**, and was hanged the same year.
- For match questions: the **Azad Hind Fauj (INA)** under Subhas Bose had its headquarters at **Rangoon** from **1944**.
