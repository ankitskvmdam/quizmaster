title: "Lota Rebellion (1855–56)"

---
The **Lota Rebellion** (लोटा विद्रोह) was a protest by **prisoners in the jails of Bihar**, above all the **Muzaffarpur jail** (then in **Tirhut** district), shortly **before the Revolt of 1857**.

**Why.** The British jail authorities decided to take away the prisoners' **brass lotas** (water pots) and give them **earthen vessels** instead. The prisoners saw this as an attack on their **religion and caste**, because a personal brass lota was tied to ritual purity.

**What happened.** The prisoners resisted the order. The anger spread outside the walls: in Muzaffarpur, **townspeople and ryots (peasants) of the district** came out in large numbers in **support of the prisoners** and gathered at the jail. Prisoners in the **Arrah jail** (Shahabad) also protested.

**Outcome.** The government **withdrew the order** and **allowed brass lotas again**. A rule change inside a jail had turned into a popular protest, and the people won.

**Significance.** It showed how strongly people resented British interference in religious and caste practice. Accounts of 1857 count such grievances among the discontent that came to the surface in the great revolt.

**Where sources differ.** Some sources date the order to **1855**; Bihar exam notes give **1856**. One account says the crowd **attacked the jail and freed the prisoners**; J. W. Kaye's account (as quoted) says officials **restored the lotas before the prisoners could escape**. Accounts also disagree on the earlier Alipore jail incident said to have prompted the order, and on who organised the protest.

> **Key facts**
> - The Lota Rebellion was a protest by **prisoners of the Muzaffarpur jail** (Tirhut), Bihar; prisoners in the **Arrah jail** also protested.
> - Cause: the British replaced the prisoners' **brass lotas** with **earthen vessels**.
> - The prisoners saw it as an attack on their **religious and caste sentiments**.
> - **Townspeople and ryots** of Muzaffarpur came out in **support of the prisoners**.
> - Outcome: the government **withdrew the order**; **brass lotas were restored**.
> - It took place **before the Revolt of 1857** (dated **1855** or **1856** in different sources).
