## Gist
The State Cabinet under Chief Minister **Samrat Choudhary** took several decisions in May: land for a food technology institute at **Hajipur** and a CISF training centre in **Kishanganj**, an amended electric vehicle policy, and a policy for hiring young professionals. A Central **MSME Technology Centre** opened at Bihta near Patna.

In education and culture, **Maithili** entered the CBSE curriculum, **Nalanda University** revived the old tradition of Shastrarth, and an animation film on the Rajgir Zoo Safari won a national festival award. Vietnam's President prayed at Bodh Gaya.

Also note the changes at the **BPSC**, the collapse of a part of **Vikramshila Setu**, the recommendation of a new Chief Justice for the Patna High Court, and the IPL record of Bihar's **Vaibhav Sooryavanshi**.

## Key facts
- Union MSME Minister **Jitan Ram Manjhi** and Chief Minister **Samrat Choudhary** inaugurated an **MSME Technology Centre** at **Sikandarpur, Bihta (Patna)**, built at a cost of **Rs 171 crore**.
- Extension centres of the MSME Technology Centre were opened in **Muzaffarpur, Rohtas, Darbhanga and Munger**.
- The Bihar Cabinet approved the free transfer of **100 acres** at **Hajipur (Vaishali)** for the country's **third NIFTEM** (National Institute of Food Technology Entrepreneurship and Management).
- The other NIFTEM campuses are at **Kundli (Haryana)** and **Thanjavur (Tamil Nadu)**; the institute is under the Ministry of Food Processing Industries.
- The Bihar Cabinet approved about 110 acres in the **Pothia** area of **Kishanganj** district for a **CISF Regional Training Centre**.
- The **Bihar Electric Vehicle (Amendment) Policy, 2026** aims to make at least **30 per cent** of new vehicles registered in the State electric by **2030**.
- Under the EV policy, an electric car registered in a **woman's name** gets a subsidy of **Rs 1 lakh**, and all electric vehicles get a 50 per cent rebate on motor vehicle tax.
- The Cabinet approved the **Youth Professional Selection Policy-2026** for hiring young professionals for technical and specialised work in departments.
- **Maithili** will be part of the **CBSE curriculum** from the **2026–27** session; it was added to the **Eighth Schedule** of the Constitution in 2003.
- The Bihar **Cyber Crime and Security Unit** is setting up what is described as India's **first AI-based cyber helpline call centre**, linked to helpline number **1930**.
- **Nalanda University** held **'Shastrarth 2026'** and placed this ancient form of debate on its academic calendar for the first time.
- ***The Wild Call***, an animation film on the **Rajgir Zoo Safari** made by Bihar's Department of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, won **Best Animation Film** at the 16th Dadasaheb Phalke Film Festival 2026.
- Vietnam's President **To Lam** offered prayers at the **Mahabodhi Temple, Bodh Gaya**, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2002.
- The Supreme Court Collegium recommended **Justice Meenakshi M Rai** of the **Sikkim High Court** as the next **Chief Justice of the Patna High Court**; Chief Justice Sangam Kumar Sahoo retires on 4 June 2026.
- The Transport Department's **One-Time Traffic Challan Settlement Scheme** settled over **20,000** pending challans in its first statewide drive, following a Patna High Court order.
- Sports Minister **Shreyasi Singh** launched the **Bihar T10 Cricket League** in Patna, with MP **Manoj Tiwari** as its brand ambassador; the league begins on 5 July.
- **Five players from Bihar** were picked for India's team for the first World Yogasana Championship.
- Bihar's **Vaibhav Sooryavanshi** hit **72 sixes** in IPL 2026, breaking **Chris Gayle's** record for the most sixes in a season.
- The **BPSC** announced a **fifth option, "E" (Not Attempted)**, for its objective questions; leaving every option blank will cost one-third of a mark.
- The BPSC cancelled its **AEDO examination** over irregularities and blacklisted the agency that conducted it.
- The **house-listing phase of Census 2027** was carried out in Bihar from **2 to 31 May 2026**.
- A section of **Vikramshila Setu**, the bridge over the Ganga at **Bhagalpur**, collapsed on **4 May 2026**; no lives were lost.
- BJP's **Suryakumar Sharma** was elected unopposed to the **Bihar Legislative Council** in a by-election.
