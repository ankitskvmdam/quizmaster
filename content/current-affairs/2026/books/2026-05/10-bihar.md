## Industry and institutions

- **MSME Technology Centre:** Union MSME Minister Jitan Ram Manjhi and Chief Minister Samrat Choudhary inaugurated it at Sikandarpur, Bihta (Patna). MSME stands for micro, small and medium enterprises.
  - It was built at a cost of Rs 171 crore.
  - Its extension centres were opened in Muzaffarpur, Rohtas, Darbhanga and Munger.
- **NIFTEM at Hajipur:** The Bihar Cabinet approved the free transfer of 100 acres at Hajipur (Vaishali) for the country's third NIFTEM, the National Institute of Food Technology Entrepreneurship and Management.
  - The other NIFTEM campuses are at Kundli (Haryana) and Thanjavur (Tamil Nadu).
  - The institute is under the Ministry of Food Processing Industries.
- **CISF Regional Training Centre:** The Bihar Cabinet approved about 110 acres for it in the Pothia area of Kishanganj district. CISF is the Central Industrial Security Force.

## State policies and services

- **Bihar Electric Vehicle (Amendment) Policy, 2026:** It aims to make at least 30 per cent of new vehicles registered in the State electric by 2030.
  - An electric car registered in a woman's name gets a subsidy of Rs 1 lakh.
  - All electric vehicles get a 50 per cent rebate on motor vehicle tax.
- **Youth Professional Selection Policy-2026:** The Cabinet approved it for hiring young professionals for technical and specialised work in departments.
- **AI cyber helpline:** The Bihar Cyber Crime and Security Unit is setting up what is described as India's first AI-based cyber helpline call centre. It is linked to helpline number 1930.
- **One-Time Traffic Challan Settlement Scheme:** This scheme of the Transport Department followed a Patna High Court order. Its first statewide drive settled over 20,000 pending challans, which are traffic fine notices.

## Education and culture

- **Maithili:** It will be part of the CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education) curriculum from the 2026–27 session. Maithili was added to the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution in 2003.
- **Shastrarth 2026:** Nalanda University held it. For the first time, it placed this ancient form of debate on its academic calendar.
- **The Wild Call:** This animation film on the Rajgir Zoo Safari won Best Animation Film at the 16th Dadasaheb Phalke Film Festival 2026. It was made by Bihar's Department of Environment, Forest and Climate Change.
- **Bodh Gaya:** Vietnam's President To Lam offered prayers at the Mahabodhi Temple, Bodh Gaya. The temple has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2002.

## BPSC

- **Fifth option:** The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) announced a fifth option, "E" (Not Attempted), for its objective questions. Leaving every option blank will cost one-third of a mark.
- **AEDO examination:** The BPSC cancelled it over irregularities. It blacklisted the agency that conducted it.

## Governance and events

- **2 to 31 May 2026:** The house-listing phase of Census 2027 was carried out in Bihar.
- **4 May 2026:** A section of Vikramshila Setu, the bridge over the Ganga at Bhagalpur, collapsed. No lives were lost.
- **Patna High Court:** The Supreme Court Collegium recommended Justice Meenakshi M Rai of the Sikkim High Court as its next Chief Justice. Chief Justice Sangam Kumar Sahoo retires on 4 June 2026.
- **Bihar Legislative Council:** BJP's Suryakumar Sharma was elected unopposed to it in a by-election.

## Sports

- **Vaibhav Sooryavanshi:** The Bihar cricketer hit 72 sixes in IPL 2026. He broke Chris Gayle's record for the most sixes in a season.
- **Bihar T10 Cricket League:** Sports Minister Shreyasi Singh launched it in Patna. MP Manoj Tiwari is its brand ambassador, and the league begins on 5 July.
- **Yogasana:** Five players from Bihar were picked for India's team for the first World Yogasana Championship.
