## Rupee, reserves and gold

The conflict in West Asia pushed up India's import costs in May.

- **Foreign exchange reserves:** They fell by about USD 38 billion in two months, to about USD 691 billion in May 2026. The rupee weakened past 95 per US dollar.
- **Import duty on gold and silver:** The Government doubled the effective duty, from 9.2 per cent to 18.4 per cent. The basic customs duty went up from 5 to 10 per cent.
- **Gold import bill:** It reached a record USD 71.98 billion in 2025-26.
- **RBI gold:** The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) brought back 168.06 tonnes of gold from abroad in 2025-26. It holds 880.52 tonnes of gold in all, and 77 per cent of it is now held within India.
- **Swap auction:** The RBI sold dollars and announced a USD 5 billion dollar–rupee buy-sell swap auction to support the rupee.

## Banking and markets

- **Rohit Jain:** He was appointed Deputy Governor of the RBI for three years. He succeeded T. Rabi Sankar.
- **Q-SAFE:** The RBI set up this eight-member expert committee, the Quantum Secure and Adaptive Financial Ecosystem. Its task is to prepare the financial sector for quantum-era cyber risks.
- **Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool:** The Department of Financial Services launched it.
  - It is backed by a sovereign guarantee of Rs 12,980 crore.
  - GIC Re, the General Insurance Corporation of India, manages it.
- **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016:** It completed ten years. S&P Global upgraded India's insolvency framework from Group C to Group B.
- **Stock markets:** Taiwan overtook India to become the world's fifth-largest stock market. India slipped to sixth.

## Trade and investment

- **Sugar exports:** The Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) placed all sugar exports in the "Prohibited" category until 30 September 2026. Maharashtra is the largest sugar-producing State.
- **Press Note 2 (2026 Series):** It eases Press Note 3 of 2020. Firms with Chinese or Hong Kong shareholding of up to 10 per cent may now invest through the automatic route, which needs no prior government approval.
- **FDI proposals:** The Commerce Ministry revised its procedure for proposals of foreign direct investment (FDI). The maximum processing time is now 12 weeks.
- **Export destinations:** Singapore overtook the UAE (United Arab Emirates) to become India's second-largest export destination. The United States remains the largest.
- **Special economic zones:** Two new SEZs (special economic zones) were notified in Puducherry. The one by Oulgaret Municipality is India's first SEZ developed by an urban local body.
- **Mangoes:** Japan suspended imports of fresh mangoes from India for the 2026 season. It had found faults in the treatment facilities.

## Agriculture and fisheries

- **Sugarcane price:** The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) raised the Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP) of sugarcane to Rs 365 per quintal for the 2026-27 season.
- **Fertiliser subsidy:** The Cabinet approved a subsidy of Rs 41,534 crore on phosphatic and potassic (P&K) fertilisers for Kharif 2026. Kharif crops are sown in the monsoon.
- **Record foodgrain harvest:** Foodgrain production in 2025-26 touched a record 376.563 million tonnes.
  - Rice: 154.024 million tonnes.
  - Wheat: 120.657 million tonnes.
- **Agricultural exports:** They grew to USD 53.1 billion in 2025-26. Coffee exports crossed USD 2 billion for the first time.
- **Marine fish:** Tamil Nadu recorded the highest marine fish landings in 2025, overtaking Gujarat. The figures come from the ICAR-Central Marine Fisheries Research Institute.

## Industry and infrastructure

- **Coal and lignite gasification:** The Cabinet approved a scheme with an outlay of Rs 37,500 crore. It aims to gasify 75 million tonnes by 2030.
- **Hutti Gold Mines:** This mine in Raichur district, Karnataka, is India's only fully working State-owned gold mine.
- **Shipyard:** An agreement was signed with HD KSOE of South Korea for India's first mega greenfield shipyard, at Thoothukudi in Tamil Nadu. A greenfield project is built on a new site.
- **Barrier-less toll:** The National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) launched India's first barrier-less (multi-lane free flow) toll system. It is on the Surat–Bharuch stretch of NH-48 in Gujarat.
