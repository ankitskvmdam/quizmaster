## Gist
The economy was under pressure from outside in May. The conflict in West Asia pushed up import costs, foreign exchange reserves fell to about **USD 691 billion** and the rupee weakened past **95 to the dollar**. The Government answered by doubling the **import duty on gold and silver**, and the Reserve Bank sold dollars and announced a swap auction.

Trade rules moved in both directions: all **sugar exports were banned** until 30 September 2026, while FDI rules for firms with a small Chinese shareholding were eased. The Cabinet approved a higher sugarcane price, a fertiliser subsidy for the kharif season and a large **coal gasification** scheme.

Farm output gave better news, with a **record foodgrain harvest**. For banking, remember the new RBI Deputy Governor, the Q-SAFE committee and ten years of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code.

## Key facts
- India's foreign exchange reserves fell by about USD 38 billion in two months to about **USD 691 billion** in May 2026, and the rupee crossed **95 per US dollar**.
- The effective import duty on **gold and silver** was raised from **9.2 per cent to 18.4 per cent**; the basic customs duty went up from 5 to 10 per cent.
- India's gold import bill reached a record **USD 71.98 billion** in 2025-26.
- The RBI brought back **168.06 tonnes** of gold from abroad in 2025-26; **77 per cent** of its 880.52 tonnes of gold is now held within India.
- The RBI announced a **USD 5 billion** dollar–rupee **buy-sell swap auction** to support the rupee.
- **Rohit Jain** was appointed **Deputy Governor of the RBI** for three years, succeeding **T. Rabi Sankar**.
- The RBI set up an eight-member expert committee called **Q-SAFE** (Quantum Secure and Adaptive Financial Ecosystem) to prepare the financial sector for quantum-era cyber risks.
- The **CCEA** raised the **Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP) of sugarcane** to **Rs 365 per quintal** for the 2026-27 season.
- The **DGFT** placed all **sugar exports** in the "Prohibited" category until **30 September 2026**; **Maharashtra** is the largest sugar-producing State.
- **Press Note 2 (2026 Series)** allows firms with Chinese or Hong Kong shareholding of up to **10 per cent** to invest through the **automatic route**, easing Press Note 3 of 2020.
- The Commerce Ministry's revised procedure for FDI proposals sets a maximum processing time of **12 weeks**.
- **Singapore** overtook the **UAE** to become India's **second-largest export destination**; the United States remains the largest.
- The Cabinet approved a subsidy of **Rs 41,534 crore** on phosphatic and potassic (P&K) fertilisers for **Kharif 2026**.
- The Cabinet approved a **coal and lignite gasification** scheme with an outlay of **Rs 37,500 crore**, aiming to gasify **75 million tonnes** by 2030.
- The Department of Financial Services launched the **Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool**, backed by a sovereign guarantee of **Rs 12,980 crore**; **GIC Re** manages it.
- The **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016** completed **ten years**; S&P Global upgraded India's insolvency framework from Group C to **Group B**.
- Two new SEZs were notified in **Puducherry**; the one by **Oulgaret Municipality** is India's **first SEZ developed by an urban local body**.
- **Taiwan** overtook India to become the world's **fifth-largest stock market**; India slipped to sixth.
- Foodgrain production in 2025-26 touched a record **376.563 million tonnes**, including 154.024 million tonnes of rice and 120.657 million tonnes of wheat.
- India's agricultural exports grew to **USD 53.1 billion** in 2025-26; **coffee exports** crossed **USD 2 billion** for the first time.
- **Hutti Gold Mines** in **Raichur district, Karnataka**, is India's only fully working State-owned gold mine.
- An agreement was signed with South Korea's **HD KSOE** for India's **first mega greenfield shipyard** at **Thoothukudi**, Tamil Nadu.
- India's **first barrier-less (multi-lane free flow) toll system** was launched on the **Surat–Bharuch** stretch of **NH-48** in Gujarat by the NHAI.
- **Japan** suspended imports of fresh **mangoes** from India for the 2026 season after finding faults in treatment facilities.
- **Tamil Nadu** recorded the highest marine fish landings in 2025, overtaking Gujarat, according to the **ICAR-Central Marine Fisheries Research Institute**.
