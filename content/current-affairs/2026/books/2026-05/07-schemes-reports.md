## Schemes that marked anniversaries

- **Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY):** This scheme of the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas completed 10 years.
  - It has given 10.54 crore LPG connections.
  - The subsidy is Rs 300 per 14.2 kg cylinder.
- **Jan Suraksha schemes:** The three schemes, launched in 2015, completed 11 years. They are the Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY), the Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) and the Atal Pension Yojana.
  - By April 2026, enrolments stood at 58.09 crore in PMSBY, 27.43 crore in PMJJBY and over 9.04 crore in the Atal Pension Yojana.
  - The Atal Pension Yojana is administered by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA).
- **Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana:** It completed 25 years. Its fourth phase, PMGSY-IV, was launched nationally at Bhairunda, Madhya Pradesh.
  - PMGSY-IV will build 62,500 km of roads to connect 25,000 habitations.
  - It will cost Rs 70,125 crore.

## New schemes and missions

- **Mission for Cotton Productivity:** The Cabinet approved it with an outlay of Rs 5,659 crore up to 2030-31. The Agriculture and Textiles Ministries will run it jointly.
- **SARTHAK-PDS:** The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) approved this umbrella scheme to modernise the Public Distribution System (PDS), the ration system. Its outlay is Rs 25,530 crore for 2026–31.
- **BHAVYA (Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojana):** The Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) issued its guidelines. It will develop 100 industrial parks at a cost of about Rs 33,660 crore.
- **VB-G RAM G Act, 2025:** Draft rules were released for it.
  - It replaces MGNREGA (the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act) from July 2026.
  - It raises guaranteed work from 100 to 125 days a year.
- **JANANI:** The Health Ministry launched this platform for mother and child care. It is an upgrade of the RCH (Reproductive and Child Health) portal.
- **"One State, one product" missions for the North-East:** The Ministry of DoNER (Development of North Eastern Region) launched the Mizoram Ginger Mission and Mission Arunachal Kiwi. Mission Queen Pineapple was launched in Tripura.
- **PM MITRA textile park:** The Prime Minister inaugurated one at Warangal, Telangana.

## Surveys and data

- **NFHS-6 (2023-24):** The Health Ministry released the sixth National Family Health Survey. Its nodal agency is the IIPS (International Institute for Population Sciences), Mumbai.
  - The total fertility rate, the average number of children per woman, was 2.0.
  - Stunting among children, low height for their age, fell from 35.5 to 29.3 per cent.
  - Institutional deliveries rose to 90.6 per cent.
- **Crime in India 2024:** The National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB) report recorded 58.85 lakh cognisable crimes, about 6 per cent fewer than in 2023. A cognisable crime is one where police may arrest without a warrant.
  - Cybercrimes rose 17 per cent.
  - Nagaland had the lowest crime rate. Telangana had the highest rate of cybercrime.
- **Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS):** The first calendar-year PLFS (2025) of the National Statistical Office was released.
  - The labour force participation rate was 59.3 per cent.
  - The unemployment rate was 3.1 per cent.

## States and panchayats

- **Sikkim:** It became India's fifth fully literate State under the ULLAS programme. It came after Mizoram, Goa, Tripura and Himachal Pradesh.
- **National Panchayat Awards 2025:** Karnataka won the most awards (6) among 42 selected panchayats.
