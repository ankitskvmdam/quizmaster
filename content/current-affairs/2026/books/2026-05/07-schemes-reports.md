## Gist
Several flagship schemes had anniversaries in May: **ten years of Ujjwala**, **eleven years of the three Jan Suraksha schemes** and **twenty-five years of the Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana**, whose fourth phase was launched. The Cabinet approved new schemes for cotton, for the ration system (**SARTHAK-PDS**) and, through DPIIT, for industrial parks (**BHAVYA**). Draft rules were issued for the law that replaces MGNREGA.

The big data releases were the **National Family Health Survey-6**, the **NCRB's Crime in India 2024** and the first calendar-year **Periodic Labour Force Survey**. Among global reports, India slipped to 157th in the **World Press Freedom Index** and stayed the world's largest receiver of remittances.

The North-East got three "one State, one product" missions: ginger in Mizoram, kiwi in Arunachal Pradesh and pineapple in Tripura.

## Key facts
- The **Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)** of the **Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas** completed **10 years**; it has given **10.54 crore** LPG connections, with a subsidy of **Rs 300** per 14.2 kg cylinder.
- The three **Jan Suraksha schemes** (PMJJBY, PMSBY and Atal Pension Yojana), launched in 2015, completed **11 years**; the Atal Pension Yojana is administered by the **PFRDA**.
- By April 2026, enrolments stood at **58.09 crore** in PMSBY, **27.43 crore** in PMJJBY and over **9.04 crore** in the Atal Pension Yojana.
- The **Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana** completed **25 years**; **PMGSY-IV** was launched nationally at **Bhairunda, Madhya Pradesh**.
- PMGSY-IV will build **62,500 km** of roads to connect **25,000 habitations**, at a cost of **Rs 70,125 crore**.
- The Cabinet approved the **Mission for Cotton Productivity** with an outlay of **Rs 5,659 crore** up to 2030-31, run jointly by the Agriculture and Textiles Ministries.
- The CCEA approved **SARTHAK-PDS**, an umbrella scheme to modernise the Public Distribution System, with an outlay of **Rs 25,530 crore** for 2026–31.
- **DPIIT** issued guidelines for **BHAVYA (Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojana)**, which will develop **100 industrial parks** at a cost of about **Rs 33,660 crore**.
- Draft rules were released for the **VB-G RAM G Act, 2025**, which replaces **MGNREGA** from **July 2026** and raises guaranteed work from 100 to **125 days** a year.
- The Health Ministry launched the **JANANI** platform for mother and child care; it is an upgrade of the RCH portal.
- The Ministry of DoNER launched the **Mizoram Ginger Mission** and **Mission Arunachal Kiwi**; **Mission Queen Pineapple** was launched in **Tripura**.
- The Prime Minister inaugurated the **PM MITRA textile park** at **Warangal, Telangana**.
- **Sikkim** became India's **fifth fully literate State** under the **ULLAS** programme, after Mizoram, Goa, Tripura and Himachal Pradesh.
- **NFHS-6 (2023-24)**, released by the Health Ministry, put the **total fertility rate at 2.0**; stunting among children fell from 35.5 to **29.3 per cent**.
- In NFHS-6, **institutional deliveries** rose to **90.6 per cent**; the nodal agency for the survey is the **IIPS, Mumbai**.
- The **NCRB's Crime in India 2024** report recorded **58.85 lakh** cognisable crimes, about 6 per cent fewer than in 2023; **cybercrimes rose 17 per cent**.
- According to the NCRB report, **Nagaland** had the lowest crime rate and **Telangana** the highest rate of cybercrime.
- The first **calendar-year PLFS (2025)** of the National Statistical Office put the labour force participation rate at **59.3 per cent** and the unemployment rate at **3.1 per cent**.
- **NITI Aayog's** report on school education found that four in ten children drop out before finishing higher secondary school, and asked for education spending of **6 per cent of GDP**.
- In the **World Press Freedom Index 2026** of **Reporters Without Borders**, India ranked **157th** of 180 countries (151st in 2025); **Norway** was first and **Eritrea** last.
- In the **Times Higher Education Asia University Rankings 2026**, India had the most ranked institutions (**128**); **IISc Bengaluru** was the best Indian institution at **43rd**.
- The **World Migration Report 2026** of the **International Organization for Migration (IOM)** named India the world's largest recipient of remittances, at **USD 137.67 billion** in 2024.
- In the **National Panchayat Awards 2025**, **Karnataka** won the most awards (6) among 42 selected panchayats.
- In the **Logistics Port Performance Index 2024-25** of the Ports Ministry, **Paradip** topped among dry bulk ports and **Mundra** among container ports.
- The Sample Registration System report for 2024 showed that **non-communicable diseases** cause **60 per cent** of all deaths in India.
