## At a glance: the five Assembly elections

Votes were counted on 4 May 2026. Five Chief Ministers were sworn in within a fortnight.

| State | Result | Chief Minister | Sworn in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| West Bengal | BJP 206 seats, Trinamool Congress 80 | Suvendu Adhikari | 9 May 2026 |
| Tamil Nadu | TVK 108 of 234 seats (hung Assembly) | C. Joseph Vijay | 10 May 2026 |
| Assam | BJP 82 of 126 seats | Himanta Biswa Sarma | 12 May 2026 |
| Puducherry | NDA kept power | N. Rangasamy (AINRC) | 13 May 2026 |
| Kerala | Congress-led UDF defeated the LDF | V.D. Satheesan | 18 May 2026 |

## Elections

- **4 May 2026:** Votes were counted for the Assembly elections of West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Assam and Puducherry. The National Democratic Alliance (NDA) kept Assam and Puducherry.
- **West Bengal:** The BJP won the State for the first time. Its win ended 15 years of TMC (Trinamool Congress) rule.
  - The Governor dissolved the West Bengal Legislative Assembly on 7 May 2026 under Article 174.
  - Suvendu Adhikari took oath as the first BJP Chief Minister of West Bengal. The ceremony was at the Brigade Parade Ground in Kolkata.
- **Tamil Nadu:** Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), led by Vijay, became the largest party. Its 108 seats were 10 short of the majority mark of 118.
  - It was the State's first hung Assembly, one where no party has a majority.
  - Outgoing Chief Minister M.K. Stalin lost his Kolathur seat.
- **Assam:** Himanta Biswa Sarma took oath for a second term.
- **Puducherry:** N. Rangasamy of the All India N.R. Congress (AINRC) became Chief Minister for a record fifth term.
- **Kerala:** The United Democratic Front (UDF), led by the Congress, defeated the Left Democratic Front (LDF).
- **30 May 2026:** The Election Commission began Phase 3 of the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls. It covers 16 States and 3 Union Territories.

## Laws and bills

- **Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Bill, 2026:** The Union Cabinet approved it. It raises the sanctioned strength of the Supreme Court from 34 to 38 judges, including the Chief Justice of India.
- **Vande Mataram:** The Cabinet cleared an amendment to the Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971. Intentional insult to Vande Mataram will be punishable with up to 3 years in jail.
- **Assam Uniform Civil Code Bill, 2026:** The Assam Assembly passed it.
  - Assam is the third State to adopt a Uniform Civil Code (UCC), after Uttarakhand and Gujarat. It is the first in the North-East.
  - Scheduled Tribes are exempt.

## Courts and rulings

- **Sikkim:** It was declared India's first State with a fully paperless judiciary. Chief Justice of India Surya Kant announced it at a conclave in Gangtok.
- **New Supreme Court tools:** The Supreme Court launched two.
  - 'One Case One Data' links case data from taluka courts up to the Supreme Court.
  - 'Su Sahay' is an AI chatbot built by the National Informatics Centre (NIC).
- **Forcible acid ingestion:** The Supreme Court held that its survivors are also "acid attack victims" under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. It used its powers under Article 142, which lets it do complete justice in a case.
- **Safe travel on National Highways:** The Supreme Court declared the right to safe travel on National Highways a part of the right to life under Article 21.
- **BCCI and RTI:** The Central Information Commission ruled that the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) is not a "public authority" under the Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005. This reversed its own order of 2018.
- **Bhojshala:** The Madhya Pradesh High Court declared the Bhojshala–Kamal Maula complex in Dhar a temple of Goddess Vagdevi (Saraswati).

## Institutions and States

- **1 May 2026:** On Labour Day, Karnataka became the first State to launch a grievance redressal system for gig workers. Gig workers are paid per task, not a salary.
- **1 May 2026:** The Directorate of Enforcement completed 70 years. It works under the Department of Revenue in the Ministry of Finance.
- **16 May:** Sikkim celebrated its 51st Statehood Day. It became India's 22nd State in 1975 through the 36th Constitutional Amendment.
- **Demographic change:** The Home Ministry set up a High-Level Committee on demographic change. It is headed by retired Supreme Court judge Justice Prakash Prabhakar Navlekar and has one year to report.
- **NEET-UG 2026:** The National Testing Agency (NTA) cancelled the exam after a paper leak. The probe was handed to the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI).
- **Kerala:** It became the first State to create a separate ministerial portfolio for Artificial Intelligence (AI).
