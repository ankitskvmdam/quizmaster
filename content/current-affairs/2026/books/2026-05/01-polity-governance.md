## Gist
The month was dominated by the results of five Assembly elections, counted on **4 May 2026**. The **BJP** won West Bengal for the first time, **Vijay's TVK** became the largest party in a hung Tamil Nadu Assembly, the **Congress-led UDF** took Kerala, and the NDA kept Assam and Puducherry. Five Chief Ministers were sworn in within a fortnight.

At the Centre, the Cabinet cleared a Bill to raise the strength of the **Supreme Court to 38 judges** and another to punish insult to **Vande Mataram**. The courts gave several rulings on rights: safe travel on highways, acid attack survivors, and the status of the BCCI under the RTI Act.

Among the States, **Sikkim** became the first with a paperless judiciary, **Karnataka** the first with a grievance system for gig workers, and **Assam** the third to pass a Uniform Civil Code.

## Key facts
- Votes for the Assembly elections of West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Assam and Puducherry were counted on **4 May 2026**.
- In **West Bengal** the **BJP won 206 seats** and the Trinamool Congress 80, ending 15 years of TMC rule.
- **Suvendu Adhikari** took oath on **9 May 2026** as the **first BJP Chief Minister of West Bengal**, at the Brigade Parade Ground in Kolkata.
- In **Tamil Nadu**, **Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK)** won **108 of 234 seats**, 10 short of the majority mark of 118; it was the State's first hung Assembly.
- TVK leader **C. Joseph Vijay** was sworn in as Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu on **10 May 2026**; outgoing Chief Minister M.K. Stalin lost his **Kolathur** seat.
- In **Kerala** the **Congress-led UDF** defeated the LDF, and **V.D. Satheesan** was sworn in as Chief Minister on **18 May 2026**.
- In **Assam** the BJP won **82 of 126 seats**; **Himanta Biswa Sarma** took oath for a second term on **12 May 2026**.
- In **Puducherry**, **N. Rangasamy** of the AINRC was sworn in as Chief Minister for a record **fifth term** on **13 May 2026**.
- The Governor dissolved the West Bengal Legislative Assembly on 7 May 2026 under **Article 174**.
- The Union Cabinet approved the **Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Bill, 2026**, raising the sanctioned strength from **34 to 38**, including the Chief Justice of India.
- The Cabinet cleared an amendment to the **Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971**, making intentional insult to **Vande Mataram** punishable with up to **3 years** in jail.
- **Sikkim** was declared India's **first State with a fully paperless judiciary**; Chief Justice of India **Surya Kant** announced it at a conclave in Gangtok.
- The Supreme Court launched **'One Case One Data'**, which links case data from taluka courts up to the Supreme Court, and **'Su Sahay'**, an AI chatbot built by the **National Informatics Centre**.
- On **1 May 2026** (Labour Day), **Karnataka** became the first State to launch a grievance redressal system for **gig workers**.
- The Election Commission announced **Phase 3 of the Special Intensive Revision (SIR)** of electoral rolls from **30 May 2026**, covering **16 States and 3 Union Territories**.
- The Supreme Court held that survivors of **forcible acid ingestion** are also "acid attack victims" under the **Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016**, using its powers under **Article 142**.
- The Supreme Court declared the **right to safe travel on National Highways** a part of the right to life under **Article 21**.
- The Assam Assembly passed the **Assam Uniform Civil Code Bill, 2026**; Assam is the **third State** after Uttarakhand and Gujarat, and the first in the North-East, to adopt a UCC. Scheduled Tribes are exempt.
- The Home Ministry set up a **High-Level Committee on demographic change** under retired Supreme Court judge **Justice Prakash Prabhakar Navlekar**; it has one year to report.
- The **Central Information Commission** ruled that the **BCCI is not a "public authority"** under the RTI Act, 2005, reversing its own order of 2018.
- The Madhya Pradesh High Court declared the **Bhojshala–Kamal Maula complex in Dhar** a temple of Goddess **Vagdevi (Saraswati)**.
- The **National Testing Agency** cancelled **NEET-UG 2026** after a paper leak; the probe was handed to the **CBI**.
- The **Directorate of Enforcement** completed **70 years** on 1 May 2026; it works under the **Department of Revenue**, Ministry of Finance.
- **Sikkim** celebrated its 51st Statehood Day on **16 May**; it became India's **22nd State** in 1975 through the **36th Constitutional Amendment**.
- **Kerala** became the first State to create a separate ministerial portfolio for **Artificial Intelligence**.
