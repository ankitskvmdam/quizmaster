## Gist
Wildlife disease was the worrying story of the month. Tigers died in **Kanha** from suspected **canine distemper virus**, and lion cubs died around **Gir** from a tick-borne infection. At the same time India prepared to host the **first summit of the International Big Cat Alliance** in New Delhi on 1–2 June 2026.

The Supreme Court was busy with the environment: it allowed dangerous stray dogs to be put down, gave District Collectors power to enforce the waste rules, agreed to examine the wetland rules, and pulled up three States over sand mining in the Chambal sanctuary.

For species questions, note the new tree from Kerala, the new frog from Nagaland, the dragonfly seen again after more than a century, and the first Ganges soft-shell turtle fitted with a satellite tag.

## Key facts
- India will host the **first International Big Cat Alliance (IBCA) Summit** in **New Delhi on 1–2 June 2026**; its theme is **"Save Big Cats, Save Humanity, Save Ecosystem"**.
- The IBCA covers **seven big cats** (tiger, lion, leopard, snow leopard, cheetah, jaguar and puma) and has **24 member countries**; the summit is expected to adopt the **Delhi Declaration**.
- A tigress and her four cubs died in **Kanha Tiger Reserve, Madhya Pradesh**; officials suspect **canine distemper virus**.
- India recorded **166 tiger deaths in 2025**, the highest in a year since 1973.
- Eight lion cubs died around **Gir** in Gujarat from suspected **Babesia** infection, a disease spread by **ticks**.
- The first photographs of **rusty-spotted cats** breeding in the **Aravallis** of Delhi-NCR were taken in Faridabad; the species is **Near Threatened** on the IUCN Red List.
- The Delhi Government declared about **670 hectares** of the **Central Ridge** a **reserved forest** under Section 20 of the **Indian Forest Act, 1927**.
- India's **first satellite-tagged Ganges soft-shell turtle** was released in **Kaziranga**, Assam, by the **Wildlife Institute of India**; the species is **Endangered**.
- A Wildlife Institute of India study found that **barn swallows** now breed all year round in the **Imphal Valley** of Manipur.
- A new evergreen tree species, ***Humboldtia nairiana***, was discovered in **Shendurney Wildlife Sanctuary**, Kerala.
- The dragonfly ***Gynacantha khasiaca*** (long-tailed duskhawker) was rediscovered in **Namdapha National Park**, Arunachal Pradesh; it was last recorded in **1914**.
- The **Zoological Survey of India** described a new cascade frog, ***Amolops kamal***, from **Kiphire district, Nagaland**.
- A study in *PLoS Biology* built a **DNA-based map** to trace the origins of trafficked **pangolins**.
- **Mayon Volcano** in **Albay province, Philippines**, erupted and the alert was raised to Level 3.
- **Vantara** of Jamnagar offered to take **80 hippos** from **Colombia**, which has declared hippos an invasive species.
- The Supreme Court allowed civic bodies to **euthanise rabid, incurably ill or dangerously aggressive stray dogs** under the Animal Birth Control Rules, 2023.
- The Supreme Court directed that **District Collectors** be given powers to enforce the **Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026**.
- The Supreme Court agreed to examine the validity of the **Wetlands (Conservation and Management) Rules, 2017**; India has **94 Ramsar sites**.
- The Supreme Court told **Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan** to act against illegal sand mining in the **National Chambal Sanctuary**, home of the gharial.
- **Kerala** finalised a draft **Oil Spill Contingency Plan** for its **590 km** coastline.
- A **Morgan Stanley** report ranked India **third in the world** in installed renewable energy capacity, after China and the United States.
- India added a record **55.3 GW** of non-fossil power capacity in 2025-26.
- An **IIT Delhi** study estimated that fully controlling **sulphur dioxide** from coal power plants could prevent **1.24 lakh deaths** a year.
