The IUCN (International Union for Conservation of Nature) Red List grades how threatened each species is.

## Big cats

- **International Big Cat Alliance (IBCA):** India will host the first IBCA Summit in New Delhi on 1–2 June 2026.
  - Its theme is "Save Big Cats, Save Humanity, Save Ecosystem".
  - The IBCA covers seven big cats: tiger, lion, leopard, snow leopard, cheetah, jaguar and puma. It has 24 member countries.
  - The summit is expected to adopt the Delhi Declaration.
- **Kanha:** A tigress and her four cubs died in Kanha Tiger Reserve, Madhya Pradesh. Officials suspect canine distemper virus.
- **Tiger deaths:** India recorded 166 tiger deaths in 2025. It was the highest in a year since 1973.
- **Gir:** Eight lion cubs died around Gir in Gujarat from suspected Babesia infection. This disease is spread by ticks.

## Court rulings

- **Stray dogs:** The Supreme Court allowed civic bodies to euthanise rabid, incurably ill or dangerously aggressive stray dogs under the Animal Birth Control Rules, 2023. To euthanise is to put to death painlessly.
- **Waste rules:** The Supreme Court directed that District Collectors be given powers to enforce the Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026.
- **Wetlands:** The Supreme Court agreed to examine the validity of the Wetlands (Conservation and Management) Rules, 2017. India has 94 Ramsar sites, which are wetlands of international importance.
- **Chambal sand mining:** The Supreme Court told Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan to act against illegal sand mining in the National Chambal Sanctuary. The sanctuary is home of the gharial.

## Forests, coasts and wildlife trade

- **Central Ridge:** The Delhi Government declared about 670 hectares of the Central Ridge a reserved forest. It used Section 20 of the Indian Forest Act, 1927.
- **Pangolins:** A study in PLoS Biology built a DNA-based map to trace the origins of trafficked pangolins.
- **Hippos:** Vantara of Jamnagar offered to take 80 hippos from Colombia. Colombia has declared hippos an invasive species.
- **Oil spills:** Kerala finalised a draft Oil Spill Contingency Plan for its 590 km coastline.
- **Mayon Volcano:** It erupted in Albay province, Philippines. The alert was raised to Level 3.

## Energy and pollution

- **Renewable energy:** A Morgan Stanley report ranked India third in the world in installed renewable energy capacity, after China and the United States.
- **Non-fossil power:** India added a record 55.3 GW of non-fossil power capacity in 2025-26.
- **Sulphur dioxide:** An IIT Delhi study estimated that fully controlling sulphur dioxide from coal power plants could prevent 1.24 lakh deaths a year.
