## Space

- **3 May 2026:** Bengaluru startup GalaxEye launched Mission Drishti on a SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket from Vandenberg, California.
  - It is the world's first OptoSAR satellite. It carries optical and synthetic aperture radar sensors together, so it takes optical and radar pictures of the same place at once.
  - At about 190 kg, it is India's largest privately built Earth observation satellite.
- **Pathfinder:** Pixxel and Sarvam AI are building 'Pathfinder', India's first orbital data centre satellite. It is to be launched by the last quarter of 2026.
- **Chandrayaan-3 hop:** ISRO scientists reported new results from this experiment. The Vikram lander lifted off and moved about 50 cm, using leftover propellant.
- **Chandrayaan-2:** Scientists of the Physical Research Laboratory, Ahmedabad, used the DFSAR radar on Chandrayaan-2 to look near the Moon's south pole. They found signs of possible buried ice, strongest inside Faustini crater.
- **Shukrayaan:** Sweden will supply the Venusian Neutrals Analyzer for ISRO's Venus orbiter. Shukrayaan is expected to launch in 2028.

## Health

- **PCOS renamed:** Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) was renamed Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome (PMOS).
- **Ebola:** The 17th Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo was confirmed.
  - It is caused by the Bundibugyo virus. There is no licensed vaccine for this virus.
  - The WHO declared the Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak in the DR Congo and Uganda a Public Health Emergency of International Concern (PHEIC).
- **Hantavirus:** A suspected hantavirus outbreak was reported aboard the Dutch cruise ship MV Hondius off Cape Verde.
- **Stroke:** The World Health Assembly passed its first-ever resolution on stroke. It called stroke a public health priority.

## Electronics, AI and cyber security

- **Cell Broadcast System:** The Ministry of Communications launched this indigenous system for disaster alerts. It was developed by C-DOT (Centre for Development of Telematics) and is linked to the SACHET platform.
- **Semiconductors:** The Union Cabinet approved two more semiconductor units in Gujarat.
  - One is India's first commercial gallium nitride (GaN) Mini/Micro-LED display plant.
  - This takes the plants under the India Semiconductor Mission to 12.
- **AI in healthcare:** IndiaAI and the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) signed an agreement. ICMR will share datasets on the AIKosh platform.
- **Post-quantum cryptography:** A task force of the Department of Science and Technology recommended that critical sectors fully adopt post-quantum cryptography by 2029. This is encryption meant to resist quantum computers.
- **Claude Mythos:** The Department of Financial Services asked banks to strengthen cyber security against this AI model of Anthropic, which can find software flaws. Access to it is restricted under Project Glasswing.
- **Supercomputing:** C-DAC (Centre for Development of Advanced Computing) and G42 of the UAE agreed to build a supercomputing cluster of 8 exaflops in India.

## Weather and energy

- **Monsoon forecasts:** The India Meteorological Department (IMD) began AI-based monsoon forecasts at block level. They are issued every Wednesday for the next four weeks.
- **BharatFS:** India's weather model runs at a resolution of 6 km. Mission Mausam is run by the Ministry of Earth Sciences.
- **Coal to nuclear:** The Government shortlisted three old coal-fired power plant sites for conversion to nuclear power. The target is 100 GWe of nuclear capacity by 2047.

## Days and anniversaries

- **11 May:** National Technology Day was observed. The 2026 theme was "Responsible Innovation for Inclusive Growth". The day recalls the Pokhran-II nuclear tests of 1998.
- **IACS:** The Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science, founded in 1876 by Dr Mahendralal Sircar, completed 150 years.
