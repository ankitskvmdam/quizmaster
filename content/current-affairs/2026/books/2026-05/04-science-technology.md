## Gist
Private space firms made the headlines. **GalaxEye's Mission Drishti**, launched on 3 May 2026, is the world's first satellite to take optical and radar pictures of the same place at once, and **Pixxel** announced India's first data-centre satellite. ISRO's older Moon missions kept giving results: new findings from the **Chandrayaan-3 hop** and radar signs of buried ice from **Chandrayaan-2**.

In health, the WHO declared an **Ebola outbreak caused by the Bundibugyo virus** an international emergency, a hantavirus cluster appeared on a cruise ship, and the World Health Assembly passed its first resolution on stroke. The condition known as PCOS was given a new name.

At home, the Government launched an indigenous disaster-alert system, approved two more semiconductor units in Gujarat, and began planning for quantum-safe encryption.

## Key facts
- Bengaluru startup **GalaxEye** launched **Mission Drishti** on **3 May 2026** on a **SpaceX Falcon 9** rocket from Vandenberg, California.
- Mission Drishti is the **world's first OptoSAR satellite**, carrying optical and synthetic aperture radar sensors together; at about **190 kg** it is India's largest privately built Earth observation satellite.
- **Pixxel** and **Sarvam AI** are building **'Pathfinder'**, India's **first orbital data centre satellite**, for launch by the last quarter of 2026.
- ISRO scientists reported that in the **Chandrayaan-3 hop experiment** the **Vikram lander** lifted off and moved about **50 cm**, using leftover propellant.
- Scientists of the **Physical Research Laboratory, Ahmedabad**, used the **DFSAR radar on Chandrayaan-2** to find signs of possible buried ice near the Moon's south pole, strongest inside **Faustini crater**.
- Sweden will supply an instrument, the **Venusian Neutrals Analyzer**, for ISRO's Venus orbiter **Shukrayaan**, expected to launch in **2028**.
- The Ministry of Communications launched an indigenous **Cell Broadcast System** for disaster alerts, developed by **C-DOT** and linked to the **SACHET** platform.
- **National Technology Day** was observed on **11 May**; the 2026 theme was **"Responsible Innovation for Inclusive Growth"**. The day recalls the Pokhran-II nuclear tests of 1998.
- The Union Cabinet approved **two more semiconductor units in Gujarat**, including India's first commercial **gallium nitride (GaN) Mini/Micro-LED display** plant; this takes the plants under the India Semiconductor Mission to **12**.
- **IndiaAI** and the **ICMR** signed an agreement on artificial intelligence in healthcare; ICMR will share datasets on the **AIKosh** platform.
- The **India Meteorological Department** began **AI-based monsoon forecasts at block level**, issued every Wednesday for the next four weeks.
- India's weather model **BharatFS** runs at a resolution of **6 km**; **Mission Mausam** is run by the Ministry of Earth Sciences.
- **Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)** was renamed **Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome (PMOS)**.
- The **17th Ebola outbreak** in the **Democratic Republic of the Congo** was confirmed; it is caused by the **Bundibugyo virus**, for which there is no licensed vaccine.
- The **WHO** declared the Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak in the DR Congo and **Uganda** a **Public Health Emergency of International Concern (PHEIC)**.
- A suspected **hantavirus** outbreak was reported aboard the Dutch cruise ship **MV Hondius** off **Cape Verde**.
- The **World Health Assembly** passed its **first-ever resolution on stroke**, calling it a public health priority.
- The **Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science (IACS)**, founded in **1876** by **Dr Mahendralal Sircar**, completed **150 years**.
- A task force of the **Department of Science and Technology** recommended that critical sectors fully adopt **post-quantum cryptography by 2029**.
- The Government shortlisted **three old coal-fired power plant sites** for conversion to nuclear power; the target is **100 GWe** of nuclear capacity by 2047.
- The Department of Financial Services asked banks to strengthen cyber security against **Claude Mythos**, an AI model of **Anthropic** that can find software flaws; access to it is restricted under **Project Glasswing**.
- **C-DAC** and the UAE's **G42** agreed to build a supercomputing cluster of **8 exaflops** in India.
