## The Prime Minister's tour

The Prime Minister visited the UAE, the Netherlands, Sweden, Norway and Italy.

- **UAE:** ADNOC, the Abu Dhabi National Oil Company, agreed to store up to 30 million barrels of crude oil in India's strategic petroleum reserves. UAE entities pledged USD 5 billion of investment.
  - India–UAE bilateral trade reached USD 101.25 billion in 2025-26.
- **Netherlands:** The new Strategic Partnership comes with a roadmap for 2026–2030.
  - The University of Leiden returned the Anaimangalam (Leiden) Copper Plates to India.
- **Norway:** The Prime Minister attended the India–Nordic Summit in Oslo.
  - It was the first visit by an Indian Prime Minister to Norway in 43 years.
  - Finland will host the fourth summit.
- **Sweden:** India and Sweden adopted a Joint Action Plan for 2026–2030. LeadIT 3.0 will be launched at COP31, the UN climate conference, in Antalya, Türkiye.
- **Italy:** India and Italy set a trade target of EUR 20 billion by 2029. They will mark 2027 as the Year of Culture and Tourism.

## Visitors to India

- **Vietnam:** India and Vietnam set a trade target of USD 25 billion by 2030 during the visit of President To Lam.
- **Cyprus:** The new Strategic Partnership comes with a defence roadmap for 2026–2031.
- **Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative (IPOI):** Vietnam, Norway and Cyprus joined this initiative of India in May 2026.

## Groupings and India's roles

- **BRICS Foreign Ministers' Meeting:** It was held in New Delhi.
  - It ended with a Chair's Statement instead of a joint declaration. The reason was differences between Iran and the UAE.
  - India's theme as BRICS chair in 2026 is "Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability".
- **11th Quad Foreign Ministers' Meeting:** It was held in New Delhi. It launched the Indo-Pacific Maritime Surveillance Collaboration (IPMSC).
- **Kimberley Process:** It regulates the trade in rough diamonds. India, as Chair of the Kimberley Process for 2026, hosted its intersessional meeting in Mumbai.
- **ITU:** The Council of the International Telecommunication Union (ITU) accepted India's proposal to host the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference in 2030. The 2026 conference is in Doha, Qatar.
- **Common Criteria Development Board (CCDB):** India took over as its Chair for April 2026 to April 2028. The board deals with IT security certification.

## The UN and world affairs

- **Next UN Secretary-General:** The UN began the process of choosing one. António Guterres's second term ends on 31 December 2026.
- **NPT Review Conference:** The review conference of the nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT) ended without a consensus document for the third time in a row. The dispute was over Iran's nuclear programme.
- **Climate resolution:** The UN General Assembly adopted a resolution moved by Vanuatu, by 141 votes to 8. India abstained.
- **Indus Waters Treaty:** India rejected an award of the Permanent Court of Arbitration on the treaty. The award concerned the Kishenganga and Ratle hydro projects.
- **WMCC:** The 35th meeting of the WMCC on India–China border affairs was held in Beijing.
- **US and China:** At a summit in China, they agreed that China would buy 200 Boeing aircraft.
- **Project Freedom:** The US launched it to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and bring out civilians stranded on ships.
