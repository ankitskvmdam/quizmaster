## Gist
May was a month of partnerships. The Prime Minister toured the **UAE, the Netherlands, Sweden, Norway and Italy**, and almost every stop ended with ties being raised a level: a Strategic Partnership with the Netherlands and Sweden, a **Special Strategic Partnership** with Italy, and a green partnership with the Nordic countries at their third summit in **Oslo**. At home, the Presidents of **Vietnam** and **Cyprus** came on State Visits.

India also hosted two groupings in New Delhi: the **BRICS Foreign Ministers**, who could not agree on a joint declaration, and the **Quad Foreign Ministers**, who launched a new maritime surveillance initiative.

Elsewhere, the review conference of the nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty failed again, the race to choose the next UN Secretary-General began, and India rejected an arbitration award on the Indus Waters Treaty.

## Key facts
- During the State Visit of Vietnam's President **To Lam**, India and Vietnam raised ties to an **Enhanced Comprehensive Strategic Partnership** and set a trade target of **USD 25 billion by 2030**.
- **Vietnam, Norway and Cyprus** joined India's **Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative (IPOI)** in May 2026.
- During the Prime Minister's visit to the **UAE**, **ADNOC** agreed to store up to **30 million barrels** of crude oil in India's strategic petroleum reserves, and UAE entities pledged **USD 5 billion** of investment.
- India–UAE bilateral trade reached **USD 101.25 billion** in 2025-26.
- India and the **Netherlands** raised ties to a **Strategic Partnership** with a roadmap for **2026–2030**.
- The University of Leiden returned the **Anaimangalam (Leiden) Copper Plates** to India.
- The **third India–Nordic Summit** was held in **Oslo, Norway**; ties were upgraded to a **Green Technology and Innovation Strategic Partnership**, and **Finland** will host the fourth summit.
- It was the first visit by an Indian Prime Minister to **Norway in 43 years**.
- India and **Sweden** raised ties to a **Strategic Partnership** and adopted a Joint Action Plan for 2026–2030; **LeadIT 3.0** will be launched at **COP31** in Antalya, Türkiye.
- India and **Italy** raised ties to a **Special Strategic Partnership**, set a trade target of **EUR 20 billion by 2029**, and will mark **2027** as the Year of Culture and Tourism.
- India and **Cyprus** raised ties to a **Strategic Partnership** during the State Visit of the President of Cyprus, with a defence roadmap for 2026–2031.
- The **BRICS Foreign Ministers' Meeting** in **New Delhi** ended with a Chair's Statement instead of a joint declaration, because of differences between Iran and the UAE.
- India's theme as BRICS chair in 2026 is **"Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability"**.
- The **11th Quad Foreign Ministers' Meeting** was held in **New Delhi**; it launched the **Indo-Pacific Maritime Surveillance Collaboration (IPMSC)**.
- India, as **Chair of the Kimberley Process for 2026**, hosted its intersessional meeting in **Mumbai**; the Kimberley Process regulates the trade in rough **diamonds**.
- The UN began the process of choosing the next Secretary-General; **António Guterres's** second term ends on **31 December 2026**.
- The **NPT Review Conference** ended without a consensus document for the **third time in a row**, over Iran's nuclear programme.
- The UN General Assembly adopted a climate resolution moved by **Vanuatu** by 141 votes to 8; **India abstained**.
- India rejected an award of the **Permanent Court of Arbitration** on the **Indus Waters Treaty**, which concerned the **Kishenganga** and **Ratle** hydro projects.
- The **35th meeting of the WMCC** on India–China border affairs was held in **Beijing**.
- At a summit in China, the US and China agreed that China would buy **200 Boeing aircraft**.
- The US launched **Project Freedom** to reopen the **Strait of Hormuz** and bring out civilians stranded on ships.
- The ITU Council accepted India's proposal to host the **ITU Plenipotentiary Conference in 2030**; the 2026 conference is in **Doha, Qatar**.
- India took over as Chair of the **Common Criteria Development Board (CCDB)**, which deals with IT security certification, for April 2026 to April 2028.
