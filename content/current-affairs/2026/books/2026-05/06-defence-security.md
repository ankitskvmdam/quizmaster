## Leadership and anniversaries

- **7 May 2026:** It marked one year of Operation Sindoor. The operation was launched after the Pahalgam terror attack of April 2025.
- **31 May 2026:** Lt Gen N.S. Raja Subramani (retd) took over as India's third Chief of Defence Staff (CDS). He succeeded General Anil Chauhan.
- **Admiral Krishna Swaminathan:** He took over as the 27th Chief of the Naval Staff. He succeeded Admiral Dinesh Kumar Tripathi.

## Exercises and cooperation

- **CINBAX-II:** The second India–Cambodia joint military exercise was held in Kampong Speu Province, Cambodia. The first edition was held in Pune.
- **Exercise PRAGATI 2026:** This multinational army exercise was held at Umroi Military Station, Meghalaya. PRAGATI stands for Partnership of Regional Armies for Growth and Transformation in the Indian Ocean Region.
- **Algeria:** India and Algeria held the first meeting of their Joint Commission on Defence Cooperation in New Delhi.
- **United States:** It approved support packages for India's AH-64E Apache helicopters and M777A2 ultra-light howitzers.

## Weapons and platforms

- **TARA:** DRDO (Defence Research and Development Organisation) and the Air Force held the first flight trial of TARA off the Odisha coast. It is India's first indigenous glide weapon.
  - TARA stands for Tactical Advanced Range Augmentation.
  - It is a kit that turns unguided bombs into precision weapons.
  - It was developed by Research Centre Imarat, Hyderabad.
- **ULPGM-V3:** DRDO tested this drone-launched missile at the National Open Area Range in Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh.
- **INS Mahendragiri:** The sixth Nilgiri-class (Project 17A) stealth frigate joined the Navy. It was built by Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders.
  - Project 17A has seven frigates.
  - Four are built by Mazagon Dock and three by GRSE (Garden Reach Shipbuilders and Engineers), Kolkata.
- **Kautilya and Q-Shakti:** The Army inducted these two AI-based platforms. It is observing 2026 as the "Year of Networking and Data Centricity".
- **Kaal Bhairava:** It is described as India's first AI-enabled combat aircraft. It was developed by Flying Wedge Defence & Aerospace and will be manufactured in Portugal.
- **Bharat Forge:** It signed an agreement with Andhra Pradesh for India's first private marine gas turbine facility, at Visakhapatnam.
- **Oreshnik:** Russia was reported to have used this hypersonic ballistic missile against Ukraine for the third time. Hypersonic means over five times the speed of sound.

## Internal and border security

- **Operation Navjeevan:** 27 Maoists surrendered before the Jharkhand Police in Ranchi.
- **Operation RAGEPILL:** This operation of the Narcotics Control Bureau led to India's first-ever seizure of the drug Captagon.
- **Smart Border Security Project:** The Government announced it. It brings drones, radars and fencing to areas such as Sir Creek and Harami Nala in Gujarat.

## Firsts for women officers

- **Mission Vande Mataram:** The BSF's (Border Security Force's) first all-women mountaineering team climbed Mount Everest. The expedition was named 'Mission Vande Mataram'.
- **Squadron Leader Saanya:** She became the first woman officer of the Air Force to earn the CAT-A Qualified Flying Instructor grade. It is the highest grade for instructors.
