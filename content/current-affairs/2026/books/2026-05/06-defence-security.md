## Gist
Two top posts changed hands. **Lt Gen N.S. Raja Subramani (retd)** was named the **third Chief of Defence Staff**, taking over from General Anil Chauhan, and **Admiral Krishna Swaminathan** became the 27th Chief of the Naval Staff. The month also marked one year of **Operation Sindoor**.

DRDO tested two new weapons, the **TARA** glide kit and the **ULPGM-V3** drone-launched missile, and the Navy received the stealth frigate **INS Mahendragiri**. The Army held exercises with Cambodia and, in Meghalaya, with a group of friendly armies under the name **PRAGATI**.

In internal security, Maoists surrendered in Jharkhand, the Narcotics Control Bureau made India's first seizure of the drug Captagon, and women officers set two firsts in the BSF and the Air Force.

## Key facts
- **Lt Gen N.S. Raja Subramani (retd)** was appointed India's **third Chief of Defence Staff (CDS)**, succeeding **General Anil Chauhan** with effect from **31 May 2026**.
- **Admiral Krishna Swaminathan** took over as the **27th Chief of the Naval Staff**, succeeding **Admiral Dinesh Kumar Tripathi**.
- **7 May 2026** marked one year of **Operation Sindoor**, launched after the Pahalgam terror attack of April 2025.
- **CINBAX-II**, the second **India–Cambodia** joint military exercise, was held in **Kampong Speu Province, Cambodia**; the first edition was held in Pune.
- **Exercise PRAGATI 2026**, a multinational army exercise, was held at **Umroi Military Station, Meghalaya**.
- PRAGATI stands for **Partnership of Regional Armies for Growth and Transformation in the Indian Ocean Region**.
- **INS Mahendragiri**, the **sixth Nilgiri-class (Project 17A)** stealth frigate, joined the Navy; it was built by **Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders**.
- Project 17A has **seven frigates**: four built by Mazagon Dock and three by **GRSE**, Kolkata.
- DRDO and the Air Force held the first flight trial of **TARA**, India's **first indigenous glide weapon**, off the **Odisha** coast.
- TARA (**Tactical Advanced Range Augmentation**) is a kit that turns unguided bombs into precision weapons; it was developed by **Research Centre Imarat, Hyderabad**.
- DRDO tested the drone-launched missile **ULPGM-V3** at the National Open Area Range in **Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh**.
- India and **Algeria** held the first meeting of their **Joint Commission on Defence Cooperation** in New Delhi.
- The United States approved support packages for India's **AH-64E Apache** helicopters and **M777A2** ultra-light howitzers.
- In **Operation Navjeevan**, **27 Maoists** surrendered before the **Jharkhand** Police in Ranchi.
- The Narcotics Control Bureau's **Operation RAGEPILL** led to India's **first-ever seizure of Captagon**.
- The **BSF's first all-women mountaineering team** climbed Mount Everest; the expedition was named **'Mission Vande Mataram'**.
- **Squadron Leader Saanya** became the first woman officer of the Air Force to earn the **CAT-A Qualified Flying Instructor** grade, the highest for instructors.
- The Army inducted two AI-based platforms, **Kautilya** and **Q-Shakti**; it is observing 2026 as the **"Year of Networking and Data Centricity"**.
- **'Kaal Bhairava'**, described as India's first AI-enabled combat aircraft, was developed by **Flying Wedge Defence & Aerospace** and will be manufactured in **Portugal**.
- **Bharat Forge** signed an agreement with Andhra Pradesh for India's **first private marine gas turbine facility**, at **Visakhapatnam**.
- Russia was reported to have used its hypersonic **Oreshnik** ballistic missile against Ukraine for the third time.
- The Government announced a **Smart Border Security Project**, with drones, radars and fencing in areas such as **Sir Creek** and Harami Nala in Gujarat.
