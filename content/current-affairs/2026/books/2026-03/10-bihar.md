## Gist
March brought big changes at the top in Bihar. **Lt Gen (retd) Syed Ata Hasnain** took over as the State's 43rd Governor, and Chief Minister **Nitish Kumar** was elected to the **Rajya Sabha** on 16 March, a step towards the end of his long run as Chief Minister.

The State cabinet approved a **Semiconductor Policy** and a new **international airport at Sonepur**, and the Chief Minister's **Samriddhi Yatra** continued with project launches in district after district. **Bihar Diwas** on 22 March marked the State's 114th foundation day.

Other items worth remembering are the third zoo planned at Gaya, the tunnel joining Patna's two museums, the two nuclear power plants proposed at Nawada and Banka, and Bihar's improved place in NITI Aayog's Fiscal Health Index.

## Key facts
- **Lt Gen (retd) Syed Ata Hasnain** was sworn in as the **43rd Governor of Bihar** in March 2026; the oath was given by Patna High Court Chief Justice **Sangam Kumar Sahoo**.
- Syed Ata Hasnain earlier commanded the Army's Srinagar-based **15 Corps**, also called the **Chinar Corps**.
- In the Rajya Sabha election of **16 March 2026**, the NDA won all **five** seats from Bihar; Chief Minister **Nitish Kumar** and BJP leader **Nitin Nabin** were among those elected.
- After his election to the Rajya Sabha, Nitish Kumar resigned his seat in the **Bihar Legislative Council**.
- Nitish Kumar had taken oath as Chief Minister for a record **tenth** time on **20 November 2025**.
- The Bihar cabinet approved the **Bihar Semiconductor Policy 2026**, which offers land at **Rs 1 per acre** for every Rs 100 crore of project cost.
- The Semiconductor Policy aims to draw about **Rs 25,000 crore** of investment and create over **2 lakh** jobs; a **Bihar Semiconductor Mission** will carry it out.
- **Bihar Diwas** on **22 March 2026** marked the State's **114th** foundation day with the theme **'Unnat Bihar, Ujjwal Bihar'**.
- The Bihar cabinet approved a greenfield international airport at **Sonepur** in **Saran** district, on about **4,200 acres**, to be completed by **2030**.
- The Chief Minister launched a tunnel of about **1.5 km** joining the **Bihar Museum** and the **Patna Museum**, costing about **Rs 542 crore**.
- Bihar gave in-principle approval for its **third zoo**, in **Gaya** district, after the Sanjay Gandhi Biological Park in Patna and the Rajgir Zoo Safari.
- Bihar has ranked **first** in the country in supplying **free medicines** at government hospitals for **18** months in a row.
- The Chief Minister's **Samriddhi Yatra** began on **16 January 2026** from **Bettiah** in West Champaran.
- During the Samriddhi Yatra, **370** projects worth **Rs 914 crore** were launched in **Jamui**, and an airport at **Forbesganj** was announced in Araria.
- The ASI approved a fresh excavation at **Balirajgarh Fort** in **Madhubani** district, whose fortification dates to about 200 BCE.
- Two **nuclear power plants**, with a combined capacity of about **1,400 MW**, are planned at **Nawada** and **Banka**.
- In NITI Aayog's **Fiscal Health Index 2026**, Bihar moved up from the 'Aspirational' category to **'Performer'**.
- A **7 km** park is being built along **JP Ganga Path** in Patna, from **Digha to Sabhyata Dwar**, at a cost of **Rs 330 crore**.
- A **Marine Drive** of about **16 km** is planned at Gaya along the **Falgu** river, between Bodh Gaya and Chakand.
- The **Patna–Gaya–Dobhi** national highway is to be developed as a **green corridor** by **2029**.
- Bihar set up a **Crisis Management Group** under Chief Secretary **Pratyaya Amrit** to watch supplies of essential goods during the West Asia conflict.
- Bihar plans a tourism circuit linking its major **Shiva temples**, on the lines of the Buddha and Ramayana circuits.
- The **Officers Training Academy, Gaya** commissioned **281** cadets at its passing-out parade on **7 March 2026**.
- Free **physiotherapy** in government hospitals is planned under **Saat Nischay-3** (2025–30).
- **Jang Bahadur Singh**, a 105-year-old folk singer and freedom fighter, received the **'Son of Siwan, Pride of Bihar'** honour at **Jeeradei**, the birthplace of Dr Rajendra Prasad.
