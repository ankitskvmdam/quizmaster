## Governor, politics and administration

- **New Governor:** In March 2026 Lt Gen (retd) Syed Ata Hasnain was sworn in as the 43rd Governor of Bihar. Patna High Court Chief Justice Sangam Kumar Sahoo gave the oath. Hasnain earlier commanded the Army's Srinagar-based 15 Corps, also called the Chinar Corps.
- **Nitish Kumar:**
  - On 20 November 2025 he had taken oath as Chief Minister for a record tenth time.
  - In the Rajya Sabha election of 16 March 2026, the National Democratic Alliance (NDA) won all five seats from Bihar. Nitish Kumar and Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) leader Nitin Nabin were among those elected.
  - After his election to the Rajya Sabha, Nitish Kumar resigned his seat in the Bihar Legislative Council.
  - The move was seen as a step towards the end of his long run as Chief Minister.
- **Crisis Management Group:** Bihar set up this group under Chief Secretary Pratyaya Amrit. It watches supplies of essential goods during the West Asia conflict.

## Economy and energy

- **Bihar Semiconductor Policy 2026:** The Bihar cabinet approved the policy.
  - It offers land at Rs 1 per acre for every Rs 100 crore of project cost.
  - It aims to draw about Rs 25,000 crore of investment and create over 2 lakh jobs.
  - A Bihar Semiconductor Mission will carry it out.
- **Nuclear power plants:** Two nuclear power plants are planned at Nawada and Banka. Their combined capacity is about 1,400 MW.
- **Fiscal Health Index 2026:** In NITI Aayog's index, Bihar moved up from the 'Aspirational' category to 'Performer'.

## Development projects

- **Samriddhi Yatra:** The Chief Minister's Samriddhi Yatra began on 16 January 2026 from Bettiah in West Champaran. It continued with project launches in district after district.
  - In Jamui, 370 projects worth Rs 914 crore were launched.
  - An airport at Forbesganj in Araria was announced.
- **Sonepur airport:** The Bihar cabinet approved a greenfield international airport, that is, one built on new land, at Sonepur in Saran district. It will cover about 4,200 acres and is to be completed by 2030.
- **Museum tunnel:** The Chief Minister launched a tunnel of about 1.5 km joining the Bihar Museum and the Patna Museum. It cost about Rs 542 crore.
- **JP Ganga Path park:** A 7 km park is being built along JP Ganga Path in Patna, from Digha to Sabhyata Dwar. It costs Rs 330 crore.
- **Gaya Marine Drive:** A Marine Drive of about 16 km is planned at Gaya along the Falgu river, between Bodh Gaya and Chakand.
- **Green corridor:** The Patna–Gaya–Dobhi national highway is to be developed as a green corridor by 2029.

## Health

- **Free medicines:** Bihar has ranked first in the country in supplying free medicines at government hospitals for 18 months in a row.
- **Physiotherapy:** Free physiotherapy in government hospitals is planned under Saat Nischay-3 (2025–30).

## Culture, heritage and wildlife

- **22 March 2026, Bihar Diwas:** The day marked the State's 114th foundation day. The theme was 'Unnat Bihar, Ujjwal Bihar'.
- **Balirajgarh Fort:** The Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) approved a fresh excavation at the fort in Madhubani district. Its fortification dates to about 200 BCE.
- **Shiva temple circuit:** Bihar plans a tourism circuit linking its major Shiva temples. It is on the lines of the Buddha and Ramayana circuits.
- **Third zoo:** Bihar gave in-principle approval for its third zoo, in Gaya district. The first two are the Sanjay Gandhi Biological Park in Patna and the Rajgir Zoo Safari.

## People and institutions

- **7 March 2026:** The Officers Training Academy, Gaya, commissioned 281 cadets at its passing-out parade.
- **Jang Bahadur Singh:** The 105-year-old folk singer and freedom fighter received the 'Son of Siwan, Pride of Bihar' honour. It was given at Jeeradei, the birthplace of Dr Rajendra Prasad.
