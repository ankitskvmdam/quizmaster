## Gist
March was a month of Supreme Court rulings on personal rights. The Court allowed **passive euthanasia** for a named patient for the first time, gave **adoptive mothers** maternity leave whatever the child's age, said that **parental income alone** cannot decide the OBC creamy layer, and struck down an upper limit on disability for government jobs. It also held that **Scheduled Caste status** is lost on conversion to a religion other than Hinduism, Sikhism or Buddhism.

Parliament passed the **Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Amendment Act, 2026**, which ends self-identification of gender, and the Lok Sabha cleared the year's Demands for Grants by the **guillotine**.

The President reshuffled **Governors and Lieutenant Governors** across nine States and Union Territories. The Election Commission announced Assembly elections in four States and Puducherry, and preparations for the digital **Census 2027** gathered pace with new mascots and a brand ambassador.

## Key facts
- In March 2026 the President reshuffled Governors and Lieutenant Governors in **nine** States and Union Territories.
- **R.N. Ravi** was moved from Tamil Nadu to become Governor of **West Bengal**, after the resignation of **C.V. Ananda Bose** was accepted.
- Former diplomat **Taranjit Singh Sandhu** became **Lieutenant Governor of Delhi**, and **Vinai Kumar Saxena** moved from Delhi to become Lieutenant Governor of **Ladakh**.
- **Shiv Pratap Shukla** became Governor of **Telangana**, **Jishnu Dev Varma** of **Maharashtra**, **Nand Kishore Yadav** of **Nagaland** and **Kavinder Gupta** of **Himachal Pradesh**.
- The Lok Sabha **Committee of Privileges** (15 members, nominated by the Speaker) was reconstituted from **3 March 2026** with **Ravi Shankar Prasad** as its chairperson.
- Home Minister Amit Shah unveiled the mascots of **Census 2027**: **Pragati** (female) and **Vikas** (male).
- Census 2027 is the **16th** census of India and the **8th** since Independence; it is the **first fully digital** census and counts caste for the first time since **1931**.
- Sand artist **Sudarsan Pattnaik** was named **brand ambassador of Census 2027** by the Ministry of Home Affairs.
- The Supreme Court allowed **passive euthanasia** for **Harish Rana**, who had been in a vegetative state for over 13 years: the first time it applied its **Common Cause (2018)** guidelines to a particular person.
- The Harish Rana bench of **Justices J.B. Pardiwala and K.V. Viswanathan** asked **AIIMS Delhi** to admit him to palliative care.
- The Supreme Court told the Centre to frame a **no-fault compensation policy** for serious adverse effects or deaths after **Covid-19 vaccination**.
- The Supreme Court ruled that **parental income alone** cannot decide **OBC creamy layer** status; the status of the parent's post also counts.
- The Supreme Court held that **adoptive mothers** are entitled to maternity leave whatever the age of the child, striking down **Section 60(4) of the Code on Social Security, 2020**.
- In **Chinthada Anand v. State of Andhra Pradesh**, the Supreme Court held that Scheduled Caste status is open only to those professing **Hinduism, Sikhism or Buddhism** and is lost on conversion to another religion.
- In **Prabhu Kumar v. State of Himachal Pradesh**, the Supreme Court held that States cannot fix an arbitrary **upper limit on disability percentage** for public employment.
- The **Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Amendment Bill, 2026** was passed by the Lok Sabha on **24 March**, by the Rajya Sabha on **25 March**, and got the President's assent on **30 March 2026**.
- The amended law removes **self-identification** of gender (Section 4(2) of the 2019 Act) and requires the recommendation of a **medical board** before a certificate is issued.
- The Lok Sabha passed Demands for Grants of over **Rs 53 lakh crore** for 2026-27 by the **guillotine**, that is, by voting the remaining demands together without discussion.
- The Election Commission announced Assembly elections in **Assam, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal and Puducherry**, bringing the Model Code of Conduct into force.
- Opposition parties considered a motion to remove Chief Election Commissioner **Gyanesh Kumar**; under **Article 324(5)** the CEC can be removed only in the manner of a **Supreme Court judge**.
- The Cabinet extended the **IVFRT** (Immigration, Visa, Foreigners Registration and Tracking) scheme of the Home Ministry from **1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031** with an outlay of **Rs 1,800 crore**.
- **Niranjan Jyoti** took charge as chairperson of the **National Commission for Backward Classes**, a constitutional body under **Article 338B**.
- **Karnataka** announced a ban on social media for children under **16**, and **Andhra Pradesh** proposed one for children under **13**.
