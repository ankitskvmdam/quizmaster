## Court rulings

All the rulings below are of the Supreme Court.

- **Passive euthanasia:** The Court allowed passive euthanasia, that is, withdrawing life support so a patient may die, for Harish Rana.
  - Harish Rana had been in a vegetative state for over 13 years.
  - It was the first time the Court applied its Common Cause (2018) guidelines to a particular person.
  - The bench of Justices J.B. Pardiwala and K.V. Viswanathan asked AIIMS Delhi to admit him to palliative care.
- **Covid-19 vaccination:** The Court told the Centre to frame a no-fault compensation policy, that is, compensation paid without anyone having to prove fault. It covers serious adverse effects or deaths after Covid-19 vaccination.
- **OBC creamy layer:** The Court ruled that parental income alone cannot decide the creamy layer status of the Other Backward Classes (OBC). The creamy layer is the better-off section, kept out of reservation. The status of the parent's post also counts.
- **Adoptive mothers:** The Court held that adoptive mothers are entitled to maternity leave whatever the age of the child. It struck down Section 60(4) of the Code on Social Security, 2020.
- **Chinthada Anand v. State of Andhra Pradesh:** The Court held that Scheduled Caste status is open only to those who profess Hinduism, Sikhism or Buddhism. The status is lost on conversion to another religion.
- **Prabhu Kumar v. State of Himachal Pradesh:** The Court held that States cannot fix an arbitrary upper limit on disability percentage for public employment.

## Parliament and laws

- **Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Amendment Act, 2026:**
  - The Bill was passed by the Lok Sabha on 24 March and by the Rajya Sabha on 25 March.
  - It got the President's assent on 30 March 2026.
  - It removes self-identification of gender, which was in Section 4(2) of the 2019 Act.
  - A certificate is now issued only on the recommendation of a medical board.
- **Demands for Grants:** The Lok Sabha passed Demands for Grants of over Rs 53 lakh crore for 2026-27 by the guillotine. Under the guillotine, the remaining demands are voted together without discussion.

## Elections

- **Assembly elections:** The Election Commission announced Assembly elections in Assam, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal and Puducherry. With this, the Model Code of Conduct came into force.
- **Chief Election Commissioner:** Opposition parties considered a motion to remove Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. Under Article 324(5), the CEC can be removed only in the manner of a Supreme Court judge.

## Census 2027

- Census 2027 is the 16th census of India and the 8th since Independence.
- It is the first fully digital census.
- It counts caste for the first time since 1931.
- **Mascots:** Home Minister Amit Shah unveiled the mascots of Census 2027. They are Pragati (female) and Vikas (male).
- **Brand ambassador:** The Ministry of Home Affairs named sand artist Sudarsan Pattnaik the brand ambassador of Census 2027.

## Government decisions

- **IVFRT:** The Cabinet extended the Home Ministry's Immigration, Visa, Foreigners Registration and Tracking (IVFRT) scheme. The extension runs from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031, with an outlay of Rs 1,800 crore.
- **Social media for children:** Karnataka announced a ban on social media for children under 16. Andhra Pradesh proposed a ban for children under 13.
