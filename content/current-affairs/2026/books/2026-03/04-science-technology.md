## Nuclear science and physics

- **Proton accelerator:** Visakhapatnam was chosen to host a high-energy proton accelerator.
  - It supports accelerator-driven systems for using thorium in India's three-stage nuclear programme.
  - The Raja Ramanna Centre for Advanced Technology (RRCAT), Indore, is developing the project under the Department of Atomic Energy.
  - India holds about 25 per cent of the world's thorium reserves.
- **Xi-cc-plus:** The LHCb experiment at CERN's Large Hadron Collider (LHC) announced the discovery of the Xi-cc-plus particle.
  - It is a baryon, a particle of three quarks: two charm quarks and one down quark.
  - It is about four times as heavy as a proton.
  - LHC experiments have now found 80 hadrons.
- **LIGO-India:** The country's first gravitational wave observatory is coming up in Hingoli district, Maharashtra. Completion is targeted for 2030.
- **National Quantum Mission:** Under this mission of the Department of Science and Technology, 23 academic institutions were approved to set up quantum teaching labs. The mission has an outlay of Rs 6,003 crore.

## Space and navigation

- **NavIC:** The NavIC navigation system lost another atomic clock. The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) said the atomic clock on the IRNSS-1F satellite had stopped working. So only four NavIC satellites now give positioning services.
  - NVS-01, launched in 2023, carries an indigenous rubidium atomic clock.
  - NavIC uses the L and S bands.
- **AssamSAT:** Assam became the first Indian State to float a tender for its own earth-observation satellites, named AssamSAT. There will be at least 5 satellites in low-earth orbit, carrying synthetic aperture radar.
- **Akasha300:** The Kerala startup Spacetime 4D delivered the Akasha300 3D printer to ISRO's Liquid Propulsion Systems Centre at Valiyamala.

## Health

- **10 March 2026:** The World Health Organization (WHO) announced that Chile is the first country in the Americas to eliminate leprosy.
- **CALM-Brain:** India's first digital repository of major psychiatric disorders was launched in Bengaluru. It was launched by the National Institute of Mental Health and Neurosciences (NIMHANS) and NCBS-TIFR.
- **GARBH-INi:** This programme of the Department of Biotechnology tracks about 12,000 pregnant women. Its aim is to predict preterm births early.

## Transport, energy and pollution

- **Hydrogen train:** India's first hydrogen train completed its oscillation trial on the Jind–Sonipat route in Haryana. RDSO conducted the trial.
  - The trainset has 10 coaches and was built by the Integral Coach Factory, Chennai.
  - Germany, Sweden, Japan and China already run hydrogen trains.
- **OTEC plant:** An ocean thermal energy conversion (OTEC) desalination plant is being built at Kavaratti, Lakshadweep. The builder is the National Institute of Ocean Technology, under the Ministry of Earth Sciences.
- **Smog-eating coatings:** The Delhi government tied up with IIT Madras to test 'smog-eating' coatings. The coatings use titanium dioxide to break down pollutants in sunlight.
