## Gist
Two stories about India's big science projects stood out. **Visakhapatnam** was chosen for a high-energy **proton accelerator** that will help India use its thorium, and the **NavIC** navigation system lost another atomic clock, leaving only four satellites able to give position.

In physics, the **LHCb** experiment at CERN found a new heavy particle, the **Xi-cc-plus baryon**. In space, **Assam** became the first State to ask for its own group of earth-observation satellites, and a Kerala startup supplied a 3D printer to ISRO.

Health and brain science gave the month a few 'firsts': the **CALM-Brain** repository of psychiatric disorders in Bengaluru, and **Chile** becoming the first country in the Americas to eliminate leprosy. India's **hydrogen train** also cleared an important trial.

## Key facts
- **Visakhapatnam** was chosen to host a **high-energy proton accelerator** that supports accelerator-driven systems for using **thorium** in India's three-stage nuclear programme.
- The proton accelerator project is being developed by the **Raja Ramanna Centre for Advanced Technology (RRCAT), Indore**, under the **Department of Atomic Energy**.
- India holds about **25 per cent** of the world's thorium reserves.
- ISRO said the atomic clock on the **IRNSS-1F** satellite had stopped working, so only **four** satellites of the **NavIC** system now give positioning services.
- **NVS-01**, launched in 2023, carries an indigenous **rubidium atomic clock**; NavIC uses the **L and S bands**.
- The **LHCb** experiment at **CERN's Large Hadron Collider** announced the discovery of the **Xi-cc-plus** particle, a baryon made of **two charm quarks and one down quark**.
- The Xi-cc-plus is about **four times** as heavy as a proton; LHC experiments have now found **80** hadrons.
- **Assam** became the first Indian State to float a tender for its own earth-observation satellites, named **AssamSAT**: at least **5** satellites in low-earth orbit carrying **synthetic aperture radar**.
- **CALM-Brain**, India's first digital repository of major **psychiatric disorders**, was launched in **Bengaluru** by **NIMHANS** and **NCBS-TIFR**.
- Under the **National Quantum Mission** of the **Department of Science and Technology** (outlay **Rs 6,003 crore**), **23** academic institutions were approved to set up quantum teaching labs.
- **LIGO-India**, the country's first gravitational wave observatory, is coming up in **Hingoli district, Maharashtra**, with completion targeted for **2030**.
- The ocean thermal energy conversion (**OTEC**) desalination plant at **Kavaratti, Lakshadweep** is being built by the **National Institute of Ocean Technology** under the Ministry of Earth Sciences.
- The Kerala startup **Spacetime 4D** delivered the **Akasha300** 3D printer to ISRO's **Liquid Propulsion Systems Centre** at Valiyamala.
- India's first **hydrogen train** completed its oscillation trial, conducted by **RDSO**, on the **Jind–Sonipat** route in Haryana.
- The hydrogen trainset, with **10 coaches**, was built by the **Integral Coach Factory, Chennai**; Germany, Sweden, Japan and China already run hydrogen trains.
- **GARBH-INi**, a programme of the **Department of Biotechnology**, tracks about **12,000** pregnant women to predict **preterm births** early.
- On **10 March 2026** the WHO announced that **Chile** is the first country in the **Americas** to eliminate **leprosy**.
- The Delhi government tied up with **IIT Madras** to test 'smog-eating' coatings that use **titanium dioxide** to break down pollutants in sunlight.
