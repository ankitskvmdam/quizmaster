## Inflation, money and public finance

- **Inflation target:** The Government kept the retail inflation target of 4 per cent, with a band of 2 to 6 per cent. The target holds from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031.
- **Economic Stabilisation Fund:** The Finance Minister announced an Economic Stabilisation Fund of Rs 1 lakh crore. The announcement came as the Lok Sabha passed the second batch of Supplementary Demands for Grants for 2025-26, under Article 115.
- **Bond purchases:** The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) announced open market purchases of Government bonds worth Rs 1 lakh crore, to add liquidity. The purchases are in two parts of Rs 50,000 crore each.
- **Variable rate repo (VRR):** The RBI also added over Rs 25,000 crore through a 3-day VRR auction. The repo rate stood at 5.25 per cent.
- **Fiscal deficit:** At the end of February 2026 the Centre's fiscal deficit stood at 80.4 per cent of the year's target. The figure is from the Controller General of Accounts.

## The West Asia war: fuel

The war in West Asia disturbed oil routes through the Strait of Hormuz. The Government's steps on fuel are below.

- **LPG:** The Government invoked the Essential Commodities Act, 1955. Refiners had to raise LPG output and give priority to households.
- **Duties:** The Government cut the Special Additional Excise Duty on petrol and diesel. It raised export duties on diesel and aviation turbine fuel.
- **Oil release:** India did not join the oil release from strategic reserves led by the International Energy Agency. India is only an associate member of the Agency.
- **Strategic petroleum reserves:** India's reserves of about 5.33 million tonnes are at Visakhapatnam, Mangaluru and Padur.

## Exports

- **RELIEF:** The Commerce Ministry launched the Resilience & Logistics Intervention for Export Facilitation (RELIEF) scheme of Rs 497 crore. It gives exporters hit by the West Asia crisis insurance cover through the Export Credit Guarantee Corporation (ECGC).
- **RoDTEP:** The Government restored in full the benefits of the Remission of Duties and Taxes on Export Products (RoDTEP) scheme. The benefits had earlier been halved.
- **Joha rice:** The first export consignment of Joha rice from Assam went to the United Kingdom and Italy. The rice carries a Geographical Indication (GI) tag. The Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA) helped send it.

## Industry, investment and energy

- **Press Note 3 of 2020:** The Cabinet eased this rule on investment from countries that share a land border with India. Investors from land-border countries may now hold up to 10 per cent non-controlling beneficial ownership under the automatic route.
- **BHAVYA (Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojna):** The Cabinet approved BHAVYA with Rs 33,660 crore.
  - It will build 100 plug-and-play, that is, ready-to-use, industrial parks.
  - The National Industrial Corridor Development Corporation (NICDC) will implement it.
  - NICDC works under the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT).
- **Bharat Electricity Summit 2026:** The summit was held in New Delhi. India's installed power capacity had reached 520 GW by January 2026.
- **LPG ATM:** Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited (BPCL) launched India's first LPG ATM, in Gurugram.

## Transport and ports

- **Modified UDAN:** The Cabinet approved the Modified UDAN regional air connectivity scheme with Rs 28,840 crore for 2026–2036. It will develop 100 airports and 200 helipads.
- **Madurai:** The Cabinet approved declaring Madurai airport in Tamil Nadu an international airport.
- **Digital Twin:** V.O. Chidambaranar Port in Tamil Nadu became the first Indian port to launch a Digital Twin, a live virtual copy, of its operations.
- **Port of Refuge:** Adani Ports launched India's first Port of Refuge arrangement, where ships in distress can shelter. It is at Dighi on the west coast and Gopalpur on the east coast.

## Banking

- **Grameen Credit Score:** Banks were told to use the Grameen Credit Score as the default tool for judging rural borrowers. The score runs from 300 to 900.
- **Regional Rural Banks (RRBs):** A parliamentary panel recommended initial public offerings (IPOs) for profitable RRBs. After consolidation there are 28 RRBs.
- **Bank of Baroda:** It became the first bank to offer an overdraft on a credit line based on the Unified Payments Interface (UPI). The overdraft is for members of women's self-help groups.
