## Gist
The war in West Asia shaped the month's economic news. With oil routes through the Strait of Hormuz disturbed, the Government used the **Essential Commodities Act** to push up LPG output, cut excise duty on petrol and diesel, restored export incentives and launched the **RELIEF** scheme to insure exporters. India chose to stay out of the oil release led by the International Energy Agency.

At home, the Government kept the **4 per cent inflation target** for five more years and created an **Economic Stabilisation Fund** of Rs 1 lakh crore. The Reserve Bank added money to the banking system by buying bonds, and the Cabinet relaxed the 2020 rule on investment from countries that share a land border with India.

The Cabinet also approved **BHAVYA**, a plan for 100 ready-to-use industrial parks, and a new version of the **UDAN** air connectivity scheme. Smaller facts worth remembering are India's rank in billionaires, metro rail and coconut output, and a few 'firsts' in ports and banking.

## Key facts
- The Government retained the retail inflation target of **4 per cent** with a band of **2 to 6 per cent** for **1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031**.
- The Finance Minister announced an **Economic Stabilisation Fund** of **Rs 1 lakh crore** as the Lok Sabha passed the second batch of **Supplementary Demands for Grants** for 2025-26 (**Article 115**).
- The RBI announced open market purchases of Government bonds worth **Rs 1 lakh crore**, in two parts of **Rs 50,000 crore** each, to add liquidity.
- The RBI also added over Rs 25,000 crore through a 3-day **variable rate repo (VRR)** auction; the repo rate stood at **5.25 per cent**.
- The Cabinet eased **Press Note 3 of 2020**: investors from **land-border countries** may now hold up to **10 per cent** non-controlling beneficial ownership under the **automatic route**.
- The Cabinet approved **BHAVYA** (Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojna) with **Rs 33,660 crore** to build **100 plug-and-play industrial parks**; the **NICDC**, under the **DPIIT**, will implement it.
- The Cabinet approved the **Modified UDAN** regional air connectivity scheme with **Rs 28,840 crore** for **2026–2036**, to develop **100 airports** and **200 helipads**.
- The Cabinet approved declaring **Madurai** airport in Tamil Nadu an **international airport**.
- The Commerce Ministry launched the **RELIEF** scheme (Resilience & Logistics Intervention for Export Facilitation) of **Rs 497 crore**, giving exporters hit by the West Asia crisis insurance cover through **ECGC**.
- The Government restored in full the benefits of the **RoDTEP** export scheme, which had earlier been halved.
- The Government invoked the **Essential Commodities Act, 1955** to make refiners raise **LPG** output and give priority to households.
- India did not join the oil release from strategic reserves led by the **International Energy Agency**, of which it is only an **associate member**.
- India's strategic petroleum reserves of about **5.33 million tonnes** are at **Visakhapatnam, Mangaluru and Padur**.
- The Government cut the **Special Additional Excise Duty** on petrol and diesel and raised export duties on diesel and aviation turbine fuel.
- The Centre's fiscal deficit stood at **80.4 per cent** of the year's target at the end of February 2026, according to the **Controller General of Accounts**.
- Banks were told to use the **Grameen Credit Score**, which runs from **300 to 900**, as the default tool for judging rural borrowers.
- A parliamentary panel recommended **IPOs** for profitable **Regional Rural Banks**; after consolidation there are **28** RRBs.
- **Bank of Baroda** became the first bank to offer an overdraft on a **UPI-based credit line** to members of women's self-help groups.
- **V.O. Chidambaranar Port** in Tamil Nadu became the first Indian port to launch a **Digital Twin** of its operations.
- **Adani Ports** launched India's first **Port of Refuge** arrangement, at **Dighi** on the west coast and **Gopalpur** on the east coast.
- **APEDA** helped send the first export consignment of GI-tagged **Joha rice** from **Assam** to the **United Kingdom and Italy**.
- **BPCL** launched India's first **LPG ATM**, in **Gurugram**.
- The **Hurun Global Rich List 2026** counted **308** billionaires in India, the **third-highest** after the United States and China; **Mukesh Ambani** remained the richest Indian.
- India has the world's **third-largest metro rail network**, over **1,143 km** in **29** cities, after China and the United States.
- India is the world's **largest coconut producer**, with about **30.37 per cent** of world output.
- The **Bharat Electricity Summit 2026** was held in New Delhi; India's installed power capacity had reached **520 GW** by January 2026.
