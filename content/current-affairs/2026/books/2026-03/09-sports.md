## Cricket

- **8 March 2026, ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026:** India beat New Zealand by 96 runs in the final at the Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad, and won the title.
  - India became the first team to win three men's T20 World Cups (2007, 2024, 2026). It is also the first to defend the title and the first to win it at home.
  - In the final India made 255/5 and bowled New Zealand out for 159.
  - The captains were Suryakumar Yadav (India) and Mitchell Santner (New Zealand).
  - Jasprit Bumrah (4 for 15) was Player of the Match in the final.
  - Sanju Samson (321 runs) was Player of the Tournament.
  - Pakistan's Sahibzada Farhan scored the most runs in the tournament (383).
  - New Zealand's Finn Allen hit the fastest century in T20 World Cup history, in 33 balls. It came against South Africa in the semi-final at Eden Gardens, Kolkata.
  - The 2026 tournament was co-hosted by India and Sri Lanka. The 2028 edition will be co-hosted by Australia and New Zealand.
- **28 March 2026, IPL 2026:** The 19th season of the IPL began in Bengaluru. The defending champions, Royal Challengers Bengaluru, played Sunrisers Hyderabad.
  - In the opening match Virat Kohli became the first batter to score 4,000 runs in IPL run chases.

## Awards in Indian sport

- **BCCI Naman Awards 2026:** The Polly Umrigar Award for best international cricketer went to Shubman Gill (men) and Smriti Mandhana (women). The Colonel C.K. Nayudu Lifetime Achievement Award went to Rahul Dravid, Roger Binny and Mithali Raj.
- **Hockey India Annual Awards 2025:** Hardik Singh and Navneet Kaur were Players of the Year. Zafar Iqbal received the Major Dhyan Chand Lifetime Achievement Award.
- **Sheetal Devi:** World Archery named the para archer from Jammu and Kashmir Para Archer of the Year 2025.
