## Gist
The month belonged to cricket. On **8 March 2026** India beat New Zealand at Ahmedabad to win the **ICC Men's T20 World Cup**, becoming the first team to win the title three times, the first to defend it and the first to win it at home. The **IPL** began its 19th season at the end of the month.

In badminton, **Lakshya Sen** reached the final of the **All England Open** but lost to Lin Chun-yi of Chinese Taipei. India topped the medal table at the **World Para Athletics Grand Prix** in New Delhi, and para archer **Sheetal Devi** won a world award.

At home, two new events began: the **Khelo India Tribal Games** in Chhattisgarh and the **National Indoor Athletics Championships** in Bhubaneswar. Abroad, Japan won the Women's Asian Cup, George Russell won the first Formula 1 race of the season, and Jannik Sinner and Aryna Sabalenka each won both Indian Wells and Miami.

## Key facts
- **India** won the **ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026**, beating **New Zealand** by **96 runs** in the final at the **Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad**, on **8 March 2026**.
- India became the **first team to win three** men's T20 World Cups (2007, 2024, 2026), the first to **defend** the title and the first to win it **at home**.
- In the final India made **255/5** and bowled New Zealand out for **159**; the captains were **Suryakumar Yadav** and **Mitchell Santner**.
- **Jasprit Bumrah** (4 for 15) was Player of the Match in the final and **Sanju Samson** (321 runs) was **Player of the Tournament**.
- The T20 World Cup 2026 was co-hosted by **India and Sri Lanka**; the 2028 edition will be co-hosted by **Australia and New Zealand**.
- New Zealand's **Finn Allen** hit the **fastest century in T20 World Cup history**, in **33 balls**, against South Africa in the semi-final at Eden Gardens, Kolkata.
- Pakistan's **Sahibzada Farhan** scored the most runs in the T20 World Cup 2026 (**383**).
- At the **All England Open** in Birmingham, **Lakshya Sen** was runner-up in men's singles, losing the final on **8 March 2026** to **Lin Chun-yi** of Chinese Taipei.
- **Wang Zhiyi** of China won the All England women's singles title, beating **An Se-young** of South Korea.
- At the **BCCI Naman Awards 2026**, the **Polly Umrigar Award** for best international cricketer went to **Shubman Gill** (men) and **Smriti Mandhana** (women).
- The BCCI's **Colonel C.K. Nayudu Lifetime Achievement Award** went to **Rahul Dravid, Roger Binny and Mithali Raj**.
- At the **Hockey India Annual Awards 2025**, **Hardik Singh** and **Navneet Kaur** were Players of the Year, and **Zafar Iqbal** received the **Major Dhyan Chand Lifetime Achievement Award**.
- **Sheetal Devi** of Jammu and Kashmir was named **Para Archer of the Year 2025** by World Archery.
- India topped the **World Para Athletics Grand Prix** in **New Delhi** with a record **208** medals, including **75** gold.
- The first **Khelo India Tribal Games** began on **25 March 2026** in **Chhattisgarh** (Raipur, Jagdalpur and Surguja); the mascot is **'Morveer'**.
- The first **National Indoor Athletics Championships** were held at the **Kalinga Stadium, Bhubaneswar**, on **24–25 March 2026**.
- **IPL 2026**, the **19th** season, began on **28 March 2026** with **Royal Challengers Bengaluru**, the defending champions, playing **Sunrisers Hyderabad** in Bengaluru.
- In the opening match of IPL 2026, **Virat Kohli** became the first batter to score **4,000 runs in IPL run chases**.
- **George Russell** of **Mercedes** won the season-opening **Australian Grand Prix** at Melbourne on **8 March 2026**.
- **Japan** won the **AFC Women's Asian Cup 2026**, beating hosts **Australia** 1–0 in the final at Sydney on **21 March 2026**; it was Japan's third title.
- **Jannik Sinner** and **Aryna Sabalenka** each won both the **Indian Wells** and **Miami** tennis titles in March 2026, a pair of wins known as the **'Sunshine Double'**.
- **China** topped the medal table at the **Winter Paralympics** held at **Milano Cortina**, Italy, from **6 to 15 March 2026**.
