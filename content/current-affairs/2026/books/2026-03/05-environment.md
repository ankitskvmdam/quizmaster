## Gist
India set out its next climate promise in March. The Cabinet approved the **Nationally Determined Contribution for 2031–35**, with targets for clean power capacity, emissions intensity and carbon sinks. India also sent its **Seventh National Report** to the Convention on Biological Diversity, which showed only two of its 23 biodiversity targets on track.

Air pollution reports again made uncomfortable reading: the **World Air Quality Report 2025** ranked India sixth among the most polluted countries and named **Loni** in Uttar Pradesh the world's most polluted city.

Wildlife news was brighter. The **Great Indian Bustard** breeding programme reached 70 birds and a chick hatched in Kutch after ten years, scientists published the first checklist of India's fireflies, and India was found to lead the world in certificates under the **Nagoya Protocol**. A UN update warned that nearly half of the world's migratory species are in decline.

## Key facts
- The Cabinet approved India's **Nationally Determined Contribution for 2031–35**, to be submitted to the **UNFCCC**.
- Under the new NDC, India aims by **2035** for **60 per cent** of installed electricity capacity from **non-fossil** sources and a **47 per cent** cut in the emissions intensity of GDP from the **2005** level.
- The new NDC also aims for an additional carbon sink of **3.5–4.0 billion tonnes** of CO2 equivalent by 2035.
- India submitted its **Seventh National Report** to the **Convention on Biological Diversity**; it tracks **23** national biodiversity targets through **142** indicators.
- The Seventh National Report puts India's forest and tree cover at **25.17 per cent** of its area and finds only **two** of the 23 targets on track.
- The **World Air Quality Report 2025** of **IQAir** ranked India the **6th** most polluted country; **Pakistan** was the most polluted.
- According to IQAir, **Loni** in Uttar Pradesh was the world's **most polluted city** and **New Delhi** the most polluted capital for the **8th** year running.
- A study of winter 2025-26 by **CREA** found **Ghaziabad** the most polluted Indian city and **Chamarajanagar** in Karnataka the cleanest.
- **World Wildlife Day** (3 March) had the 2026 theme **'Medicinal and Aromatic Plants: Conserving Health, Heritage and Livelihoods'**.
- A UN update on the **State of the World's Migratory Species** found **49 per cent** of the populations of species listed under the **Convention on Migratory Species (CMS)** in decline.
- **CMS COP15** was held in March 2026 at **Campo Grande, Brazil**; the **Hudsonian godwit**, a shorebird, was among 42 species proposed for protection.
- The **NHAI** released the first **National Highways Green Cover Index** (2025-26), prepared with ISRO's **National Remote Sensing Centre**.
- The Ministry of New and Renewable Energy notified standards for **green ammonia** (at most **0.38 kg** of CO2 equivalent per kg) and **green methanol** (at most **0.44 kg**) under the National Green Hydrogen Mission.
- Scientists published the first full checklist of India's **fireflies**: **92** species in **27** genera, with the **Western Ghats** the richest region.
- **Project Great Indian Bustard** entered its fourth year of captive breeding in Rajasthan with **70** birds in captivity.
- A Great Indian Bustard chick hatched in **Kutch, Gujarat** after a decade through the **'jumpstart'** method, in which an egg from the breeding centre is placed in a wild bird's nest: the first inter-State effort of its kind.
- The Supreme Court took up on its own the issue of illegal sand mining in the **National Chambal Sanctuary**, India's only **tri-State** protected area and the main home of the **gharial**.
- The Karnataka High Court ordered that 55 sq km left out earlier be added to the **Kappatagudda Wildlife Sanctuary** in **Gadag** district.
- **Earth Hour 2026** was observed on **28 March** with the theme **'Give An Hour For Earth'**; it is organised by the **WWF**.
- Nearly **20,000** Olive Ridley turtle eggs were protected on **Hope Island** in Kakinada Bay, **Andhra Pradesh**.
- The **Bhavasagara** centre of **CMLRE, Kochi** was made India's first **National Repository for Deep-Sea Fauna**.
- India has issued **3,561** of the world's 6,311 compliance certificates under the **Nagoya Protocol** on sharing the benefits of biological resources, more than any other country.
- The **Geological Survey of India** declared the **Kalinjar Fort** area in **Banda** district, Uttar Pradesh, a national **geo-heritage site**.
- The **Bureau of Energy Efficiency** marked its **25th** foundation day on **1 March 2026**.
- A freshwater crab with both male and female features (**gynandromorphy**), of the species **Vela carli**, was recorded in **Silent Valley National Park**.
