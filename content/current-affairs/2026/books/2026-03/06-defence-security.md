## Plans and rulings for the forces

- **Defence Forces Vision 2047:** The Defence Minister released 'Defence Forces Vision 2047', prepared by Headquarters Integrated Defence Staff. It is a road map for turning the three services into one integrated, technology-led force by the centenary of Independence.
  - It plans three stages: transition (till 2030), consolidation (2030–2040) and excellence (2040–2047).
  - It proposes a Space Command, a Cyber Command, a Drone Force and a Data Force.
- **Permanent Commission:** The Supreme Court upheld the right of women officers to Permanent Commission in the armed forces. It pointed to bias in how they were assessed.

## Ships, aircraft and weapons

- **Contracts of Rs 5,083 crore:** The Ministry of Defence signed contracts for two purchases.
  - Six Advanced Light Helicopter (ALH) Mk-III helicopters for the Coast Guard, from Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL).
  - Shtil surface-to-air missiles for the Navy, from Russia.
- **Project 17A stealth frigates:** There are seven frigates in the project. Four are built by Mazagon Dock and three by Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers (GRSE), Kolkata.
  - INS Nilgiri is the first of the seven.
  - The Navy received Dunagiri, the fifth Project 17A frigate. It is the second built by GRSE.
- **S-400 'Sudarshan':** India is hurrying the delivery of the remaining S-400 air defence systems from Russia. In Indian service they are called 'Sudarshan'. Three of the five squadrons are in service.
- **Sheshnag-150:** NewSpace Research and Technologies, Bengaluru, developed this long-range strike drone. Its range is over 1,000 km.
- **Arms imports:** According to the Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (SIPRI), India was the world's second-largest arms importer in 2021–2025.

## Exercises and naval cooperation

- **6 to 18 March 2026:** The Army's Southern Command held Exercise Amogh Jwala at the Babina field firing ranges, Uttar Pradesh. It tested mechanised warfare.
- **9 to 20 March 2026:** Exercise LAMITIYE-2026, the 11th India–Seychelles military exercise, was held.
  - LAMITIYE means 'friendship' in Creole.
  - In 2026 India took part with all three services for the first time. The ship INS Trikand was among them.
- **Exercise Kakadu 2026:** INS Nilgiri sailed to Australia for this multinational exercise of the Royal Australian Navy.
- **Indian Ocean Naval Symposium (IONS):**
  - India took the chair of IONS for 2026–2028 after a gap of 16 years.
  - The Philippines joined as its newest observer.
  - The Navy hosted IMEX 2026, the table-top maritime exercise of IONS, at Kochi.

## Borders and terrorism

- **China's border villages:** Military leaders drew attention to the 628 'Xiaokang' villages built by China along its border with India.
- **India–Myanmar border:** The border is 1,643 km long. Under the Free Movement Regime, visa-free movement is now limited to 10 km.
- **Global Terrorism Index 2026:** The index of the Institute for Economics and Peace ranks countries affected by terrorism. It ranked Pakistan first and India 13th.
