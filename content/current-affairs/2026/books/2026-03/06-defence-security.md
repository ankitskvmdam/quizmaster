## Gist
The Defence Minister released **Defence Forces Vision 2047**, a road map for turning the three services into one integrated, technology-led force by the centenary of Independence. It proposes new commands for space and cyber and forces for drones and data.

The Navy had a full month. The frigate **Dunagiri** was delivered, **INS Nilgiri** sailed to Australia for **Exercise Kakadu**, and India, now chair of the **Indian Ocean Naval Symposium**, hosted its maritime exercise at Kochi. The Army exercised with Seychelles in **LAMITIYE** and tested mechanised warfare in **Amogh Jwala**.

On security, the **Global Terrorism Index 2026** ranked Pakistan first and India thirteenth among countries affected by terrorism.

## Key facts
- The Defence Minister released **'Defence Forces Vision 2047'**, prepared by **Headquarters Integrated Defence Staff**.
- Vision 2047 plans three stages: transition (till **2030**), consolidation (2030–**2040**) and excellence (2040–**2047**).
- Vision 2047 proposes a **Space Command**, a **Cyber Command**, a **Drone Force** and a **Data Force**.
- The Ministry of Defence signed contracts worth **Rs 5,083 crore** for **six ALH Mk-III helicopters** for the **Coast Guard** from **HAL** and **Shtil** surface-to-air missiles for the Navy from Russia.
- **Exercise LAMITIYE-2026**, the **11th** India–**Seychelles** military exercise, was held from **9 to 20 March 2026**.
- LAMITIYE means 'friendship' in Creole; in 2026 India took part with all three services for the first time, including the ship **INS Trikand**.
- The Army's **Southern Command** held **Exercise Amogh Jwala** at the **Babina** field firing ranges, Uttar Pradesh, from **6 to 18 March 2026**.
- **INS Nilgiri** took part in **Exercise Kakadu 2026**, the multinational exercise of the **Royal Australian Navy**.
- INS Nilgiri is the first of the seven **Project 17A** stealth frigates.
- The Navy received **Dunagiri**, the **fifth** Project 17A frigate and the second built by **Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers (GRSE), Kolkata**.
- Of the seven Project 17A frigates, **four** are built by **Mazagon Dock** and **three** by GRSE.
- The Navy hosted the table-top maritime exercise **IMEX 2026** of the **Indian Ocean Naval Symposium (IONS)** at **Kochi**.
- India took the chair of **IONS** for **2026–2028** after a gap of **16** years; the **Philippines** joined as its newest observer.
- India is hurrying the delivery of the remaining **S-400** air defence systems from Russia, called **'Sudarshan'** in Indian service; **three** of the five squadrons are in service.
- According to **SIPRI**, India was the world's **second-largest arms importer** in 2021–2025.
- **Sheshnag-150**, a long-range strike drone with a range of over **1,000 km**, was developed by **NewSpace Research and Technologies**, Bengaluru.
- Military leaders drew attention to the **628 'Xiaokang' villages** built by **China** along its border with India.
- The **Global Terrorism Index 2026** of the **Institute for Economics and Peace** ranked **Pakistan 1st** and **India 13th**.
- The India–Myanmar border is **1,643 km** long; under the Free Movement Regime, visa-free movement is now limited to **10 km**.
- The Supreme Court upheld the right of women officers to **Permanent Commission** in the armed forces and pointed to bias in how they were assessed.
