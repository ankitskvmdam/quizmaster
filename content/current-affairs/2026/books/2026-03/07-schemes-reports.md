## Gist
The largest scheme decision of the month was the extension of the **Jal Jeevan Mission** to December 2028, with a bigger budget and a shift from building pipelines to running a reliable water service. The Cabinet also approved a new **Small Hydro Power** scheme, and the electric vehicle scheme **PM E-DRIVE** got more time.

Among reports, the **World Happiness Report 2026** again put Finland first and India at 116, NITI Aayog's **Fiscal Health Index** ranked Odisha the best-managed large State, and the **World Obesity Atlas** warned that India has the second-largest number of overweight children.

Other items to remember are the pause in rice fortification, the first national survey of manuscripts, three media-sector initiatives for the 'orange economy', and two new schemes of the Delhi government.

## Key facts
- On **10 March 2026** the Cabinet extended the **Jal Jeevan Mission** to **December 2028** (JJM 2.0), raising its total outlay to **Rs 8.69 lakh crore**.
- JJM aims to give tap water to all **19.36 crore** rural households; about **15.80 crore** (81.6 per cent) are covered so far.
- JJM 2.0 brings in the **Sujalam Bharat** digital framework, which gives each village water system a unique ID; **Meghalaya** became the **12th** State to sign the reform-linked agreement under it.
- **Jal Mahotsav 2026** ran from **8 to 22 March** with the tagline **'Gaon ka Utsav, Desh ka Mahotsav'**.
- The Cabinet approved the **Small Hydro Power Development Scheme** for 2026-27 to 2030-31 with **Rs 2,584 crore**, to add about **1,500 MW** from projects of **1 to 25 MW**.
- The **PM E-DRIVE** scheme of the **Ministry of Heavy Industries** (outlay **Rs 10,900 crore**) was extended to **31 March 2028**.
- The Centre paused **rice fortification** under **PMGKAY** and allied schemes after an **IIT Kharagpur** study found that the added nutrients decline during long storage.
- **Jan Aushadhi Diwas** is observed on **7 March**; there are over **18,000** Jan Aushadhi Kendras, with a target of **25,000** by March 2027.
- The Jan Aushadhi scheme (PMBJP) is run by the **Department of Pharmaceuticals** under the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilisers.
- NITI Aayog released the second **Fiscal Health Index (2026)**; **Odisha** topped the major States, followed by **Goa** and **Jharkhand**.
- Among the North-Eastern and Himalayan States, ranked separately in the Fiscal Health Index, **Arunachal Pradesh** came first.
- The **World Happiness Report 2026** ranked **Finland** first for the **9th** year in a row; **India** was **116th** of 147 countries and **Afghanistan** last.
- The World Happiness Report is published by the **Wellbeing Research Centre of the University of Oxford** with Gallup and the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network, on **20 March**, the International Day of Happiness.
- The **World Obesity Atlas 2026**, released on **World Obesity Day (4 March)**, found that India has the **second-highest** number of children with high BMI (**41 million**), after **China**.
- A UN report on child mortality (**UN IGME**) estimated that **4.9 million** children under five died in 2024; India's under-five mortality rate fell from 127 in 1990 to **27** in 2024.
- Maharashtra announced the **Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Farmers Loan Waiver Scheme** of **Rs 35,000 crore**, waiving overdue crop loans up to **Rs 2 lakh**.
- The **Ministry of Culture** began India's first nationwide survey to map **manuscripts**, lasting three months, under the **Gyan Bharatam Mission**.
- The **Ministry of Information and Broadcasting** launched three initiatives for the **orange (creative) economy**, including a **National AI Skilling Initiative** with **Google and YouTube** to train **15,000** professionals.
- **Chandigarh** became the **second fully literate Union Territory**, after **Ladakh**, with a literacy rate of **99.93 per cent** under the **ULLAS** programme.
- Licences and registrations of the **FSSAI** will now be valid permanently, without periodic renewal.
- At the **World Water Day Conclave 2026** (theme **'Industry for Water'**), the Ministry of Jal Shakti launched the **National Water Data Policy 2026** and released the **first Census of Springs**.
- The Delhi government launched **ANMOL**, which offers free genetic screening of all newborns for **56** disorders.
- Delhi's **DURGA** scheme will give electric auto-rickshaw permits to **women and transgender persons**.
- A **National Steering Committee** was set up for **PM SETU**, the **Rs 60,000 crore** scheme to upgrade ITIs on a hub-and-spoke model.
