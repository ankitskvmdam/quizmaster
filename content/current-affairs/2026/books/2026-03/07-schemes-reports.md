## At a glance: reports and indices

| Report | By | First | India |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| World Happiness Report 2026 | Wellbeing Research Centre of the University of Oxford | Finland, for the 9th year in a row | 116th of 147 countries |
| Fiscal Health Index (2026), the second | NITI Aayog | Odisha, followed by Goa and Jharkhand (major States) | — |
| World Obesity Atlas 2026 | — | China (children with high body mass index, BMI) | Second-highest, with 41 million children |

- **World Happiness Report:** Afghanistan came last. The report is published with Gallup and the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network on 20 March, the International Day of Happiness.
- **Fiscal Health Index:** The North-Eastern and Himalayan States are ranked separately. Among them, Arunachal Pradesh came first.
- **World Obesity Atlas:** It was released on World Obesity Day (4 March).
- **Child mortality:** A UN report on child mortality (UN IGME) estimated that 4.9 million children under five died in 2024. India's under-five mortality rate fell from 127 in 1990 to 27 in 2024.

## Water

- **8 to 22 March:** Jal Mahotsav 2026 ran with the tagline 'Gaon ka Utsav, Desh ka Mahotsav'.
- **10 March 2026:** The Cabinet extended the Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) to December 2028. This is JJM 2.0.
  - Its total outlay rose to Rs 8.69 lakh crore.
  - The focus shifted from building pipelines to running a reliable water service.
  - JJM aims to give tap water to all 19.36 crore rural households. About 15.80 crore (81.6 per cent) are covered so far.
  - JJM 2.0 brings in the Sujalam Bharat digital framework. It gives each village water system a unique ID.
  - Meghalaya became the 12th State to sign the reform-linked agreement under Sujalam Bharat.
- **World Water Day Conclave 2026:** Its theme was 'Industry for Water'. At the conclave the Ministry of Jal Shakti launched the National Water Data Policy 2026. It also released the first Census of Springs.

## Energy and electric vehicles

- **Small Hydro Power Development Scheme:** The Cabinet approved this new scheme for 2026-27 to 2030-31 with Rs 2,584 crore. It is to add about 1,500 MW from projects of 1 to 25 MW.
- **PM E-DRIVE:** This electric vehicle scheme of the Ministry of Heavy Industries has an outlay of Rs 10,900 crore. It was extended to 31 March 2028.

## Health, food and nutrition

- **Jan Aushadhi Diwas (7 March):** There are over 18,000 Jan Aushadhi Kendras, with a target of 25,000 by March 2027. The Jan Aushadhi scheme (PMBJP) is run by the Department of Pharmaceuticals, under the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilisers.
- **Rice fortification paused:** The Centre paused rice fortification under PMGKAY and allied schemes. A study by IIT Kharagpur had found that the added nutrients decline during long storage.
- **FSSAI licences:** Licences and registrations of the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) will now be valid permanently, without periodic renewal.

## Education, skills and culture

- **Manuscripts:** The Ministry of Culture began India's first nationwide survey to map manuscripts, under the Gyan Bharatam Mission. It will last three months.
- **Orange economy:** The Ministry of Information and Broadcasting launched three initiatives for the orange, or creative, economy. One is a National AI Skilling Initiative with Google and YouTube, to train 15,000 professionals.
- **PM SETU:** A National Steering Committee was set up for PM SETU. This Rs 60,000 crore scheme will upgrade Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs) on a hub-and-spoke model.
- **Chandigarh:** It became the second fully literate Union Territory, after Ladakh. Its literacy rate is 99.93 per cent under the ULLAS programme.

## State schemes

- **Maharashtra:** The State announced the Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Farmers Loan Waiver Scheme of Rs 35,000 crore. It waives overdue crop loans up to Rs 2 lakh.
- **Delhi:** The Delhi government launched two new schemes.
  - ANMOL offers free genetic screening of all newborns for 56 disorders.
  - DURGA will give electric auto-rickshaw permits to women and transgender persons.
