## Indian awards: details

March is the season of literary awards in India.

- **Jnanpith Award:** Vairamuthu is the third Tamil writer to win it, after Akilan (1975) and Jayakanthan (2002). The award carries Rs 11 lakh and a bronze statuette of Vagdevi (Saraswati).
- **Sahitya Akademi Awards 2025:** The awards cover 24 languages.
- **Saraswati Samman:** The award is given by the K.K. Birla Foundation.
- **Sangita Kalanidhi and Nritya Kalanidhi:** Both awards are of the Music Academy, Madras.

## The Oscars

The 98th Academy Awards were held on 15 March 2026 in Los Angeles.

- **Best Picture:** 'One Battle After Another'. Its maker, Paul Thomas Anderson, won Best Director.
- **Best Actor:** Michael B. Jordan, for 'Sinners'.
- **Best Actress:** Jessie Buckley, for 'Hamnet'.
- **Best International Feature:** 'Sentimental Value' of Norway.
- **Best Original Song:** 'Golden' became the first K-pop song to win it.

## Appointments

- **31 March 2026:** Vir Vikram Yadav was appointed head of the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA).
- **NITI Aayog:** Nidhi Chhibber was given additional charge as Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of NITI Aayog. This followed the end of B.V.R. Subrahmanyam's term.
- **Manipur inquiry:** Former Supreme Court judge Justice Balbir Singh Chauhan was appointed head of the Commission of Inquiry into the Manipur violence. He replaced Justice Ajai Lamba.

## Deaths and books

- **4 March 2026:** The veteran journalist H.K. Dua died in New Delhi, aged 88. He was a Padma Bhushan awardee and a former Ambassador to Denmark.
- **28 March 2026:** Vijaypat Singhania, the industrialist, died in Mumbai, aged 87. He was a former chairman of the Raymond Group and a Padma Bhushan awardee.
- **Kailash Satyarthi:** The Nobel laureate released his book 'Karuna: The Power of Compassion'.
