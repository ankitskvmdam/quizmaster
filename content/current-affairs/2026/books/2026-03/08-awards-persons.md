## Gist
March is the season of literary awards in India. The Tamil poet **Vairamuthu** was chosen for the **60th Jnanpith Award**, the **Sahitya Akademi Awards 2025** were announced in 24 languages, and the Bengali novelist **Ramkumar Mukhopadhyay** won the **Saraswati Samman**.

Abroad, the **98th Academy Awards** honoured 'One Battle After Another' as Best Picture, the **Abel Prize** in mathematics went to Gerd Faltings and the **World Food Prize** to Huub Lelieveld.

The country lost the journalist **H.K. Dua** and the industrialist **Vijaypat Singhania**. The month's important days include International Women's Day (8 March), World Water Day (22 March) and World Tuberculosis Day (24 March).

## Key facts
- The Tamil poet and lyricist **Vairamuthu** was chosen for the **60th Jnanpith Award**; he is the **third Tamil writer** to win it, after **Akilan** (1975) and **Jayakanthan** (2002).
- The Jnanpith Award carries **Rs 11 lakh** and a bronze statuette of **Vagdevi** (Saraswati).
- The **Sahitya Akademi Award 2025** for **Hindi** went to **Mamta Kalia** for her memoir **'Jeete Jee Allahabad'**.
- The Sahitya Akademi Award 2025 for **English** went to **Navtej Sarna** for his novel **'Crimson Spring'**; the awards cover **24** languages.
- The **Saraswati Samman 2025** went to the Bengali writer **Ramkumar Mukhopadhyay** for his novel **'Hara Parbati Katha'**; the award is given by the **K.K. Birla Foundation**.
- The **98th Academy Awards** were held on **15 March 2026** in Los Angeles; **'One Battle After Another'** won **Best Picture** and its maker **Paul Thomas Anderson** won Best Director.
- At the Oscars, **Michael B. Jordan** ('Sinners') won Best Actor and **Jessie Buckley** ('Hamnet') Best Actress.
- **'Sentimental Value'** of **Norway** won the Oscar for Best International Feature, and **'Golden'** became the first **K-pop** song to win Best Original Song.
- The **Abel Prize 2026** in mathematics went to the German mathematician **Gerd Faltings**.
- The **World Food Prize 2026** went to **Huub Lelieveld** of the **Netherlands** for his work on food safety.
- The veena player **Jayanthi Kumaresh** was chosen for the **Sangita Kalanidhi** award of the Music Academy, Madras; the dancer **Malavika Sarukkai** was chosen for the **Nritya Kalanidhi**.
- **Nidhi Chhibber** was given additional charge as **CEO of NITI Aayog** after **B.V.R. Subrahmanyam** completed his term.
- Former Supreme Court judge **Justice Balbir Singh Chauhan** was appointed head of the Commission of Inquiry into the **Manipur** violence, replacing Justice Ajai Lamba.
- On **31 March 2026**, **Vir Vikram Yadav** was appointed head of the **Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA)**.
- The veteran journalist **H.K. Dua**, a Padma Bhushan awardee and former Ambassador to **Denmark**, died in New Delhi on **4 March 2026** aged 88.
- **Vijaypat Singhania**, former chairman of the **Raymond Group** and a Padma Bhushan awardee, died in Mumbai on **28 March 2026** aged 87.
- Nobel laureate **Kailash Satyarthi** released his book **'Karuna: The Power of Compassion'**.
- **International Women's Day** (8 March) had the 2026 UN theme **'Rights. Justice. Action. For ALL Women and Girls.'**
- **World Tuberculosis Day** (24 March) had the 2026 theme **'Yes! We Can End TB'**.
- **World Water Day** (22 March) had the 2026 theme **'Water and Gender'**.
- **National Vaccination Day** is **16 March**, marking the first dose of oral polio vaccine given in India in 1995.
- **20 March 2026** began the centenary year of the **Mahad Satyagraha** led by **B.R. Ambedkar** in 1927; the day is observed as **Social Empowerment Day**.
- **Shaheed Diwas** on **23 March** marks the hanging of **Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev** in 1931.
- The first **International Film Festival of Delhi** was held at **Bharat Mandapam** from **25 to 31 March 2026**, with **Spain** as the focus country.
