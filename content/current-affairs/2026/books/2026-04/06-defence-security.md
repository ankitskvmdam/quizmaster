## Navy

- **April 2026:** INS Aridaman was inducted into the Navy. It is India's third nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine (SSBN). So the Navy's nuclear deterrent grew: three such boats can now be kept in service.
  - It displaces about 7,000 tonnes.
  - It has 8 launch tubes for K-15 and K-4 missiles.
- **INS Taragiri:** This stealth frigate was also commissioned into the Navy.

## Internal security

- **Left Wing Extremism (LWE):** The Government said it is all but over. The Home Ministry's new classification has three groups.
  - Only Bijapur (Chhattisgarh) and West Singhbhum (Jharkhand) are "LWE Affected Districts".
  - Kanker is the lone "District of Concern".
  - 35 districts in nine States are "Legacy and Thrust" districts.
- **North-East:** The Centre has set 2029 as the target for ending insurgency there. Manipur is the first priority.
- **Operation Global-Hunt:** Under it, the Narcotics Control Bureau brought back the drug trafficker Salim Dola from Türkiye.

## Defence ties

- **Egypt:** India and Egypt held the 11th Joint Defence Committee meeting in Cairo. Their first Navy-to-Navy staff talks were held along with it.
- **Germany:** Defence Minister Rajnath Singh met his German counterpart, Boris Pistorius, in Berlin. The two sides exchanged a Defence Industrial Cooperation Roadmap.
- **Sri Lanka:** During DIVEX 2026, India gave Sri Lanka two BHISM cubes (portable medical units) under the Aarogya Maitri programme.
- **SCO:** The SCO (Shanghai Cooperation Organisation) Defence Ministers' Meeting was held in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan.

## Spending and equipment

- **Military spending:** According to SIPRI (Stockholm International Peace Research Institute), India was the fifth-largest military spender in the world in 2025. It spent USD 92.1 billion.
- **Drones:** The Indian Army released a technology roadmap for unmanned aerial systems and loitering munitions. A loitering munition is a drone that waits over a target and then strikes it.
- **Aerial bomb:** The Ministry of Defence began the indigenous development of a 1,000-kg aerial bomb for the Air Force, under the Make-II route.
- **"Prajna":** This is an AI-based satellite imagery intelligence system. The DRDO (Defence Research and Development Organisation) handed it over to the Ministry of Home Affairs. It was developed by CAIR (Centre for Artificial Intelligence and Robotics).
