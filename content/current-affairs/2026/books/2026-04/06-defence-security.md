## Gist
The Navy's nuclear deterrent grew with the induction of **INS Aridaman**, India's **third ballistic missile submarine**, so that three such boats can now be kept in service. A SIPRI report placed India **fifth** among the world's military spenders.

In internal security, the Government said Left Wing Extremism is all but over, and the Home Ministry's new list has only **two "LWE Affected" districts**. The next target is to end insurgency in the North-East by **2029**.

The month's exercises were **Cyclone** with Egypt, **Dustlik** with Uzbekistan and the diving exercise **DIVEX** with Sri Lanka. Defence talks were held with Egypt and Germany, and the SCO defence ministers met in Bishkek.

## Key facts
- **INS Aridaman**, India's **third nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine (SSBN)**, was inducted into the Navy in April 2026.
- INS Aridaman displaces about **7,000 tonnes** and has **8 launch tubes** for **K-15 and K-4** missiles.
- The stealth frigate **INS Taragiri** was also commissioned into the Navy.
- In the Home Ministry's new classification, only **Bijapur** (Chhattisgarh) and **West Singhbhum** (Jharkhand) are **"LWE Affected Districts"**.
- **Kanker** is the lone **"District of Concern"**, and 35 districts in nine States are **"Legacy and Thrust"** districts.
- The Centre has set **2029** as the target for ending insurgency in the **North-East**, with **Manipur** as the first priority.
- **Exercise Cyclone-IV**, the fourth India–**Egypt** joint special forces exercise, was held at **Anshas, Egypt**, from **9 to 17 April 2026**.
- The **7th edition of Exercise Dustlik**, between India and **Uzbekistan**, was held at **Namangan** in Uzbekistan.
- **DIVEX 2026**, the fourth India–**Sri Lanka** diving exercise, was held at **Colombo**; the Navy sent **INS Nireekshak**.
- During DIVEX 2026 India gave Sri Lanka two **BHISM cubes** (portable medical units) under the **Aarogya Maitri** programme.
- India and Egypt held the **11th Joint Defence Committee** meeting in **Cairo**, along with their **first Navy-to-Navy staff talks**.
- Defence Minister **Rajnath Singh** met his German counterpart **Boris Pistorius** in **Berlin**; the two sides exchanged a **Defence Industrial Cooperation Roadmap**.
- The German Air Force is expected to take part in **Exercise Tarang Shakti**, to be held in India later in 2026.
- The **SCO Defence Ministers' Meeting** was held in **Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan**.
- According to **SIPRI**, India was the **fifth-largest military spender** in the world in 2025, spending **USD 92.1 billion**.
- The **Indian Army** released a technology roadmap for **unmanned aerial systems and loitering munitions**.
- The Ministry of Defence began the indigenous development of a **1,000-kg aerial bomb** for the Air Force under the **Make-II** route.
- The **DRDO** handed over **"Prajna"**, an AI-based satellite imagery intelligence system, to the **Ministry of Home Affairs**; it was developed by **CAIR**.
- Under **Operation Global-Hunt**, the **Narcotics Control Bureau** brought back drug trafficker **Salim Dola** from **Türkiye**.
- The Indian Army announced **Exercise PRAGATI**, a multilateral exercise with **11 countries** at **Umroi, Meghalaya**.
