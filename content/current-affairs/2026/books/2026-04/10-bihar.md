## Government and governance

April brought a change of guard in Patna.

- **14 April 2026:** Nitish Kumar resigned as Chief Minister. He had been elected to the Rajya Sabha in March 2026.
- **15 April 2026:** Samrat Choudhary was sworn in as Chief Minister of Bihar. He is the first BJP Chief Minister of the State.
  - Governor Syed Ata Hasnain administered the oath at Lok Bhawan, Patna.
  - Bijendra Prasad Yadav and Vijay Kumar Chaudhary, both of the JD(U), were sworn in as Deputy Chief Ministers.
- **22 April 2026:** The first Cabinet meeting under Samrat Choudhary approved 22 proposals. Among them:
  - Rs 680 crore to develop the area around the Baba Hariharnath Temple at Sonepur, on the lines of the Kashi Vishwanath corridor.
  - 11 greenfield satellite townships and the upgrading of 75 ITIs (Industrial Training Institutes), at a cost of Rs 3,615 crore.
- **State song:** The Bihar government ordered that government programmes begin with Vande Mataram and that State functions end with the State song. Bihar's State song, "Mere Bharat Ke Kanth Haar", was written by Satya Narayan and adopted in 2012.
- **Panchayat Advancement Index 2.0:** Only 2 of Bihar's 8,053 gram panchayats were graded "Front Runner".

## Projects and economy

- **BiharOne:** A contract of Rs 87 crore was awarded for this single-window digital platform for government services. It is due to start by July 2026.
- **Emergency landing strips:** Bihar plans three of them for Air Force aircraft. They will be on the Patna–Purnia Expressway, the Gorakhpur–Siliguri Expressway and the Ram Janaki Marg.
- **Danapur–Bihta elevated corridor:** This corridor, about 25 km long, will link Patna with the coming Bihta airport, Patna's second airport.
- **High-security prison:** Bihar plans one in the Kaimur hills, at Sarodag village in Kaimur district.
- **Global Model Education City:** It is planned on more than 250 acres near Patna, at an estimated cost of about Rs 547 crore.
- **MSME Technology Centre:** This centre for micro, small and medium enterprises (MSME) cost Rs 171 crore. Union Minister Jitan Ram Manjhi and the Chief Minister opened it at Bihta. It has extension centres at Muzaffarpur, Rohtas, Darbhanga and Munger.
- **Pulses:** Bihar's first structured procurement of pulses began with masoor. It is under PM-AASHA and is led by the NCCF.

## Education, health and welfare

- **Mahatma Gandhi Central University, Motihari:** Vice-President C.P. Radhakrishnan addressed its 3rd convocation.
- **IGIMS, Patna:** The Indira Gandhi Institute of Medical Sciences performed Bihar's first low-cost robotic surgery for pancreatic cancer.
- **Project Udaan:** The Patna Nagar Nigam launched it to give free coaching to the children of sanitation workers.
- **PM VIKAS:** Under this scheme, the Ministry of Minority Affairs signed an MoU with IIT Patna to train about 600 minority youth.

## Honours, events and sport

- **President's Police Colour:** The Bihar Police was awarded this honour, also called the Nishan. Odisha and Sikkim received it too.
- **Bihar Nursery and Green-Tech Conclave 2026:** It was held at Gyan Bhavan, Patna, with the theme "Parampara Se Pragati Tak".
- **DRDO exhibition:** The DRDO held a defence technology exhibition at Motihari. MP Radha Mohan Singh inaugurated it.
- **Hockey:** Uttar Pradesh won the 16th Hockey India Sub-Junior Men National Championship at Rajgir. It beat Madhya Pradesh 5–2 in the final.
- **Khelo India Tribal Games:** In the first edition, Bihar finished 19th, with one gold and one silver medal.
