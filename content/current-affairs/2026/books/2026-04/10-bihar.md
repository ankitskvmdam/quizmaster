## Gist
April brought a change of guard in Patna. **Nitish Kumar**, elected to the Rajya Sabha in March, resigned as Chief Minister on **14 April 2026**, and **Samrat Choudhary** took the oath the next day as the **first BJP Chief Minister of Bihar**, with two Deputy Chief Ministers from the JD(U).

The new Cabinet's first meeting cleared a temple corridor at **Sonepur**, new satellite townships and the upgrading of ITIs. Other projects in the news were the **BiharOne** digital platform, emergency airstrips on three highways, a high-security prison in **Kaimur** and an education city near Patna.

The **Bihar Police** received the **President's Police Colour**, an MSME Technology Centre opened at **Bihta**, and the State's first organised procurement of pulses began.

## Key facts
- **Samrat Choudhary** was sworn in as Chief Minister of Bihar on **15 April 2026**; he is the **first BJP Chief Minister** of the State.
- Governor **Syed Ata Hasnain** administered the oath to Samrat Choudhary at **Lok Bhawan, Patna**.
- **Bijendra Prasad Yadav** and **Vijay Kumar Chaudhary**, both of the JD(U), were sworn in as **Deputy Chief Ministers**.
- **Nitish Kumar** resigned as Chief Minister on **14 April 2026**, after being elected to the **Rajya Sabha** in March 2026.
- The first Cabinet meeting under Samrat Choudhary, on **22 April 2026**, approved **22 proposals**.
- The Cabinet approved **Rs 680 crore** to develop the area around the **Baba Hariharnath Temple** at **Sonepur** on the lines of the Kashi Vishwanath corridor.
- The same Cabinet meeting approved **11 greenfield satellite townships** and the upgrading of **75 ITIs** at a cost of Rs 3,615 crore.
- The **Bihar Police** was awarded the **President's Police Colour**, also called the **Nishan**; Odisha and Sikkim received it too.
- The **Bihar Nursery and Green-Tech Conclave 2026** was held at **Gyan Bhavan, Patna**, with the theme **"Parampara Se Pragati Tak"**.
- Bihar plans **three emergency landing strips** for Air Force aircraft on the **Patna–Purnia Expressway**, the **Gorakhpur–Siliguri Expressway** and the **Ram Janaki Marg**.
- Vice-President C.P. Radhakrishnan addressed the **3rd convocation** of **Mahatma Gandhi Central University, Motihari**.
- **IGIMS, Patna**, performed Bihar's first low-cost **robotic surgery for pancreatic cancer**.
- A contract of **Rs 87 crore** was awarded for **BiharOne**, a single-window digital platform for government services, due to start by **July 2026**.
- The **Danapur–Bihta elevated corridor**, about **25 km** long, will link Patna with the coming **Bihta airport**, Patna's second airport.
- Bihar plans a **high-security prison** in the **Kaimur hills**, at **Sarodag** village in Kaimur district.
- **Uttar Pradesh** won the **16th Hockey India Sub-Junior Men National Championship** at **Rajgir**, beating Madhya Pradesh 5–2 in the final.
- A **Global Model Education City** is planned on more than **250 acres** near **Patna**, at an estimated cost of about Rs 547 crore.
- The **DRDO** held a defence technology exhibition at **Motihari**; it was inaugurated by MP **Radha Mohan Singh**.
- The **Patna Nagar Nigam** launched **Project Udaan** to give free coaching to the children of sanitation workers.
- The **Ministry of Minority Affairs** signed an MoU with **IIT Patna** under the **PM VIKAS** scheme to train about **600** minority youth.
- The Bihar government ordered that government programmes begin with **Vande Mataram** and that State functions end with the State song.
- Bihar's State song, **"Mere Bharat Ke Kanth Haar"**, was written by **Satya Narayan** and adopted in **2012**.
- An **MSME Technology Centre** costing **Rs 171 crore** was opened at **Bihta** by Union Minister **Jitan Ram Manjhi** and the Chief Minister, with extension centres at Muzaffarpur, Rohtas, Darbhanga and Munger.
- Bihar's **first structured procurement of pulses** began with **masoor** under **PM-AASHA**, led by the **NCCF**.
- In the first Khelo India Tribal Games, Bihar finished **19th** with one gold and one silver medal.
- In the Panchayat Advancement Index 2.0, only **2** of Bihar's **8,053** gram panchayats were graded "Front Runner".
