## Gist
India got its **99th Ramsar site**, **Shekha Jheel** in Aligarh, and its first national report on **bats**. On the world stage, the IUCN moved the **emperor penguin** to the Endangered list, and India **withdrew its bid to host COP33** in 2028.

At home the Government eased the recycling targets in the plastic waste rules, wind power had its best year, and several "firsts" were reported: a water-neutral railway depot, a green methanol plant and a university for wildlife conservation.

Two worrying items were the death of **25 Himalayan Griffon vultures** in Dudhwa and the Supreme Court's warning to three States over sand mining in the **Chambal** sanctuary.

## Key facts
- **Shekha Jheel Bird Sanctuary** in **Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh**, was designated India's **99th Ramsar site**; it is Uttar Pradesh's **12th**.
- The **IUCN** declared the **emperor penguin** **Endangered**; warming could halve its numbers by the 2080s.
- India **withdrew its bid to host COP33** of the UNFCCC in **2028**; South Korea is now the only contender.
- **COP31** (2026) will be hosted by **Turkey** with Australia, and **COP32** (2027) by **Ethiopia**.
- **"State of India's Bats (2024–25)"** is the **first national assessment** of bats; India has about **135** bat species, of which **16** are endemic.
- The bats report was prepared by experts led by the **Nature Conservation Foundation** and **Bat Conservation International**.
- **25 Himalayan Griffon vultures** died in **Dudhwa Tiger Reserve**, Uttar Pradesh, probably from secondary poisoning.
- India added a record **6.05 GW** of wind power capacity in 2025-26; total wind capacity crossed **56 GW**, the **fourth highest** in the world after China, the US and Germany.
- The amended **Plastic Waste Management Rules** let companies carry forward a shortfall in recycling targets for up to **three years**.
- Under the plastic waste rules, **rigid plastic** packaging must contain **30 per cent** recycled content in 2025-26.
- The Supreme Court warned **Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh** to stop illegal sand mining in the **National Chambal Gharial Sanctuary**.
- **Earth Day** was observed on **22 April** with the theme **"Our Power, Our Planet"**.
- **Varkala** in **Kerala** was chosen for the UN's **"20 Cities Towards Zero Waste"** initiative.
- **Project Him Sarovar**, launched on **10 April 2026** in **Ladakh**, will build **50** small water bodies in Leh and Kargil to store snowmelt.
- **NHAI** launched the **Arogya Van** initiative to plant **medicinal trees** on vacant land along National Highways.
- **Kankaria Coaching Depot** in **Ahmedabad** became India's **first water-neutral railway depot**.
- **Vantara** announced a university of wildlife conservation and veterinary sciences at **Jamnagar**, Gujarat.
- Tamil Nadu, with Kerala, began the **third synchronised survey of the Nilgiri Tahr**, using a mobile app called **"Varudai"**.
- India's **first green methanol plant** is being set up at **Deendayal Port**, Gujarat; it will use the invasive shrub ***Prosopis juliflora*** from the **Banni grasslands** as raw material.
- The Government set indicative targets for blending **Sustainable Aviation Fuel**: **1 per cent** by 2027, **2 per cent** by 2028 and **5 per cent** by 2030.
- The **World Bank** launched the **"Water Forward"** initiative to improve water security for over **1 billion** people by 2030.
- India will host the **9th India International Water Week** in **September 2026**; its theme is climate-resilient water management.
- **Kerala** began a pilot programme to restore **sacred groves**, locally called **Kavu**.
