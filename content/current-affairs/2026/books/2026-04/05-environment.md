## Wetlands and wildlife

- **Shekha Jheel Bird Sanctuary:** This sanctuary in Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh, was designated India's 99th Ramsar site. It is Uttar Pradesh's 12th.
- **Emperor penguin:** The IUCN (International Union for Conservation of Nature) declared it Endangered. Warming could halve its numbers by the 2080s.
- **"State of India's Bats (2024–25)":** This is the first national assessment of bats, and India's first national report on them.
  - India has about 135 bat species. Of these, 16 are endemic, that is, found only in India.
  - Experts led by the Nature Conservation Foundation and Bat Conservation International prepared it.
- **Vultures:** 25 Himalayan Griffon vultures died in Dudhwa Tiger Reserve, Uttar Pradesh. The probable cause was secondary poisoning, that is, eating a poisoned animal.
- **National Chambal Gharial Sanctuary:** The Supreme Court warned Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh to stop illegal sand mining in it.
- **Nilgiri Tahr:** Tamil Nadu, with Kerala, began the third synchronised survey of the Nilgiri Tahr. It uses a mobile app called "Varudai".
- **Sacred groves:** Kerala began a pilot programme to restore them. They are locally called Kavu.
- **Vantara:** It announced a university of wildlife conservation and veterinary sciences at Jamnagar, Gujarat.

## Water

- **10 April 2026:** Project Him Sarovar was launched in Ladakh. It will build 50 small water bodies in Leh and Kargil to store snowmelt.
- **September 2026:** India will host the 9th India International Water Week. Its theme is climate-resilient water management.
- **Kankaria Coaching Depot:** This depot in Ahmedabad became India's first water-neutral railway depot.
- **"Water Forward":** The World Bank launched this initiative. It aims to improve water security for over 1 billion people by 2030.

## Energy, fuels and plastic

- **Wind power:** India added a record 6.05 GW of wind power capacity in 2025-26, its best year. Total wind capacity crossed 56 GW. This is the fourth highest in the world, after China, the US and Germany.
- **Plastic Waste Management Rules:** The amended rules ease the recycling targets.
  - Companies can carry forward a shortfall in recycling targets for up to three years.
  - Rigid plastic packaging must contain 30 per cent recycled content in 2025-26.
- **Green methanol:** India's first green methanol plant is being set up at Deendayal Port, Gujarat. Its raw material will be the invasive shrub Prosopis juliflora from the Banni grasslands.
- **Sustainable Aviation Fuel:** The Government set indicative targets for blending it.
  - 1 per cent by 2027.
  - 2 per cent by 2028.
  - 5 per cent by 2030.

## Days and green initiatives

- **22 April:** Earth Day was observed with the theme "Our Power, Our Planet".
- **Varkala:** Varkala in Kerala was chosen for the UN's "20 Cities Towards Zero Waste" initiative.
- **Arogya Van:** NHAI (National Highways Authority of India) launched this initiative. It will plant medicinal trees on vacant land along National Highways.
