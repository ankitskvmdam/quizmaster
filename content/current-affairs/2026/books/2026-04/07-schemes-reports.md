## Gist
Several long-running schemes were extended or reviewed in April. The **Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana** completed **11 years**, **PMGSY-III** was extended to March 2028, the deadline under **PM-KUSUM** was pushed to March 2027, and the **8th Poshan Pakhwada** was held from 9 to 23 April.

Among reports and indices, the ones most likely to be asked are the **World Press Freedom Index 2026** (India 157th), the **Panchayat Advancement Index 2.0** (Tripura on top), the Access Now count of **internet shutdowns** (India second) and NITI Aayog's report on women borrowers.

New initiatives included **e-SafeHER** for the cyber safety of rural women and the **RELIEF** scheme for exporters hit by the West Asia crisis.

## Key facts
- The **Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana** completed **11 years**; about **57 crore** loans worth over **Rs 40.07 lakh crore** have been sanctioned, and **67 per cent** of beneficiaries are women.
- Under **PMAY-Gramin**, nearly **3 crore** houses had been completed by March 2026, against a target of **4.95 crore** by 2029.
- The Ministry of Women and Child Development held the **8th Poshan Pakhwada** from **9 to 23 April 2026**, focused on brain development in the first six years of life.
- The Union Cabinet extended **PMGSY-III** till **March 2028**, with the outlay raised to **Rs 83,977 crore**.
- The **MNRE** extended the completion deadline for projects under **PM-KUSUM** to **31 March 2027**.
- The **River Basin Management** scheme of the **Ministry of Jal Shakti** was extended for 2026-27 to 2030-31; one of its two parts is the **Brahmaputra Board**.
- The **RELIEF** scheme for exporters hit by the West Asia crisis has an outlay of **Rs 497 crore** and is run by the **Export Credit Guarantee Corporation (ECGC)** under the Export Promotion Mission.
- **e-SafeHER**, under MeitY's ISEA programme, will train **one million** rural women in cyber safety over three years; it is run by **C-DAC** with the **Reliance Foundation**.
- Women trained under e-SafeHER are called **"Cyber Sakhis"**; the programme starts in **Madhya Pradesh and Odisha**.
- Registration opened for **Phase VI of Yuva Sangam**, a youth exchange programme under **Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat** run by the **Ministry of Education**.
- The **SEED** scheme, for the welfare of **De-notified, Nomadic and Semi-Nomadic** communities, is run by the **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment**.
- The **Urban Challenge Fund** of **Rs 1 lakh crore** runs from 2025-26 to 2030-31; cities must raise at least 50 per cent of project cost from bonds, loans or PPPs.
- The report **"From Borrowers to Builders"**, by **NITI Aayog** with TransUnion CIBIL and MicroSave Consulting, found that women borrowers hold **Rs 76 lakh crore** of credit, **26 per cent** of the total.
- NITI Aayog released two reports on the **"Ease of Doing Research and Development"** in India; India is **38th** in the **Global Innovation Index 2025** of WIPO.
- NITI Aayog also released a **DPI@2047** roadmap for digital public infrastructure and a tourism anthology called **"Divya Bharat"**.
- In the **World Press Freedom Index 2026** of **Reporters Without Borders (RSF)**, India is **157th of 180** countries, down from 151st in 2025.
- **Norway** is first in the World Press Freedom Index 2026 for the tenth year in a row, and **Eritrea** is last.
- The **Panchayat Advancement Index (PAI) 2.0**, for 2023-24, was released by the **Ministry of Panchayati Raj**; **Tripura** was the best-performing State.
- No gram panchayat reached the top **"Achiever" (A+)** grade in PAI 2.0; **3,635** were graded **"Front Runner"**.
- According to **Access Now**, India had **65 internet shutdowns** in 2025, the **second highest** in the world after **Myanmar (95)**.
- The Ministry of Textiles, with **NIFT**, launched **"Vishwa Sutra"**, a collection that reworks about 30 Indian handloom weaves.
- The **AICTE** launched the third edition of **VAANI**, which funds technical conferences in **22 Indian languages**.
- **Telangana** launched **Project Sanjeevani**, a highway trauma rescue system, on a stretch of **NH-44**.
