## Schemes extended or reviewed

Several long-running schemes were extended or reviewed in April.

- **9 to 23 April 2026:** The Ministry of Women and Child Development held the 8th Poshan Pakhwada. Its focus was brain development in the first six years of life.
- **Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana:** It completed 11 years.
  - About 57 crore loans worth over Rs 40.07 lakh crore have been sanctioned.
  - 67 per cent of beneficiaries are women.
- **PMAY-Gramin (Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Gramin):** Nearly 3 crore houses had been completed by March 2026. The target is 4.95 crore by 2029.
- **PMGSY-III (Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana):** The Union Cabinet extended it till March 2028. Its outlay was raised to Rs 83,977 crore.
- **PM-KUSUM:** The MNRE (Ministry of New and Renewable Energy) extended the completion deadline for projects under it to 31 March 2027.
- **River Basin Management:** This scheme of the Ministry of Jal Shakti was extended for 2026-27 to 2030-31. One of its two parts is the Brahmaputra Board.

## New schemes and initiatives

- **RELIEF:** This scheme is for exporters hit by the West Asia crisis. Its outlay is Rs 497 crore. The Export Credit Guarantee Corporation (ECGC) runs it under the Export Promotion Mission.
- **e-SafeHER:** It comes under MeitY's ISEA (Information Security Education and Awareness) programme.
  - It will train one million rural women in cyber safety over three years.
  - C-DAC (Centre for Development of Advanced Computing) runs it with the Reliance Foundation.
  - Women trained under it are called "Cyber Sakhis".
  - The programme starts in Madhya Pradesh and Odisha.
- **Urban Challenge Fund:** This fund of Rs 1 lakh crore runs from 2025-26 to 2030-31. Cities must raise at least 50 per cent of project cost from bonds, loans or PPPs (public–private partnerships).
- **Yuva Sangam:** Registration opened for its Phase VI. It is a youth exchange programme under Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat, run by the Ministry of Education.
- **SEED:** This scheme is for the welfare of De-notified, Nomadic and Semi-Nomadic communities. The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment runs it.
- **"Vishwa Sutra":** The Ministry of Textiles launched this collection with NIFT (National Institute of Fashion Technology). It reworks about 30 Indian handloom weaves.
- **VAANI:** The AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education) launched its third edition. It funds technical conferences in 22 Indian languages.
- **Project Sanjeevani:** Telangana launched this highway trauma rescue system on a stretch of NH-44.
