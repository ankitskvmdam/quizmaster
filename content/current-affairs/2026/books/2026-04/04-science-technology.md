## Gist
Two events stand out. NASA's **Artemis II**, launched on **1 April 2026**, took four astronauts around the Moon, the first people to travel that far since Apollo 17 in 1972. At home, the **Prototype Fast Breeder Reactor at Kalpakkam** attained criticality, taking India into the **second stage** of its three-stage nuclear power programme.

In health, India approved its **first dengue vaccine**, Qdenga. In quantum technology, a **1,000 km** secure communication network was completed under the National Quantum Mission and an open-access testing facility was opened at **Amaravati**.

The private space company Skyroot moved closer to launching **Vikram-1**, and the CBI and the Gujarat Police introduced AI tools.

## Key facts
- NASA launched **Artemis II** on **1 April 2026** on the **Space Launch System (SLS)** rocket; it is the first crewed flight of the **Orion** spacecraft.
- Artemis II is a flyby of the Moon lasting about ten days, with no landing; it is the first time humans have travelled to the Moon's vicinity since **Apollo 17 (1972)**.
- The Artemis II crew are **Reid Wiseman** (commander), **Victor Glover**, **Christina Koch** and **Jeremy Hansen** of the **Canadian Space Agency**.
- **Christina Koch** is the first woman to travel to the vicinity of the Moon and **Jeremy Hansen** the first non-American to do so.
- Artemis II set a record for the farthest distance travelled by humans from Earth, going beyond the mark set by **Apollo 13** in 1970.
- The **Prototype Fast Breeder Reactor (PFBR)** at **Kalpakkam, Tamil Nadu**, attained its **first criticality**.
- The PFBR has a capacity of **500 MWe**; it was designed by **IGCAR** and built by **BHAVINI**.
- The PFBR marks India's entry into **Stage 2** of the three-stage nuclear programme; India will be the **second country after Russia** to run a commercial fast breeder reactor.
- India approved its **first dengue vaccine**, **Qdenga (TAK-003)** made by **Takeda**, for people aged 4 to 60.
- An Indian dengue vaccine, **DengiAll**, is being developed by **Panacea Biotec** and the **ICMR**.
- A **1,000 km quantum communication network** was built under the **National Quantum Mission** by **QNu Labs**; it uses **Quantum Key Distribution**.
- Andhra Pradesh opened India's **first open-access** quantum testing centre, the **Amaravati Quantum Reference Facility**, on **World Quantum Day (14 April)**.
- **Vikram-1**, built by **Skyroot Aerospace** of Hyderabad, is India's **first private orbital rocket**; it can carry up to 350 kg to low Earth orbit.
- The **CBI** launched an AI chatbot called **"Abhay"** to help people check whether a notice claiming to be from the CBI is genuine.
- "Abhay" was launched by Chief Justice of India **Surya Kant** at the **22nd D.P. Kohli Memorial Lecture**.
- The **Gujarat Police** launched **"NARIT AI"**, an AI tool to help investigate cases under the **NDPS Act**.
- India depends wholly on imports for **helium**; **Qatar** supplies about 34 per cent of world helium exports, and the West Asia crisis disrupted supply.
- The **National One Health Mission** is anchored by the **National Institute for One Health** at **Nagpur**.
- Studies in **Tanzania** showed that mosquitoes modified with **gene drives** can block the malaria parasite.
- Fossil leaves from the **Kasauli Formation** in Himachal Pradesh, about 20 million years old, were named ***Syzygium paleosalicifolium***, an ancient relative of the jamun.
- The upgraded **HAL Aerospace Museum** in **Bengaluru**, India's first aerospace museum, was reopened to the public.
