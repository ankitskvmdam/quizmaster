## Space

- **1 April 2026:** NASA launched Artemis II on the Space Launch System (SLS) rocket. It is the first crewed flight of the Orion spacecraft. It took four astronauts around the Moon.
  - It is a flyby of the Moon lasting about ten days, with no landing.
  - It is the first time humans have travelled to the Moon's vicinity since Apollo 17 (1972).
  - The crew are Reid Wiseman (commander), Victor Glover, Christina Koch and Jeremy Hansen of the Canadian Space Agency.
  - Christina Koch is the first woman to travel to the vicinity of the Moon. Jeremy Hansen is the first non-American to do so.
  - Artemis II set a record for the farthest distance travelled by humans from Earth. It went beyond the mark set by Apollo 13 in 1970.
- **Vikram-1:** It is India's first private orbital rocket, built by Skyroot Aerospace of Hyderabad. It can carry up to 350 kg to low Earth orbit. Skyroot moved closer to launching it.

## Nuclear energy

- **Prototype Fast Breeder Reactor (PFBR):** The PFBR at Kalpakkam, Tamil Nadu, attained its first criticality. Criticality is the point where a steady chain reaction sustains itself.
  - Its capacity is 500 MWe (megawatts of electricity).
  - It was designed by IGCAR (Indira Gandhi Centre for Atomic Research) and built by BHAVINI (Bharatiya Nabhikiya Vidyut Nigam).
  - It marks India's entry into Stage 2 of its three-stage nuclear power programme.
  - India will be the second country after Russia to run a commercial fast breeder reactor.

## Quantum technology

- **14 April (World Quantum Day):** Andhra Pradesh opened the Amaravati Quantum Reference Facility. It is India's first open-access quantum testing centre.
- **Quantum network:** A 1,000 km quantum communication network was built under the National Quantum Mission, by QNu Labs. This secure network uses Quantum Key Distribution, a way of sharing secret keys using quantum physics.

## Health

- **Qdenga:** India approved its first dengue vaccine, Qdenga (TAK-003), made by Takeda. It is for people aged 4 to 60.
- **DengiAll:** This Indian dengue vaccine is being developed by Panacea Biotec and the ICMR (Indian Council of Medical Research).
- **National One Health Mission:** It is anchored by the National Institute for One Health at Nagpur.
- **Gene drives:** Studies in Tanzania showed that mosquitoes modified with gene drives can block the malaria parasite. A gene drive spreads a chosen gene through a population.

## AI tools

- **"Abhay":** The CBI (Central Bureau of Investigation) launched this AI chatbot. It helps people check whether a notice claiming to be from the CBI is genuine. Chief Justice of India Surya Kant launched it at the 22nd D.P. Kohli Memorial Lecture.
- **"NARIT AI":** The Gujarat Police launched this AI tool. It helps investigate cases under the NDPS (Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances) Act.

## Other news

- **Helium:** India depends wholly on imports for helium. Qatar supplies about 34 per cent of world helium exports. The West Asia crisis disrupted supply.
- **Fossil leaves:** Leaves about 20 million years old were found in the Kasauli Formation in Himachal Pradesh. They were named Syzygium paleosalicifolium, an ancient relative of the jamun.
- **HAL Aerospace Museum:** The upgraded museum in Bengaluru, India's first aerospace museum, was reopened to the public.
