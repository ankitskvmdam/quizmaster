## Gist
The month's biggest story in Parliament was the **Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2026**. It proposed raising the maximum strength of the Lok Sabha to **850** so that a fresh delimitation could bring in women's reservation by 2029, but it **failed to get the special majority** in the Lok Sabha and the two Bills that came with it were withdrawn.

On the ground, the first phase of **Census 2027** began on **1 April 2026**, the first census in India to be taken digitally. Parliament also dealt with Bills making **Amaravati** the only capital of Andhra Pradesh and decriminalising hundreds of minor offences (**Jan Vishwas**), and **Harivansh** was elected Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha for a third time.

Smaller items to remember are the Supreme Court's ruling on safe highways, the new districts of **Ladakh**, official status for **Khasi and Garo** in Meghalaya, and the new rules for online gaming.

## Key facts
- Phase I of **Census 2027** (houselisting and housing census) began on **1 April 2026** and runs till 30 September 2026.
- Census 2027 is India's **16th census** and the **8th since Independence**; it is the **first digital census**, with online self-enumeration.
- **President Droupadi Murmu** was the first citizen to complete self-enumeration; the houselisting phase has **33 questions**.
- The Union Government introduced the **Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2026** along with the Delimitation Bill and the Union Territories Laws (Amendment) Bill.
- The 131st Amendment Bill proposed raising the maximum strength of the Lok Sabha from **550 to 850** (815 from States and 35 from Union Territories).
- The Bill was **defeated in the Lok Sabha**: it got **298 votes** against the **352** needed for a special majority, and the two other Bills were withdrawn.
- **Harivansh** was re-elected unopposed as **Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha** for a **third consecutive term**; he is the first nominated member to hold the post.
- The Lok Sabha passed the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill, 2026, making **Amaravati** the **sole permanent capital** of Andhra Pradesh, with effect from 2 June 2024.
- The Lok Sabha passed the **Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Bill, 2026**, which amends 784 provisions in **80 Central Acts** and decriminalises **717** of them.
- The **CAPF (General Administration) Act, 2026** reserves for IPS officers 50 per cent of posts at the Inspector General level and **100 per cent** at the Special DG and DG levels in the central armed police forces.
- **NCERT** was given the status of an **institution deemed to be university** under the distinct category, so it can now award degrees.
- On **10 April 2026** (Sindhi Bhasha Diwas), Vice-President **C.P. Radhakrishnan** released the Sindhi version of the Constitution in the **Devanagari and Persian** scripts.
- **Sindhi** was added to the Eighth Schedule by the **21st Amendment Act, 1967**.
- The Supreme Court held that **safe travel on National Highways** is part of the right to life under **Article 21** and barred new dhabas and commercial structures within the highway right-of-way.
- The Supreme Court asked a committee headed by **Justice S. Ravindra Bhat** to prepare a plan for **disabled-friendly prisons**.
- The Lieutenant Governor of **Ladakh**, Vinai Kumar Saxena, approved **five new districts**: **Nubra, Sham, Changthang, Zanskar and Drass**; until then Ladakh had only Leh and Kargil.
- The foundation stone of India's **first Petroglyph Conservation Park** was laid at **Sindhu Ghat, Leh**.
- The Meghalaya Cabinet approved an ordinance making **Khasi and Garo** official languages of the State along with English, using **Article 345**.
- **Telangana** declared **cancer** a **notifiable disease**, so every diagnosed case must be reported.
- The Karnataka High Court directed the State to implement its **menstrual leave** policy of **one day a month** for women employees.
- **MeitY** notified the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Rules, 2026, in force from **1 May 2026**; the regulator is the **Online Gaming Authority of India**.
- The **National Building Construction Standards 2026**, published by the **Bureau of Indian Standards**, replaced the National Building Code 2016.
- **K.C. Venugopal** was reappointed Chairman of the **Public Accounts Committee** for 2026–27.
