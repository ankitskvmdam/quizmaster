## Gist
The new financial year opened with a new tax law: the **Income-tax Act, 2025** replaced the 1961 Act on **1 April 2026**. A week later the RBI's Monetary Policy Committee left the **repo rate at 5.25 per cent** and lowered its growth forecast for 2026-27, because the conflict in West Asia had pushed up oil prices and pulled the **rupee to a record low of about Rs 95 to the dollar**.

The Reserve Bank was busy on other fronts too. It curbed banks' dealings in offshore rupee derivatives, cancelled the licence of **Paytm Payments Bank**, started a new way of issuing State government bonds and tightened the rules on bad loans from 2027.

In the real economy, the major ports handled record cargo, **UPI** completed ten years, India notified its **first semiconductor fab** at Dholera, and **Google** began work on a very large data centre at Visakhapatnam.

## Key facts
- The **Income-tax Act, 2025** came into force on **1 April 2026**, replacing the Income-tax Act, 1961.
- The new income-tax law merges the financial year and assessment year into a single **"Tax Year"**, and **Form 130** replaces Form 16.
- In April 2026 the RBI's Monetary Policy Committee kept the **repo rate unchanged at 5.25 per cent** with a **neutral** stance; the SDF rate stays at 5 per cent and the MSF and Bank Rate at 5.50 per cent.
- The RBI lowered its GDP growth forecast for **2026-27 to 6.9 per cent** and projected CPI inflation at **4.6 per cent**.
- The rupee touched a record low of about **Rs 95 per US dollar**; the HSBC manufacturing PMI fell to **53.9** in March 2026, a 45-month low.
- The RBI barred banks from taking part in rupee **non-deliverable derivative** contracts to curb offshore speculation, and later eased the curbs in part.
- The RBI began a pilot **Benchmark Issuance Strategy** for **State Development Loans** from 2026-27 with **nine States**, including Bihar.
- The RBI cancelled the banking licence of **Paytm Payments Bank** under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.
- New RBI directions on bad loans take effect on **1 April 2027** and move banks to an **Expected Credit Loss** framework.
- The Lok Sabha passed the **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (Amendment) Bill**, which adds a creditor-initiated out-of-court process to be completed in **150 days**.
- The IMF cut its forecast of global growth in 2026 to about **3.1 per cent** and projected India's growth at about **6.5 per cent**.
- The major ports handled a record **915.17 million tonnes** of cargo in 2025-26; **Deendayal Port** handled the most, followed by **Paradip**.
- **UPI** completed **10 years**; India accounts for about **49 per cent** of the world's real-time payment transactions.
- The Government notified the **Startup India Fund of Funds 2.0** with a corpus of **Rs 10,000 crore**, to be run by **SIDBI**.
- A record of over **55,200 startups** were recognised in 2025-26, taking the total past **2.23 lakh**.
- India notified its **first semiconductor fabrication plant** in a special economic zone at **Dholera, Gujarat**.
- The CCEA approved about **Rs 40,175 crore** for two hydropower projects in Arunachal Pradesh: **Kamala (1,720 MW, NHPC)** and **Kalai-II (1,200 MW, THDC)** on the Lohit river.
- The Union Cabinet approved the **Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool** with a sovereign guarantee of **Rs 12,980 crore**.
- The **Delhi–Dehradun Economic Corridor**, a six-lane expressway of about **210 km**, was inaugurated; it has a wildlife corridor through **Rajaji National Park**.
- Customs duty was waived on **40 petrochemical products** till **30 June 2026** to soften the effect of the West Asia conflict.
- **Amul** became the **first Indian FMCG brand** to cross a turnover of **Rs 1 trillion** (in 2025-26).
- **Mundra Port** in Gujarat overtook Chennai and Ennore as India's **largest automobile export port**.
- India's **first private gold mine** is coming up at **Jonnagiri** in **Kurnool district**, Andhra Pradesh.
- On **28 April 2026** the foundation stone was laid for **Google's AI data centre** at **Visakhapatnam**, an investment of about **Rs 1.35 lakh crore**.
- **Oil India Ltd** reported an oil and gas discovery in the **Ghadames Basin** of **Libya**.
- The Ministry of Coal signed agreements for four coal mines that, for the first time, provide for **Underground Coal Gasification**.
