## Tax and customs

- **1 April 2026:** The Income-tax Act, 2025 came into force. It replaced the Income-tax Act, 1961. So the new financial year opened with a new tax law.
  - The new law merges the financial year and the assessment year into a single "Tax Year".
  - Form 130 replaces Form 16.
- **Customs duty:** It was waived on 40 petrochemical products till 30 June 2026. The aim was to soften the effect of the West Asia conflict.

## Banking and payments

- **April 2026:** A week after the new tax law, the RBI's Monetary Policy Committee kept the repo rate unchanged, with a neutral stance. It also lowered its growth forecast for 2026-27. The rates and forecasts are in the table above.
  - The reason was the conflict in West Asia, which had pushed up oil prices.
  - The conflict also pulled the rupee to a record low of about Rs 95 per US dollar.
- **1 April 2027:** New RBI directions on bad loans take effect. They move banks to an Expected Credit Loss framework.
- **Offshore rupee derivatives:** The RBI barred banks from taking part in rupee non-deliverable derivative contracts. The aim was to curb offshore speculation. Later the RBI eased these curbs in part.
- **State Development Loans:** These are bonds issued by State governments. From 2026-27 the RBI began a pilot Benchmark Issuance Strategy for them with nine States, including Bihar.
- **Paytm Payments Bank:** The RBI cancelled its banking licence under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.
- **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (Amendment) Bill:** The Lok Sabha passed it. It adds a creditor-initiated out-of-court process, to be completed in 150 days.
- **UPI (Unified Payments Interface):** It completed 10 years. India accounts for about 49 per cent of the world's real-time payment transactions.

## Industry and startups

- **28 April 2026:** The foundation stone of Google's AI data centre was laid at Visakhapatnam. The investment is about Rs 1.35 lakh crore.
- **Semiconductors:** India notified its first semiconductor fabrication plant (fab), in a special economic zone at Dholera, Gujarat.
- **Startup India Fund of Funds 2.0:** The Government notified it with a corpus of Rs 10,000 crore. It is to be run by SIDBI (Small Industries Development Bank of India).
- **Startups recognised:** A record of over 55,200 startups were recognised in 2025-26. This took the total past 2.23 lakh.
- **Amul:** In 2025-26 it became the first Indian FMCG (fast-moving consumer goods) brand to cross a turnover of Rs 1 trillion.

## Ports and transport

- **Major ports:** They handled a record 915.17 million tonnes of cargo in 2025-26. Deendayal Port handled the most, followed by Paradip.
- **Mundra Port:** This port in Gujarat overtook Chennai and Ennore as India's largest automobile export port.
- **Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool:** The Union Cabinet approved it with a sovereign guarantee of Rs 12,980 crore.
- **Delhi–Dehradun Economic Corridor:** This six-lane expressway of about 210 km was inaugurated. It has a wildlife corridor through Rajaji National Park.

## Energy and mining

- **Hydropower:** The CCEA (Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs) approved about Rs 40,175 crore for two projects in Arunachal Pradesh.
  - Kamala: 1,720 MW, by NHPC.
  - Kalai-II: 1,200 MW, by THDC, on the Lohit river.
- **Underground Coal Gasification:** The Ministry of Coal signed agreements for four coal mines. For the first time, these agreements provide for turning coal into gas underground.
- **Gold:** India's first private gold mine is coming up at Jonnagiri in Kurnool district, Andhra Pradesh.
- **Oil India Ltd:** It reported an oil and gas discovery in the Ghadames Basin of Libya.
