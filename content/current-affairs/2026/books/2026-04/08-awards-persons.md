## Gist
India lost two great artists in April: playback singer **Asha Bhosle**, who died on **12 April 2026**, and photographer **Raghu Rai**, who died on **26 April 2026**.

Among appointments, economist **Ashok Kumar Lahiri** became Vice-Chairperson of NITI Aayog, **Justice Lisa Gill** became the first woman Chief Justice of the Andhra Pradesh High Court, and Apple named **John Ternus** to succeed Tim Cook. **Menaka Guruswamy** entered the Rajya Sabha as India's first openly queer MP.

The awards to remember are the **Lata Deenanath Mangeshkar Award** for Kailash Satyarthi, the **Goldman Environmental Prize** won for the first time by six women, and the **Whitley Award** for Parveen Shaikh. The important days of the month are listed at the end.

## Key facts
- Playback singer **Asha Bhosle** died in Mumbai on **12 April 2026** at the age of **92**.
- Asha Bhosle received the **Dadasaheb Phalke Award** in 2000 and the **Padma Vibhushan** in 2008.
- Photographer **Raghu Rai** died on **26 April 2026** at the age of 83; he was the **first Indian photographer** nominated to **Magnum Photos** (1977).
- **Ashok Kumar Lahiri** was appointed **Vice-Chairperson of NITI Aayog**, succeeding **Suman Bery**.
- **Justice Lisa Gill** became the **first woman Chief Justice** of the **Andhra Pradesh High Court**.
- Senior advocate **Menaka Guruswamy** took oath as a Rajya Sabha member from West Bengal, becoming India's **first openly queer MP**.
- Indian diplomat **Preeti Saran** was re-elected to the UN **Committee on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights (CESCR)**; she is the first Indian to chair it.
- **Apple** announced that **John Ternus** will succeed **Tim Cook** as CEO on 1 September 2026.
- **Kailash Satyarthi** was chosen for the **Lata Deenanath Mangeshkar Award 2026**, presented in **Pune** on **24 April 2026**.
- **Dr Ch. Srinivasa Rao**, Director of **ICAR-NAARM**, received the **9th Prof. M.S. Swaminathan Award**.
- The **Goldman Environmental Prize 2026** went to **six women**, the first all-women group of winners in the prize's history.
- **Parveen Shaikh** won the **Whitley Award 2026** for her work to protect the **Indian Skimmer** along the Ganga.
- The **TIME100** list of 2026 includes actor **Ranbir Kapoor**, **Sundar Pichai** and chef **Vikas Khanna**.
- **Sadhvi Satish Sail** of **Goa** won **Femina Miss India 2026** on 18 April 2026 at **Bhubaneswar**.
- On Civil Services Day the Prime Minister gave the **Prime Minister's Awards for Excellence in Public Administration**.
- **National Maritime Day** is **5 April**; the 63rd was observed in 2026, recalling the 1919 voyage of the **SS Loyalty** from Mumbai to London.
- **World Health Day** is **7 April**; the theme for 2026 was **"Together for Health: Stand with Science"**.
- **14 April 2026** was the **135th birth anniversary** of **Dr B.R. Ambedkar**.
- **National Civil Services Day** is **21 April**; the **18th** was observed in 2026.
- **National Panchayati Raj Day** is **24 April**; the theme for 2026 was **"Sashakt Panchayat, Sarvangeen Vikas"**.
- **World Malaria Day** is **25 April**; the theme for 2026 was **"Driven to End Malaria: Now We Can. Now We Must."**
- The **bicentenary** of the birth of social reformer **Jyotiba Phule** (born 1827) is being observed.
