## Deaths

India lost two great artists in April.

- **12 April 2026:** Playback singer Asha Bhosle died in Mumbai at the age of 92. She had received the Dadasaheb Phalke Award in 2000 and the Padma Vibhushan in 2008.
- **26 April 2026:** Photographer Raghu Rai died at the age of 83. In 1977 he became the first Indian photographer nominated to Magnum Photos.

## Appointments and persons in the news

- **1 September 2026:** Apple announced that John Ternus will succeed Tim Cook as CEO on this date.
- **Ashok Kumar Lahiri:** The economist was appointed Vice-Chairperson of NITI Aayog. He succeeded Suman Bery.
- **Justice Lisa Gill:** She became the first woman Chief Justice of the Andhra Pradesh High Court.
- **Menaka Guruswamy:** The senior advocate took oath as a Rajya Sabha member from West Bengal. She became India's first openly queer MP.
- **Preeti Saran:** The Indian diplomat was re-elected to the UN Committee on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights (CESCR). She is the first Indian to chair it.
- **TIME100:** The 2026 list includes actor Ranbir Kapoor, Sundar Pichai and chef Vikas Khanna.
