## West Asia

The war in West Asia shaped the month.

- **26 April 2026:** The US sanctions waiver for India's work at Iran's Chabahar Port ended. India runs the Shahid Beheshti Terminal there.
- **US–Iran ceasefire:** The United States and Iran agreed to a two-week ceasefire. Pakistan mediated it.
  - Under it, Iran agreed to reopen the Strait of Hormuz.
  - The arrangement is being called the "Islamabad Accord".
- **UAE and OPEC:** The UAE announced its withdrawal from OPEC and OPEC+, with effect from 1 May 2026. This leaves OPEC with 11 active members.

## Bilateral relations

- **12 April 2026:** India rejected attempts to give made-up names to Indian territory. China had created Cenling County in Xinjiang. It is China's third new county there, after Hean and Hekang.
- **New Zealand:** India and New Zealand signed a Free Trade Agreement (FTA).
  - New Zealand committed to invest USD 20 billion in India over 15 years.
  - All Indian exports get duty-free access.
  - India kept dairy out of its tariff cuts.
  - The two sides aim to double trade to USD 5 billion in five years.
- **South Korea:** President Lee Jae Myung paid a state visit to India.
  - India and the Republic of Korea adopted a five-year Strategic Vision 2026–2030.
  - South Korea joined the International Solar Alliance.
  - India joined the Global Green Growth Institute.
- **Austria:** The Federal Chancellor of Austria paid an official visit to India. It was the first visit by an Austrian Chancellor in over four decades. The last was in 1984.
- **Sri Lanka:** Vice-President C.P. Radhakrishnan visited Sri Lanka. It was the first visit by an Indian Vice-President to that country.
  - India extended OCI (Overseas Citizenship of India) eligibility to the fifth and sixth generations of people of Indian origin in Sri Lanka.
  - Sri Lanka thanked India for a relief package of USD 450 million after Cyclone Ditwah.
- **Japan:** The first India–Japan AI Strategic Dialogue was held in Mumbai.
- **Antiquities:** The United States returned 657 antiquities to India, valued at nearly USD 14 million.
- **Swami Vivekananda:** The first life-size statue of him in the United States was unveiled in Seattle.

## Groupings and organisations

- **Arab League:** It approved Nabil Fahmy of Egypt as its next Secretary-General. He will succeed Ahmed Aboul Gheit from 1 July 2026.
- **BRICS:** India hosted the first BRICS Youth Coordination Meeting, under its BRICS chairship of 2026.
- **Indian Ocean Conference:** The 9th conference was held in Mauritius. The India Foundation started the conference in 2016.
- **UN Secretary-General:** António Guterres's second term ends on 31 December 2026. The process to choose his successor is under way.

## Other news

- **Japan's arms exports:** Japan scrapped its "five-category" limit on arms exports. Lethal weapons can now be sold to 17 countries, including India.
- **Operation Wings of Dawn:** Under this operation, Israel flew about 240 members of the Bnei Menashe community from Mizoram to Tel Aviv.
