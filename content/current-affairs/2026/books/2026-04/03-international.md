## Gist
The war in West Asia shaped the month. The **United States and Iran** agreed to a **two-week ceasefire** brokered by **Pakistan**, the US waiver that had protected India's work at **Chabahar** ran out, and the **UAE** announced that it would leave **OPEC**.

India's own diplomacy was busy. It signed a **free trade agreement with New Zealand**, agreed a five-year **Strategic Vision with South Korea** during President **Lee Jae Myung's** visit, received the Chancellor of **Austria** after four decades, and sent its Vice-President to **Sri Lanka** for the first time.

Also worth remembering: India protested a new Chinese county in Xinjiang, the Arab League chose a new Secretary-General, and Japan relaxed its rules on exporting weapons.

## Key facts
- The **United States and Iran** agreed to a **two-week ceasefire** mediated by **Pakistan**; under it Iran agreed to reopen the **Strait of Hormuz**.
- The US–Iran ceasefire arrangement is being called the **"Islamabad Accord"**.
- The US sanctions waiver for India's work at Iran's **Chabahar Port** ended on **26 April 2026**; India runs the **Shahid Beheshti Terminal** there.
- The **UAE** announced its withdrawal from **OPEC and OPEC+** with effect from **1 May 2026**, leaving OPEC with **11** active members.
- India and **New Zealand** signed a **Free Trade Agreement**; New Zealand committed to invest **USD 20 billion** in India over 15 years.
- Under the India–New Zealand FTA all Indian exports get duty-free access, India kept **dairy** out of its tariff cuts, and the two sides aim to double trade to **USD 5 billion** in five years.
- India and the Republic of Korea adopted a **Strategic Vision 2026–2030** during the state visit of South Korean President **Lee Jae Myung**.
- During the visit **South Korea joined the International Solar Alliance** and India joined the **Global Green Growth Institute**.
- The Federal Chancellor of **Austria** paid an official visit to India, the first by an Austrian Chancellor in over four decades (the last was in **1984**).
- Vice-President **C.P. Radhakrishnan** visited **Sri Lanka**, the **first visit by an Indian Vice-President** to that country.
- India extended **OCI** eligibility to the **fifth and sixth generations** of people of Indian origin in Sri Lanka.
- Sri Lanka thanked India for a relief package of **USD 450 million** after **Cyclone Ditwah**.
- China created **Cenling County** in **Xinjiang**, its third new county there after **Hean and Hekang**; on **12 April 2026** India rejected such attempts to give made-up names to Indian territory.
- The Arab League approved **Nabil Fahmy** of **Egypt** as its next Secretary-General, to succeed **Ahmed Aboul Gheit** from **1 July 2026**.
- The **9th Indian Ocean Conference** was held in **Mauritius**; the conference was started in 2016 by the India Foundation.
- **Japan** scrapped its "five-category" limit on arms exports, allowing lethal weapons to be sold to **17 countries**, including India.
- India hosted the **first BRICS Youth Coordination Meeting** under its **BRICS chairship of 2026**.
- The **first India–Japan AI Strategic Dialogue** was held in **Mumbai**.
- Israel flew about **240** members of the **Bnei Menashe** community from **Mizoram** to Tel Aviv under **Operation Wings of Dawn**.
- The **United States returned 657 antiquities** to India, valued at nearly USD 14 million.
- The first **life-size statue of Swami Vivekananda** in the United States was unveiled in **Seattle**.
- **António Guterres's** second term as UN Secretary-General ends on **31 December 2026**, and the process to choose his successor is under way.
