## Rankings and studies

India fared badly in the year's main green ranking.

- **Environmental Performance Index (EPI) 2026:** This index of Yale ranked India 176th of 177 countries, with a score of 22.46.
  - Estonia was first, with 74.79.
  - Laos was last.
- **Monsoon rain:** A study by IITM Pune (Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology) found that about 25 per cent of monsoon rain evaporates in mid-air over the northern Western Ghats.
- **Heat stress:** A study in Scientific Reports estimated an annual loss of about 3.2 million tonnes of milk due to heat stress.

## Courts and rules

- **Ex post facto environmental clearances:** The Supreme Court quashed the 2021 office memorandum that allowed them. Such a clearance is given after the work has begun.
- **Kukrail, Lucknow:** The Supreme Court allowed India's first night safari here. It will be the world's fifth.
- **Western Ghats Ecologically Sensitive Area:** The seventh draft notification on it was issued.
  - It covers 56,825.7 sq km in six States.
  - Karnataka has the largest area (20,668 sq km).
  - Gujarat has the smallest (449 sq km).
- **Paraquat dichloride:** A draft order proposes to ban this weedkiller under Section 27 of the Insecticides Act, 1968.
- **Mizoram University:** Its Natural History Museum became the 21st Designated Repository under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002.

## Wildlife

- **29 July:** On Global Tiger Day, India's tiger count stood at 3,682, in 58 tiger reserves. The day was started in 2010 at St Petersburg.
- **Panna Tiger Reserve:** The Daudhan reservoir of the Ken–Betwa link project will submerge about 4,141 hectares of its core area.
- **Indian Grey Hornbill:** It bred in Gir for the fourth year in a row. It was reintroduced there in 2021 with 40 birds.
- **Spotted deer:** 50 spotted deer were moved from Sasan Gir to the Mandvi range in Surat.
- **White-bellied Heron:** It is Critically Endangered, with fewer than 60 birds left. It is in the news over the 1,200 MW Lohit project in Arunachal Pradesh.
- **Thane Creek Flamingo Sanctuary:** It got India's first AI-powered bird monitoring centre, costing Rs 45 crore.

## Weather, water and climate

- **4 July to 4 August 2026:** A water conservation campaign ran.
- **Cloudburst:** The IMD (India Meteorological Department) defines it as rainfall of 100 mm or more in one hour over an area of 20–30 sq km.
- **Bonn climate talks (SB64):** India spoke on climate tipping points.
  - COP31 will be held at Antalya, Türkiye.
- **Manasbal Lake:** This lake in Ganderbal, Jammu and Kashmir, is India's deepest freshwater lake.

## Floods, fires and accidents

- **Assam:** The monsoon brought Assam its worst floods in over 60 years.
- **Teesta Stage VI:** A tunnel explosion hit this 500 MW project at Namchi, Sikkim.
- **Amazon:** Fires in the Amazon were at their lowest since 1985.
- **Spain:** It declared a national emergency over wildfires.
