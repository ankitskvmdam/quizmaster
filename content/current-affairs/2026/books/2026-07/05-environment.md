## Gist
India fared badly in the year's main green ranking: the **Environmental Performance Index 2026** placed it 176th of 177 countries. On **Global Tiger Day** the country's tiger count was put at 3,682 across 58 reserves.

The Supreme Court struck down the 2021 memorandum that allowed **ex post facto environmental clearances**, and it allowed India's first night safari at **Kukrail** in Lucknow. A seventh draft notification on the **Western Ghats** eco-sensitive area was issued.

The monsoon brought the worst **Assam floods** in over 60 years. Smaller items concern the Gir forests, a heron on the edge of extinction, and a draft ban on the weedkiller **paraquat**.

## Key facts
- Yale's **Environmental Performance Index (EPI) 2026** ranked India **176th of 177** countries with a score of **22.46**; **Estonia** was first (74.79) and **Laos** last.
- On **Global Tiger Day (29 July)** India's tiger count stood at **3,682**, in **58 tiger reserves**; the day was started in **2010** at **St Petersburg**.
- The Supreme Court quashed the **2021 office memorandum** that allowed **ex post facto environmental clearances**.
- The Supreme Court allowed India's **first night safari** at **Kukrail, Lucknow**; it will be the **world's fifth**.
- The **seventh draft** notification on the **Western Ghats Ecologically Sensitive Area** covers **56,825.7 sq km** in **six States**.
- In the Western Ghats draft, **Karnataka** has the largest area (20,668 sq km) and **Gujarat** the smallest (449 sq km).
- The **Daudhan reservoir** of the **Ken–Betwa** link project will submerge about **4,141 hectares** of the core area of **Panna Tiger Reserve**.
- The **IMD** defines a **cloudburst** as rainfall of **100 mm or more in one hour** over an area of 20–30 sq km.
- **Assam** faced its **worst floods in over 60 years**.
- A study by **IITM Pune** found that about **25 per cent** of monsoon rain **evaporates in mid-air** over the northern Western Ghats.
- At the **Bonn** climate talks (**SB64**) India spoke on **climate tipping points**; **COP31** will be held at **Antalya, Türkiye**.
- A study in **Scientific Reports** estimated an annual loss of about **3.2 million tonnes of milk** due to **heat stress**.
- The **Natural History Museum** of **Mizoram University** became the **21st Designated Repository** under the **Biological Diversity Act, 2002**.
- A draft order proposes to ban the weedkiller **paraquat dichloride** under **Section 27** of the **Insecticides Act, 1968**.
- **Manasbal Lake** in **Ganderbal**, Jammu and Kashmir, is India's **deepest freshwater lake**.
- The **Indian Grey Hornbill** bred in **Gir** for the **fourth year in a row**; it was reintroduced there in **2021** with 40 birds.
- **50 spotted deer** were moved from **Sasan Gir** to the **Mandvi range** in Surat.
- The **White-bellied Heron** is **Critically Endangered**, with **fewer than 60** birds left; it is in the news over the **1,200 MW Lohit project** in Arunachal Pradesh.
- **Thane Creek Flamingo Sanctuary** got India's **first AI-powered bird monitoring centre**, costing Rs 45 crore.
- A tunnel explosion hit the **Teesta Stage VI** (500 MW) project at **Namchi, Sikkim**.
- Fires in the **Amazon** were at their **lowest since 1985**.
- **Spain** declared a **national emergency** over **wildfires**.
- A **water conservation campaign** ran from **4 July to 4 August 2026**.
