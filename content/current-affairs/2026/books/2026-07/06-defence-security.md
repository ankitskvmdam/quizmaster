## Army and borders

- **General Dhiraj Seth:** He took over as the 31st Chief of the Army Staff. He set out a roadmap called VIJAY.
  - VIJAY stands for Vigilance, Innovation, Jointness, Atmanirbharta and Yodha First.
- **26 July 2026:** The 27th Kargil Vijay Diwas was observed.
- **Agniveers:** The armed services asked to retain more Agniveers than the 25 per cent cap allows.
- **TRINETRA:** It was integrated with Project SANJAY.
- **Sundarbans:** The BSF (Border Security Force) plans about 90 km of border fencing there.

## Partners and industry

- **Project UNICORN (Unified Complex Radio Antenna):** It is the first India–Japan defence co-development project.
  - It is a mast for the Navy, to be made with BEL (Bharat Electronics Limited).
  - Under the project India is the second Asian recipient, after the Philippines.
- **BrahMos:** Indonesia signed a deal worth USD 200 million for two BrahMos batteries.
- **2025-26:** India's defence production reached Rs 1.78 lakh crore. Defence exports reached Rs 38,424 crore.

## Weapons tests

- **July 2026:** Kusha, India's long-range surface-to-air missile (LR-SAM), had its maiden flight test.
- **Pinaka Long Range Guided Rocket:** It was tested at ITR (Integrated Test Range) Chandipur.
  - Its minimum range of 60 km was validated.
  - Its maximum range is 120 km.
  - It was developed by ARDE (Armament Research and Development Establishment), Pune.

## Navy

- **RIMPAC 2026:** The 30th edition of RIMPAC (Rim of the Pacific) was held off Hawaii with 30 nations. The Indian Navy sent a P-8I aircraft.
  - Its theme was "Partners: Integrated and Prepared".
  - The exercise has been held since 1971.
- **INS Trikand:** It foiled a piracy attempt on the merchant vessel MV Golden Arsenal in the Gulf of Aden.
- **Samudra Pradakshina:** It was the first tri-service all-women circumnavigation, a voyage around the world.
  - The crew set out on 11 September 2025 on IASV Triveni.
  - They sailed about 25,500 nautical miles.
  - The voyage ended at the Gateway of India after 314 days.
