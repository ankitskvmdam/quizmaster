## Gist
**General Dhiraj Seth** took over as the 31st Chief of the Army Staff and set out a roadmap called **VIJAY**. India and Japan began their first joint defence development, **Project UNICORN**, and Indonesia signed for two BrahMos batteries.

New weapons were tested: the long-range guided rocket of the **Pinaka** system and the **Kusha** long-range surface-to-air missile. The Indian Navy took part in **RIMPAC 2026** off Hawaii, and **INS Trikand** foiled a piracy attempt in the Gulf of Aden.

The month ended with the **27th Kargil Vijay Diwas** and the return of **Samudra Pradakshina**, the first all-women tri-service voyage around the world.

## Key facts
- **General Dhiraj Seth** became the **31st Chief of the Army Staff**; his roadmap **VIJAY** stands for Vigilance, Innovation, Jointness, Atmanirbharta and Yodha First.
- **Project UNICORN** (Unified Complex Radio Antenna) is the **first India–Japan defence co-development** project; it is a mast for the Navy, to be made with **BEL**.
- Under Project UNICORN India is the **second Asian recipient**, after the **Philippines**.
- **Indonesia** signed a deal worth **USD 200 million** for **two BrahMos batteries**.
- The **Pinaka Long Range Guided Rocket** was tested at **ITR Chandipur**; its minimum range of **60 km** was validated, and its maximum range is **120 km**.
- The Pinaka rocket was developed by **ARDE, Pune**.
- **Kusha**, India's **long-range surface-to-air missile (LR-SAM)**, had its **maiden flight test** in July 2026.
- **RIMPAC 2026**, the **30th edition**, was held off **Hawaii** with **30 nations**; the Indian Navy sent a **P-8I** aircraft.
- The theme of RIMPAC 2026 was **"Partners: Integrated and Prepared"**; the exercise has been held since **1971**.
- **INS Trikand** foiled a piracy attempt on the merchant vessel **MV Golden Arsenal** in the **Gulf of Aden**.
- The armed services asked to retain more than the **25 per cent cap** on **Agniveers**.
- **TRINETRA** was integrated with **Project SANJAY**.
- The **BSF** plans about **90 km** of border fencing in the **Sundarbans**.
- The **27th Kargil Vijay Diwas** was observed on **26 July 2026**.
- India's **defence production** reached **Rs 1.78 lakh crore** in **2025-26**, and **defence exports** **Rs 38,424 crore**.
- **Samudra Pradakshina**, the **first tri-service all-women circumnavigation**, ended at the **Gateway of India** after **314 days**.
- The Samudra Pradakshina crew sailed about **25,500 nautical miles** on **IASV Triveni**, having set out on **11 September 2025**.
