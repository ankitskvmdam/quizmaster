## Awards

- **72nd National Film Awards:** Other winners:
  - Best Actress: Yami Gautam.
  - Best Actor, shared: Mammootty (Bramayugam) and Kartik Aaryan (Chandu Champion).
  - Best Direction: Rajkumar Periasamy, for Amaran.
  - Most popular film: Kalki 2898 AD.
- **R. Vairamuthu:** He is the third Tamil winner of the Jnanpith, after Akilan and Jayakanthan.
- **Narendra Modi:** He is the second Indian Prime Minister, after Jawaharlal Nehru, to receive the Bintang Adipurna.
- **Hong Wang:** She is only the third woman to win the Fields Medal.
- **ICMR-MINDS:** Its award was given at the 29th National Conference on e-Governance.

## Appointments

- **Varsha Ashok Aglawe:** She became the first woman Director General of the Geological Survey of India, its 54th DG.
  - The Geological Survey of India was set up in 1851.
  - Its headquarters is in Kolkata.
- **Anurag Jain:** This IAS officer of the 1989 batch (Madhya Pradesh cadre) became CEO of NITI Aayog.
- **Harjit Singh Grewal:** He became Chairman of the National Commission for Minorities. The post had been vacant since April 2025.

## Places in news

- **Himachal Pradesh:** It got 8 new GI tags, taking its total to 17. They include Kinnauri Apple and Chamba Metal Art. A GI (geographical indication) tag marks a product from a particular place.
- **Shabdalok:** This Museum of Word at the National Library, Kolkata, is India's first immersive language museum.
