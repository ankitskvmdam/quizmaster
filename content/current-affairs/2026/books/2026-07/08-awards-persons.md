## Gist
The month's main awards were the **72nd National Film Awards**, where **Article 370** was named best feature film, and the **60th Jnanpith Award**, which went to the Tamil writer **R. Vairamuthu**. Abroad, Prime Minister **Narendra Modi** received Indonesia's highest honour, and the **Fields Medals** were announced.

New heads were named for **NITI Aayog**, the **National Commission for Minorities** and the **Geological Survey of India**, where a woman became Director General for the first time.

The month also had several important days, among them **World Population Day** and **World Hepatitis Day**, and the opening of India's first immersive language museum in Kolkata.

## Key facts
- Prime Minister **Narendra Modi** received the **Bintang Adipurna**, **Indonesia's highest honour**; it is his **35th international honour**.
- Modi is the **second Indian Prime Minister**, after **Jawaharlal Nehru**, to receive the Bintang Adipurna.
- The **60th Jnanpith Award** (for 2025) went to the Tamil writer **R. Vairamuthu**, the third Tamil winner after **Akilan** and **Jayakanthan**.
- The **72nd National Film Awards**, for films of **2024**, were announced on **18 July 2026**; **Article 370** won Best Feature Film.
- **Yami Gautam** was named Best Actress; Best Actor was shared by **Mammootty** (Bramayugam) and **Kartik Aaryan** (Chandu Champion).
- **Rajkumar Periasamy** won Best Direction for **Amaran**, and **Kalki 2898 AD** was named the most popular film.
- The **Fields Medal 2026** of the **International Mathematical Union** went to **Yu Deng, John Pardon, Jacob Tsimerman and Hong Wang**.
- **Hong Wang** is only the **third woman** to win the Fields Medal.
- **Ajit Doval** received the **Lokmanya Tilak National Award 2026**.
- **ICMR-MINDS** won a **Gold** award at the **National Awards for e-Governance 2026**, given at the 29th National Conference on e-Governance in **Jaipur**.
- **Harjit Singh Grewal** became Chairman of the **National Commission for Minorities**, a post vacant since April 2025.
- **Anurag Jain**, an IAS officer of the 1989 batch (Madhya Pradesh cadre), became **CEO of NITI Aayog**.
- **Varsha Ashok Aglawe** became the **first woman Director General** of the **Geological Survey of India**, its **54th** DG.
- The Geological Survey of India was set up in **1851** and has its headquarters in **Kolkata**.
- **Himachal Pradesh** got **8 new GI tags**, taking its total to **17**; they include **Kinnauri Apple** and **Chamba Metal Art**.
- **Shabdalok**, the Museum of Word at the **National Library, Kolkata**, is India's **first immersive language museum**.
- **World Zoonoses Day** is observed on **6 July**; the 2026 theme was **"One World, One Health: Prevent Zoonoses"**.
- **World Population Day**, **11 July**, was started by the **UNDP** in **1989**.
- **World Hepatitis Day** is **28 July**; the 2026 theme was **"Hepatitis: Let's Break It Down"**.
- The birth anniversary of **Syama Prasad Mookerjee** falls on **6 July**.
- The birth anniversary of **K. Kamaraj**, **15 July**, is observed as **Education Development Day** in Tamil Nadu.
- **Mangal Pandey** was born on **19 July 1827** in **Ballia**.
