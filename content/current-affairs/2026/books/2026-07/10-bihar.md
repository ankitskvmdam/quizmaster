## Gist
The State Cabinet under Chief Minister **Samrat Choudhary** approved a new prisons law to replace the Prisons Act of 1894, and the **Bihar Special Universities Act, 2026** set up new universities with the Chief Minister as Chancellor. A heli-tourism scheme and an eco-tourism master plan were launched.

Bihar's first river-linking channel was planned in **Sheohar**, and three districts stopped mass drug administration against filariasis after clearing a key survey. A 700-year-old banyan tree in **Munger** was dated as the oldest accurately dated tree of its kind.

In sport, **Jhandu Kumar** of Nalanda won India's first medal at the Commonwealth Games, and **Patna High Court** got an acting Chief Justice after **Meenakshi Madan Rai** retired.

## Key facts
- The State Cabinet on **20 July 2026** approved the **Bihar Prison and Correctional Services Bill, 2026**, replacing the **Prisons Act, 1894**.
- The Cabinet also gave **former Agniveers** a **12.5 per cent sub-quota** in recruitment of **jail warders**.
- Under the **Bihar Special Universities Act, 2026** the **Chief Minister** is the **Chancellor** of the new universities.
- The new universities are the **Shaheed Jubba Sahni** University of Architecture and Civil Engineering (**Muzaffarpur**) and the **Dr Shyama Prasad Mukherjee** University of AI and Computer Science (**Bhagalpur**).
- **Ramdhari Singh Dinkar University** is to come up in **Begusarai**, and **551 Model Schools** are planned.
- The **Belwa–Minapur Link Channel** in **Sheohar** is Bihar's **first river-linking** project, joining the **Bagmati** and the **Burhi Gandak**; it is **68.80 km** long and costs **Rs 130 crore**.
- **Patna High Court** set up **three fast-track courts** for examination offences, at **Patna, Lakhisarai and Purnia**.
- **Justice Sudhir Singh** became acting Chief Justice of Patna High Court under **Article 223**.
- Chief Justice **Meenakshi Madan Rai** retired on **11 July 2026**; she was the **48th** Chief Justice and the **second woman** Chief Justice of the court.
- The **Mukhyamantri Bihar Heli-Tourism Evam Air Tourism Seva Yojana 2026** runs its first phase from **15 July 2026 to 15 January 2027**, run by the **BSTDC**.
- The heli-tourism scheme covers **Valmikinagar**, **Mundeshwari** (Kaimur) and **Rajgir**; a joyride over Patna costs **Rs 2,100**.
- An **Eco-Tourism Master Plan** covers **Valmiki Tiger Reserve**, **Tutla Bhawani**, **Dhua Kund** and **Karkatgarh**.
- A banyan tree about **700 years** old in **Munger** became the **oldest accurately dated** banyan, dated by the **BSIP** using radiocarbon (AMS) methods.
- In the fight against **lymphatic filariasis**, **Araria, Madhepura and Supaul** cleared the first **Transmission Assessment Survey (TAS-1)** and stopped mass drug administration.
- **38 districts** of Bihar are endemic for filariasis; the national target for its elimination is **2027**.
- The **Mithila Multi-Commodity Horticulture Cluster** in **Darbhanga** and **Madhubani**, worth **Rs 100 crore**, covers **15,100 hectares** of banana, mango, litchi and **makhana**.
- The first bulk **sea consignment** of flavoured **makhana** was sent to **Canada** through **Kolkata port**, with help from **APEDA**.
- **Jhandu Kumar** of **Harnaut, Nalanda**, won **bronze** in men's heavyweight **para-powerlifting** (190 kg), **India's first medal** at the 2026 Commonwealth Games.
- **Akash Deep** (Sasaram) and **Mukesh Kumar** (Gopalganj) were appointed **DSPs** under the **"Medal Lao, Naukri Pao"** scheme.
- **Pragati Raj** of **Muzaffarpur** won **gold** in the **64 kg** class at the Sub-Junior Girls National Boxing Championship for the second year running; **Nitya Pandey** of Aurangabad also won gold, and Bihar won **4 medals**.
- The **Mansi–Saharsa** rail doubling project of the **East Central Railway** is **44.40 km** long and costs **Rs 499 crore**.
- **Birpur Airport** in **Supaul** will be developed by the **AAI** at a cost of **Rs 28.42 crore**.
- A permanent campus of the **NSTI (Women)** will be built at **Bihta** for **Rs 99.97 crore**.
- **CSIR** and **Nalanda University** signed an MoU to set up a **CSIR Technology Gallery**.
- In NITI Aayog's **Investment Friendliness Index 2026**, Bihar was among the **bottom performers**.
