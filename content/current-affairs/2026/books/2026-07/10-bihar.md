## Cabinet and laws

- **20 July 2026:** The State Cabinet under Chief Minister Samrat Choudhary approved the Bihar Prison and Correctional Services Bill, 2026. It replaces the Prisons Act, 1894.
  - The Cabinet also gave former Agniveers a 12.5 per cent sub-quota in recruitment of jail warders.
- **Bihar Special Universities Act, 2026:** It set up new universities. The Chief Minister is their Chancellor.
  - Shaheed Jubba Sahni University of Architecture and Civil Engineering, Muzaffarpur.
  - Dr Shyama Prasad Mukherjee University of AI and Computer Science, Bhagalpur.

## Education

- **Ramdhari Singh Dinkar University:** It is to come up in Begusarai.
- **Model Schools:** 551 Model Schools are planned.
- **CSIR Technology Gallery:** CSIR (Council of Scientific and Industrial Research) and Nalanda University signed an MoU to set it up.

## Patna High Court

- **11 July 2026:** Chief Justice Meenakshi Madan Rai retired. She was the 48th Chief Justice of the court and its second woman Chief Justice.
- **Justice Sudhir Singh:** He became acting Chief Justice of Patna High Court under Article 223.
- **Fast-track courts:** The High Court set up three fast-track courts for examination offences, at Patna, Lakhisarai and Purnia.

## Tourism

- **15 July 2026 to 15 January 2027:** The first phase of the Mukhyamantri Bihar Heli-Tourism Evam Air Tourism Seva Yojana 2026 runs in this period. It is run by the BSTDC (Bihar State Tourism Development Corporation).
  - It covers Valmikinagar, Mundeshwari (Kaimur) and Rajgir.
  - A joyride over Patna costs Rs 2,100.
- **Eco-Tourism Master Plan:** It covers Valmiki Tiger Reserve, Tutla Bhawani, Dhua Kund and Karkatgarh.

## Environment and health

- **Munger banyan:** A banyan tree about 700 years old in Munger became the oldest accurately dated banyan.
  - The BSIP (Birbal Sahni Institute of Palaeosciences) dated it using radiocarbon (AMS) methods.
- **Lymphatic filariasis:** Araria, Madhepura and Supaul cleared the first Transmission Assessment Survey (TAS-1). After this, they stopped mass drug administration, that is, giving the medicine to everyone in the area.
  - 38 districts of Bihar are endemic for filariasis, that is, the disease is regularly present there.
  - The national target for its elimination is 2027.

## Sports

- **Jhandu Kumar:** This athlete from Harnaut, Nalanda, won bronze in men's heavyweight para-powerlifting (190 kg). It was India's first medal at the 2026 Commonwealth Games.
- **"Medal Lao, Naukri Pao":** Under this scheme, Akash Deep (Sasaram) and Mukesh Kumar (Gopalganj) were appointed DSPs (Deputy Superintendents of Police).
- **Pragati Raj:** She is from Muzaffarpur. She won gold in the 64 kg class at the Sub-Junior Girls National Boxing Championship, for the second year running.
  - Nitya Pandey of Aurangabad also won gold.
  - Bihar won 4 medals.

## Economy and trade

- **Investment Friendliness Index 2026:** In this NITI Aayog index, Bihar was among the bottom performers.
- **Makhana:** The first bulk sea consignment of flavoured makhana was sent to Canada through Kolkata port, with help from APEDA.
