## Gist
The headline was in space: **Vikram-1**, built by the Hyderabad start-up Skyroot Aerospace, reached orbit, making India the third country where a private company has done so on its own. Astronomers reported a new planet around **Beta Pictoris** and a possible moon around a brown dwarf.

In health, India approved its **first dengue vaccine**, brought cell and gene therapies under central licensing, and joined a global trial on sepsis in newborns. The patent on the weight-loss drug **semaglutide** expired in India.

In energy and transport, the first **hydrogen train** ran in Haryana, the first **geothermal wells** were commissioned in Ladakh, and a commercial jet made its first landing approach guided by the **GAGAN** satellite system.

## Key facts
- **Vikram-1** of **Skyroot Aerospace**, India's **first privately developed orbital rocket**, reached low Earth orbit on its maiden flight, named **Mission Aagaman**.
- With Vikram-1, India became the **third country**, after the **US and China**, where a private company has reached orbit on its own.
- The drug regulator **CDSCO** approved **Qdenga**, India's **first dengue vaccine**, made by **Takeda**; it is tetravalent and meant for ages **4 to 60**.
- The **DGCA** conducted India's first satellite-based landing approach on a commercial jet using **GAGAN**, developed by **ISRO** and the **Airports Authority of India**.
- The Prime Minister flagged off **'NaMo Green Rail'**, India's **first hydrogen-powered train**, on the 89-km **Jind–Sonipat** section in Haryana.
- India's **first geothermal wells**, each 1,000 m deep, were commissioned in **Puga Valley**, Ladakh, by the **ONGC Energy Centre** for a 1 MW pilot plant.
- The **Drugs Rules, 1945** were amended to bring **cell and gene therapy** products and **xenografts** under the Central Licence Approving Authority.
- **JIPMER, Puducherry** became the first Indian centre in the **NeoSep1** trial on **neonatal sepsis**, sponsored by GARDP.
- **Telangana** made **cancer a notifiable disease**; every case must be reported to a State portal within a month.
- Novo Nordisk's patent on **semaglutide** expired in India, opening the way for cheaper generic versions.
- The **Department of Health Research** released the draft **National Health Research Policy 2026**, which aims to raise health research spending to **0.15 per cent of GDP by 2047**.
- **Mission Drishti**, described as the world's first **OptoSAR** satellite, lost communication after a geomagnetic solar storm.
- A **White Rabbit** technology network for spreading **Indian Standard Time** was commissioned at Bengaluru; **CSIR-NPL** is the keeper of IST.
- A consortium of Microsoft, Singtel, Tata Communications and Lightstorm will build the **I-2SEA** submarine cable, landing at **Machilipatnam** and South Chennai.
- The **James Webb Space Telescope** found a hidden gas giant, **Beta Pictoris d**, about **63 light-years** away.
- Astronomers using the **Very Large Telescope** in Chile found signs of the first possible **exomoon around a brown dwarf**.
- NASA selected **PRAXIS**, a concept to collect particles directly from **planetary rings**, for early-stage funding.
- US researchers built **SpudCell**, a **synthetic cell** made wholly from non-living chemicals that can grow and divide.
- **IASST Guwahati** showed the first use of **borophene** as a lubricant additive, in castor oil.
- **Parrot Bornavirus 4** was detected in birds in India for the first time.
- **The ePlane Company**, incubated at **IIT Madras**, unveiled India's first **eVTOL air taxi** prototype.
- **Cyclosporiasis**, which surged in the US, is caused by the parasite ***Cyclospora cayetanensis*** and spreads through contaminated food and water.
