## Space

The headline of the month was in space.

- **Vikram-1:** This rocket of Skyroot Aerospace, a Hyderabad start-up, is India's first privately developed orbital rocket.
  - On its maiden flight, named Mission Aagaman, it reached low Earth orbit.
  - With it, India became the third country, after the US and China, where a private company has reached orbit on its own.
- **Mission Drishti:** It is described as the world's first OptoSAR satellite. It lost communication after a geomagnetic solar storm.
- **Beta Pictoris d:** The James Webb Space Telescope found this hidden gas giant, a new planet around Beta Pictoris. It is about 63 light-years away.
- **Exomoon:** Astronomers using the Very Large Telescope in Chile found signs of the first possible exomoon around a brown dwarf. An exomoon is a moon outside our solar system. A brown dwarf is bigger than a planet but too small to be a star.
- **PRAXIS:** NASA selected this concept for early-stage funding. It would collect particles directly from planetary rings.

## Health

- **Qdenga:** The drug regulator CDSCO (Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation) approved it. It is India's first dengue vaccine.
  - It is made by Takeda.
  - It is tetravalent, that is, it protects against four types of dengue virus.
  - It is meant for ages 4 to 60.
- **Cell and gene therapy:** The Drugs Rules, 1945 were amended to bring cell and gene therapy products and xenografts under the Central Licence Approving Authority. Xenografts are tissues or organs taken from animals.
- **Neonatal sepsis:** JIPMER, Puducherry became the first Indian centre in the NeoSep1 trial on sepsis in newborns. The trial is sponsored by GARDP.
- **Telangana:** The State made cancer a notifiable disease. Every case must be reported to a State portal within a month.
- **Semaglutide:** Novo Nordisk's patent on this weight-loss drug expired in India. This opens the way for cheaper generic versions.
- **National Health Research Policy 2026:** The Department of Health Research released its draft. It aims to raise health research spending to 0.15 per cent of GDP by 2047.
- **Parrot Bornavirus 4:** It was detected in birds in India for the first time.
- **Cyclosporiasis:** It surged in the US. It is caused by the parasite Cyclospora cayetanensis and spreads through contaminated food and water.

## Energy and transport

- **'NaMo Green Rail':** The Prime Minister flagged off India's first hydrogen-powered train on the 89-km Jind–Sonipat section in Haryana.
- **Geothermal wells:** India's first were commissioned in Puga Valley, Ladakh, by the ONGC Energy Centre.
  - Each is 1,000 m deep.
  - They are for a 1 MW pilot plant.
- **GAGAN:** The DGCA (Directorate General of Civil Aviation) conducted India's first satellite-based landing approach on a commercial jet using GAGAN. GAGAN was developed by ISRO and the Airports Authority of India.
- **eVTOL air taxi:** The ePlane Company, incubated at IIT Madras, unveiled India's first eVTOL (electric vertical take-off and landing) air taxi prototype.

## Technology and research

- **Indian Standard Time:** A White Rabbit technology network for spreading IST was commissioned at Bengaluru. CSIR-NPL is the keeper of IST.
- **I-2SEA:** A consortium of Microsoft, Singtel, Tata Communications and Lightstorm will build this submarine cable. It will land at Machilipatnam and South Chennai.
- **SpudCell:** US researchers built this synthetic cell wholly from non-living chemicals. It can grow and divide.
- **Borophene:** IASST Guwahati showed its first use as a lubricant additive, in castor oil.
