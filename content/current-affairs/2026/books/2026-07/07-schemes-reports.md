## Rural jobs and livelihoods

The biggest change of the month was in rural jobs.

- **1 July 2026:** The VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 came into force. It replaced MGNREGA (the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act).
  - It guarantees 125 days of work.
  - The wage floor is Rs 300 a day.
  - Costs are shared between the Centre and States in the ratio 60:40.
- **PM-KISAN:** The Cabinet continued it for five more years, 2026-27 to 2030-31, with an outlay of Rs 3.15 lakh crore.
- **Lakhpati Didis:** The SHE-LEAPS platform was launched. The target of Lakhpati Didis was doubled from 3 crore to 6 crore.

## Industry and infrastructure

- **BHAVYA (Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojana):** The Cabinet approved this scheme of the DPIIT (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade).
  - It will set up 100 industrial parks at a cost of Rs 33,660 crore, from 2026-27 to 2031-32.
  - Under BHAVYA Rasayan, 3 chemical parks will be set up at a cost of Rs 3,030 crore.
- **PM Surya Sarovar Yojana:** It aims at 5,000 MW of floating solar power. Its outlay is Rs 5,070 crore.
- **Viksit UDAN:** The Prime Minister launched it in Rajasthan, for regional air links.
  - It has Rs 28,840 crore for FY27 to FY36.
  - It covers 100 airports and 200 helipads.
- **Amrit Bharat Station Scheme:** The Prime Minister inaugurated 75 rebuilt stations in 20 States. The scheme covers 1,309 stations.

## Cooperation, panchayats and Ayurveda

- **6 July 2026:** The Ministry of Cooperation marked its 5th Foundation Day. Tribhuvan Sahkari University is India's first cooperative university.
- **DAANVEER:** It is a platform of the Ministry of Panchayati Raj, the NIC (National Informatics Centre) and DigiHaat. It is linked to the Meri Panchayat App.
- **Ayurveda:** NITI Aayog released a roadmap for Ayurveda for 2025–2047.
