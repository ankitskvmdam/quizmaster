## Gist
The biggest change of the month was in rural jobs: the **VB-G RAM G Act, 2025** came into force on 1 July 2026 and replaced MGNREGA, raising the guarantee to 125 days. The Cabinet also continued **PM-KISAN** for five more years and approved **BHAVYA**, a scheme for 100 industrial parks.

The Prime Minister launched **Viksit UDAN** for regional air links in Rajasthan and inaugurated 75 stations rebuilt under the **Amrit Bharat Station Scheme**.

Among reports, **NITI Aayog** brought out its first **Investment Friendliness Index**, topped by Gujarat, India was ranked 94th in the UN **Sustainable Development Report**, and the education surveys **AISHE** and **UDISE+** gave fresh figures.

## Key facts
- The **VB-G RAM G Act, 2025** came into force on **1 July 2026**, replacing **MGNREGA**; it guarantees **125 days** of work.
- Under VB-G RAM G the wage floor is **Rs 300 a day**, and costs are shared between the Centre and States in the ratio **60:40**.
- **PM-KISAN** was continued for **2026-27 to 2030-31** with an outlay of **Rs 3.15 lakh crore**.
- **BHAVYA (Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojana)** of the **DPIIT** will set up **100 industrial parks** at a cost of **Rs 33,660 crore** from 2026-27 to 2031-32.
- **BHAVYA Rasayan** will set up **3 chemical parks** at a cost of **Rs 3,030 crore**.
- **PM Surya Sarovar Yojana** aims at **5,000 MW** of **floating solar** power, with an outlay of **Rs 5,070 crore**.
- The Prime Minister launched **Viksit UDAN** in **Rajasthan**; with **Rs 28,840 crore** for FY27 to FY36, it covers **100 airports** and **200 helipads**.
- Under the **Amrit Bharat Station Scheme** the Prime Minister inaugurated **75 stations** in **20 States**; the scheme covers **1,309** stations.
- The **SHE-LEAPS** platform was launched, and the target of **Lakhpati Didis** was doubled from 3 crore to **6 crore**.
- The **Ministry of Cooperation** marked its **5th Foundation Day** on **6 July 2026**; **Tribhuvan Sahkari University** is India's **first cooperative university**.
- **DAANVEER** is a platform of the **Ministry of Panchayati Raj**, the NIC and DigiHaat, linked to the **Meri Panchayat App**.
- NITI Aayog released a **roadmap for Ayurveda** for **2025–2047**.
- NITI Aayog's first **Investment Friendliness Index 2026** was topped by **Gujarat** (56.6), followed by **Maharashtra** and **Tamil Nadu**; it uses **84 indicators** under **8 pillars**.
- The UN **Sustainable Development Report 2026** (SDSN) ranked India **94th of 167** countries with a score of **68.3**; **Finland** was first.
- The **Global Terrorism Index 2026**, the 13th edition by the **Institute for Economics and Peace**, found **Pakistan** the most affected country; India was **13th**.
- In the **Market Distortions Performance Index** of the Competere Foundation, India moved up from **82nd to 57th**.
- In **TRG Datacenters'** ranking of preparedness for **internet blackouts**, India was **9th** and the **US** first.
- The **India Skills Report 2026** put the employability of Indian graduates at **56.4 per cent**.
- **AISHE 2023-24** put the **gross enrolment ratio** in higher education at **30**, with **4.50 crore** students and a gender parity index of **1.08**.
- **UDISE+ 2025-26** counted **1.02 crore** school teachers; **Chandigarh** topped the **Performance Grading Index 2.0** at the Uttam-3 level.
- The Registrar General's **Civil Registration System Report 2024** found registration levels of **99.1 per cent** for births and **99.4 per cent** for deaths.
- The **Digital Threat Report 2025-26**, prepared by MeitY, **CERT-In**, CSIRT-Fin and SISA, warned of an **"AI Asymmetry"**.
