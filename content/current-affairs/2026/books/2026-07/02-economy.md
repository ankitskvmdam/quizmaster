## Gist
Prices were the worry of the month. With crude oil above USD 110 a barrel, **wholesale inflation** rose to **9.87 per cent** in June 2026 and retail inflation to an 18-month high of **4.38 per cent**; the Government raised the import duty on gold and silver to protect the current account.

On trade, the **India–UK CETA** came into force on **15 July 2026**, foreign investment was allowed in inventory-based e-commerce meant only for exports, and imports made with forced labour were banned. The Cabinet cleared big manufacturing packages: **Semicon 2.0** and the **Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme**.

Statistics also changed. **Iron ore** became the ninth core industry in a new series with base year 2022-23, and the Statistics Ministry began a monthly **Index of Services Production**. The World Bank kept India in the lower-middle-income group.

## Key facts
- **Wholesale (WPI) inflation** rose to **9.87 per cent** in June 2026, and **retail (CPI) inflation** to **4.38 per cent**, an 18-month high.
- The Government raised the import duty on **gold and silver** from 6 to **15 per cent** to contain the current account deficit.
- The **India–UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA)** and its **Double Contribution Convention** took effect on **15 July 2026**.
- Under the CETA the UK gives **zero-duty access to about 99 per cent** of India's exports; India kept dairy, apples, edible oils and gold out of its concessions.
- The Union Cabinet approved **Semicon 2.0** with an outlay of **Rs 1,27,500 crore**; India's first chip fabrication plant is expected in **2028**.
- The Cabinet approved the **Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme** with **Rs 62,500 crore** for 2026-27 to 2030-31; mobile phones became India's **largest export product** in 2025-26.
- The Cabinet approved a further **Rs 30,000 crore** for the **National Investment and Infrastructure Fund (NIIF)**, taking the Government's commitment to Rs 60,000 crore.
- In the new **Index of Core Industries** (base year **2022-23**), **iron ore** was added, raising the core industries from **eight to nine**; their weight in the IIP is now **32.88 per cent**.
- The Statistics Ministry (MoSPI) released the first trial **Index of Services Production** for **19 sub-sectors**, with **2024-25** as base year.
- In the World Bank's classification for FY2027 India remains a **lower-middle-income** economy with a GNI per capita of **USD 2,760**; **Sri Lanka** and **Vietnam** moved up to upper-middle income.
- **GST** completed **nine years** in July 2026; taxpayers rose from 66.5 lakh in 2017 to **1.65 crore**, and gross collections reached **Rs 22.27 lakh crore** in 2025-26.
- Foreign portfolio investors are exempt from tax on interest and capital gains from **Government securities**, and the **Fully Accessible Route** now covers new 15-, 30- and 40-year bonds.
- The **Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha Act, 2025** raised the FDI limit in insurance from 74 to **100 per cent**; **LIC** is classed as a **Domestic Systemically Important Insurer**.
- The **DPIIT** allowed FDI in **inventory-based e-commerce** only for **exports** of goods made in India; it stays barred for domestic retail.
- The **DGFT** added para 2.20B to the Foreign Trade Policy 2023, banning imports of goods made with **forced labour**, as defined in **ILO Convention No. 29**.
- The RBI revived its plan for **polymer currency notes**; a pilot may begin with **Rs 10 and Rs 20** notes.
- The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs approved the **National Investment Policy for Urea-2026**, to set up **8–9 new gas-based urea plants**.
- India's first indigenous **EXIM shipping container** was unveiled at the Maersk–CONCOR inland container depot at **Dadri**, Uttar Pradesh.
- India's first **Telecom Manufacturing Zone** is to come up at **Gwalior**, Madhya Pradesh.
- **NABARD** launched **'Gramodyam'** on its **45th Foundation Day** to create about 4,000 rural entrepreneurs in three years.
- The Prime Minister inaugurated the **CG Semi** chip assembly and testing (OSAT) plant at **Sanand**, Gujarat.
- The RBI issued draft guidance asking banks to set up a **Board-level Data Governance Committee**.
