## Prices

Prices were the worry of the month. Crude oil was above USD 110 a barrel.

- **June 2026:** Wholesale (WPI, Wholesale Price Index) inflation rose to 9.87 per cent. Retail (CPI, Consumer Price Index) inflation rose to 4.38 per cent, an 18-month high.
- **Gold and silver:** The Government raised the import duty on them from 6 to 15 per cent. The aim is to contain the current account deficit.

## Trade

- **15 July 2026:** The India–UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA) took effect. Its Double Contribution Convention took effect the same day.
  - The UK gives zero-duty access to about 99 per cent of India's exports.
  - India kept dairy, apples, edible oils and gold out of its concessions.
- **Inventory-based e-commerce:** The DPIIT (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) allowed foreign direct investment (FDI) in it only for exports of goods made in India. It stays barred for domestic retail.
- **Forced labour:** The DGFT (Directorate General of Foreign Trade) added para 2.20B to the Foreign Trade Policy 2023. It bans imports of goods made with forced labour, as defined in ILO Convention No. 29.

## Manufacturing

- **CG Semi:** The Prime Minister inaugurated its chip assembly and testing (OSAT) plant at Sanand, Gujarat.
- **EXIM shipping container:** India's first indigenous one was unveiled at Dadri, Uttar Pradesh, at the Maersk–CONCOR inland container depot.
- **Telecom Manufacturing Zone:** India's first is to come up at Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh.

## Statistics

- **Index of Core Industries:** In its new series (base year 2022-23), iron ore was added.
  - The core industries rose from eight to nine.
  - Their weight in the IIP (Index of Industrial Production) is now 32.88 per cent.
- **Index of Services Production:** The Statistics Ministry (MoSPI) released the first trial index, for 19 sub-sectors. Its base year is 2024-25. It is a monthly index.
- **World Bank classification for FY2027:** India remains a lower-middle-income economy, with a GNI (gross national income) per capita of USD 2,760. Sri Lanka and Vietnam moved up to upper-middle income.
- **GST:** It completed nine years in July 2026.
  - Taxpayers rose from 66.5 lakh in 2017 to 1.65 crore.
  - Gross collections reached Rs 22.27 lakh crore in 2025-26.

## Banking, investment and insurance

- **Government securities:** Foreign portfolio investors are exempt from tax on interest and capital gains from them. The Fully Accessible Route now covers new 15-, 30- and 40-year bonds.
- **Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha Act, 2025:** It raised the FDI limit in insurance from 74 to 100 per cent.
- **LIC:** It is classed as a Domestic Systemically Important Insurer.
- **Polymer currency notes:** The RBI revived its plan for them. A pilot may begin with Rs 10 and Rs 20 notes.
- **Data governance:** The RBI issued draft guidance asking banks to set up a Board-level Data Governance Committee.
- **Gramodyam:** NABARD (National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development) launched it on its 45th Foundation Day. It aims to create about 4,000 rural entrepreneurs in three years.
