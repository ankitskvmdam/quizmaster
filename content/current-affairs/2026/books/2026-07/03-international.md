## At a glance: summits and visits

| Summit or visit | Main outcome |
| --- | --- |

## Summits and visits

July was a month of summits. After the India–Japan summit in New Delhi, the Prime Minister travelled to Indonesia, Australia and New Zealand. The President visited Moldova, North Macedonia and Romania.

- **Japan:** At the summit the two countries signed a memorandum for a USD 10 billion investment package.
  - Japan backed India's full membership of the International Energy Agency.
- **Indonesia:** The two countries named 2026-27 the Tagore-Dewantara Year.
  - Indonesia launched the Indonesia Open Network (ION), modelled on India's ONDC.
  - India will help conserve the Prambanan Temple complex.
- **Australia:** The summit also launched a Maritime Security Collaboration Roadmap.
  - India and Australia put into operation the Administrative Arrangement of their civil nuclear agreement. This opens the way for supply of Australian uranium.
  - Flinders University (Bengaluru) and Victoria University (Gurugram) are to open campuses in India.
  - Australia will host a Gaganyaan tracking terminal on the Cocos (Keeling) Islands.
- **New Zealand:** It was the first visit by an Indian Prime Minister in four decades.
  - The roadmap aims to double trade to NZ$7 billion by 2030.
  - New Zealand joined the Global Biofuels Alliance.
- **North Macedonia:** The President unveiled a bust of Mahatma Gandhi in Skopje.

## United Nations and global bodies

- **UN Security Council:** India is contesting a non-permanent seat for 2028-29 against Tajikistan.
  - With its candidature India proposed the SHANTI framework (Securing Holistic Advancement through Norms, Trust and Integrity), a maritime framework.
  - Its pilot region is the Bay of Bengal.
- **Sarnath:** Sarnath (Uttar Pradesh) became India's 45th UNESCO World Heritage Site. This happened at the 48th session of the World Heritage Committee in Busan, South Korea.
- **WTO Agreement on Fisheries Subsidies:** India became the 123rd WTO member to accept it. It covers marine capture fishing only.
- **BRICS:** The 16th BRICS Health Ministers' Meeting was held in Chandigarh, under India's BRICS chairship of 2026.

## AI governance

- **UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance:** The first was held in Geneva. The second will be in New York in May 2027.
- **World AI Cooperation Organization (WAICO):** China launched it. It is headquartered in Shanghai and has 29 signatory countries.
- **Rome Declaration for an Unarmed and Disarming Peace:** It called for a treaty to keep autonomous AI out of nuclear launch decisions.

## Other world news

- **July 2026:** The US struck Qeshm. Qeshm is the largest island in the Persian Gulf and lies at the mouth of the Strait of Hormuz.
- **India–Mali Forum for Promotion of Exports:** The first was held in Bamako.
- **Helium:** China imposed a temporary ban on helium exports. India imports all its helium, more than half of it from Qatar.
- **US and Saudi Arabia:** They signed a civil nuclear cooperation ('123') agreement for a 30-year partnership.
- **France:** It became the first EU country to ban social media for children under 15.
