## Gist
July was a month of summits. The **16th India–Japan Annual Summit** in New Delhi produced a declaration on economic security and the first AI dialogue between the two countries. The Prime Minister then travelled to **Indonesia**, to **Australia** for the third annual leaders' summit, where the uranium supply arrangement was put into operation, and to **New Zealand**, where ties were raised to a **Strategic Partnership**.

The President made the first visits by an Indian Head of State to **Moldova** and **North Macedonia**, and went on to Romania. India also opened its campaign for a non-permanent seat on the **UN Security Council for 2028-29** and offered a maritime framework called **SHANTI**.

Elsewhere, **Sarnath** became India's 45th World Heritage Site, India joined the WTO's fisheries subsidies agreement, China launched a world AI body from Shanghai, and the US signed a civil nuclear agreement with Saudi Arabia.

## Key facts
- The **16th India–Japan Annual Summit** was held in **New Delhi**; the two sides adopted a **Joint Declaration on Economic Security Cooperation** and held their first **AI Strategic Dialogue**.
- At the summit Japan and India signed a memorandum for a **USD 10 billion** investment package, and Japan backed India's full membership of the **International Energy Agency**.
- During the Prime Minister's state visit to **Indonesia**, the two countries agreed to develop **Sabang Port** jointly and named 2026-27 the **Tagore-Dewantara Year**.
- Indonesia launched the **Indonesia Open Network (ION)**, modelled on India's **ONDC**; India will help conserve the **Prambanan Temple** complex.
- The **3rd India–Australia Annual Leaders' Summit** adopted a **Joint Declaration on Defence and Security Cooperation** and launched a Maritime Security Collaboration Roadmap.
- India and Australia put into operation the Administrative Arrangement of their civil nuclear agreement, opening the way for supply of Australian **uranium**.
- **Flinders University** (Bengaluru) and **Victoria University** (Gurugram) are to open campuses in India; Australia will host a Gaganyaan tracking terminal on the **Cocos (Keeling) Islands**.
- India and **New Zealand** raised their ties to a **Strategic Partnership** and adopted a **Roadmap to 2030**, during the first visit by an Indian Prime Minister in four decades.
- The India–New Zealand roadmap aims to double trade to **NZ$7 billion** by 2030; New Zealand joined the **Global Biofuels Alliance**.
- India is contesting the **UN Security Council** non-permanent seat for **2028-29** against **Tajikistan**.
- With its candidature India proposed the **SHANTI** framework (Securing Holistic Advancement through Norms, Trust and Integrity), with the **Bay of Bengal** as the pilot region.
- The President made the first-ever visits by an Indian Head of State to **Moldova** and **North Macedonia**, and unveiled a bust of Mahatma Gandhi in **Skopje**.
- During the President's visit to **Romania**, 2028 was named the **India–Romania Year of Innovation**.
- **Sarnath** (Uttar Pradesh) became India's **45th UNESCO World Heritage Site** at the 48th session of the World Heritage Committee in **Busan**, South Korea.
- India became the **123rd WTO member** to accept the **Agreement on Fisheries Subsidies**, which covers marine capture fishing only.
- The first **UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance** was held in **Geneva**; the second will be in New York in May 2027.
- China launched the **World AI Cooperation Organization (WAICO)**, headquartered in **Shanghai**, with **29** signatory countries.
- The inaugural **India–Mali Forum for Promotion of Exports** was held in **Bamako**.
- The **16th BRICS Health Ministers' Meeting** was held in **Chandigarh** under India's BRICS chairship of 2026.
- China imposed a temporary ban on **helium** exports; India imports all its helium, more than half of it from **Qatar**.
- The US and **Saudi Arabia** signed a civil nuclear cooperation ('123') agreement for a **30-year** partnership.
- **France** became the first EU country to ban social media for children **under 15**.
- The **Rome Declaration for an Unarmed and Disarming Peace** called for a treaty to keep autonomous AI out of nuclear launch decisions.
- **Qeshm**, struck by the US in July 2026, is the **largest island in the Persian Gulf** and lies at the mouth of the Strait of Hormuz.
