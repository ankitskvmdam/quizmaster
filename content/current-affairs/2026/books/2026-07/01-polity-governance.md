## Gist
Examination reform dominated the month. After protests over the NEET-UG paper leak, the Centre brought the **Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill, 2026** with stiffer jail terms and fines, the Prime Minister announced fast-track courts for paper-leak cases, and a task force under **Nandan Nilekani** was asked to rebuild the testing system.

The Supreme Court was busy on rights and procedure: it limited the use of the **Special Intensive Revision** of electoral rolls to elections, set aside 27 High Court judgments in Assam citizenship cases, ordered a fresh trial in a death-sentence case, and asked States for a policy on early release of old and terminally ill prisoners.

On the federal side, the Home Ministry offered **Ladakh** a tailor-made **Article 371** arrangement instead of the Sixth Schedule, four States settled the old **Sardar Sarovar** cost dispute, and Parliament extended legal protection to **Vande Mataram**.

## Key facts
- The Home Ministry proposed a customised **Article 371** model for **Ladakh**, with an elected UT-level body, instead of the **Sixth Schedule**.
- Ladakh's four demands are **statehood**, Sixth Schedule safeguards, a separate **Public Service Commission** and **two Lok Sabha seats**.
- The Ladakh administration extended the **Autonomous Hill Development Council** framework to all **seven districts** and created 17 new tehsils, taking the total to **32**.
- Ladakh's five new districts, created in April 2026, are **Sham, Nubra, Changthang, Zanskar and Drass**.
- The **Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill, 2026** raises the minimum jail term from **3 to 5 years** and the maximum fine for general offences from Rs 10 lakh to **Rs 50 lakh**.
- A task force chaired by **Nandan Nilekani** was set up to make the National Testing Agency's examinations leak-proof; **Pralhad Joshi** took charge as Union Education Minister.
- The Prime Minister announced **fast-track courts** for paper-leak cases; the Delhi High Court set up a special court under the **Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024**.
- The Supreme Court said the **Special Intensive Revision (SIR)** of electoral rolls is only for electoral purposes and cannot decide **citizenship** or welfare benefits.
- In **Sabitri Dey v. Union of India**, the Supreme Court set aside **27 Gauhati High Court judgments** in Assam citizenship cases and held that **Articles 14 and 21** protect foreigners too.
- In **Abdul Hameed v. State of Rajasthan**, the Supreme Court set aside a death sentence in the **1996 Samleti bus blast** case and ordered a fresh trial because the accused had no effective lawyer.
- On a petition by **NALSA**, the Supreme Court told all States and UTs to frame within **three months** a uniform policy for early release of **elderly and terminally ill prisoners**.
- The Supreme Court stayed the Madras High Court's blanket ban on **cow slaughter** in Tamil Nadu; the High Court had relied on **Article 48**, and animal preservation is **Entry 15 of the State List**.
- The **Allahabad High Court** held that the **Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006** applies to every citizen whatever the religion or personal law.
- The **NCLAT** ruled that the moratorium under the **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code** does not protect proceeds of crime from attachment under the **PMLA**.
- The draft **National Food Security (Amendment) Bill, 2026** changes the Antyodaya Anna Yojana entitlement from 35 kg per household to **7 kg per person**, capped at 35 kg.
- The **Viksit Bharat Shiksha Adhishthan Bill, 2025**, now before a Joint Parliamentary Committee, would replace the **UGC, AICTE and NCTE** with one regulator having three councils.
- The Home Ministry's **Deportation Policy, 2026** requires the nationality of suspected illegal migrants to be verified within **90 days**.
- **Maharashtra, Gujarat, Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh** signed a one-time settlement of the **Sardar Sarovar Project** cost dispute; Gujarat's share rises from 50 to **75 per cent**.
- Internet shutdowns are governed by the **Telecommunications (Temporary Suspension of Services) Rules, 2024**; a suspension order cannot run beyond **15 days**.
- Parliament passed the **Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Bill, 2026**, which extends the protection of the 1971 Act to **Vande Mataram**.
- The **Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C)** of the Home Ministry asked GitHub to remove **BitChat**, a Bluetooth mesh messaging app that works without the internet.
