## Examination reform

Examination reform dominated the month.

- **NEET-UG paper leak:** After protests over the leak, the Centre brought the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill, 2026, with stiffer jail terms and fines.
- **Fast-track courts:** The Prime Minister announced them for paper-leak cases.
  - The Delhi High Court set up a special court under the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024.
- **Task force:** It is chaired by Nandan Nilekani. It was set up to rebuild the testing system and make the National Testing Agency's examinations leak-proof.
- **Pralhad Joshi:** He took charge as Union Education Minister.

## Court rulings

- **Special Intensive Revision (SIR):** The Supreme Court said the SIR of electoral rolls is only for electoral purposes. It cannot decide citizenship or welfare benefits.
- **Sabitri Dey v. Union of India:** The Supreme Court set aside 27 Gauhati High Court judgments in Assam citizenship cases. It held that Articles 14 and 21 protect foreigners too.
- **Abdul Hameed v. State of Rajasthan:** The Supreme Court set aside a death sentence in the 1996 Samleti bus blast case. It ordered a fresh trial because the accused had no effective lawyer.
- **Early release of prisoners:** The National Legal Services Authority (NALSA) filed a petition. On it, the Supreme Court told all States and UTs to frame within three months a uniform policy for early release of elderly and terminally ill prisoners.
- **Cow slaughter:** The Supreme Court stayed the Madras High Court's blanket ban on cow slaughter in Tamil Nadu.
  - The High Court had relied on Article 48.
  - Animal preservation is Entry 15 of the State List.
- **Child marriage:** The Allahabad High Court held that the Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006 applies to every citizen, whatever the religion or personal law.
- **Proceeds of crime:** The National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT) ruled on the moratorium under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. The moratorium does not protect proceeds of crime from attachment under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA).

## Ladakh

- **Article 371 model:** The Home Ministry proposed a customised Article 371 model for Ladakh instead of the Sixth Schedule. It would have an elected UT-level body.
- **Ladakh's four demands:**
  - Statehood.
  - Sixth Schedule safeguards.
  - A separate Public Service Commission.
  - Two Lok Sabha seats.
- **April 2026:** Ladakh got five new districts: Sham, Nubra, Changthang, Zanskar and Drass.
- **Autonomous Hill Development Council:** The Ladakh administration extended its framework to all seven districts.
  - It also created 17 new tehsils, taking the total to 32.

## Centre and States

- **Sardar Sarovar Project:** Maharashtra, Gujarat, Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh signed a one-time settlement of the old dispute over its cost. Gujarat's share rises from 50 to 75 per cent.
- **Deportation Policy, 2026:** Under this Home Ministry policy, the nationality of suspected illegal migrants must be verified within 90 days.

## Internet and cyber security

- **Internet shutdowns:** They are governed by the Telecommunications (Temporary Suspension of Services) Rules, 2024. A suspension order cannot run beyond 15 days.
- **BitChat:** The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) of the Home Ministry asked GitHub to remove it. BitChat is a Bluetooth mesh messaging app that works without the internet.
