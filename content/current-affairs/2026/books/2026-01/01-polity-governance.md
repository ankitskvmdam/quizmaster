## Gist
January 2026 opened with the notification of the first phase of **Census 2027**, India's first digital census and the first since 1931 to count castes. Parliament's clean-up law, the **Repealing and Amending Act, 2025**, removed 71 dead laws from the statute book.

The Supreme Court was busy. It **stayed the UGC's new equity regulations of 2026**, gave a **split verdict on Section 17A** of the Prevention of Corruption Act, and told every State to write a policy for police briefings to the media.

India celebrated its **77th Republic Day** on the theme of 150 years of Vande Mataram and hosted the **28th Conference of Speakers and Presiding Officers of the Commonwealth**.

## Key facts
- The **Repealing and Amending Act, 2025** repealed **71** obsolete laws and amended **four** Acts.
- The same Act replaced the word "prevention" with **"preparation"** in the **Disaster Management Act, 2005**, and "registered post" with speed post in the Civil Procedure Code.
- **Census 2027** will be India's **16th census** and its **first digital census**.
- The house-listing phase of Census 2027 will run from **1 April to 30 September 2026**; the reference date for the population count is **1 March 2027**.
- Census 2027 will include the first nationwide **caste enumeration since 1931**.
- **NATGRID** was linked with the **National Population Register (NPR)**; its analytics tool is called **Gandiva**.
- The **Delhi government** decided to make human **rabies** a notifiable disease under the **Epidemic Diseases Act, 1897**.
- The nation is marking **Somnath Swabhiman Parv (1026–2026)**, 1,000 years after **Mahmud of Ghazni** first attacked the Somnath temple in Gujarat.
- **UIDAI** introduced **"Udai"** as the mascot of Aadhaar.
- The Governor of **Mizoram** extended Governor's Rule in the **Chakma Autonomous District Council** by six months; the council was formed in **1972** under the **Sixth Schedule**.
- The Prime Minister inaugurated the **28th Conference of Speakers and Presiding Officers of the Commonwealth (CSPOC)** in New Delhi; India hosted it after **16 years** (the last time was 2010).
- A new **Ministry of Home Affairs** procedure allows refunds in cyber frauds below **Rs 50,000** without a court order.
- **Praveen Vashista** was appointed **Vigilance Commissioner** in the Central Vigilance Commission.
- The UGC notified the **UGC (Promotion of Equity in Higher Education Institutions) Regulations, 2026**; the **Supreme Court stayed** them and said the **2012 regulations** would apply for now.
- The Supreme Court agreed to examine whether the **Enforcement Directorate** can move High Courts under **Article 226**, on petitions by **Tamil Nadu and Kerala**.
- A two-judge Supreme Court bench gave a **split verdict on Section 17A** of the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988: **Justice K.V. Viswanathan** upheld it and **Justice B.V. Nagarathna** struck it down.
- The **77th Republic Day** (26 January 2026) had the theme **"150 Years of Vande Mataram"**; **30 tableaux** took part in the parade.
- The Election Commission's **ECINET** platform brings more than **40** of its apps into one.
- The Supreme Court directed all States to frame a **police media-briefing policy within three months**.
- The President inaugurated **Granth Kutir** at **Rashtrapati Bhavan**, a collection of about 2,300 books in the **11 classical languages** of India.
