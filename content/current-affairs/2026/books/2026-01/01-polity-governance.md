## Census and identity

- **Census 2027:** The first phase of Census 2027 was notified as January 2026 opened.
  - It will be India's 16th census and its first digital census.
  - The house-listing phase will run from 1 April to 30 September 2026.
  - The reference date for the population count is 1 March 2027.
  - It will include the first nationwide caste enumeration since 1931.
- **NATGRID:** The National Intelligence Grid (NATGRID) was linked with the National Population Register (NPR). Its analytics tool is called Gandiva.
- **Udai:** The Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) introduced "Udai" as the mascot of Aadhaar.

## Laws and rules

- **Repealing and Amending Act, 2025:** This is Parliament's clean-up law, which removes dead laws from the statute book.
  - It repealed 71 obsolete laws and amended four Acts.
  - In the Disaster Management Act, 2005, it replaced the word "prevention" with "preparation".
  - In the Civil Procedure Code, it replaced "registered post" with speed post.
- **Cyber fraud refunds:** A new Ministry of Home Affairs procedure allows refunds in cyber frauds below Rs 50,000 without a court order.
- **Rabies in Delhi:** The Delhi government decided to make human rabies a notifiable disease under the Epidemic Diseases Act, 1897. A notifiable disease is one that must be reported to the authorities.

## Court rulings

- **UGC equity regulations:** The University Grants Commission (UGC) notified the UGC (Promotion of Equity in Higher Education Institutions) Regulations, 2026. The Supreme Court stayed them. It said the 2012 regulations would apply for now.
- **Section 17A:** A two-judge Supreme Court bench gave a split verdict on Section 17A of the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988.
  - Justice K.V. Viswanathan upheld it.
  - Justice B.V. Nagarathna struck it down.
- **Enforcement Directorate:** The Supreme Court agreed to examine whether the Enforcement Directorate can move High Courts under Article 226. The question came on petitions by Tamil Nadu and Kerala.
- **Police briefings:** The Supreme Court directed all States to frame a police media-briefing policy within three months.

## Institutions and appointments

- **Praveen Vashista:** He was appointed Vigilance Commissioner in the Central Vigilance Commission.
- **ECINET:** The Election Commission's ECINET platform brings more than 40 of its apps into one.
- **Chakma Autonomous District Council:** The Governor of Mizoram extended Governor's Rule in the council by six months. The council was formed in 1972 under the Sixth Schedule.

## Events and observances

- **26 January 2026:** India celebrated its 77th Republic Day. The theme was "150 Years of Vande Mataram". 30 tableaux took part in the parade.
- **CSPOC:** The Prime Minister inaugurated the 28th Conference of Speakers and Presiding Officers of the Commonwealth (CSPOC) in New Delhi. India hosted it after 16 years; the last time was 2010.
- **Somnath Swabhiman Parv (1026–2026):** The nation is marking 1,000 years since Mahmud of Ghazni first attacked the Somnath temple in Gujarat.
- **Granth Kutir:** The President inaugurated Granth Kutir at Rashtrapati Bhavan. It is a collection of about 2,300 books in the 11 classical languages of India.
