## Space

- **January 2026, PSLV-C62:** The PSLV-C62 mission failed. It carried EOS-N1 (Anvesha), a satellite for the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO), and 15 smaller satellites.
  - This was the second failure of the workhorse rocket in under a year. The first was C61 (May 2025).
  - Both failures were traced to the rocket's third stage (PS3).
- **Pushpak:** This is the name of ISRO's winged reusable launch vehicle demonstrator.
- **ExoMiner++:** NASA released this open-source AI model. It finds exoplanets, planets around other stars, in data from the Kepler and TESS missions.
- **Wolf Supermoon:** The first full moon of 2026 was a Wolf Supermoon. A supermoon occurs when a full moon is near perigee, its closest point to the Earth.

## Computing, AI and telecom

- **PARAM SHAKTI:** This 3.1 petaflop supercomputer was launched at IIT Madras under the National Supercomputing Mission. It uses RUDRA servers made by the Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC).
- **Shrutlekh:** This is an AI tool for real-time speech-to-text and translation. It works under BHASHINI, the language platform of the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).
- **6 GHz band:** The Department of Telecommunications de-licensed the lower half of the band for indoor use. This opens the way for WiFi 6E and WiFi 7.
- **Cell Broadcast Solution:** The Centre for Development of Telematics (C-DOT) developed this disaster alert system. It sends location-based warnings to mobile phones in 21 Indian languages.

## Health and life sciences

- **BSL-4 laboratory:** The foundation stone of India's first State-funded BSL-4 laboratory was laid at Gandhinagar, Gujarat. It is a high-containment laboratory. It will be the country's second civilian BSL-4 lab, after the National Institute of Virology (NIV), Pune.
- **Nipah virus:** Two suspected Nipah virus infections were reported among health workers in West Bengal. Fruit bats are the natural host of the virus.
- **Bird flu:** An H5N1 (bird flu) outbreak was reported in Kuttanad, Kerala.
- **State Microbe:** Kerala became the first State to declare a State Microbe. It chose Bacillus subtilis.
- **Darwin's bark spider:** This spider of Madagascar makes the toughest known spider silk. A study found that only large adult females produce it.

## Research and discoveries

- **Mpemba effect:** In this effect, hot water can freeze faster than cold. Scientists of the Jawaharlal Nehru Centre for Advanced Scientific Research (JNCASR), Bengaluru, explained it using supercomputer simulations.
- **Deep-sea rare earth mining:** Japan began the world's first experimental deep-sea rare earth mining. The site is near Minami Torishima, at a depth of about 6,000 m. It uses the drilling ship Chikyu.
- **Graviton detector:** Researchers in the United States, at the Stevens Institute of Technology and Yale, are planning the world's first detector for the graviton. The graviton is the supposed particle of gravity.
- **Popocatépetl:** Mexico's national university made the first 3D image of the inside of this volcano.
