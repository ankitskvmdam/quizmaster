## Gist
The month began badly for ISRO: the **PSLV-C62** mission failed, the second failure of the workhorse rocket in under a year, and again because of the third stage. On the ground, India added a new supercomputer, **PARAM SHAKTI**, at IIT Madras and began work on a high-containment **BSL-4 laboratory** in Gujarat.

Health news came from two States: suspected **Nipah** cases in West Bengal and a **bird flu (H5N1)** outbreak in Kerala's Kuttanad.

Abroad, **Japan** started the world's first trial of mining rare earths from the deep sea, and **NASA** released an AI model that hunts for planets around other stars.

## Key facts
- The **PSLV-C62** mission failed in **January 2026**; it carried **EOS-N1 (Anvesha)**, a satellite for **DRDO**, and 15 smaller satellites.
- Both recent PSLV failures, **C61 (May 2025)** and **C62**, were traced to the rocket's **third stage (PS3)**.
- ISRO's winged reusable launch vehicle demonstrator is named **Pushpak**.
- **PARAM SHAKTI**, a **3.1 petaflop** supercomputer, was launched at **IIT Madras** under the **National Supercomputing Mission**; it uses **C-DAC's RUDRA** servers.
- **Shrutlekh** is an AI tool for real-time speech-to-text and translation under **BHASHINI**, the language platform of **MeitY**.
- The foundation stone of India's **first State-funded BSL-4 laboratory** was laid at **Gandhinagar, Gujarat**; it will be the country's second civilian BSL-4 lab after **NIV Pune**.
- Two suspected **Nipah virus** infections were reported among health workers in **West Bengal**; **fruit bats** are the natural host of the virus.
- An **H5N1 (bird flu)** outbreak was reported in **Kuttanad, Kerala**.
- **Kerala** became the first State to declare a **State Microbe**: ***Bacillus subtilis***.
- Scientists of **JNCASR, Bengaluru**, used supercomputer simulations to explain the **Mpemba effect**, in which hot water can freeze faster than cold.
- **Japan** began the world's first experimental **deep-sea rare earth mining**, at a depth of about 6,000 m near **Minami Torishima**, using the drilling ship ***Chikyu***.
- The **Department of Telecommunications** de-licensed the lower half of the **6 GHz band** for indoor use, opening the way for **WiFi 6E and WiFi 7**.
- **C-DOT** developed the **Cell Broadcast Solution**, a disaster alert system that sends location-based warnings to mobile phones in 21 Indian languages.
- **NASA** released **ExoMiner++**, an open-source AI model that finds **exoplanets** in data from the Kepler and TESS missions.
- Researchers in the United States (Stevens Institute of Technology and Yale) are planning the world's first detector for the **graviton**, the supposed particle of gravity.
- **Darwin's bark spider** of **Madagascar** makes the toughest known spider silk; a study found that only large adult females produce it.
- Mexico's national university made the first 3D image of the inside of the **Popocatépetl** volcano.
- The first full moon of 2026 was a **Wolf Supermoon**; a supermoon occurs when a full moon is near **perigee**, its closest point to the Earth.
