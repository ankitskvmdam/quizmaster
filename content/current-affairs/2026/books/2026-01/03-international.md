## Gist
The biggest story for India was Europe. The two top leaders of the European Union were the **chief guests at Republic Day**, and at the **16th India–EU Summit** the next day the two sides announced that talks on the long-pending **India–EU Free Trade Agreement** were concluded. Earlier in the month the German Chancellor and the President of the UAE visited India.

The United States dominated world news: it **seized Venezuela's President Nicolás Maduro** in a military operation, **walked out of 66 international organisations**, and set up a **Board of Peace for Gaza**.

Two treaty-and-organisation facts are sure to be asked: **Bulgaria became the 21st member of the eurozone**, and the **High Seas Treaty (BBNJ Agreement)** came into force.

## Key facts
- On **1 January 2026**, **Bulgaria** adopted the euro and became the **21st member of the eurozone**; its old currency was the **lev**.
- In early January the United States carried out **Operation Absolute Resolve**, capturing Venezuelan President **Nicolás Maduro** and his wife.
- The United States announced its withdrawal from **66 international organisations**, including **31 UN bodies**; the list includes the **UNFCCC, the IPCC, the IUCN and the International Solar Alliance**.
- The **International Solar Alliance** has its headquarters at **Gurugram**; India and **France** launched it in **2015**.
- **Pax Silica** is a **US-led** grouping for a secure silicon and AI supply chain; the US Ambassador said India would be invited to join.
- The German Chancellor's visit marked **25 years** of the India–Germany strategic partnership; **Germany** agreed to **visa-free transit** for Indian passport holders at its airports.
- India rejected China's building work in the **Shaksgam Valley**, which Pakistan handed to China under their **1963** boundary agreement.
- **D.B. Venkatesh Varma** became the **first Indian** to chair the UN Secretary-General's **Advisory Board on Disarmament Matters** (for 2026–27).
- India and **Israel** signed a Joint Ministerial Declaration on **fisheries and aquaculture**.
- The **BBNJ Agreement (High Seas Treaty)** came into force on **17 January 2026**; it was adopted under **UNCLOS** and needed **60 ratifications**.
- The **Board of Peace for Gaza** was set up under **UN Security Council Resolution 2803**; **Donald Trump** is its chair and **Nickolay Mladenov** the High Representative for Gaza.
- During the **UAE President's** visit, India and the UAE set a trade target of **USD 200 billion by 2032**; India's **first data embassy** is to be set up in the UAE.
- The United States announced a **25 per cent tariff** on any country trading with **Iran**.
- India and **Spain** are marking **70 years** of diplomatic ties; 2026 is their **Dual Year of Culture, Tourism and AI**.
- The **6th ASEAN–India Digital Ministers' Meeting** was co-chaired by India and **Vietnam**.
- The chief guests at the **77th Republic Day** were **António Costa**, President of the **European Council**, and **Ursula von der Leyen**, President of the **European Commission**.
- The **16th India–EU Summit** was held on **27 January 2026**.
- India and the EU concluded talks on a **Free Trade Agreement**; the **EU is India's 22nd FTA partner**.
- Under the India–EU FTA, the EU will open **97 per cent** of its tariff lines and India **92.1 per cent** of its own.
- Britain defended its deal to hand the **Chagos Islands** to **Mauritius** while keeping the **Diego Garcia** base on a **99-year lease**.
- **2026** is the **ASEAN–India Year of Maritime Cooperation**.
- **India Energy Week 2026** was held in **Goa**; the **Canada–India Ministerial Energy Dialogue** was relaunched there.
- The **2nd Global Buddhist Summit** was held in **New Delhi**, organised by the **International Buddhist Confederation** with the Ministry of Culture.
