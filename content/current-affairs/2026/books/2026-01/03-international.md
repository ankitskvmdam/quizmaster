## India and Europe

- **German Chancellor's visit:** It marked 25 years of the India–Germany strategic partnership. Germany agreed to visa-free transit for Indian passport holders at its airports.
- **India–EU Summit, 27 January 2026:** This was the 16th India–EU Summit.
  - The day before, the two top leaders of the European Union were the chief guests at the 77th Republic Day: António Costa, President of the European Council, and Ursula von der Leyen, President of the European Commission.
  - At the summit, the two sides announced that talks on the long-pending India–EU Free Trade Agreement (FTA) were concluded.
  - The EU is India's 22nd FTA partner.
  - Under the FTA, the EU will open 97 per cent of its tariff lines. India will open 92.1 per cent of its own.
- **Spain:** India and Spain are marking 70 years of diplomatic ties. 2026 is their Dual Year of Culture, Tourism and AI.

## India and Asia

- **UAE President's visit:** India and the UAE set a trade target of USD 200 billion by 2032. India's first data embassy is to be set up in the UAE.
- **Israel:** India and Israel signed a Joint Ministerial Declaration on fisheries and aquaculture.
- **Shaksgam Valley:** India rejected China's building work in the valley. Pakistan handed it to China under their 1963 boundary agreement.
- **ASEAN:** India and Vietnam co-chaired the 6th ASEAN–India Digital Ministers' Meeting. 2026 is the ASEAN–India Year of Maritime Cooperation.

## The United States

- **Early January:** The United States carried out Operation Absolute Resolve, a military operation. In it, the US seized Venezuela's President Nicolás Maduro and his wife.
- **Walk-out from 66 international organisations:** The United States announced its withdrawal from 66 international organisations, including 31 UN bodies.
  - The list includes the UNFCCC, the IPCC, the IUCN and the International Solar Alliance.
  - The International Solar Alliance has its headquarters at Gurugram. India and France launched it in 2015.
- **Board of Peace for Gaza:** It was set up under UN Security Council Resolution 2803. Donald Trump is its chair. Nickolay Mladenov is the High Representative for Gaza.
- **Iran tariff:** The United States announced a 25 per cent tariff on any country trading with Iran.
- **Pax Silica:** This is a US-led grouping for a secure silicon and AI supply chain. The US Ambassador said India would be invited to join.

## Treaties, bodies and meetings

- **1 January 2026:** Bulgaria adopted the euro and became the 21st member of the eurozone. Its old currency was the lev.
- **17 January 2026:** The High Seas Treaty, or BBNJ (Biodiversity Beyond National Jurisdiction) Agreement, came into force. It was adopted under UNCLOS, the UN Convention on the Law of the Sea. It needed 60 ratifications.
- **D.B. Venkatesh Varma:** He became the first Indian to chair the UN Secretary-General's Advisory Board on Disarmament Matters, for 2026–27.
- **Chagos Islands:** Britain defended its deal to hand the islands to Mauritius. Under the deal it keeps the Diego Garcia base on a 99-year lease.
- **India Energy Week 2026:** It was held in Goa. The Canada–India Ministerial Energy Dialogue was relaunched there.
- **Global Buddhist Summit:** The second Global Buddhist Summit was held in New Delhi. The International Buddhist Confederation organised it with the Ministry of Culture.
