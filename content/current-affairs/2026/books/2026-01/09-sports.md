## Cricket

- **Vijay Hazare Trophy:** This is India's national one-day (List A) cricket championship. In the final, Atharva Taide scored 128 for Vidarbha.
- **ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026:** India and Sri Lanka co-host it. It has 20 teams.
  - Bangladesh declined to send its team to India.
  - The ICC replaced Bangladesh with Scotland.
- **BCCI sponsorship:** The BCCI signed a three-year sponsorship deal worth Rs 270 crore with Google Gemini ahead of IPL 2026.

## Badminton and tennis

- **18 January 2026:** The India Open 2026 final was played in New Delhi. The India Open is a BWF World Tour Super 750 tournament.
- **31 January 2026:** Elena Rybakina won the Australian Open 2026 women's singles. It was her second Grand Slam title.
- **Novak Djokovic:** At the Australian Open 2026, he became the first player to win 400 Grand Slam singles matches.

## Winter sports

- **Sixth Khelo India Winter Games:** The Games opened at the Nawang Dorjan Stobdan (NDS) Stadium in Leh, Ladakh.
  - Figure skating was included for the first time.
  - The Games have two legs: ice events at Leh and snow events at Gulmarg.

## Coaches and honours

- **Amelia Valverde:** The All India Football Federation (AIFF) appointed her head coach of the Indian senior women's football team. She is from Costa Rica.
- **Padma Shri 2026:** Cricketers Rohit Sharma and Harmanpreet Kaur and hockey goalkeeper Savita Punia were named for it.
- **Padma Bhushan 2026:** Former tennis player Vijay Amritraj was named for it.
