## Gist
In domestic cricket, **Vidarbha** won the **Vijay Hazare Trophy** for the first time. Ahead of the **Men's T20 World Cup 2026**, which India and Sri Lanka host, the ICC replaced **Bangladesh** with **Scotland** after Bangladesh refused to travel to India.

Delhi hosted the **India Open** badminton tournament, where the singles titles went to **Lin Chun-Yi** and **An Se Young**. At the **Australian Open**, **Elena Rybakina** won the women's title and **Novak Djokovic** reached a record 400 match wins at the Grand Slams.

The **sixth Khelo India Winter Games** opened in Leh, and Indian women's football got a new head coach. Three sportspersons were named for the Padma Shri and one for the Padma Bhushan.

## Key facts
- **Vidarbha** won their **first Vijay Hazare Trophy** (2025-26), beating **Saurashtra** by **38 runs** in the final at **Bengaluru**.
- **Atharva Taide** scored **128** for Vidarbha in the Vijay Hazare Trophy final.
- The **Vijay Hazare Trophy** is India's national **one-day (List A)** cricket championship.
- **Scotland** replaced **Bangladesh** in the **ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026**, after Bangladesh declined to send its team to India.
- The **ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026** is co-hosted by **India and Sri Lanka** and has **20 teams**.
- At the **India Open 2026** badminton tournament in **New Delhi** (final on **18 January 2026**), **Lin Chun-Yi** of **Chinese Taipei** won the men's singles, beating Jonatan Christie of Indonesia.
- **An Se Young** of **South Korea** won the women's singles at the India Open 2026, retaining her title.
- The India Open is a **BWF World Tour Super 750** tournament.
- **Elena Rybakina** won the **Australian Open 2026 women's singles** on **31 January 2026**, beating world No. 1 **Aryna Sabalenka** in three sets; it was her **second** Grand Slam title.
- At the Australian Open 2026, **Novak Djokovic** became the **first player to win 400 Grand Slam singles matches**.
- The **sixth Khelo India Winter Games** opened at the **Nawang Dorjan Stobdan (NDS) Stadium** in **Leh, Ladakh**.
- **Figure skating** was included in the Khelo India Winter Games for the first time; the Games have two legs, ice events at **Leh** and snow events at **Gulmarg**.
- **Amelia Valverde** of **Costa Rica** was appointed head coach of the **Indian senior women's football team** by the AIFF.
- The **BCCI** signed a three-year sponsorship deal worth **Rs 270 crore** with **Google Gemini** ahead of IPL 2026.
- Cricketers **Rohit Sharma** and **Harmanpreet Kaur** and hockey goalkeeper **Savita Punia** were named for the **Padma Shri 2026**.
- Former tennis player **Vijay Amritraj** was named for the **Padma Bhushan 2026**.
