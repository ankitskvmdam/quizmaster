## Rules and rulings

- **Aravalli hills:** In November 2025 the Supreme Court had accepted a 100-metre height test for defining the Aravalli hills. It has now kept that judgment in abeyance. There were fears that most hills would lose protection.
- **Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026:** The Environment Ministry notified these rules. They replace the rules of 2016 and come into force on 1 April 2026.
  - Waste must be separated at source into four streams: wet, dry, sanitary and special care.

## Wetlands and grasslands

- **Ramsar sites:** India's Ramsar wetlands rose to 98 with two additions.
  - Patna Bird Sanctuary in Etah district, Uttar Pradesh.
  - Chhari-Dhand in Kutch, Gujarat.
- **International Year of Rangelands and Pastoralists:** The UN has declared 2026 the International Year of Rangelands and Pastoralists. The Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) leads it.
- **Banni grassland:** This grassland in Kutch, Gujarat, is the largest grassland in Asia.

## Species in the news

- **Galaxy frog:** The galaxy frog (Melanobatrachus indicus) lives only in the southern Western Ghats. The IUCN lists it as Vulnerable.
- **White-bellied heron:** It is Critically Endangered. Fewer than 60 survive in the wild.
- **Olive ridley turtle:** Gahirmatha in Odisha is India's largest mass nesting site of the turtle. Tamil Nadu began a two-year study that tracks the turtles with satellite tags.
- **Citrine wagtail:** It is the first winter migratory bird to reach the wetlands of Assam. Assam lies on the Central Asian Flyway, a bird migration route.
- **Gegeneophis valmiki:** This new legless amphibian (caecilian) was found in Satara district, Maharashtra, in the northern Western Ghats.
- **Stingless bees:** Satipo municipality in Peru gave legal rights to native stingless bees. They are the first insects in the world to have such rights.
- **Striped Tiger:** It was declared the State Butterfly of Chandigarh.
- **Dugong:** An International Dugong Conservation Centre is proposed at Manora in Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu.
- **"Sikkim Sundari":** This is the Himalayan plant Rheum nobile. It flowers only once in its life.

## Projects in the Northeast

- **Kalai-II:** This hydropower project (1,200 MW) is on the Lohit River in Anjaw district, Arunachal Pradesh. THDC India is building it.
- **Kamala:** This hydroelectric project (1,720 MW) is in Kamle district, Arunachal Pradesh, on a tributary of the Subansiri.
- **Kaziranga Elevated Corridor:** The Prime Minister laid its foundation stone. It is about 34.5 km long, on NH-715 in Assam.
- **Sunrise Festival:** The first Sunrise Festival was held at Dong village in Anjaw district, Arunachal Pradesh. Dong is the place in India that sees the sunrise first.

## Climate and land

- **Ice Memory Sanctuary:** The world's first store of mountain ice cores was opened at Concordia Station in Antarctica.
- **Sinking deltas:** A study of 40 river deltas found the Brahmani and Mahanadi deltas among the fastest sinking. Pumping out groundwater is the main cause.
- **Kondagai Lake:** Mud from this lake in Sivaganga district, Tamil Nadu, near Keeladi, gave a climate record of the last 4,500 years.
- **Valley of Flowers:** A forest fire broke out in winter in the Valley of Flowers National Park in Chamoli, Uttarakhand. It came during a season of very little snow in the western Himalaya.
