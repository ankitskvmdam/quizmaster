## Gist
Two announcements stand out. India's list of **Ramsar wetlands grew to 98** with one site each in Uttar Pradesh and Gujarat, and the Environment Ministry notified new **Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026**, which take effect on 1 April 2026.

The Supreme Court put on hold its own November 2025 ruling on how the **Aravalli hills** are to be defined, after fears that most hills would lose protection. The United Nations is observing **2026 as the International Year of Rangelands and Pastoralists**.

Species in the news are a favourite of examiners. This month they were the galaxy frog, the white-bellied heron, the olive ridley turtle, a new legless amphibian from Maharashtra, and the stingless bees of Peru, the first insects in the world to be given legal rights.

## Key facts
- India's **Ramsar sites rose to 98** with the addition of **Patna Bird Sanctuary** in **Etah district, Uttar Pradesh**, and **Chhari-Dhand** in **Kutch, Gujarat**.
- The **Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026** replace the rules of 2016 and come into force on **1 April 2026**.
- The new waste rules require waste to be separated at source into **four streams**: wet, dry, sanitary and special care.
- The Supreme Court kept in abeyance its **November 2025** judgment that had accepted a **100-metre height** test for defining the **Aravalli hills**.
- The UN has declared **2026** the **International Year of Rangelands and Pastoralists**; the **FAO** leads it.
- The **Banni grassland** in **Kutch, Gujarat**, is the largest grassland in Asia.
- **Satipo** municipality in **Peru** gave legal rights to native **stingless bees**, making them the first insects in the world to have such rights.
- The **galaxy frog** (*Melanobatrachus indicus*) lives only in the southern **Western Ghats**; the IUCN lists it as **Vulnerable**.
- The **white-bellied heron** is **Critically Endangered**; fewer than 60 survive in the wild.
- The **Kalai-II** hydropower project (1,200 MW) is on the **Lohit River** in **Anjaw district, Arunachal Pradesh**; **THDC India** is building it.
- The **Kamala** hydroelectric project (1,720 MW) is in **Kamle district, Arunachal Pradesh**, on a tributary of the **Subansiri**.
- **Gahirmatha** in **Odisha** is India's largest mass nesting site of the **olive ridley turtle**; **Tamil Nadu** began a two-year study that tracks the turtles with satellite tags.
- The **citrine wagtail** is the first winter migratory bird to reach the wetlands of **Assam**, which lies on the **Central Asian Flyway**.
- The Prime Minister laid the foundation stone of the **Kaziranga Elevated Corridor**, about **34.5 km** long, on **NH-715** in Assam.
- The **Ice Memory Sanctuary**, the world's first store of mountain ice cores, was opened at **Concordia Station** in **Antarctica**.
- A study of 40 river deltas found the **Brahmani** and **Mahanadi** deltas among the fastest sinking; pumping out **groundwater** is the main cause.
- A new legless amphibian (caecilian), ***Gegeneophis valmiki***, was found in **Satara district, Maharashtra**, in the northern Western Ghats.
- The **Striped Tiger** was declared the **State Butterfly of Chandigarh**.
- An **International Dugong Conservation Centre** is proposed at **Manora** in **Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu**.
- **"Sikkim Sundari"** is the Himalayan plant ***Rheum nobile***, which flowers only once in its life.
- The first **Sunrise Festival** was held at **Dong** village in **Anjaw district, Arunachal Pradesh**, the place in India that sees the sunrise first.
- Mud from **Kondagai Lake** in **Sivaganga district, Tamil Nadu**, near Keeladi, gave a climate record of the last **4,500 years**.
- A forest fire broke out in winter in the **Valley of Flowers National Park** in **Chamoli, Uttarakhand**, during a season of very little snow in the western Himalaya.
