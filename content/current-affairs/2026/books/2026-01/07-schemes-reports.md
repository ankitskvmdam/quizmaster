## Schemes and campaigns

For each scheme, remember the Ministry behind it.

- **1 January 2026:** The Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) made star labelling compulsory for more household appliances, among them refrigerators, televisions and LPG stoves. BEE works under the Ministry of Power.
- **Bal Vivah Mukt Bharat Abhiyan:** This campaign of the Ministry of Women and Child Development was launched in 2024.
  - It marked one year with a 100-day awareness drive.
  - Balod in Chhattisgarh is India's first child-marriage-free district.
- **PANKHUDI:** This portal of the Ministry of Women and Child Development is a single window for CSR (corporate social responsibility) and partnership support to women and child development.
- **VB-G RAM G Act, 2025:** It raises the legal guarantee of rural employment to 125 days a year.
- **Tex-RAMPS:** Under this scheme, the Ministry of Textiles signed MoUs (memorandums of understanding) with 15 States at a conference in Guwahati.
- **Kaladi:** This GI-tagged dairy product of Udhampur (Jammu and Kashmir) is to be promoted under One District One Product. A GI (geographical indication) tag marks a product from a particular place.

## Jobs, skills and education

- **Adult Skill Assessment Survey:** The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) will conduct India's first nationwide Adult Skill Assessment Survey in 2026. The Skill Development Ministry asked for it.
- **India Skills Accelerator:** The Skill Development Ministry signed an MoU with the World Economic Forum for it.
- **SPREE:** About 1.03 crore workers were newly enrolled under this one-time registration window of the Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC).
- **Internationalisation of higher education:** NITI Aayog's report on bringing foreign students and teachers to Indian universities proposes two things.
  - Vishwa Bandhu scholarships.
  - A research fund of USD 10 billion called Bharat Vidya Kosh.
- **National Model Youth Gram Sabha Awards 2025-26:** JNV Una (Himachal Pradesh) won the first prize among Navodaya schools. EMRS Kosambuda (Chhattisgarh) won it among Eklavya schools.
