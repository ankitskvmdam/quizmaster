## Gist
January is the season of global reports. The World Economic Forum's **Global Risks Report 2026** named **geoeconomic confrontation** the world's top risk, the ILO reported on jobs, and UNEP counted how much money flows into activities that harm nature. At home, NITI Aayog released its **Export Preparedness Index** and a report on bringing foreign students and teachers to Indian universities.

Among schemes, the campaign against child marriage completed a year, the Women and Child Development Ministry opened the **PANKHUDI** portal, and the Textiles Ministry signed up States for **Tex-RAMPS**. Star labels for energy efficiency became compulsory on more household appliances from 1 January.

For each report, remember who publishes it; for each scheme, the Ministry behind it.

## Key facts
- From **1 January 2026**, the **Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE)** made **star labelling** compulsory for more appliances, among them refrigerators, televisions and LPG stoves; BEE works under the **Ministry of Power**.
- The **VB-G RAM G Act, 2025** raises the legal guarantee of rural employment to **125 days** a year.
- **MoSPI** will conduct India's first nationwide **Adult Skill Assessment Survey** in 2026, at the request of the Skill Development Ministry.
- NITI Aayog's report on the **internationalisation of higher education** proposes **Vishwa Bandhu** scholarships and a research fund of USD 10 billion called **Bharat Vidya Kosh**.
- The **Bal Vivah Mukt Bharat Abhiyan** of the **Ministry of Women and Child Development**, launched in **2024**, marked one year with a 100-day awareness drive.
- **Balod** in **Chhattisgarh** is India's first **child-marriage-free district**.
- The **PANKHUDI** portal of the **Ministry of Women and Child Development** is a single window for CSR and partnership support to women and child development.
- The **Responsible Nations Index**, covering **154 countries**, was launched by the **World Intellectual Foundation**.
- Under the **Tex-RAMPS** scheme, the **Ministry of Textiles** signed MoUs with **15 States** at a conference in **Guwahati**.
- The **Global Risks Report 2026** of the **World Economic Forum** ranked **geoeconomic confrontation** as the most severe global risk for 2026.
- The same report named **cybersecurity** as the top risk for **India**.
- In NITI Aayog's **Export Preparedness Index 2024**, **Maharashtra** ranked first among large States, followed by **Tamil Nadu** and **Gujarat**.
- Under **SPREE**, a one-time registration window of the **ESIC**, about **1.03 crore** workers were newly enrolled.
- The **ILO's** *Employment and Social Trends 2026* report put global unemployment at **4.9 per cent** in 2025.
- **Kaladi**, a GI-tagged dairy product of **Udhampur (Jammu and Kashmir)**, is to be promoted under **One District One Product**.
- *The Lancet* published a commission report titled **"A Citizen-Centred Health System for India"**.
- The **Skill Development Ministry** signed an MoU with the **World Economic Forum** for the **India Skills Accelerator**.
- UNEP's **State of Finance for Nature 2026** found that finance harming nature reached **USD 7.3 trillion** in 2023.
- India ranked **71st** among 118 countries in the World Economic Forum's **Energy Transition Index 2025**.
- In the **National Model Youth Gram Sabha Awards 2025-26**, **JNV Una (Himachal Pradesh)** won the first prize among Navodaya schools and **EMRS Kosambuda (Chhattisgarh)** among Eklavya schools.
