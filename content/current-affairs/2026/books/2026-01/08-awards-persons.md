## Gist
Republic Day brings the year's biggest honours lists. The **Padma Awards 2026** named **131** people, with the Padma Vibhushan going to five, among them the actor Dharmendra and former Kerala Chief Minister V.S. Achuthanandan, both after their death. The **Ashoka Chakra**, the highest gallantry award of peacetime, went to the astronaut **Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla**.

Other awards of the month were the **Indira Gandhi Peace Prize** for Graça Machel and the **Subhash Chandra Bose Aapda Prabandhan Puraskar** for disaster management. The country lost the ecologist **Madhav Gadgil**, remembered for his report on the Western Ghats.

January is also full of important days, from World Braille Day on the 4th to Martyrs' Day on the 30th. The New Delhi World Book Fair gave the month its book news.

## Key facts
- The **Padma Awards 2026** went to **131** people: **5 Padma Vibhushan, 13 Padma Bhushan and 113 Padma Shri**.
- The **Padma Vibhushan 2026** went to **Dharmendra** (posthumous), **V.S. Achuthanandan** (posthumous), **K.T. Thomas**, **N. Rajam** and **P. Narayanan**.
- The **Padma Bhushan 2026** list includes **Mammootty**, **Alka Yagnik**, **Uday Kotak** and **Shibu Soren** (posthumous).
- The **Ashoka Chakra** was awarded to **Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla**, the first Indian to visit the **International Space Station**.
- On the eve of Republic Day 2026 the President approved **70 gallantry awards**, including **one Ashoka Chakra, three Kirti Chakras and 13 Shaurya Chakras**.
- The **Kirti Chakra** went to **Major Arshdeep Singh**, **Naib Subedar Doleshwar Subba** and **Group Captain Prashanth Balakrishnan Nair**.
- The **Indira Gandhi Peace Prize for 2025** was awarded to **Graça Machel** of **Mozambique**.
- The **Subhash Chandra Bose Aapda Prabandhan Puraskar 2026** went to the **Sikkim State Disaster Management Authority** (institution) and **Lt Col Seeta Ashok Shelke** (individual).
- The Aapda Prabandhan Puraskar is announced on **23 January (Parakram Diwas)**, the birth anniversary of **Subhas Chandra Bose**; 2026 was his **129th**.
- **Maharashtra** won the first prize for the best tableau at the Republic Day parade of 2026; the **Indian Navy** was the best marching contingent among the services.
- Ecologist **Madhav Gadgil** died in January 2026; he chaired the **Western Ghats Ecology Expert Panel** (2010), known as the **Gadgil Committee**.
- The **New Delhi World Book Fair 2026**, organised by the **National Book Trust**, had the theme **"Indian Military History: Valour and Wisdom @75"**; **Qatar** was the Focus Country.
- **Tamil Nadu** announced a new annual literary award, the **Semmozhi Illakiya Virudhu**.
- **World Braille Day** is observed on **4 January** and **World Hindi Day** on **10 January**.
- **National Youth Day** is observed on **12 January**, the birth anniversary of **Swami Vivekananda**.
- **National Girl Child Day** is observed on **24 January**; the Ministry of Women and Child Development started it in **2008**.
- **National Voters' Day** is observed on **25 January**; the theme for 2026 was **"My India, My Vote"**.
- **Martyrs' Day (Shaheed Diwas)** is observed on **30 January**; 2026 was the **78th** death anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi.
- **Lokpal Foundation Day** is **16 January** and **International Data Privacy Day** is **28 January**.
- **19 January 2026** was the birth centenary of **Parbati Giri**, freedom fighter of **Odisha**, known as "Banhi Kanya".
- The **National Commission for Women** marked its **34th** Foundation Day on **31 January**; it was set up in **1992**.
