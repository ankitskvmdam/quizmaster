## Padma Awards 2026

Republic Day brings the year's biggest honours lists. The Padma Awards 2026 went to 131 people: 5 Padma Vibhushan, 13 Padma Bhushan and 113 Padma Shri.

- **Padma Vibhushan 2026:** It went to Dharmendra (posthumous), V.S. Achuthanandan (posthumous), K.T. Thomas, N. Rajam and P. Narayanan. Dharmendra was an actor. Achuthanandan was a former Chief Minister of Kerala.
- **Padma Bhushan 2026:** The list includes Mammootty, Alka Yagnik, Uday Kotak and Shibu Soren (posthumous).

## Gallantry awards

- **70 gallantry awards:** On the eve of Republic Day 2026 the President approved 70 gallantry awards. They included one Ashoka Chakra, three Kirti Chakras and 13 Shaurya Chakras.
- **Ashoka Chakra:** It is the highest gallantry award of peacetime. It went to the astronaut Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla, the first Indian to visit the International Space Station.
- **Kirti Chakra:** It went to Major Arshdeep Singh, Naib Subedar Doleshwar Subba and Group Captain Prashanth Balakrishnan Nair.

## Other awards

- **Indira Gandhi Peace Prize for 2025:** It was awarded to Graça Machel of Mozambique.
- **Subhash Chandra Bose Aapda Prabandhan Puraskar 2026:** This award is for disaster management. It is announced on 23 January, Parakram Diwas.
  - Institution: the Sikkim State Disaster Management Authority.
  - Individual: Lt Col Seeta Ashok Shelke.
- **Republic Day parade 2026:** Maharashtra won the first prize for the best tableau. The Indian Navy was the best marching contingent among the services.
- **Semmozhi Illakiya Virudhu:** Tamil Nadu announced this new annual literary award.

## Persons in the news

- **19 January 2026:** It was the birth centenary of Parbati Giri, freedom fighter of Odisha, known as "Banhi Kanya".
- **January 2026, Madhav Gadgil:** The ecologist died. He chaired the Western Ghats Ecology Expert Panel (2010), known as the Gadgil Committee. He is remembered for its report on the Western Ghats.

## Books

- **New Delhi World Book Fair 2026:** The National Book Trust organised it. The theme was "Indian Military History: Valour and Wisdom @75". Qatar was the Focus Country.
