## Gist
The month's headline numbers came from two documents. The **First Advance Estimates** put India's real GDP growth for 2025-26 at **7.4 per cent**, and the **Economic Survey 2025-26**, tabled at the end of January, projected **6.8 to 7.2 per cent** for 2026-27. India is now counted as the **world's fourth-largest economy**, ahead of Japan.

The Reserve Bank's reports showed banks in good health, with bad loans at a multi-decade low, while warning about the growing deficits of the States. Foreign direct investment rose, and the labour force participation rate touched a record.

Smaller items worth remembering are the committees set up by the pension regulator, the ten years of **Startup India**, the Supreme Court's tax ruling against **Tiger Global**, and coking coal joining the list of critical minerals.

## Key facts
- India became the **world's fourth-largest economy** with a nominal GDP of about **USD 4.18 trillion**, overtaking **Japan**.
- The **First Advance Estimates** for **2025-26**, released by the **National Statistics Office (MoSPI)**, put real GDP growth at **7.4 per cent** (6.5 per cent in 2024-25).
- The same estimates put **nominal GDP growth at 8.0 per cent** and real GVA growth at 7.3 per cent.
- The base year of the GDP series is being changed from **2011-12 to 2022-23**.
- The **Economic Survey 2025-26** projected growth of **6.8–7.2 per cent** for **2026-27**.
- According to the Economic Survey, average retail (CPI) inflation was **1.7 per cent** in April–December 2025 and foreign exchange reserves reached **USD 701.4 billion**.
- The RBI's **Report on Trend and Progress of Banking in India 2024-25** put the gross NPA ratio of banks at **2.2 per cent** in March 2025, a multi-decade low.
- The RBI's study of State budgets found that the States' gross fiscal deficit widened to **3.3 per cent of GSDP** in 2024-25.
- FDI inflows in **April–September 2025** rose 18 per cent to **USD 35.2 billion**; **Singapore** was the largest source and **Maharashtra** the top recipient State.
- India overtook **China** to become the world's **largest rice producer** in 2025; it supplies about **40 per cent** of world rice exports.
- The Commerce Ministry notified the first market access guidelines under the **Export Promotion Mission**, whose two parts are **Niryat Protsahan** and **Niryat Disha**; the **DGFT** implements it.
- **99.2 per cent** of the broad gauge network of Indian Railways had been electrified by November 2025.
- Railways have approved **306 Gati Shakti Cargo Terminals**; India's logistics cost has fallen to **7.97 per cent of GDP**.
- The pension regulator **PFRDA** set up a 15-member committee under **M.S. Sahoo** on assured payouts in the **National Pension System**.
- PFRDA also set up a committee on asset allocation and risk governance (SAARG) under **Narayan Ramachandran**.
- **National Startup Day** is **16 January**; in 2026 it marked **ten years of Startup India**, with over **2 lakh** recognised startups and more than **120 unicorns**.
- The **Financial Intelligence Unit–India (FIU-IND)** issued stricter KYC guidelines for **virtual digital assets** (crypto) platforms.
- The RBI proposed linking the **central bank digital currencies of BRICS** countries; **India hosts the BRICS summit in 2026**.
- The Supreme Court held that **Tiger Global's** sale of its **Flipkart** stake to Walmart is **taxable in India**, denying it the benefit of the India–Mauritius tax treaty.
- The **labour force participation rate** reached a record **56.1 per cent** in December 2025 (PLFS); the unemployment rate was **4.8 per cent**.
- The Union Cabinet approved an equity infusion of **Rs 5,000 crore** into **SIDBI**, whose headquarters are in **Lucknow**.
- **Coking coal** was declared a **critical and strategic mineral** under the **MMDR Act, 1957**.
- **Akasa Air** became the **fifth Indian airline** to join the International Air Transport Association (**IATA**).
- **Tata Group** was ranked India's **most valuable brand** by **Brand Finance**; **Apple** was the world's most valuable.
- The Government announced India's first **Tailings Policy**, to recover critical minerals from mine waste.
- **Vizhinjam** in Kerala is India's **first deep-water transshipment port**; work began to raise its capacity from 1 million to 5.7 million TEUs.
