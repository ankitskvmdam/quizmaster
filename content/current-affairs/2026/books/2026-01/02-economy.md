## Growth and outlook

- **Fourth-largest economy:** India became the world's fourth-largest economy, overtaking Japan. Its nominal GDP is about USD 4.18 trillion.
- **First Advance Estimates for 2025-26:** The National Statistics Office (NSO) of the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) released them.
  - Real GDP growth was put at 7.4 per cent, against 6.5 per cent in 2024-25.
  - Nominal GDP growth was put at 8.0 per cent.
  - Real growth in gross value added (GVA) was put at 7.3 per cent.
- **New base year:** The base year of the GDP series is being changed from 2011-12 to 2022-23.
- **Economic Survey 2025-26:** It was tabled at the end of January.
  - It projected growth of 6.8–7.2 per cent for 2026-27.
  - Average retail inflation, measured by the consumer price index (CPI), was 1.7 per cent in April–December 2025.
  - Foreign exchange reserves reached USD 701.4 billion.

## Banking and finance

- **March 2025:** The gross ratio of non-performing assets (NPA), or bad loans, of banks was 2.2 per cent. This was a multi-decade low. The figure is from the RBI's Report on Trend and Progress of Banking in India 2024-25.
- **State deficits:** The RBI's study of State budgets found that the States' gross fiscal deficit widened to 3.3 per cent of gross state domestic product (GSDP) in 2024-25.
- **Pension committees:** The pension regulator, the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA), set up two committees.
  - A 15-member committee under M.S. Sahoo will look at assured payouts in the National Pension System.
  - A committee on asset allocation and risk governance (SAARG) is headed by Narayan Ramachandran.
- **SIDBI:** The Union Cabinet approved an equity infusion of Rs 5,000 crore into the Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI). Its headquarters are in Lucknow.
- **Crypto platforms:** The Financial Intelligence Unit–India (FIU-IND) issued stricter KYC (know your customer) guidelines for virtual digital assets (crypto) platforms.
- **BRICS digital currencies:** The RBI proposed linking the central bank digital currencies of BRICS countries. India hosts the BRICS summit in 2026.

## Trade and investment

- **April–September 2025:** Foreign direct investment (FDI) inflows rose 18 per cent to USD 35.2 billion. Singapore was the largest source. Maharashtra was the top recipient State.
- **2025:** India overtook China to become the world's largest rice producer. It supplies about 40 per cent of world rice exports.
- **Export Promotion Mission:** The Commerce Ministry notified the first market access guidelines under the mission. Its two parts are Niryat Protsahan and Niryat Disha. The Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) implements it.
- **Tiger Global:** The Supreme Court held that Tiger Global's sale of its Flipkart stake to Walmart is taxable in India. It denied the company the benefit of the India–Mauritius tax treaty.

## Jobs and startups

- **December 2025:** The labour force participation rate reached a record 56.1 per cent, according to the Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS). The unemployment rate was 4.8 per cent.
- **16 January:** This is National Startup Day. In 2026 it marked ten years of Startup India. There were over 2 lakh recognised startups and more than 120 unicorns.

## Transport and infrastructure

- **November 2025:** 99.2 per cent of the broad gauge network of Indian Railways had been electrified.
- **Gati Shakti Cargo Terminals:** Railways have approved 306 of them. India's logistics cost has fallen to 7.97 per cent of GDP.
- **Vizhinjam:** This port in Kerala is India's first deep-water transshipment port. Work began to raise its capacity from 1 million to 5.7 million TEUs (twenty-foot containers).
- **Akasa Air:** It became the fifth Indian airline to join the International Air Transport Association (IATA).

## Minerals and companies

- **Coking coal:** It was declared a critical and strategic mineral under the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 (MMDR Act).
- **Tailings Policy:** The Government announced India's first Tailings Policy, to recover critical minerals from mine waste.
- **Most valuable brands:** Brand Finance ranked the Tata Group India's most valuable brand. Apple was the world's most valuable.
