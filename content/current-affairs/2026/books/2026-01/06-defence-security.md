## Gist
The Army held its **78th Army Day** parade in **Jaipur**, where its new fast-moving **Bhairav Battalions** marched for the first time. At the Republic Day parade DRDO showed its **long-range anti-ship hypersonic missile** in public for the first time.

DRDO had a good month of tests: two **Pralay** missiles were fired in a salvo and the **Akash-NG** air defence missile finished its user trials. The Coast Guard commissioned its first home-built pollution control vessel, **ICGS Samudra Pratap**.

The Navy was at sea on goodwill and training voyages: the sail training ship **INS Sudarshini**, the research ship **INS Sagardhwani**, and the stitched sailing vessel **INSV Kaundinya**, which reached Oman.

## Key facts
- **DRDO** carried out a **salvo launch of two Pralay missiles** from the same launcher off the **Odisha** coast.
- **Pralay** is a **surface-to-surface, quasi-ballistic** missile with a range of **150–500 km**.
- The Army signed an emergency contract with **NIBE Limited** for the **Suryastra** rocket launcher, which can hit targets at **150 km and 300 km**; it is based on a system of Israel's **Elbit Systems**.
- **ICGS Samudra Pratap**, the first of two indigenous **pollution control vessels** of the **Indian Coast Guard**, was commissioned by Defence Minister **Rajnath Singh**.
- Samudra Pratap was built by **Goa Shipyard Limited**; it is the **largest ship** in the Coast Guard fleet and will be based at **Kochi**.
- DRDO completed the user evaluation trials of the **Akash-NG** surface-to-air missile, which has a range of up to **70 km**; it will go to the **Indian Air Force**.
- The **78th Army Day** was observed on **15 January 2026** with a parade in **Jaipur**, the first Army Day parade held outside a cantonment.
- The Army's new **Bhairav Battalions**, light and fast strike units of about 200–250 soldiers, made their debut at the Army Day parade.
- The **National Investigation Agency (NIA)** recorded a conviction rate of over **92 per cent** in 2025.
- The **NDRF** observes its Raising Day on **19 January**; it was raised in **2006** and now has **16 battalions**.
- **INS Sagardhwani**, a marine acoustic research ship, left **Kochi** for **Sagar Maitri V**, a joint programme of the **Navy and DRDO**.
- The sail training ship **INS Sudarshini** began **Lokayan 26** on **20 January 2026**, a ten-month voyage of over 22,000 nautical miles to **13 countries**.
- **INSV Kaundinya**, India's first **"stitched ship"** built without metal nails, reached **Muscat (Oman)** on its first voyage from **Porbandar**.
- DRDO showed its **Long Range Anti-Ship Hypersonic Missile (LR-AShM)**, with a range of about **1,500 km**, for the first time at the Republic Day parade.
- A military contingent of the **European Union** marched in the Republic Day parade, its first such participation outside Europe.
- The Navy's **First Training Squadron** (INS Tir, INS Shardul, INS Sujata and ICGS Sarathi) visited **Phuket, Thailand**.
- The Union Home Minister inaugurated the **National IED Data Management System (NIDMS)**, India's first national database of bomb blasts, developed by the **National Security Guard (NSG)**.
- The first **Made-in-India C-295** transport aircraft is due to roll out before September 2026 from the **Airbus–Tata** plant at **Vadodara**; India is buying **56** of these aircraft.
