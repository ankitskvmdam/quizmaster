## At a glance: missiles and rockets in the news

| Weapon | Type | Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pralay | Surface-to-surface, quasi-ballistic missile | 150–500 km |
| Akash-NG | Surface-to-air missile | Up to 70 km |
| Suryastra | Rocket launcher | 150 km and 300 km |
| LR-AShM | Long Range Anti-Ship Hypersonic Missile | About 1,500 km |

## Tests and purchases

- **Pralay salvo:** DRDO carried out a salvo launch of two Pralay missiles from the same launcher off the Odisha coast. A salvo launch fires several missiles together.
- **Akash-NG:** DRDO completed the user evaluation trials of this surface-to-air missile. It will go to the Indian Air Force.
- **Suryastra:** The Army signed an emergency contract with NIBE Limited for this rocket launcher. It is based on a system of Israel's Elbit Systems.
- **C-295:** The first Made-in-India C-295 transport aircraft is due to roll out before September 2026. It will come from the Airbus–Tata plant at Vadodara. India is buying 56 of these aircraft.

## Parades

- **15 January 2026, 78th Army Day:** The Army held its Army Day parade in Jaipur. It was the first Army Day parade held outside a cantonment.
  - The Army's new Bhairav Battalions made their debut at the parade. They are light and fast strike units of about 200–250 soldiers.
- **Republic Day parade:** DRDO showed its Long Range Anti-Ship Hypersonic Missile (LR-AShM) in public for the first time. A military contingent of the European Union also marched, its first such participation outside Europe.

## Navy and Coast Guard

- **20 January 2026:** The sail training ship INS Sudarshini began Lokayan 26. It is a ten-month voyage of over 22,000 nautical miles to 13 countries.
- **ICGS Samudra Pratap:** Defence Minister Rajnath Singh commissioned this ship of the Indian Coast Guard.
  - It is the first of two indigenous pollution control vessels.
  - Goa Shipyard Limited built it.
  - It is the largest ship in the Coast Guard fleet and will be based at Kochi.
- **INS Sagardhwani:** This marine acoustic research ship left Kochi for Sagar Maitri V, a joint programme of the Navy and DRDO.
- **INSV Kaundinya:** India's first "stitched ship", built without metal nails, reached Muscat (Oman) on its first voyage from Porbandar.
- **First Training Squadron:** The Navy's squadron (INS Tir, INS Shardul, INS Sujata and ICGS Sarathi) visited Phuket, Thailand.

## Internal security

- **19 January:** The National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) observes its Raising Day. It was raised in 2006 and now has 16 battalions.
- **NIA:** The National Investigation Agency (NIA) recorded a conviction rate of over 92 per cent in 2025.
- **NIDMS:** The Union Home Minister inaugurated the National IED Data Management System (NIDMS). It is India's first national database of bomb blasts. The National Security Guard (NSG) developed it. An IED is an improvised explosive device.
