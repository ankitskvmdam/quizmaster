## Judiciary

- **New Chief Justice:** Justice Sangam Kumar Sahoo took oath as the 47th Chief Justice of the Patna High Court. Governor Arif Mohammed Khan administered the oath. Before coming to Patna, Justice Sahoo was a judge of the Orissa High Court.
- **New judges:** Ritesh Kumar and Praveen Kumar were appointed judges of the Patna High Court. This took its working strength to 36.
- **Patna High Court:** It was established on 3 February 1916.

## Government and schemes

- **Samriddhi Yatra:** Chief Minister Nitish Kumar set out on this yatra to review schemes district by district.
  - **16 January 2026:** He began it from West Champaran (Bettiah).
  - **16–24 January 2026:** The first phase covered nine districts and ended in Vaishali.
  - **Gopalganj:** During the yatra, 40 schemes worth Rs 316 crore were launched or started in the district.
- **Mukhyamantri Mahila Rojgar Yojana:** The Cabinet approved further help of up to Rs 2 lakh under the scheme. It is for women who had received the first instalment of Rs 10,000.

## Water and power

- **Indrapuri Reservoir Project:** The Bihar Cabinet approved this project in Rohtas district on the Sone river. It came after a draft agreement with Jharkhand on sharing the Sone's water.
  - Under the draft agreement, out of 7.75 million acre-feet, Bihar gets 5.75 and Jharkhand 2.0 million acre-feet.
  - Undivided Bihar's share of Sone water was fixed by the Bansagar Agreement of 1973.
- **Kajra solar plant:** The first phase of a solar power project with battery storage was commissioned at Kajra in Lakhisarai district.
  - The first phase has 185 MW of solar with 254 MWh of storage.
  - When complete, the project will have 301 MW of solar and 495 MWh of battery storage.
  - Bihar State Power Generation Company Limited is building it.
- **Power ratings:** Bihar's power distribution companies, NBPDCL and SBPDCL, received an 'A' grade. The grade came in the 14th Integrated Rating of distribution utilities by the Power Finance Corporation.

## Wildlife and tourism

- **Kaimur Wildlife Sanctuary:** It is proposed as Bihar's second tiger reserve. The State Cabinet has approved it. The final approval of the National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA) is awaited.
- **Valmiki Tiger Reserve:** This reserve in West Champaran is Bihar's first and so far only tiger reserve.
- **Gaya:** The Gaya district administration proposed a single-window system for tourism in Gaya and Bodh Gaya.

## Honours and other news

- **Padma Shri 2026:** Three people from Bihar were named: folk singer Bharat Singh Bharti, Vishvabandhu (posthumous) and scientist Gopal Ji Trivedi.
- **Best Electoral Practices Award:** Bihar was among the States that received it on National Voters' Day 2026.
- **Median age:** An RBI study of State finances found that Bihar has the lowest median age (23 years) among States. Kerala has the highest (37).
- **Consumer workshop:** A regional consumer protection workshop for the eastern States was held in Patna. The National Consumer Helpline number is 1915.
