## Gist
The Patna High Court got a new Chief Justice, **Justice Sangam Kumar Sahoo**, and two new judges. Chief Minister **Nitish Kumar** set out on the **Samriddhi Yatra** from West Champaran to review schemes district by district.

Two long-term projects moved forward. The State Cabinet cleared the **Indrapuri Reservoir Project** after a draft agreement with Jharkhand on sharing the water of the **Sone**, and the first phase of a large **solar-plus-battery plant at Kajra** in Lakhisarai was commissioned. **Kaimur** is on its way to becoming the State's second tiger reserve.

Bihar's two power distribution companies earned an 'A' grade in the national ratings, and three people from the State were named for the Padma Shri.

## Key facts
- **Justice Sangam Kumar Sahoo** took oath as the **47th Chief Justice of the Patna High Court**; Governor **Arif Mohammed Khan** administered the oath.
- Before coming to Patna, Justice Sangam Kumar Sahoo was a judge of the **Orissa High Court**.
- **Ritesh Kumar** and **Praveen Kumar** were appointed judges of the Patna High Court, taking its working strength to **36**.
- The **Patna High Court** was established on **3 February 1916**.
- Chief Minister **Nitish Kumar** began the **Samriddhi Yatra** from **West Champaran (Bettiah)** on **16 January 2026**.
- The first phase of the Samriddhi Yatra (16–24 January 2026) covered **nine districts** and ended in **Vaishali**.
- During the Samriddhi Yatra, 40 schemes worth **Rs 316 crore** were launched or started in **Gopalganj** district.
- The Bihar Cabinet approved the **Indrapuri Reservoir Project** in **Rohtas** district on the **Sone** river.
- Under the draft Sone water-sharing agreement, out of 7.75 million acre-feet, **Bihar gets 5.75** and **Jharkhand 2.0** million acre-feet.
- Undivided Bihar's share of Sone water was fixed by the **Bansagar Agreement of 1973**.
- The first phase of a solar power project with battery storage was commissioned at **Kajra** in **Lakhisarai** district: **185 MW** of solar with **254 MWh** of storage.
- When complete, the Kajra project will have **301 MW** of solar and **495 MWh** of battery storage; **Bihar State Power Generation Company Limited** is building it.
- **Kaimur Wildlife Sanctuary** is proposed as Bihar's **second tiger reserve**; the State Cabinet has approved it and the **NTCA's** final approval is awaited.
- **Valmiki Tiger Reserve** in **West Champaran** is Bihar's first and so far only tiger reserve.
- Bihar's power distribution companies, **NBPDCL and SBPDCL**, received an **'A' grade** in the **14th Integrated Rating** of distribution utilities by the **Power Finance Corporation**.
- Under the **Mukhyamantri Mahila Rojgar Yojana**, the Cabinet approved further help of up to **Rs 2 lakh** for women who had received the first instalment of **Rs 10,000**.
- Three people from Bihar were named for the **Padma Shri 2026**: folk singer **Bharat Singh Bharti**, **Vishvabandhu** (posthumous) and scientist **Gopal Ji Trivedi**.
- Bihar was among the States that received the **Best Electoral Practices Award** on National Voters' Day 2026.
- An RBI study of State finances found that **Bihar** has the **lowest median age (23 years)** among States; Kerala has the highest (37).
- A regional consumer protection workshop for the eastern States was held in **Patna**; the National Consumer Helpline number is **1915**.
- The **Gaya** district administration proposed a **single-window system** for tourism in Gaya and **Bodh Gaya**.
