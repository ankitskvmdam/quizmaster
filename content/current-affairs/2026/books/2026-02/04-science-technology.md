## India-AI Impact Summit 2026

- **India-AI Impact Summit 2026:** It was held at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, and organised by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY). It was the first major global AI summit hosted in the Global South.
  - Its theme was "Sarvajana Hitaya, Sarvajana Sukhaya". Its three pillars were People, Planet and Progress.
  - The summit's New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact was signed by 89 countries and international organisations.
  - The Prime Minister presented the M.A.N.A.V. vision for human-centred AI: Moral and ethical systems, Accountable governance, National sovereignty, Accessible and inclusive AI, Valid and legitimate systems.
  - India formally joined the US-led Pax Silica initiative on semiconductor supply chains and critical minerals.
  - Indian firms used the summit to show home-grown language models, such as Sarvam-105B and Param2 (below).
  - The next AI Impact Summit will be held in Geneva, Switzerland, in 2027. The UAE will host it in 2028.

## Artificial intelligence

- **Sarvam AI:** It unveiled two Indian large language models, Sarvam-30B and Sarvam-105B. A large language model is an AI trained on vast amounts of text. Sarvam AI also unveiled a multilingual chatbot named Vikram, after Vikram Sarabhai.
- **BharatGen:** It launched Param2, a model with 17 billion parameters covering 22 Indian languages.
- **Bodhan AI:** This non-profit under the Ministry of Education was developed at IIT Madras. It will build the Bharat EduAI Stack.
- **SAHI and BODH:** The Health Ministry launched SAHI (Secure AI for Health Initiative). It also launched BODH, a platform to test health AI tools.
- **Guinness World Record:** India set a record for the most AI responsibility pledges in 24 hours, over 250,946.

## Semiconductors, quantum and cyber security

- **India Semiconductor Mission 2.0:** It was announced in the Union Budget. It targets 3 nm and 2 nm chip manufacturing by 2035 (nm is nanometre).
- **Amaravati Quantum Centre:** Its foundation stone was laid in Andhra Pradesh under the National Quantum Mission.
- **Space assets:** CERT-In and the SatCom Industry Association released a cyber security framework for space assets.

## Space, astronomy and research

- **Telescopes in Ladakh:** The Union Budget sanctioned two large new telescopes in Ladakh.
  - The National Large Solar Telescope (2-metre) will be at Merak near Pangong Tso.
  - The National Large Optical–Near Infrared Telescope (13.7-metre) will be at Hanle.
- **Gaganyaan:** The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) tested the drogue parachute of the Gaganyaan crew module. A drogue parachute is a small parachute that slows and steadies a capsule. The test was at the rail track rocket sled facility of TBRL, Chandigarh.
- **CHEOPS:** Europe's CHEOPS space telescope found a four-planet system around the red dwarf star LHS 1903. A red dwarf is a small, cool star.
- **Photo-capacitor:** Scientists at CeNS, Bengaluru built a photo-capacitor. It is a single device that both collects and stores solar energy.
- **Tsunami warning centre:** A Rs 300 crore tsunami warning centre is planned at Vijaynagar on Swaraj Dweep (Andaman and Nicobar). INCOIS is the nodal agency.

## Health

- **Lung cancer:** India released its first national lung cancer treatment guidelines. They were prepared by the Department of Health Research and the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS).
- **Td vaccine:** An Indian-made Td (tetanus and adult diphtheria) vaccine was launched at the Central Research Institute, Kasauli. Td replaces the TT (tetanus) vaccine in the Universal Immunisation Programme.
- **HPV vaccination:** India is to start a free, single-dose HPV (human papillomavirus) vaccination drive for girls aged 14. It will use the vaccine Gardasil and be tracked on U-WIN.
- **nOPV2:** The WHO prequalified the novel oral polio vaccine type 2 (nOPV2) made by Biological E, Hyderabad.
- **Stem cell therapy:** The Supreme Court ruled that stem cell therapy for autism cannot be offered outside approved clinical trials.
