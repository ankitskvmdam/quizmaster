## Gist
Artificial intelligence dominated the month. India hosted the **India-AI Impact Summit 2026** at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi, the first big global AI summit held in the Global South. It produced the **New Delhi Declaration**, the Prime Minister's **M.A.N.A.V. vision** for human-centred AI, and India's entry into the US-led **Pax Silica** group on chip supply chains. Indian firms used the stage to show home-grown language models such as **Sarvam-105B** and **Param2**.

The Union Budget added to the hardware side with **India Semiconductor Mission 2.0** and two large new telescopes in **Ladakh**.

In health, India began replacing the tetanus vaccine with the **Td vaccine**, prepared a free **HPV vaccination** drive for 14-year-old girls, and released its first national **lung cancer** treatment guidelines.

## Key facts
- The **India-AI Impact Summit 2026** was held at **Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi**, organised by **MeitY**; it was the first major global AI summit hosted in the **Global South**.
- The summit's theme was **"Sarvajana Hitaya, Sarvajana Sukhaya"**, and its three pillars were **People, Planet and Progress**.
- The summit's **New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact** was signed by **89** countries and international organisations.
- The Prime Minister presented the **M.A.N.A.V. vision** for AI: Moral and ethical systems, Accountable governance, National sovereignty, Accessible and inclusive AI, Valid and legitimate systems.
- India formally joined the US-led **Pax Silica** initiative on semiconductor supply chains and critical minerals.
- The next AI Impact Summit will be held in **Geneva, Switzerland, in 2027**; the **UAE** will host in 2028.
- India set a **Guinness World Record** for the most AI responsibility pledges in 24 hours (over **250,946**).
- **Sarvam AI** unveiled two Indian large language models, **Sarvam-30B** and **Sarvam-105B**, and a multilingual chatbot named **Vikram** (after Vikram Sarabhai).
- **BharatGen** launched **Param2**, a model with **17 billion parameters** covering **22 Indian languages**.
- **Bodhan AI**, a non-profit under the **Ministry of Education** developed at **IIT Madras**, will build the **Bharat EduAI Stack**.
- The Health Ministry launched **SAHI** (Secure AI for Health Initiative) and **BODH**, a platform to test health AI tools.
- **India Semiconductor Mission 2.0** was announced in the Union Budget; it targets **3 nm and 2 nm** chip manufacturing by **2035**.
- The Budget sanctioned two telescopes in **Ladakh**: the **National Large Solar Telescope** (2-metre) at **Merak** near Pangong Tso and the **National Large Optical–Near Infrared Telescope** (13.7-metre) at **Hanle**.
- **DRDO** tested the **drogue parachute** of the **Gaganyaan** crew module at the rail track rocket sled facility of **TBRL, Chandigarh**.
- Europe's **CHEOPS** space telescope found a **four-planet system** around the red dwarf star **LHS 1903**.
- Scientists at **CeNS, Bengaluru** built a **photo-capacitor**, a single device that both collects and stores solar energy.
- India released its **first national lung cancer treatment guidelines**, prepared by the Department of Health Research and the DGHS.
- An Indian-made **Td (tetanus and adult diphtheria) vaccine** was launched at the **Central Research Institute, Kasauli**; Td replaces the TT vaccine in the Universal Immunisation Programme.
- India is to start a free, single-dose **HPV vaccination** drive for girls aged **14**, using the vaccine **Gardasil** and tracked on **U-WIN**.
- The WHO prequalified the **novel oral polio vaccine type 2 (nOPV2)** made by **Biological E**, Hyderabad.
- The Supreme Court ruled that **stem cell therapy for autism** cannot be offered outside approved clinical trials.
- A **Rs 300 crore** tsunami warning centre is planned at **Vijaynagar on Swaraj Dweep** (Andaman and Nicobar); **INCOIS** is the nodal agency.
- The foundation stone of the **Amaravati Quantum Centre** in Andhra Pradesh was laid under the **National Quantum Mission**.
- **CERT-In** and the SatCom Industry Association released a **cyber security framework for space assets**.
