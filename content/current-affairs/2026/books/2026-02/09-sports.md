## Gist
Indian cricket had a strong month. The **Under-19 team** won a record **sixth World Cup**, with **Vaibhav Sooryavanshi** scoring 175 in the final; **Royal Challengers Bengaluru** took their second **Women's Premier League** title; and **Jammu and Kashmir** won the **Ranji Trophy** for the first time.

The **25th Winter Olympics** were held at **Milan and Cortina d'Ampezzo** in Italy from 6 to 22 February. **Norway** topped the medal table and its skier **Johannes Høsflot Klæbo** won six gold medals.

Elsewhere, **Carlos Alcaraz** won his first Australian Open, **Services** won the Santosh Trophy, **Tejaswin Shankar** won gold at the Asian Indoor Athletics Championships, and **Magnus Carlsen** became the first official world champion in freestyle chess.

## Key facts
- **India** won the **ICC Under-19 Men's World Cup 2026**, its record **sixth** title, beating **England** by **100 runs** in the final at **Harare**, Zimbabwe.
- **Vaibhav Sooryavanshi** scored **175** in the Under-19 final and was both Player of the Match and Player of the Tournament; the Indian captain was **Ayush Mhatre**.
- **Royal Challengers Bengaluru** won the **Women's Premier League 2026**, their second title, beating **Delhi Capitals** in the final.
- **Smriti Mandhana** of RCB won the **Orange Cap** of WPL 2026 with **377 runs**.
- **Jammu and Kashmir** won their **first Ranji Trophy** (2025-26), beating eight-time champions **Karnataka** on first-innings lead in the final at **Hubballi**.
- Jammu and Kashmir's Ranji-winning captain was **Paras Dogra**; pacer **Auqib Nabi** took **60 wickets** in the season.
- **Carlos Alcaraz** of Spain won his **first Australian Open** men's singles title, beating **Novak Djokovic** in the final; it was his seventh Grand Slam title.
- The **25th Winter Olympics**, **Milano Cortina 2026**, were held in **Italy** from **6 to 22 February 2026**; the mascots were **Tina and Milo**.
- The Winter Olympics opened at **San Siro Stadium, Milan**, and closed at the **Verona Arena**.
- **Norway** topped the Winter Olympics medal table with **18 gold** and **41 medals** in all; the **United States** was second with 12 gold.
- Norwegian cross-country skier **Johannes Høsflot Klæbo** won **six gold medals** at the Games.
- India was represented at the Winter Olympics by **Arif Mohammad Khan** (alpine skiing) and **Stanzin Lundup** (cross-country skiing).
- **Services** beat **Kerala 1–0** in the final of the **79th Santosh Trophy** at **Dhakuakhana, Assam**, on **8 February 2026**, to win their **eighth** title.
- **Tejaswin Shankar** won **gold in the men's heptathlon** at the **Asian Indoor Athletics Championships** in **Tianjin, China**, with a national indoor record of **5,993 points**.
- India won **five medals** at the Asian Indoor Athletics Championships and finished sixth.
- **Magnus Carlsen** of Norway won the first official **FIDE Freestyle Chess World Championship**, beating **Fabiano Caruana** in the final at **Weissenhaus, Germany**.
- **Devika Sihag** won her first **BWF Super 300** title at the **Thailand Masters 2026**.
- **Harmanpreet Kaur** became the **most-capped player in women's international cricket** with her 356th match, passing New Zealand's **Suzie Bates**.
- **Soraya Aghaei** became the **first woman from Iran** to be elected a member of the **International Olympic Committee**.
