## Cricket

- **Under-19 World Cup:** India beat England by 100 runs in the final. It was India's record sixth title.
  - Vaibhav Sooryavanshi scored 175 in the final. He was both Player of the Match and Player of the Tournament.
  - The Indian captain was Ayush Mhatre.
- **Women's Premier League:** RCB beat Delhi Capitals in the final to win their second title. Smriti Mandhana of RCB won the Orange Cap with 377 runs.
- **Ranji Trophy:** Jammu and Kashmir won their first Ranji Trophy. They beat eight-time champions Karnataka on first-innings lead in the final.
  - The captain was Paras Dogra.
  - Pacer Auqib Nabi took 60 wickets in the season.
- **Harmanpreet Kaur:** She became the most-capped player in women's international cricket with her 356th match. She passed New Zealand's Suzie Bates.

## Winter Olympics

- **6 to 22 February 2026:** The 25th Winter Olympics, Milano Cortina 2026, were held at Milan and Cortina d'Ampezzo in Italy.
  - The mascots were Tina and Milo.
  - The Games opened at San Siro Stadium, Milan, and closed at the Verona Arena.
  - Norway topped the medal table with 18 gold and 41 medals in all. The United States was second with 12 gold.
  - Norwegian cross-country skier Johannes Høsflot Klæbo won six gold medals.
  - India was represented by Arif Mohammad Khan (alpine skiing) and Stanzin Lundup (cross-country skiing).

## Other sports

- **8 February 2026:** Services beat Kerala 1–0 in the final of the 79th Santosh Trophy. It was their eighth title.
- **Australian Open:** Carlos Alcaraz of Spain beat Novak Djokovic in the final to win his first Australian Open men's singles title. It was his seventh Grand Slam title.
- **Asian Indoor Athletics Championships:** The event was held in Tianjin, China.
  - Tejaswin Shankar won gold in the men's heptathlon with a national indoor record of 5,993 points.
  - India won five medals and finished sixth.
- **Chess:** Magnus Carlsen of Norway beat Fabiano Caruana in the final. He became the first official FIDE Freestyle Chess World Champion.
- **Badminton:** The Thailand Masters 2026 was Devika Sihag's first BWF Super 300 title.
- **Soraya Aghaei:** She became the first woman from Iran to be elected a member of the International Olympic Committee (IOC).
