## Gist
The first decision taken from the new **Seva Teerth** office was the **PM RAHAT** scheme, which gives road accident victims cashless treatment of up to **Rs 1.5 lakh**. The Cabinet also cleared the **Urban Challenge Fund** of **Rs 1 lakh crore** for cities, and the target for **Lakhpati Didis** was doubled.

For farmers, the Budget's AI advisory tool **Bharat-VISTAAR** went live in its first phase and the roadmap of the **Mission for Aatmanirbharta in Pulses** was finalised. **NITI Aayog** began the second **Sampoornata Abhiyan** for aspirational districts and blocks.

Among indices, India rose to **91st** in the **Corruption Perceptions Index 2025** and to **45th** in the **Network Readiness Index 2025**.

## Key facts
- **PM RAHAT**, a scheme of the **Ministry of Road Transport and Highways**, gives road accident victims **cashless treatment up to Rs 1.5 lakh** for the first **7 days**; it was the first decision taken from Seva Teerth.
- The Union Cabinet approved the **Urban Challenge Fund**, a scheme of the **Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs** with central assistance of **Rs 1 lakh crore** for 2025-26 to 2030-31.
- The target for **Lakhpati Didis** was doubled from 3 crore to **6 crore by March 2029**.
- Phase 1 of **Bharat-VISTAAR**, a multilingual AI advisory system for farmers, was launched in **Jaipur** by the Agriculture Ministry; its helpline number is **155261**.
- **NITI Aayog** launched **Sampoornata Abhiyan 2.0**, running from **28 January to 14 April 2026**, in **112 Aspirational Districts** and **513 Aspirational Blocks**.
- The **Mission for Aatmanirbharta in Pulses** has an outlay of **Rs 11,440 crore** for 2025-26 to 2030-31 and targets **350 lakh tonnes** of pulses by 2030-31.
- Under the pulses mission, **Tur, Urad and Masoor** will be fully procured for four years by **NAFED and NCCF**.
- The second phase of the **Vibrant Villages Programme (VVP-II)** covers **1,954 border villages** in 17 States with an outlay of **Rs 6,839 crore**.
- India ranked **91st of 182 countries** in the **Corruption Perceptions Index 2025** of **Transparency International**, with a score of **39**; it was 96th in 2024.
- **Denmark** topped the Corruption Perceptions Index 2025; **Somalia and South Sudan** were at the bottom.
- India rose four places to **45th** in the **Network Readiness Index 2025**, released by the **Portulans Institute**.
- India ranked **54th** in the **Innovators Business Environment Index 2026** of **StartupBlink**; the **United States** was first.
- In the **Panchayat Advancement Index 2.0** of the Ministry of Panchayati Raj, **Jugal Kishore Nagar** Village Committee of **Tripura** ranked first in the country.
- UNESCO's seventh **State of the Education Report for India**, titled **"Bhasha Matters"**, deals with mother-tongue and multilingual education.
- The Health Ministry began the yearly **mass drug administration** campaign against **lymphatic filariasis** in **12 States**; India aims to eliminate the disease by **2027**.
- **Delhi** announced the **Lakhpati Bitiya Yojana** for girls' education, replacing the **Ladli** scheme from 1 April 2026.
- Kerala's **Sthree Suraksha** scheme gives **Rs 1,000 a month** to unemployed women and transgender women aged 35 to 60.
- The **UN Road Safety Fund** launched a road safety financing project in four States: **Rajasthan, Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Assam**.
- The **Indian Institute of Foreign Trade (IIFT)** topped the **Times B-School Ranking 2026**.
- The Public Accounts Committee criticised slow progress of the **SANKALP** scheme of the **Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship**, which is part-funded by a **World Bank** loan.
- The Railways announced a **Rail Tech Policy** under its **"52 Reforms in 52 Weeks"** initiative.
- **FSSAI** gave **Eat Right Station** certificates to five stations of the **Northeast Frontier Railway**, including Dimapur.
