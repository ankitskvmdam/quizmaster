## Road safety and health

- **PM RAHAT:** This scheme of the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways gives road accident victims cashless treatment up to Rs 1.5 lakh for the first 7 days. It was the first decision taken from the new Seva Teerth office.
- **UN Road Safety Fund:** It launched a road safety financing project in four States: Rajasthan, Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Assam.
- **Lymphatic filariasis:** The Health Ministry began the yearly mass drug administration campaign against the disease in 12 States. In mass drug administration, everyone in an affected area takes the medicine. India aims to eliminate the disease by 2027.
- **Eat Right Station:** The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) gave Eat Right Station certificates to five stations of the Northeast Frontier Railway, including Dimapur.

## Development schemes

- **28 January to 14 April 2026:** NITI Aayog launched Sampoornata Abhiyan 2.0, the second such campaign, to run over these dates. It covers 112 Aspirational Districts and 513 Aspirational Blocks.
- **1 April 2026:** Delhi's new Lakhpati Bitiya Yojana for girls' education replaces the Ladli scheme from this date.
- **Urban Challenge Fund:** The Union Cabinet approved this scheme of the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs for cities. Its central assistance is Rs 1 lakh crore for 2025-26 to 2030-31.
- **Lakhpati Didis:** The target was doubled from 3 crore to 6 crore by March 2029.
- **Vibrant Villages Programme (VVP-II):** Its second phase covers 1,954 border villages in 17 States. Its outlay is Rs 6,839 crore.
- **Sthree Suraksha:** This Kerala scheme gives Rs 1,000 a month to unemployed women and transgender women aged 35 to 60.

## Farmers

- **Bharat-VISTAAR:** This multilingual AI advisory system for farmers was announced in the Budget. The Agriculture Ministry launched its Phase 1 in Jaipur. Its helpline number is 155261.
- **Mission for Aatmanirbharta in Pulses:** Its roadmap was finalised.
  - Its outlay is Rs 11,440 crore for 2025-26 to 2030-31.
  - It targets 350 lakh tonnes of pulses by 2030-31.
  - Tur, Urad and Masoor will be fully procured for four years by NAFED and NCCF.

## Skills, education and railways

- **SANKALP:** The Public Accounts Committee criticised the slow progress of this scheme of the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. The scheme is part-funded by a World Bank loan.
- **Bhasha Matters:** UNESCO's seventh State of the Education Report for India has this title. It deals with mother-tongue and multilingual education.
- **Rail Tech Policy:** The Railways announced it under their "52 Reforms in 52 Weeks" initiative.
