## Court rulings

- **Dr. Jaya Thakur v. Government of India (2026):** The Supreme Court held that menstrual health and hygiene is part of the right to life under Article 21. It ordered free sanitary napkins and working toilets in every school.
- **Spectrum:** The Supreme Court held that telecom spectrum is a public resource owned by the Union of India. It cannot be treated or sold as a company's asset under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016.
- **Ad hoc judges:** The Supreme Court Collegium approved retired judges as ad hoc judges of the Allahabad High Court. An ad hoc judge is appointed for a limited purpose. The approval was made under Article 224A.
- **Kalpetta:** Kalpetta in Wayanad, Kerala, became India's first fully paperless district court. Chief Justice of India Surya Kant inaugurated it online.

## States and regions

- **Frontier Nagaland Territorial Authority (FNTA):** The Centre, the Nagaland government and the Eastern Nagaland People's Organisation (ENPO) signed a tripartite (three-party) agreement to set it up.
  - It covers six eastern districts: Tuensang, Mon, Kiphire, Longleng, Noklak and Shamator.
  - It has powers over 46 subjects.
  - It does not affect Article 371(A).
- **Manipur:** Yumnam Khemchand Singh became Chief Minister of Manipur. This ended nearly a year of President's Rule. Nemcha Kipgen became Manipur's first woman Deputy Chief Minister.
- **Keralam:** The Union Cabinet approved renaming Kerala as Keralam. The Kerala (Alteration of Name) Bill, 2026 will be processed under Article 3.
- **Kerala urban policy:** Kerala became the first State to frame an urban policy.

## Parliament and elections

- **Om Birla:** Opposition MPs gave notice to remove Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla. Removal under Article 94(c) needs 14 days' notice. It also needs a majority of all the members of the House.
- **National Declaration 2026:** The Election Commission and the State Election Commissioners adopted it. They did so at their first joint conference in 27 years.
- **Rajya Sabha:** The Election Commission announced biennial elections to 37 Rajya Sabha seats in 10 States.

## Rules, guidelines and panels

- **20 February 2026:** The Information Technology (IT) Amendment Rules, 2026 took effect. The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) had notified them.
  - The time to take down unlawful content was cut to 3 hours.
  - Deepfakes and non-consensual intimate images must be removed within 2 hours.
  - AI-generated content must be labelled.
- **Vande Mataram:** A new Home Ministry protocol says that when both are played, the National Song Vande Mataram comes before the National Anthem.
- **Disaster Victim Identification:** The National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) issued India's first national guidelines on it. They marked 25 years of the 2001 Gujarat earthquake.
- **Transgender healthcare:** The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment formed an expert panel on it. This followed the Supreme Court's order in Jane Kaushik v. Union of India (2025).

## Government buildings

- **13 February 2026:** The Prime Minister inaugurated Seva Teerth. It houses the Prime Minister's Office (PMO), the National Security Council Secretariat and the Cabinet Secretariat. The PMO had worked from South Block for nearly eight decades. Seva Teerth was designed by architect Bimal Patel.
- **Rashtrapati Bhavan:** A statue of C. Rajagopalachari will replace that of architect Edwin Lutyens.

## Heritage and culture

- **Ol Chiki:** The President opened the centenary celebrations of the Ol Chiki script of the Santhali language. It was invented in 1925 by Pandit Raghunath Murmu.
- **Tamil Brahmi:** About 30 inscriptions in Tamil Brahmi, Prakrit and Sanskrit were found in tombs in the Valley of the Kings, Egypt.
- **Living root bridges:** India nominated Meghalaya's living root bridges (the Jingkieng Jri / Lyu Chrai Cultural Landscape) for the UNESCO World Heritage List.
