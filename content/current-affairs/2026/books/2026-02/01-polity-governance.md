## Gist
The Supreme Court widened the right to life again: in **Dr. Jaya Thakur v. Government of India** it held that **menstrual health** is part of **Article 21** and ordered free sanitary napkins and working toilets in every school. It also ruled that telecom **spectrum** belongs to the Union and cannot be treated as a company's asset in insolvency.

The map of government changed in several places. The Prime Minister's Office left **South Block** for the new **Seva Teerth** complex, the Union Cabinet agreed to rename Kerala as **Keralam**, **President's Rule ended in Manipur**, and a new **Frontier Nagaland Territorial Authority** was agreed for six eastern districts of Nagaland.

New rules worth remembering are the **IT Amendment Rules, 2026**, which give platforms only **three hours** to remove unlawful content, and the Home Ministry's protocol that **Vande Mataram** is played before the National Anthem. Heritage news came from the centenary of the **Ol Chiki** script and Tamil Brahmi inscriptions found in Egypt.

## Key facts
- In **Dr. Jaya Thakur v. Government of India (2026)**, the Supreme Court held that **menstrual health and hygiene** is part of the right to life under **Article 21**.
- The Supreme Court held that telecom **spectrum** is a public resource owned by the **Union of India** and cannot be sold as a company's asset under the **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016**.
- The Centre, the Nagaland government and the **Eastern Nagaland People's Organisation (ENPO)** signed a tripartite agreement to set up the **Frontier Nagaland Territorial Authority (FNTA)**.
- The FNTA covers **six eastern districts** (Tuensang, Mon, Kiphire, Longleng, Noklak and Shamator), has powers over **46 subjects**, and does not affect **Article 371(A)**.
- **Yumnam Khemchand Singh** became Chief Minister of **Manipur**, ending nearly a year of **President's Rule**.
- **Nemcha Kipgen** became Manipur's **first woman Deputy Chief Minister**.
- The Supreme Court Collegium approved retired judges as **ad hoc judges** of the **Allahabad High Court** under **Article 224A**.
- Opposition MPs gave notice to remove Lok Sabha Speaker **Om Birla**; removal under **Article 94(c)** needs **14 days' notice** and a majority of all the members of the House.
- A new Home Ministry protocol says that when both are played, the National Song **Vande Mataram** comes **before** the National Anthem.
- The **IT Amendment Rules, 2026**, notified by **MeitY**, took effect on **20 February 2026** and cut the time to take down unlawful content to **3 hours**.
- Under the same rules, **deepfakes and non-consensual intimate images** must be removed within **2 hours**, and AI-generated content must be labelled.
- On **13 February 2026** the Prime Minister inaugurated **Seva Teerth**, which houses the **PMO**, the National Security Council Secretariat and the Cabinet Secretariat; the PMO had worked from **South Block** for nearly eight decades.
- Seva Teerth was designed by architect **Bimal Patel**.
- The Union Cabinet approved renaming **Kerala** as **Keralam**; the Kerala (Alteration of Name) Bill, 2026 will be processed under **Article 3**.
- The Election Commission and the State Election Commissioners adopted the **National Declaration 2026** at their first joint conference in **27 years**.
- The Election Commission announced biennial elections to **37 Rajya Sabha seats** in **10 States**.
- **Kalpetta** in Wayanad, Kerala, became India's **first fully paperless district court**; it was inaugurated online by Chief Justice of India **Surya Kant**.
- **Kerala** became the **first State to frame an urban policy**.
- The **NDMA** issued India's **first national guidelines on Disaster Victim Identification**, marking 25 years of the **2001 Gujarat earthquake**.
- The Ministry of **Social Justice and Empowerment** formed an expert panel on **transgender healthcare**, following the Supreme Court's order in **Jane Kaushik v. Union of India (2025)**.
- A statue of **C. Rajagopalachari** will replace that of architect **Edwin Lutyens** at **Rashtrapati Bhavan**.
- The President opened the centenary celebrations of the **Ol Chiki** script of the **Santhali** language, invented in **1925** by **Pandit Raghunath Murmu**.
- About 30 inscriptions in **Tamil Brahmi**, Prakrit and Sanskrit were found in tombs in the **Valley of the Kings, Egypt**.
- India nominated Meghalaya's **living root bridges** (the Jingkieng Jri / Lyu Chrai Cultural Landscape) for the **UNESCO World Heritage List**.
