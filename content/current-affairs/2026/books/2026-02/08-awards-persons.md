## Gist
Indians and India-linked names won on world stages this month. The **Dalai Lama** received his first **Grammy** at the age of 90, the Manipuri film ***Boong*** won a **BAFTA**, teacher **Rouble Nagi** took the **Global Teacher Prize**, and climate scientist **Veerabhadran Ramanathan** was awarded the **Crafoord Prize**. At home, **Smriti Mandhana** was named BBC Indian Sportswoman of the Year.

Two women set firsts in public service: **Nivedita Dubey** on the board of the Airports Authority of India and **Thanya Nathan**, who is set to be Kerala's first blind woman judge.

February also carries a cluster of important days, from **World Cancer Day** (4 February) to **National Science Day** (28 February), each with a theme for 2026.

## Key facts
- The **Dalai Lama** won his **first Grammy**, at the **68th Grammy Awards**, for the audio album ***Meditations: The Reflections of His Holiness the Dalai Lama***.
- The Dalai Lama's Grammy came in the **Best Audio Book, Narration and Storytelling Recording** category; **Ustad Amjad Ali Khan** and his sons contributed music to the album.
- Indian-origin climate scientist **Veerabhadran Ramanathan** won the **Crafoord Prize 2026** in Geosciences, given by the **Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences**.
- Indian teacher **Rouble Nagi** won the **Global Teacher Prize 2026**, worth **USD 1 million**, given by the **Varkey Foundation**.
- **Smriti Mandhana** was named **BBC Indian Sportswoman of the Year 2025**.
- At the same BBC awards, chess player **Divya Deshmukh** was Emerging Player of the Year, **Preethi Pal** was Para-Sportswoman of the Year, and shooter **Anjali Bhagwat** received the Lifetime Achievement Award.
- The Manipuri film ***Boong***, directed by **Lakshmipriya Devi**, won **Best Children's and Family Film** at the **79th BAFTA Awards**, the first Indian film to win in this category.
- India was named **Country of the Year** at **BIOFACH 2026**, the organic products trade fair at **Nuremberg, Germany**; **APEDA** led India's participation.
- **Nivedita Dubey** became the **first woman member of the board of the Airports Authority of India**, as Member (Human Resources).
- **Thanya Nathan** is set to become **Kerala's first completely blind woman judge**.
- Defence analyst **Major Maroof Raza** (retired) died on **26 February 2026**.
- The **649th birth anniversary of Guru Ravidas** was marked on **1 February 2026** (Magha Purnima); he was born at **Seer Goverdhanpur, Varanasi**.
- **18 February 2026** marked **80 years** of the **Royal Indian Navy revolt** of 1946, which began on **HMIS Talwar** in Bombay.
- **World Cancer Day** is **4 February**; the theme for 2025–27 is **"United by Unique"**.
- **World Pulses Day** is **10 February**.
- **World Radio Day** is **13 February**; the 2026 theme was **"Radio and Artificial Intelligence: AI is a tool, not a voice"**.
- **World Day of Social Justice** is **20 February**.
- **International Mother Language Day** is **21 February**; the 2026 theme was **"Youth voices on multilingual education"**.
- **National Science Day** is **28 February**; the 2026 theme was **"Women in Science: Catalysing Viksit Bharat"**.
- **Rare Disease Day** is observed on **28 February** (29 February in leap years).
- **Mizoram** and **Arunachal Pradesh** celebrate their **Statehood Day on 20 February**; both became States in 1987.
- ***AI-Preneurs of India***, a coffee table book on 45 AI startups, was released by **NITI Aayog's Atal Innovation Mission**.
- ***Four Stars of Destiny*** is the unpublished memoir of former Army Chief **General M.M. Naravane**; the controversy over it led the Defence Ministry to draft rules on books by armed forces personnel.
