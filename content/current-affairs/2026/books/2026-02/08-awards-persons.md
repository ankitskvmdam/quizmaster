## Awards

- **Grammy:** The Dalai Lama won his first Grammy, at the age of 90.
  - It was for the audio album 'Meditations: The Reflections of His Holiness the Dalai Lama'.
  - The category was Best Audio Book, Narration and Storytelling Recording.
  - Ustad Amjad Ali Khan and his sons contributed music to the album.
- **BAFTA:** Boong was directed by Lakshmipriya Devi. It is the first Indian film to win in the Best Children's and Family Film category.
- **Crafoord Prize:** Veerabhadran Ramanathan is an Indian-origin climate scientist. The prize is given by the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences.
- **Global Teacher Prize:** Rouble Nagi is an Indian teacher. The prize is worth USD 1 million and is given by the Varkey Foundation.
- **BBC awards:** Besides Smriti Mandhana, three others were honoured at the same BBC awards.
  - Chess player Divya Deshmukh was Emerging Player of the Year.
  - Preethi Pal was Para-Sportswoman of the Year.
  - Shooter Anjali Bhagwat received the Lifetime Achievement Award.
- **BIOFACH 2026:** This organic products trade fair was held at Nuremberg, Germany. APEDA led India's participation.

## Appointments and firsts

- **Nivedita Dubey:** She became the first woman member of the board of the Airports Authority of India, as Member (Human Resources).
- **Thanya Nathan:** She is set to become Kerala's first completely blind woman judge.

## Anniversaries and deaths

- **1 February 2026:** The 649th birth anniversary of Guru Ravidas was marked, on Magha Purnima. He was born at Seer Goverdhanpur, Varanasi.
- **18 February 2026:** This day marked 80 years of the Royal Indian Navy revolt of 1946. The revolt began on HMIS Talwar in Bombay.
- **26 February 2026:** Defence analyst Major Maroof Raza (retired) died.

- **AI-Preneurs of India:** This coffee table book on 45 AI startups was released by NITI Aayog's Atal Innovation Mission.
