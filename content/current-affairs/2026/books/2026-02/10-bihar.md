## Legislature and Economic Survey

- **2 February 2026:** The Budget Session of the Bihar Legislature began with an address by Governor Arif Mohammed Khan. On the same day the Bihar Economic Survey 2025-26, the State's 20th, was tabled in the Assembly.
  - The Survey put Bihar's Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP) for 2024-25 at Rs 9,91,997 crore at current prices.
  - Real growth was 8.6 per cent.
  - Among districts, Patna, Begusarai and Munger have the highest per capita income. Sheohar has the lowest.
- **7 February 2026:** Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla launched the National e-Vidhan Application (NeVA) at the Bihar Legislative Assembly. The day was the Assembly's 105th foundation day.

## Bihar Budget 2026-27

- **3 February 2026:** Finance Minister Bijendra Prasad Yadav presented the Bihar Budget 2026-27.
  - Its total size is Rs 3,47,589.76 crore, the largest in Bihar's history. This is about Rs 30,694 crore more than in 2025-26.
  - Education got the highest allocation, about Rs 68,216 crore.
  - The fiscal deficit is estimated at about 2.99 per cent of GSDP.
  - Assistance to farmers under the Jananayak Karpoori Thakur Kisan Samman Nidhi was raised by Rs 3,000 to Rs 9,000 a year.
  - The Budget announced five new expressways and a model school in every panchayat.
- **Saat Nischay-3:** The Budget follows this roadmap. It aims to double per capita income and create 1 crore jobs.
  - Degree colleges will be opened in the 213 blocks (of Bihar's 534) that have none.
  - 55 institutions will become centres of excellence.

## Union Budget and Bihar

- **Ship repair:** The Union Budget 2026-27 announced a ship-repair facility at Patna on National Waterway 1 (the Ganga).
- **High-speed rail:** The proposed Varanasi–Siliguri high-speed rail corridor is to pass through Patna. It is one of seven corridors announced in the Union Budget.

## Other news

- **Naxal-free:** Bihar was declared Naxal-free after senior Maoist commander Suresh Koda surrendered. He surrendered before the Special Task Force in Munger district.
- **Riga sugar mill:** The mill in Sitamarhi district restarted after being closed for five years.
