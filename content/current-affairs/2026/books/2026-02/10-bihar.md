## Gist
February is budget month in Bihar. The **Bihar Economic Survey 2025-26**, the State's 20th, was tabled on **2 February 2026**, and the next day Finance Minister **Bijendra Prasad Yadav** presented the **Budget for 2026-27**. At **Rs 3,47,589.76 crore** it is the largest in the State's history, with education getting the biggest share and farmers an extra Rs 3,000 a year.

The Budget follows the **Saat Nischay-3** programme, under which degree colleges are to open in blocks that have none. The Assembly went digital with the **National e-Vidhan Application**.

The State was also declared **free of Naxalism** after a senior Maoist commander surrendered, and the Union Budget brought Bihar a ship-repair facility at Patna and a place on a high-speed rail corridor.

## Key facts
- The **Bihar Budget 2026-27** was presented on **3 February 2026** by Finance Minister **Bijendra Prasad Yadav**.
- The Budget's total size is **Rs 3,47,589.76 crore**, the largest in Bihar's history and about Rs 30,694 crore more than in 2025-26.
- **Education** got the highest allocation in the Budget, about **Rs 68,216 crore**.
- The Budget estimates the **fiscal deficit** at about **2.99 per cent of GSDP**.
- Assistance to farmers under the **Jananayak Karpoori Thakur Kisan Samman Nidhi** was raised by Rs 3,000 to **Rs 9,000 a year**.
- The Budget follows the **Saat Nischay-3** roadmap, which aims to double per capita income and create **1 crore jobs**.
- The Budget announced **five new expressways** and a **model school in every panchayat**.
- The **Bihar Economic Survey 2025-26**, the State's **20th**, was tabled in the Assembly on **2 February 2026**.
- The Survey put Bihar's GSDP for 2024-25 at **Rs 9,91,997 crore** at current prices, with real growth of **8.6 per cent**.
- According to the Survey, **Patna, Begusarai and Munger** have the highest per capita income among districts, and **Sheohar** the lowest.
- Under **Saat Nischay-3**, degree colleges will be opened in the **213 blocks** (of Bihar's **534**) that have none, and 55 institutions will become centres of excellence.
- Lok Sabha Speaker **Om Birla** launched the **National e-Vidhan Application (NeVA)** at the Bihar Legislative Assembly on **7 February 2026**, the Assembly's **105th foundation day**.
- Bihar was declared **Naxal-free** after senior Maoist commander **Suresh Koda** surrendered before the Special Task Force in **Munger** district.
- The Union Budget 2026-27 announced a **ship-repair facility at Patna** on **National Waterway 1** (the Ganga).
- The proposed **Varanasi–Siliguri high-speed rail corridor**, one of seven announced in the Union Budget, is to pass through **Patna**.
- The **Riga sugar mill** in **Sitamarhi** district restarted after being closed for five years.
- The Budget Session of the Bihar Legislature began on 2 February 2026 with an address by Governor **Arif Mohammed Khan**.
