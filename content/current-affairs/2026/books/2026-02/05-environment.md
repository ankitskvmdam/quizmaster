## Wildlife

- **2 February:** The theme of World Wetlands Day in 2026 was "Wetlands and Traditional Knowledge: Celebrating Cultural Heritage".
- **Gujarat:** A tiger has settled in the Ratanmahal and Jambughoda forests of Gujarat. This makes it the only State with Asiatic lions, tigers and leopards.
- **Cheetahs:** Nine cheetahs from Botswana arrived at Kuno National Park, Madhya Pradesh. This took India's cheetah count to 48.
- **Eurasian otter:** It was photographed along the Sindh Canal. This is the first direct photographic evidence of the species in the Kashmir Valley.
- **Global Big Cat Summit:** The Union Budget announced it. India will host the first such summit in 2026, with 95 range countries invited. Range countries are those where big cats live in the wild.
- **Turtle trails:** The Union Budget proposed them at nesting sites in Odisha, Karnataka and Kerala.
- **Bee Corridor:** The National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) launched this initiative to plant flowering native trees along national highways for pollinators.
- **India and Nepal:** They signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) on cooperation in forests, wildlife and biodiversity conservation across their border.
- **Bird atlas:** Goa became the second State to publish a bird atlas, after Kerala.

## Plants and new species

- **Lyriothemis keralensis:** This new dragonfly species was identified in Kerala.
- **Hoya nagaensis:** This new flowering plant was found in Phek district, Nagaland. It is rated Critically Endangered.
- **NeophyteID:** Kerala launched this AI-based app to identify invasive plant species.
- **Tulip garden:** CSIR-IHBT opened the country's second tulip garden at Palampur, Himachal Pradesh. The first is in Srinagar.

## Climate and reports

- **NITI Aayog:** It released 11 reports on scenarios towards Viksit Bharat (a developed India) and Net Zero.
  - The economy is to reach USD 30 trillion by 2047.
  - Net zero emissions are to be reached by 2070.
- **State of India's Environment 2026:** This report of the Centre for Science and Environment said seven of nine planetary boundaries are breached. Planetary boundaries are safe limits for the Earth's natural systems. Ocean acidification is the seventh boundary to be crossed.
- **Thwaites Glacier:** This glacier in West Antarctica, called the "Doomsday Glacier", is thinning and retreating fast.

## Clean energy

- **Carbon capture:** The Union Budget set aside Rs 20,000 crore over five years for a Carbon Capture, Utilisation and Storage (CCUS) scheme.
- **Green ammonia:** The Solar Energy Corporation of India (SECI) held a successful green ammonia auction under the SIGHT programme of the National Green Hydrogen Mission.
- **Offshore wind:** India and the United Kingdom launched an Offshore Wind Taskforce.
- **Green urea:** India's first large indigenous green urea plant will come up at the Pudimadaka Green Hydrogen Hub, Andhra Pradesh, with NTPC Green Energy.

## Projects, mining and disasters

- **Great Nicobar:** The National Green Tribunal approved the Rs 81,000 crore Great Nicobar project with strict environmental conditions.
- **Rat-hole mine blast:** At least 18 workers died in a blast in an illegal rat-hole coal mine in East Jaintia Hills, Meghalaya. Rat-hole mining digs narrow tunnels to reach coal. The blast showed that this banned practice goes on.
- **Tidal flooding:** Kerala declared tidal flooding a State-specific disaster.
- **Mud volcano:** A mud volcano dormant for over two decades erupted at Diglipur in North Andaman.
- **Dam safety:** The second International Conference on Dam Safety was held in Bengaluru.
