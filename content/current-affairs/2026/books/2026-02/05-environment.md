## Gist
Wildlife gave the month its headlines. A tiger settled in **Gujarat**, which now holds lions, tigers and leopards together; **nine cheetahs** arrived from **Botswana**; and the **Eurasian otter** was photographed in the Kashmir Valley for the first time. The Union Budget promised **turtle trails** and the first **Global Big Cat Summit**.

On climate, the Budget set aside **Rs 20,000 crore** for carbon capture, and **NITI Aayog** published its scenarios for reaching a developed India and **net zero by 2070**. A report by the Centre for Science and Environment warned that **seven of the nine planetary boundaries** have been crossed.

The **National Green Tribunal** cleared the **Great Nicobar** project with conditions, and a blast in an illegal **rat-hole coal mine** in Meghalaya showed that the banned practice goes on.

## Key facts
- The theme of **World Wetlands Day** (**2 February**) in 2026 was **"Wetlands and Traditional Knowledge: Celebrating Cultural Heritage"**.
- A tiger has settled in the **Ratanmahal** and Jambughoda forests of **Gujarat**, making it the only State with **Asiatic lions, tigers and leopards**.
- The Union Budget proposed **turtle trails** at nesting sites in **Odisha, Karnataka and Kerala**.
- India will host the first **Global Big Cat Summit** in **2026**, with **95** range countries invited.
- The Budget set aside **Rs 20,000 crore** over five years for a **Carbon Capture, Utilisation and Storage (CCUS)** scheme.
- **NITI Aayog** released **11 reports** on scenarios towards Viksit Bharat and Net Zero: a **USD 30 trillion** economy by **2047** and net zero emissions by **2070**.
- The **Thwaites Glacier** in **West Antarctica**, called the **"Doomsday Glacier"**, is thinning and retreating fast.
- A new dragonfly species, ***Lyriothemis keralensis***, was identified in **Kerala**.
- A new flowering plant, ***Hoya nagaensis***, was found in **Phek district, Nagaland**; it is rated **Critically Endangered**.
- The **Eurasian otter** was photographed along the Sindh Canal, the first direct photographic evidence of the species in the **Kashmir Valley**.
- The **National Green Tribunal** approved the **Rs 81,000 crore Great Nicobar project** with strict environmental conditions.
- The **NHAI** launched the **Bee Corridor** initiative to plant flowering native trees along national highways for pollinators.
- The **State of India's Environment 2026** report of the **Centre for Science and Environment** said **seven of nine planetary boundaries** are breached; **ocean acidification** is the seventh.
- India and **Nepal** signed an MoU on cooperation in forests, wildlife and biodiversity conservation across their border.
- **Nine cheetahs from Botswana** arrived at **Kuno National Park**, Madhya Pradesh, taking India's cheetah count to **48**.
- **SECI** held a successful **green ammonia** auction under the **SIGHT** programme of the National Green Hydrogen Mission.
- India and the **United Kingdom** launched an **Offshore Wind Taskforce**.
- **Kerala** declared **tidal flooding** a **State-specific disaster**.
- A **mud volcano** dormant for over two decades erupted at **Diglipur in North Andaman**.
- **Goa** became the **second State** to publish a **bird atlas**, after **Kerala**.
- Kerala launched **NeophyteID**, an AI-based app to identify **invasive plant species**.
- India's first large indigenous **green urea plant** will come up at the **Pudimadaka Green Hydrogen Hub**, Andhra Pradesh, with **NTPC Green Energy**.
- **CSIR-IHBT** opened the country's **second tulip garden** at **Palampur**, Himachal Pradesh; the first is in Srinagar.
- At least **18 workers** died in a blast in an illegal **rat-hole coal mine** in **East Jaintia Hills, Meghalaya**.
- The second **International Conference on Dam Safety** was held in **Bengaluru**.
