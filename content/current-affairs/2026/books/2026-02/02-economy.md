## Gist
The month opened with the **Union Budget 2026-27**, presented on **1 February 2026** and built around three **Kartavyas** (duties). It kept income tax slabs unchanged, set the fiscal deficit at **4.3 per cent of GDP**, raised capital spending to **Rs 12.2 lakh crore**, and announced seven high-speed rail corridors. The report of the **16th Finance Commission**, tabled with it, kept the States' share of central taxes at **41 per cent**.

India's statistics were rebuilt on new base years: the **Consumer Price Index** moved to **2024** and the **GDP series** to **2022-23**. The Reserve Bank left the **repo rate at 5.25 per cent** and doubled the collateral-free loan limit for small enterprises.

Other items to remember are the second **National Monetisation Pipeline**, the easier definition of a startup, the opening of the full **Delhi–Meerut Namo Bharat** corridor, and the many firsts of **V.O. Chidambaranar Port**.

## Key facts
- The **Union Budget 2026-27** was presented on **1 February 2026**; it was the first Budget prepared in **Kartavya Bhawan** and is built on three **Kartavyas**.
- The Budget put the **fiscal deficit** for 2026-27 at **4.3 per cent of GDP** (4.4 per cent in the revised estimates for 2025-26) and the debt-to-GDP ratio at **55.6 per cent**.
- Budget **capital expenditure** was raised to **Rs 12.2 lakh crore**; total expenditure is **Rs 53.5 lakh crore**.
- The **Income Tax Act, 2025** replaces the Income Tax Act, 1961 from **1 April 2026**; tax slabs were left unchanged.
- The Budget announced **seven high-speed rail corridors** and a new **Dedicated Freight Corridor from Dankuni to Surat**.
- **Rare Earth Corridors** were announced in **Odisha, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu**.
- The Budget announced **Biopharma SHAKTI** with **Rs 10,000 crore** over five years and raised the outlay of the **Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme** to **Rs 40,000 crore**.
- The **16th Finance Commission**, chaired by **Arvind Panagariya**, covers **2026–31** and kept the States' share of the divisible pool at **41 per cent**.
- The 16th Finance Commission added a new criterion, **contribution to GDP**, with a weight of **10 per cent**, and recommended grants of **Rs 9.47 lakh crore**.
- The RBI's Monetary Policy Committee kept the **repo rate at 5.25 per cent** in February 2026 with a **neutral** stance.
- The RBI raised the limit for **collateral-free loans to micro and small enterprises** from Rs 10 lakh to **Rs 20 lakh**, from 1 April 2026.
- MoSPI changed the **base year of the Consumer Price Index** from 2012 to **2024**; the basket grew from 299 to **358 items**.
- Under the new CPI series, retail inflation in **January 2026** was **2.75 per cent**, and the weight of food and beverages fell to **36.75 per cent**.
- MoSPI released the new **GDP series with base year 2022-23**, which put real GDP growth in 2025-26 at **7.6 per cent**.
- **DPIIT** doubled the turnover limit for recognised startups to **Rs 200 crore** and created a **Deep Tech Startup** category (up to **20 years** old, turnover up to Rs 300 crore).
- **National Monetisation Pipeline 2.0**, prepared by **NITI Aayog** for 2025–30, has a potential of **Rs 16.72 lakh crore**.
- **EASE 9.0**, the reform agenda for public sector banks, rests on four pillars called **R.I.S.E.** (Risk and Resilience, Innovation, Socio-economic Impact, Excellence).
- A pilot to pay the food subsidy under **PMGKAY** in **central bank digital currency** began in **Puducherry**.
- The **State Bank of India** launched **CHAKRA**, a centre of excellence for financing sunrise sectors.
- The Government imposed a **minimum import price** on **Penicillin G** and related drug inputs for one year to curb cheap imports.
- India's **first mega shipbuilding cluster** will come up at **Thoothukudi**, Tamil Nadu, as a joint venture of V.O. Chidambaranar Port Authority and SIPCOT.
- **V.O. Chidambaranar Port** (Tuticorin) became the first Indian port to deploy an **anti-drone system**; it is also the first to produce **green hydrogen** on site.
- On **22 February 2026** the Prime Minister opened the full **82 km Delhi–Ghaziabad–Meerut Namo Bharat** corridor together with the **Meerut Metro**.
- Indian Railways commissioned a record **472.3 route km of Kavach 4.0** in a single day.
- MoSPI launched the **PAIMANA** portal to monitor central infrastructure projects worth **Rs 150 crore** and above.
- The **Startup India Fund of Funds 2.0** was approved with a corpus of **Rs 10,000 crore**.
- The **Food Corporation of India** and the **World Food Programme** signed an MoU for the supply of **200,000 tonnes of rice** for humanitarian aid.
