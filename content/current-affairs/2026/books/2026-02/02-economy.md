## Union Budget and the Finance Commission

- **1 February 2026:** The Union Budget 2026-27 was presented. It was the first Budget prepared in Kartavya Bhawan. It is built around three Kartavyas (duties).
  - The fiscal deficit for 2026-27 was put at 4.3 per cent of GDP. In the revised estimates for 2025-26 it was 4.4 per cent.
  - The debt-to-GDP ratio was put at 55.6 per cent.
  - Capital expenditure was raised to Rs 12.2 lakh crore. Total expenditure is Rs 53.5 lakh crore.
  - The Income Tax Act, 2025 replaces the Income Tax Act, 1961 from 1 April 2026. Income tax slabs were left unchanged.
  - Seven high-speed rail corridors were announced, along with a new Dedicated Freight Corridor from Dankuni to Surat.
  - Biopharma SHAKTI was announced with Rs 10,000 crore over five years.
  - The outlay of the Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme was raised to Rs 40,000 crore.
- **16th Finance Commission:** Its report was tabled with the Budget. It is chaired by Arvind Panagariya and covers 2026–31.
  - It kept the States' share of the divisible pool (central taxes shared with States) at 41 per cent.
  - It added a new criterion, contribution to GDP, with a weight of 10 per cent.
  - It recommended grants of Rs 9.47 lakh crore.

## Reserve Bank and banking

- **February 2026:** The Monetary Policy Committee of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) kept the repo rate at 5.25 per cent. The repo rate is the rate at which the RBI lends to banks. The stance stayed neutral.
- **1 April 2026:** The RBI raised the limit for collateral-free loans to micro and small enterprises from Rs 10 lakh to Rs 20 lakh. Such loans need no security from the borrower.
- **EASE 9.0:** This is the reform agenda for public sector banks. It rests on four pillars called R.I.S.E. (Risk and Resilience, Innovation, Socio-economic Impact, Excellence).
- **Digital currency pilot:** A pilot to pay the food subsidy under PMGKAY in central bank digital currency began in Puducherry.
- **CHAKRA:** The State Bank of India launched CHAKRA, a centre of excellence for financing sunrise sectors.

## Statistics

- **Consumer Price Index (CPI):** The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) changed its base year from 2012 to 2024. The basket grew from 299 to 358 items.
  - Under the new series, retail inflation in January 2026 was 2.75 per cent.
  - The weight of food and beverages fell to 36.75 per cent.
- **GDP series:** MoSPI released the new GDP series with base year 2022-23. It put real GDP growth in 2025-26 at 7.6 per cent.
- **PAIMANA:** MoSPI launched the PAIMANA portal to monitor central infrastructure projects worth Rs 150 crore and above.

## Startups, industry and trade

- **Startups:** The Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) doubled the turnover limit for recognised startups to Rs 200 crore.
  - It created a Deep Tech Startup category, for startups up to 20 years old with a turnover up to Rs 300 crore.
- **Startup India Fund of Funds 2.0:** It was approved with a corpus of Rs 10,000 crore.
- **Rare Earth Corridors:** They were announced in Odisha, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu.
- **Penicillin G:** The Government imposed a minimum import price on Penicillin G and related drug inputs for one year. A minimum import price is the lowest price allowed for imports. The aim is to curb cheap imports.
- **Rice for aid:** The Food Corporation of India and the World Food Programme signed an MoU (memorandum of understanding). It is for the supply of 200,000 tonnes of rice for humanitarian aid.

## Infrastructure, railways and ports

- **22 February 2026:** The Prime Minister opened the full 82 km Delhi–Ghaziabad–Meerut Namo Bharat corridor together with the Meerut Metro.
- **National Monetisation Pipeline 2.0:** NITI Aayog prepared this second pipeline for 2025–30. Its potential is Rs 16.72 lakh crore.
- **Kavach 4.0:** Indian Railways commissioned a record 472.3 route km of Kavach 4.0 in a single day.
- **V.O. Chidambaranar Port (Tuticorin):** It had several firsts.
  - It became the first Indian port to deploy an anti-drone system.
  - It is also the first to produce green hydrogen on site.
  - India's first mega shipbuilding cluster will come up at Thoothukudi, Tamil Nadu. It is a joint venture of V.O. Chidambaranar Port Authority and SIPCOT.
