## Trade

- **India and the US:** The two countries issued a joint statement on the framework of an Interim Trade Agreement.
  - The US reciprocal tariff on Indian goods was cut from 25 per cent to 18 per cent.
  - The extra 25 per cent duty linked to Russian oil was removed.
- **Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC):** India and the GCC signed the terms of reference for a free trade agreement. Terms of reference set the scope of the talks. The GCC is India's largest trading partner bloc, with trade of USD 178.56 billion in 2024-25.
- **Israel:** The first round of trade talks with Israel was completed.

## Visits and partnerships

- **Malaysia:** The Prime Minister visited Malaysia.
  - The Malaysia–India Digital Council was set up.
  - Malaysia joined the International Big Cat Alliance.
  - India announced Thiruvalluvar Scholarships for Malaysian nationals.
  - India's Unified Payments Interface (UPI) is being linked with Malaysia's DuitNow QR.
- **France:** The French President visited India. India–France ties were raised to a Special Global Strategic Partnership.
- **Brazil:** The Brazilian President made a state visit. India and Brazil set a trade target of USD 30 billion by 2030.
- **Israel:** The Prime Minister's visit to Israel raised ties to a Special Strategic Partnership for Peace, Innovation & Prosperity.
- **Greece:** India and Greece signed a Joint Declaration of Intent on defence industrial cooperation. Greece will post a liaison officer at the IFC-IOR in Gurugram.
- **Tata and Airbus:** Their H125 helicopter final assembly line was inaugurated. It is India's first private-sector helicopter facility.
- **European Union:** The EU opened its first Legal Gateway Office in a partner country in New Delhi.

## Groupings and India's roles

- **BRICS:** India chairs BRICS in 2026 and will host the 18th BRICS Summit in 2026. The first BRICS Sherpa meeting was held in New Delhi, on the theme "Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability". A Sherpa is a leader's personal representative at a summit.
- **India–Arab meeting:** The second India–Arab Foreign Ministers' Meeting was held in New Delhi.
- **Kimberley Process:** India took over as chair of the Kimberley Process for 2026. The body has 60 participants. It works to stop trade in conflict diamonds, which are diamonds sold to fund wars.
- **International Energy Agency (IEA):** The IEA welcomed progress on India's request for full membership. India has been an associate member since 2017.
- **Board of Peace:** India attended the first meeting of the US-led Board of Peace on Gaza as an observer. The meeting was held in Washington D.C.

## Security and conflict

- **5 February 2026:** The New START treaty expired. It was the last nuclear arms control treaty between the US and Russia. It limited each side to 1,550 deployed warheads.
- **End of February 2026:** The US (Operation Epic Fury) and Israel (Operation Lion's Roar) struck Iran. Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei was reported killed.
- **Strait of Hormuz:** Iran briefly closed parts of the Strait of Hormuz for a naval drill. The drill was named "Smart Control of the Strait of Hormuz".
- **Arctic Sentry:** The North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) launched this mission to strengthen its defence in the Arctic.
- **Ottawa Convention:** Poland formally withdrew from the Ottawa Convention (1997), which bans anti-personnel landmines.
- **Sawalkot:** Pakistan sought consultations under the Indus Waters Treaty on the 1,856 MW Sawalkot hydroelectric project. The project is on the Chenab in Ramban district, Jammu and Kashmir.

## Elections and leaders

- **Bangladesh:** The Bangladesh Nationalist Party (BNP), led by Tarique Rahman, won the general election.
- **Netherlands:** Rob Jetten, aged 38, became the youngest Prime Minister of the Netherlands.
- **Barbados:** Mia Mottley won a third consecutive term as Prime Minister of Barbados.
