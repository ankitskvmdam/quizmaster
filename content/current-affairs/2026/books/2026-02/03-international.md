## Gist
Trade came first. India and the United States announced the framework of an **interim trade agreement**, under which the US tariff on Indian goods fell to **18 per cent**. India and the **Gulf Cooperation Council** signed the terms of reference for a free trade agreement, and the first round of trade talks with **Israel** was completed.

It was a month of visits. The Prime Minister went to **Malaysia** and **Israel**; the Presidents of **France** and **Brazil** came to India. Ties with France became a **Special Global Strategic Partnership**, and India, which chairs **BRICS** in 2026, held the first Sherpa meeting.

Elsewhere, the **New START** treaty between the US and Russia ran out, the **BNP** won the election in Bangladesh, and the month ended with **US and Israeli strikes on Iran**.

## Key facts
- Under the India–US trade understanding, the US **reciprocal tariff** on Indian goods was cut from 25 per cent to **18 per cent** and the extra 25 per cent duty linked to Russian oil was removed.
- India and the US issued a joint statement on the framework of an **Interim Trade Agreement**.
- The **New START** treaty, the last nuclear arms control treaty between the **US and Russia**, expired on **5 February 2026**; it limited each side to **1,550** deployed warheads.
- India and the **Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC)** signed the **terms of reference** for a free trade agreement; the GCC is India's largest trading partner bloc, with trade of **USD 178.56 billion** in 2024-25.
- The **second India–Arab Foreign Ministers' Meeting** was held in **New Delhi**.
- During the Prime Minister's visit to **Malaysia**, the **Malaysia–India Digital Council** was set up and Malaysia joined the **International Big Cat Alliance**.
- India announced **Thiruvalluvar Scholarships** for Malaysian nationals; India's **UPI** is being linked with Malaysia's **DuitNow QR**.
- India took over as **chair of the Kimberley Process for 2026**; the body, which has 60 participants, works to stop trade in **conflict diamonds**.
- India and **Greece** signed a **Joint Declaration of Intent** on defence industrial cooperation; Greece will post a liaison officer at the **IFC-IOR in Gurugram**.
- **NATO** launched a mission called **Arctic Sentry** to strengthen its defence in the Arctic.
- The **Bangladesh Nationalist Party (BNP)**, led by **Tarique Rahman**, won the general election in **Bangladesh**.
- During the French President's visit, India–France ties were raised to a **Special Global Strategic Partnership**.
- The **H125 helicopter final assembly line** of **Tata and Airbus**, India's first private-sector helicopter facility, was inaugurated.
- During the Brazilian President's state visit, India and **Brazil** set a trade target of **USD 30 billion by 2030**.
- India will host the **18th BRICS Summit** in **2026**; the first BRICS Sherpa meeting was held in **New Delhi** on the theme "Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability".
- The Prime Minister's visit to **Israel** raised ties to a **Special Strategic Partnership for Peace, Innovation & Prosperity**.
- India attended the first meeting of the US-led **Board of Peace** on Gaza, held in **Washington D.C.**, as an **observer**.
- **Poland** formally withdrew from the **Ottawa Convention** (1997), which bans anti-personnel landmines.
- The **International Energy Agency (IEA)** welcomed progress on India's request for full membership; India has been an **associate member since 2017**.
- **Iran** briefly closed parts of the **Strait of Hormuz** for a naval drill named "Smart Control of the Strait of Hormuz".
- At the end of February 2026, the **US (Operation Epic Fury)** and **Israel (Operation Lion's Roar)** struck Iran, and Supreme Leader **Ayatollah Ali Khamenei** was reported killed.
- **Rob Jetten**, aged 38, became the **youngest Prime Minister of the Netherlands**.
- **Mia Mottley** won a **third consecutive term** as Prime Minister of **Barbados**.
- The **European Union** opened its first **Legal Gateway Office** in a partner country in **New Delhi**.
- Pakistan sought consultations under the Indus Waters Treaty on the **1,856 MW Sawalkot** hydroelectric project on the **Chenab** in Ramban district, Jammu and Kashmir.
