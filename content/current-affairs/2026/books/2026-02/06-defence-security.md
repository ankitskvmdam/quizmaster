## Budget, industry and missile tests

- **Budget 2026-27:** The Union Budget gave defence its highest-ever allocation of Rs 7.85 lakh crore.
- **Solid Fuel Ducted Ramjet (SFDR):** The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) flight-tested this engine technology from the Integrated Test Range, Chandipur, Odisha. It is needed for long-range air-to-air missiles.
- **Agni-III:** This intermediate range ballistic missile was test-fired from Chandipur under the Strategic Forces Command. Its range is over 3,000 km.
- **Yantra India Limited:** This defence public sector undertaking (PSU) was formed from the Ordnance Factory Board. It was given Miniratna Category-I status.

## Navy

- **15 to 25 February 2026:** Visakhapatnam hosted three naval events together.
  - The 13th Exercise MILAN was held with over 70 countries. Germany, the Philippines and the UAE took part for the first time.
  - The International Fleet Review 2026 was held.
  - The IONS Conclave of Chiefs was held. India chairs the Indian Ocean Naval Symposium (IONS) for 2025–2027.
- **Combined Task Force 154:** The Indian Navy took command of it for the first time. It is the training task force of the Combined Maritime Forces, based in Bahrain.
- **P-8I:** The Defence Procurement Board cleared the purchase of six more P-8I maritime patrol aircraft from the US. The Navy already flies 12.
- **Anjadip:** It is being commissioned into the Navy. It is the third of eight anti-submarine warfare shallow water craft built by GRSE, Kolkata.
- **Krishna:** The Navy's first indigenously designed cadet training ship was launched at the L&T shipyard, Kattupalli.
- **Indian Coast Guard:** It opened a waterjet production and testing facility in Goa. Its Raising Day is 1 February.

## Army and Air Force

Joint exercises with other countries:

| Exercise | Between | Place |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 13th KHANJAR | Special forces of India and Kyrgyzstan | Missamari, Assam |
| 16th VAJRA PRAHAR (began) | Special forces of India and the United States | Bakloh, Himachal Pradesh |
| 7th DHARMA GUARDIAN (began) | Armies of India and Japan | Chaubattia, Uttarakhand |
| Joint air exercise | IAF and the Royal Thai Air Force | Over the Indian Ocean Region |

- **Exercise Vajra Ghaat:** The Army held it at Pokhran. It tested the K9 Vajra self-propelled gun.
- **Exercise Agni Pariksha:** It was a joint drill of the Army and the Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP) in Arunachal Pradesh.
- **Captain Hansja Sharma:** She became the first woman Army pilot qualified to fly the Rudra armed helicopter.
- **British pilots:** For the first time, Indian Air Force (IAF) instructors will train British pilots. The training will be at RAF Valley in the UK.
- **Assam Rifles:** It plans to induct Indian dog breeds such as the Tangkhul Hui (Manipur) and the Kombai (Tamil Nadu).

## Internal security

- **PRAHAAR:** The Home Ministry unveiled PRAHAAR, India's first National Counter-Terrorism Policy and Strategy.
- **Ricin plot:** The National Investigation Agency (NIA) took over the probe into India's first alleged bioterrorism plot. The plot involved the poison ricin.
