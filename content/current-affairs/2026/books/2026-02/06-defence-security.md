## Gist
The Union Budget gave defence its **highest-ever allocation, Rs 7.85 lakh crore**. DRDO followed with two tests from Chandipur: the **Solid Fuel Ducted Ramjet**, the engine technology for long-range air-to-air missiles, and the **Agni-III** ballistic missile.

**Visakhapatnam** hosted three naval events together: the **International Fleet Review**, **Exercise MILAN 2026** and the conclave of the Indian Ocean Naval Symposium. The Navy also took command of a multinational training task force for the first time. Army exercises were held with **Kyrgyzstan, the United States and Japan**.

On internal security, the Home Ministry unveiled **PRAHAAR**, India's first national counter-terrorism policy and strategy.

## Key facts
- The Union Budget 2026-27 gave defence its **highest-ever allocation of Rs 7.85 lakh crore**.
- **DRDO** flight-tested **Solid Fuel Ducted Ramjet (SFDR)** technology from the Integrated Test Range, **Chandipur**, Odisha; it is needed for long-range **air-to-air missiles**.
- The intermediate range ballistic missile **Agni-III** (range over **3,000 km**) was test-fired from Chandipur under the **Strategic Forces Command**.
- The **13th Exercise KHANJAR**, between the special forces of India and **Kyrgyzstan**, was held at **Missamari, Assam**.
- **Yantra India Limited**, a defence PSU formed from the Ordnance Factory Board, was given **Miniratna Category-I** status.
- The Defence Procurement Board cleared the purchase of **six more P-8I** maritime patrol aircraft from the US; the Navy already flies 12.
- For the first time, **Indian Air Force instructors** will train **British pilots**, at **RAF Valley** in the UK.
- **Assam Rifles** plans to induct Indian dog breeds such as the **Tangkhul Hui** (Manipur) and the **Kombai** (Tamil Nadu).
- The **Indian Navy** took command of **Combined Task Force 154** for the first time; it is the training task force of the **Combined Maritime Forces**, based in **Bahrain**.
- **Exercise Vajra Ghaat** was held by the Army at **Pokhran**, testing the **K9 Vajra** self-propelled gun.
- The **13th Exercise MILAN** was held at **Visakhapatnam** from **15 to 25 February 2026** with over 70 countries; **Germany, the Philippines and the UAE** took part for the first time.
- Visakhapatnam also hosted the **International Fleet Review 2026** and the **IONS Conclave of Chiefs**; India chairs the Indian Ocean Naval Symposium for **2025–2027**.
- ***Anjadip***, the third of eight **anti-submarine warfare shallow water craft** built by **GRSE, Kolkata**, is being commissioned into the Navy.
- ***Krishna***, the Navy's first indigenously designed **cadet training ship**, was launched at the **L&T shipyard, Kattupalli**.
- The Home Ministry unveiled **PRAHAAR**, India's first **National Counter-Terrorism Policy and Strategy**.
- The **16th Exercise VAJRA PRAHAR**, between the special forces of India and the **United States**, began at **Bakloh, Himachal Pradesh**.
- The **7th Exercise DHARMA GUARDIAN**, between the armies of India and **Japan**, began at **Chaubattia, Uttarakhand**.
- **Exercise Agni Pariksha** was a joint drill of the **Army and the ITBP** in **Arunachal Pradesh**.
- **Captain Hansja Sharma** became the first woman Army pilot qualified to fly the **Rudra** armed helicopter.
- The **Indian Coast Guard**, whose Raising Day is **1 February**, opened a waterjet production and testing facility in **Goa**.
- The **NIA** took over the probe into India's first alleged **bioterrorism** plot, which involved the poison **ricin**.
- The **IAF** and the **Royal Thai Air Force** held a joint air exercise over the Indian Ocean Region.
