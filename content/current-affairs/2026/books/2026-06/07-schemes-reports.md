## Gist
The biggest change in rural welfare was prepared this month: the new employment guarantee law, **VB-G RAM G**, replaces **MGNREGS** from 1 July 2026 and promises **125 days** of work. The Agriculture Ministry ran a month-long **Khet Bachao Abhiyan**, and the 23rd instalment of **PM-KISAN** was released.

In health, the Ministry launched the **SUMAN Roadmap 2030** to cut maternal deaths, widened the **Anemia Mukt Bharat** strategy and relaunched **Aarogya Setu** as a national health app. Two older schemes marked anniversaries: **PM SVANidhi** (six years) and **PMSMA** (ten years).

Among rankings, India stood **70th** in the Energy Transition Index and **13th** in the QS World Future Skills Index.

## Key facts
- The **Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin)**, or **VB-G RAM G**, replaces **MGNREGS** from **1 July 2026** and guarantees **125 days** of wage employment to a rural household in a year.
- The Centre announced an interim allocation of **Rs 95,692 crore** for VB-G RAM G; the cost is shared **60:40** between the Centre and most States.
- The Union Agriculture Minister launched the **Khet Bachao Abhiyan**, a campaign on soil health and safe use of fertilisers, from **Raisen district, Madhya Pradesh**; it ran from **1 to 30 June 2026**.
- **PM SVANidhi**, the loan scheme for street vendors run by the **Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs**, completed **six years**; it was launched on **1 June 2020**.
- PM SVANidhi gives loans in three steps of **Rs 15,000, Rs 25,000 and Rs 50,000**; lending has been extended to March 2030.
- The **Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA)**, launched on **9 June 2016**, completed ten years; it gives free antenatal check-ups on the **9th of every month**.
- The Health Minister launched the **SUMAN Roadmap 2030**, which aims to bring the maternal mortality ratio **below 70** per lakh live births by 2030.
- The SUMAN Roadmap focuses on **130 districts** in **13 high-focus States**.
- The **Anemia Mukt Bharat** strategy, launched in 2018, was upgraded from **6x6x6 to 7x7x7**.
- **Aarogya Setu 2.0** was launched as a national health app under the **Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission**, run by the **National Health Authority**.
- The Prime Minister released the **23rd instalment of PM-KISAN**, over **Rs 18,880 crore** for about **9.44 crore** farmers, at an event in **West Bengal**.
- **Mission Senehjori** was launched for the **Muga silk** sector of **Assam**.
- The **Navachar Mantra** initiative of the **Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship** will find and mentor grassroots innovators.
- The **MAHA Water Mission** was launched by the **Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF)** and the **Ministry of Jal Shakti**, with **Rs 200 crore** over five years.
- The **Jan Samarth Portal**, a single window for credit-linked government schemes launched in June 2022, completed **four years**.
- The Union Rural Development Minister launched the AI-enabled **Rural Internal Audit Portal** at the Rashtriya Gramin Vikas Sammelan in New Delhi.
- The pilot of the **PM Family Care Tracker** was launched at **Gandhinagar, Gujarat**.
- The **SAPLING Dialogue 2026** on nutrition and food processing was organised at **Ahmedabad** by the **Ministry of Food Processing Industries** and the World Bank Group.
- India ranked **70th** in the **Energy Transition Index 2026** of the **World Economic Forum**; **Sweden** was first.
- India ranked **13th** in the **QS World Future Skills Index 2027**; the **United States** was first.
- **Jaipur** was ranked **18th** in Time Out's list of the world's best cities for art and culture in 2026; **London** topped the list.
- MoSPI released the **SDGs National Indicator Framework Progress Report 2026** on Statistics Day; the framework has **277** national indicators.
- The **Panchayat Advancement Index** of the Ministry of Panchayati Raj won the **Gold Award** at the National Awards for e-Governance 2026.
