## Rural jobs and farmers

- **1 to 30 June 2026, Khet Bachao Abhiyan:** The Union Agriculture Minister launched this campaign from Raisen district, Madhya Pradesh. It is about soil health and the safe use of fertilisers.
- **From 1 July 2026, VB-G RAM G:** The Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin), or VB-G RAM G, replaces MGNREGS (the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme).
  - It guarantees 125 days of wage employment to a rural household in a year.
  - The Centre announced an interim allocation of Rs 95,692 crore for it.
  - The cost is shared 60:40 between the Centre and most States.
- **PM-KISAN:** The Prime Minister released the 23rd instalment at an event in West Bengal. Over Rs 18,880 crore went to about 9.44 crore farmers.
- **Rural Internal Audit Portal:** The Union Rural Development Minister launched this AI-enabled portal at the Rashtriya Gramin Vikas Sammelan in New Delhi.

## Health

- **PMSMA:** The Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA) completed ten years. It was launched on 9 June 2016.
  - It gives free antenatal check-ups on the 9th of every month.
- **SUMAN Roadmap 2030:** The Health Minister launched this roadmap to cut maternal deaths.
  - It aims to bring the maternal mortality ratio below 70 per lakh live births by 2030.
  - It focuses on 130 districts in 13 high-focus States.
- **Anemia Mukt Bharat:** This strategy, launched in 2018, was upgraded from 6x6x6 to 7x7x7.
- **Aarogya Setu 2.0:** It was relaunched as a national health app under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission. The mission is run by the National Health Authority.
- **PM Family Care Tracker:** Its pilot was launched at Gandhinagar, Gujarat.

## Livelihoods, innovation and other missions

- **PM SVANidhi:** This loan scheme for street vendors, run by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, completed six years. It was launched on 1 June 2020.
  - It gives loans in three steps of Rs 15,000, Rs 25,000 and Rs 50,000.
  - Lending has been extended to March 2030.
- **Jan Samarth Portal:** This single window for credit-linked government schemes completed four years. It was launched in June 2022.
- **Mission Senehjori:** It was launched for the Muga silk sector of Assam.
- **Navachar Mantra:** This initiative of the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship will find and mentor grassroots innovators.
- **MAHA Water Mission:** The Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF) and the Ministry of Jal Shakti launched it, with Rs 200 crore over five years.
- **SAPLING Dialogue 2026:** The Ministry of Food Processing Industries and the World Bank Group organised this dialogue on nutrition and food processing at Ahmedabad.
