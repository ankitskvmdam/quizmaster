## Weather and climate

- **SkyCast:** India's first SkyCast System was inaugurated at Indira Gandhi International Airport, New Delhi. It watches the lower atmosphere for aviation weather.
  - India is the 19th country to deploy one.
  - SkyCast is part of Mission Mausam.
  - The next such facility is planned at Jewar airport.
- **Regional Meteorological Centre:** India's seventh Regional Meteorological Centre was inaugurated at Jammu. The next one was announced for Lucknow.
- **Mega Science Vision-2035:** This report on climate research was prepared with IISc Bengaluru as the nodal institution. It was submitted to the Principal Scientific Adviser's office.

## Nuclear energy and physics

- **Hydrogen from nuclear heat:** The Department of Atomic Energy inaugurated the world's first nuclear heat-based hydrogen production facility. It is at IGCAR (Indira Gandhi Centre for Atomic Research), Kalpakkam.
  - The plant uses the copper–chlorine thermochemical cycle.
  - The heat comes from the Fast Breeder Test Reactor.
  - The technology was developed by BARC (Bhabha Atomic Research Centre).
- **Kudankulam:** The reactor pressure vessel of Unit 5 of the Kudankulam Nuclear Power Project (Tamil Nadu) was installed. With six units the plant will reach 6,000 MWe.
- **Quark-gluon plasma:** Researchers at the Large Hadron Collider collided oxygen nuclei. They created the smallest known droplet of quark-gluon plasma.
- **GRAPES-3:** This cosmic-ray muon detector is located at Ooty in Tamil Nadu.

## Computing and AI

- **Sarvam AI:** The Bengaluru firm became a unicorn, a start-up valued at over USD 1 billion. Its valuation is about USD 1.5 billion.
  - It is developing India's first indigenous large language model under the IndiaAI Mission.
- **Semiconductors:** NITI Aayog's Frontier Tech Hub released the report "Future of India's Semiconductor Industry". It targets a chip value chain of USD 120–150 billion by 2035.
- **LineShine:** China's LineShine, at Shenzhen, topped the TOP500 list as the world's fastest supercomputer. It replaced the US machine El Capitan.
- **Smart Village:** CSIR (the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research) is deploying AI-based tools in Kusunpur village of Odisha under its Smart Village initiative. They include a system to warn of crocodiles.

## Space

- **Artemis III:** NASA named the four-member crew. Randy Bresnik is the Commander.
  - The pilot, Luca Parmitano, is the first European Space Agency astronaut assigned to an Artemis mission.
  - Artemis III is now an Earth-orbit test flight planned for 2027.
  - The crewed Moon landing has moved to Artemis IV in 2028.
- **MAVEN:** NASA's MAVEN mission came to an end. It was launched in 2013 to study the atmosphere of Mars.
- **SOLAR-1:** The US agency NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) made SOLAR-1 operational. It is NOAA's first dedicated space-weather satellite, placed at the Sun–Earth Lagrange Point 1 (L1).
- **BRICS space agencies:** ISRO hosted the meeting of the BRICS Heads of Space Agencies in Bengaluru.

## Agriculture and health

- **Herbicide-tolerant mustard:** India will begin large-scale sowing of herbicide-tolerant (IMI-resistant) mustard hybrids in the 2026-27 rabi season. They are meant to fight the parasitic weed Orobanche.
  - The hybrids were developed by mutation breeding.
  - Mutation breeding is not genetic modification, so the hybrids need no approval from the GEAC (Genetic Engineering Appraisal Committee).
- **Nipah virus:** A new case was reported from Kozhikode, Kerala. Fruit bats are the natural host of the virus.
