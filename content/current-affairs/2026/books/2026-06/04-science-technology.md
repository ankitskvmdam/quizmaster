## Gist
Two Indian firsts stand out: a **SkyCast** weather system for aviation at Delhi airport, and a plant at Kalpakkam that makes **hydrogen from nuclear heat**. **Sarvam AI**, which is building an Indian large language model, became a unicorn, and NITI Aayog set out a semiconductor vision for 2035.

In space, NASA named the crew of **Artemis III** and ended its **MAVEN** Mars mission, and the US switched on a satellite that watches space weather. China's **LineShine** became the world's fastest supercomputer.

At home, the **Kudankulam** nuclear project reached a milestone on Unit 5, a new weather centre opened at Jammu, and a herbicide-tolerant mustard bred without genetic modification is ready for sowing.

## Key facts
- **India's first SkyCast System**, which watches the lower atmosphere for aviation weather, was inaugurated at **Indira Gandhi International Airport, New Delhi**; India is the **19th country** to deploy one.
- SkyCast is part of **Mission Mausam**; the next such facility is planned at **Jewar** airport.
- India's **seventh Regional Meteorological Centre** was inaugurated at **Jammu**; the next one was announced for **Lucknow**.
- The Department of Atomic Energy inaugurated the **world's first nuclear heat-based hydrogen production facility** at **IGCAR, Kalpakkam**.
- The Kalpakkam hydrogen plant uses the **copper–chlorine thermochemical cycle**, with heat from the **Fast Breeder Test Reactor**; the technology was developed by **BARC**.
- **Sarvam AI** of **Bengaluru** became a unicorn at a valuation of about **USD 1.5 billion**; it is developing India's first indigenous large language model under the **IndiaAI Mission**.
- NITI Aayog's **Frontier Tech Hub** released the report "Future of India's Semiconductor Industry", which targets a chip value chain of **USD 120–150 billion by 2035**.
- NASA named the four-member crew of **Artemis III**, with **Randy Bresnik** as Commander.
- **Luca Parmitano**, the pilot of Artemis III, is the first **European Space Agency** astronaut assigned to an Artemis mission.
- Artemis III is now an **Earth-orbit** test flight planned for 2027; the crewed Moon landing has moved to **Artemis IV** in 2028.
- NASA's **MAVEN** mission, launched in **2013** to study the atmosphere of **Mars**, came to an end.
- The US agency **NOAA** made operational **SOLAR-1**, its first dedicated space-weather satellite, placed at the Sun–Earth **Lagrange Point 1 (L1)**.
- China's **LineShine**, at Shenzhen, topped the **TOP500** list as the world's fastest supercomputer, replacing the US machine **El Capitan**.
- The **reactor pressure vessel** of **Unit 5** of the **Kudankulam** Nuclear Power Project (Tamil Nadu) was installed; with six units the plant will reach **6,000 MWe**.
- India will begin large-scale sowing of **herbicide-tolerant (IMI-resistant) mustard** hybrids in the 2026-27 rabi season to fight the parasitic weed **Orobanche**.
- The new mustard hybrids were developed by **mutation breeding**, which is not genetic modification and so needs no GEAC approval.
- Researchers at the **Large Hadron Collider** created the smallest known droplet of **quark-gluon plasma** by colliding **oxygen** nuclei.
- **GRAPES-3**, a cosmic-ray **muon** detector, is located at **Ooty** in Tamil Nadu.
- **ISRO** hosted the meeting of the **BRICS Heads of Space Agencies** in **Bengaluru**.
- The **Mega Science Vision-2035** report on climate research was prepared with **IISc Bengaluru** as the nodal institution and submitted to the Principal Scientific Adviser's office.
- A new case of **Nipah virus** was reported from **Kozhikode**, Kerala; fruit bats are the natural host of the virus.
- CSIR is deploying AI-based tools in **Kusunpur** village of **Odisha** under its Smart Village initiative, including a system to warn of crocodiles.
