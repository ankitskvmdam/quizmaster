## At a glance: new statistical series

India's price and output statistics are being rebuilt on newer base years.

| Index | Base year | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wholesale Price Index (WPI) | 2022-23, earlier 2011-12 | Its basket grew from 697 to 957 items. |
| Producer Price Index (PPI) | — | A new series, begun from 15 June 2026. |
| Index of Industrial Production (IIP) | 2022-23 | A new series. |
| Consumer Price Index (CPI) | 2024 | A new series. |
| Index of Services Production | 2024-25 | A monthly index that MoSPI will launch. |

- **WPI and PPI:** Both are compiled by the Office of the Economic Adviser in the DPIIT (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade).
- **Index of Services Production:** It will be launched by MoSPI (the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation). The committee that designed it was chaired by Debjani Ghosh.

## The Reserve Bank

- **June 2026, monetary policy:** The RBI's Monetary Policy Committee unanimously kept the repo rate at 5.25 per cent. Its stance stayed neutral.
  - After the review the Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate is 5.00 per cent.
  - The Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate and the Bank Rate are 5.50 per cent.
  - The RBI cut its real GDP growth forecast for 2026-27 to 6.6 per cent, from 6.9 per cent.
  - It raised its CPI inflation forecast to 5.1 per cent.
- **Export proceeds:** The RBI reduced the time allowed to bring back export proceeds from 15 months to 9 months.
- **Surplus transfer:** The RBI approved a record surplus transfer of Rs 2.87 lakh crore to the Union Government for 2025-26.
  - The surplus is decided under the Economic Capital Framework.
  - The framework was adopted in 2019 on the recommendation of the Bimal Jalan Committee.
- **RBI Annual Report 2025-26:** It noted two things.
  - The Cash Reserve Ratio was cut to 3.0 per cent.
  - China overtook the United States as India's largest trading partner.

## Growth and the external sector

- **Provisional Estimates:** MoSPI put real GDP growth for 2025-26 at 7.7 per cent, against 7.1 per cent in 2024-25. Manufacturing grew 10.7 per cent.
- **Remittances:** India received about USD 135 billion in remittances in 2024-25. The United States is now the largest source, ahead of the UAE.
- **US tariff proposal:** The US Trade Representative ran a Section 301 investigation into forced labour. After it, the US Trade Representative proposed an additional 12.5 per cent tariff on imports from India and 53 other economies.
- **Ship recycling:** According to UNCTAD (the UN Conference on Trade and Development), India was the world's leading ship recycling nation in 2025. Its share was 35.4 per cent.

## Industry, finance and infrastructure

- **Blue bond:** Sagarmala Finance Corporation plans to issue India's first blue bond, to fund maritime and coastal infrastructure. The world's first sovereign blue bond was issued by Seychelles in 2018.
- **Aviation fuel:** The Union Cabinet approved a one-time support of Rs 10,000 crore to public sector oil marketing companies. It is meant to stabilise the price of aviation turbine fuel (ATF) for Indian airlines.
- **BHAVYA portal:** The Commerce and Industry Minister launched it for the Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojana. The scheme has an outlay of Rs 33,660 crore for 100 plug-and-play industrial parks.
- **Microfinance:** The Credit Guarantee Scheme for Microfinance Institutions-2.0 was extended till 31 August 2026. It runs through the NCGTC (National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company).
- **NSIC:** The National Small Industries Corporation (NSIC) was upgraded to a Schedule 'A' public sector enterprise. MSME Day is 27 June.
- **Zojila Tunnel:** The tunnel achieved its final breakthrough. It will give all-weather road access between Kashmir and Ladakh.
- **Textiles Summit 2026:** It backed India's target of USD 100 billion in textile exports by 2030.

## GI tags

A GI (geographical indication) tag marks a product from a particular place.

- **Jharkhand:** The State received GI tags for 11 traditional products. Among them are Kuchai Silk Saree, Dokra Craft and Jadopatia Painting.
- **Ponduru Khadi:** This khadi of Srikakulam (Andhra Pradesh) received a GI tag.
