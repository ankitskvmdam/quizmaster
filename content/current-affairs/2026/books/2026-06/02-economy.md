## Gist
The Reserve Bank kept the **repo rate at 5.25 per cent** in its June review, but lowered its growth forecast and raised its inflation forecast for 2026-27. It also approved a **record surplus transfer of Rs 2.87 lakh crore** to the Centre. The Provisional Estimates put growth for 2025-26 at **7.7 per cent**.

India's price and output statistics are being rebuilt on newer base years: the **WPI and IIP** now use **2022-23**, a **Producer Price Index** has begun, and an **Index of Services Production** is coming.

Other items to remember: the first **blue bond**, the **BHAVYA** portal for industrial parks, a fuel-price cushion for airlines, a US tariff proposal over forced labour, and India becoming the world's top **ship recycler**.

## Key facts
- In its June 2026 review, the RBI's Monetary Policy Committee unanimously kept the **repo rate at 5.25 per cent** with a **neutral** stance.
- After the June review the **Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate is 5.00 per cent** and the **MSF rate and Bank Rate are 5.50 per cent**.
- The RBI cut its real GDP growth forecast for **2026-27** to **6.6 per cent** (from 6.9 per cent) and raised its CPI inflation forecast to **5.1 per cent**.
- The RBI reduced the time allowed to bring back **export proceeds** from 15 months to **9 months**.
- The RBI approved a record surplus transfer of **Rs 2.87 lakh crore** to the Union Government for 2025-26.
- The RBI's surplus is decided under the **Economic Capital Framework**, adopted in 2019 on the recommendation of the **Bimal Jalan Committee**.
- The **RBI Annual Report 2025-26** noted that the **Cash Reserve Ratio** was cut to **3.0 per cent** and that **China** overtook the United States as India's largest trading partner.
- The **Provisional Estimates** (MoSPI) put real GDP growth for **2025-26** at **7.7 per cent**, against 7.1 per cent in 2024-25; manufacturing grew **10.7 per cent**.
- The base year of the **Wholesale Price Index (WPI)** was changed from 2011-12 to **2022-23**, and its basket grew from 697 to **957 items**.
- The WPI is compiled by the **Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT**, which also began a new **Producer Price Index (PPI)** series from **15 June 2026**.
- The new **Index of Industrial Production (IIP)** series also has **2022-23** as its base year, while the new **CPI** series has **2024** as its base year.
- MoSPI will launch a monthly **Index of Services Production** with base year **2024-25**; the committee that designed it was chaired by **Debjani Ghosh**.
- **Sagarmala Finance Corporation** plans to issue **India's first blue bond**, to fund maritime and coastal infrastructure.
- The world's first sovereign blue bond was issued by **Seychelles** in 2018.
- The Union Cabinet approved a one-time support of **Rs 10,000 crore** to public sector oil marketing companies to stabilise the price of **aviation turbine fuel (ATF)** for Indian airlines.
- India received about **USD 135 billion** in remittances in 2024-25; the **United States** is now the largest source, ahead of the UAE.
- The **US Trade Representative** proposed an additional **12.5 per cent** tariff on imports from India and 53 other economies after a **Section 301** investigation into forced labour.
- The Commerce and Industry Minister launched the **BHAVYA portal** for the **Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojana**, which has an outlay of **Rs 33,660 crore** for **100 plug-and-play industrial parks**.
- The **Credit Guarantee Scheme for Microfinance Institutions-2.0** was extended till **31 August 2026**; it runs through the **NCGTC**.
- According to **UNCTAD**, India was the world's leading **ship recycling** nation in 2025 with a share of **35.4 per cent**.
- **Jharkhand** received GI tags for **11 traditional products**, including Kuchai Silk Saree, Dokra Craft and Jadopatia Painting.
- **Ponduru Khadi** of Srikakulam (Andhra Pradesh) received a GI tag, and the **NSIC** was upgraded to a **Schedule 'A'** public sector enterprise; **MSME Day** is **27 June**.
- The **Zojila Tunnel**, which will give all-weather road access between **Kashmir and Ladakh**, achieved its final breakthrough.
- The **Textiles Summit 2026** backed India's target of **USD 100 billion** in textile exports by 2030.
