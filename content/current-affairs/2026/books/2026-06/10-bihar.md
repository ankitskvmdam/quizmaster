## Gist
The **Patna High Court** got a new Chief Justice, **Justice Meenakshi Madan Rai**, and the State got a new **Advocate General**. Three more crafts of Bihar received **GI tags**, from Nalanda, Gaya and Bhojpur.

Several projects were announced for industry and farming: a **Technology Centre** at Khizarsarai in Gaya Ji, an **Integrated Aqua Park** in Bhojpur, a **compressed biogas plant** in West Champaran, and the four-laning of the **Khagaria–Purnea** stretch of NH-31. The State Cabinet decided to adopt the **PM Fasal Bima Yojana** in place of its own crop assistance scheme.

Two young people from the State made news: **Akshara Gupta** of Raxaul scored a triple century in a women's under-19 match, and **Shubham Kumar** of Gaya topped **JEE Advanced 2026**.

## Key facts
- **Justice Meenakshi Madan Rai** became Chief Justice of the **Patna High Court** from **5 June 2026**; she earlier served in the **Sikkim High Court**.
- She succeeded **Justice Sangam Kumar Sahoo**, who retired on **4 June 2026**.
- The Patna High Court was established on **3 February 1916**.
- **S.D. Sanjay** was appointed **Advocate General of Bihar** in place of **P.K. Shahi**; the Advocate General of a State is appointed under **Article 165(1)**.
- Three products of Bihar received **GI tags**: **Bawan Buti Saree and Fabric** (Nalanda), **Patharkatti Stone Craft** (Gaya) and **Pidiya Painting** (Bhojpur).
- A **Technology Centre** costing **Rs 170 crore** is being set up at **Khizarsarai** in **Gaya Ji**; Union Minister **Jitan Ram Manjhi** and Chief Minister **Samrat Choudhary** took part in the event.
- An **Integrated Aqua Park** costing **Rs 31.21 crore** will come up at the **Vanasaur Fish Seed Farm** in **Bhojpur** under the **PM Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)**.
- A **Regional Centre of the National Fisheries Development Board (NFDB)** will be set up at **Patna**.
- Bihar is India's **fourth-largest inland fish producer**; it produced over **10.2 lakh tonnes** of fish in 2025.
- A **compressed biogas (CBG) plant** costing **Rs 121 crore** will be built by **Bharat GPS Bioenergy** in the **Kumarbagh Industrial Area** of **West Champaran**.
- The **Bihar Industrial Area Development Authority (BIADA)** was set up under an Act of **1974**.
- The **Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs** approved the four-laning of the **143-km Khagaria–Purnea** section of **NH-31** at a cost of **Rs 3,936.05 crore**, on the **Hybrid Annuity Mode**.
- The Bihar Cabinet approved the adoption of **VB-G RAM G** and the **PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)** in the State.
- PMFBY will apply from **Rabi 2026-27** and will replace the **Bihar State Crop Assistance Scheme** of **2018**.
- The **Rural Development Department** launched the **CGRM Portal**.
- The **National Education Society for Tribal Students (NESTS)** of the Ministry of Tribal Affairs signed an MoU with the **Bihar Mahadalit Vikas Mission** on **Eklavya Model Residential Schools**.
- A **medical college** will be set up in **Sheikhpura**, and a township between **Sheikhpura and Barbigha**.
- The State aims to have **25 sugar mills by 2030**; a new mill is planned at **Motipur (Muzaffarpur)**.
- The airports at **Patna, Gaya and Purnea** are operational.
- The Myanmar President visited **Bodh Gaya**, where he went to the **Mahabodhi Temple** and the **Sujata Temple at Bakraur**.
- **Akshara Gupta** of **Raxaul** scored **306 not out off 126 balls** on **18 June 2026** in the BCA Women's Under-19 One-Day tournament at **Bhagalpur**.
- **Shubham Kumar** of **Gaya** secured **All India Rank 1** in **JEE Advanced 2026**, organised by **IIT Roorkee**, with **330 marks**.
- **Harsh Vardhan**, President of the **Bihar Cricket Association (BCA)**, received the **Global Prestige Award** at the **House of Lords**, London.
- A **heli-tourism** service linking **Valmikinagar, Rajgir and Mundeshwari** is to start from **July 2026**.
- The **Mukhyamantri Home Stay Promotion Scheme 2026** offers up to **Rs 11 lakh** for home stays in **Tharu** villages near the **Valmiki Tiger Reserve**.
- The State's **first eco-resort** is coming up in the **Rajgir** forest.
