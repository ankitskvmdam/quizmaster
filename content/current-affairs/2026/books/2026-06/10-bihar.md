## Courts and law

- **5 June 2026, Patna High Court:** Justice Meenakshi Madan Rai became Chief Justice of the Patna High Court. She earlier served in the Sikkim High Court.
  - She succeeded Justice Sangam Kumar Sahoo, who retired on 4 June 2026.
  - The Patna High Court was established on 3 February 1916.
- **Advocate General:** S.D. Sanjay was appointed Advocate General of Bihar in place of P.K. Shahi. The Advocate General of a State is appointed under Article 165(1).

## Industry, crafts and fisheries

- **GI tags:** Three products of Bihar received GI tags. A GI (geographical indication) tag marks a product from a particular place.
  - Bawan Buti Saree and Fabric, from Nalanda.
  - Patharkatti Stone Craft, from Gaya.
  - Pidiya Painting, from Bhojpur.
- **Technology Centre:** A Technology Centre costing Rs 170 crore is being set up at Khizarsarai in Gaya Ji. Union Minister Jitan Ram Manjhi and Chief Minister Samrat Choudhary took part in the event.
- **Integrated Aqua Park:** An aqua park costing Rs 31.21 crore will come up at the Vanasaur Fish Seed Farm in Bhojpur. It comes under the PM Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY).
- **NFDB centre:** A Regional Centre of the National Fisheries Development Board (NFDB) will be set up at Patna.
- **Fish output:** Bihar is India's fourth-largest inland fish producer. It produced over 10.2 lakh tonnes of fish in 2025.
- **Biogas plant:** Bharat GPS Bioenergy will build a compressed biogas (CBG) plant costing Rs 121 crore. It will stand in the Kumarbagh Industrial Area of West Champaran.
- **BIADA:** The Bihar Industrial Area Development Authority (BIADA) was set up under an Act of 1974.
- **Sugar mills:** The State aims to have 25 sugar mills by 2030. A new mill is planned at Motipur (Muzaffarpur).

## Roads, airports and towns

- **NH-31:** The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) approved the four-laning of the 143-km Khagaria–Purnea section of National Highway 31 (NH-31). The cost is Rs 3,936.05 crore, on the Hybrid Annuity Mode.
- **Airports:** The airports at Patna, Gaya and Purnea are operational.
- **Sheikhpura:** A medical college will be set up in Sheikhpura. A township will come up between Sheikhpura and Barbigha.

## Schemes and welfare

- **State Cabinet decisions:** The Bihar Cabinet approved the adoption of VB-G RAM G and the PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) in the State.
  - PMFBY will apply from Rabi 2026-27.
  - It will replace the State's own crop assistance scheme, the Bihar State Crop Assistance Scheme of 2018.
- **CGRM Portal:** The Rural Development Department launched it.
- **Eklavya schools:** The National Education Society for Tribal Students (NESTS) of the Ministry of Tribal Affairs signed an MoU with the Bihar Mahadalit Vikas Mission. The MoU is on Eklavya Model Residential Schools.

## Tourism and heritage

- **From July 2026, heli-tourism:** A heli-tourism service linking Valmikinagar, Rajgir and Mundeshwari is to start.
- **Home stays:** The Mukhyamantri Home Stay Promotion Scheme 2026 offers up to Rs 11 lakh for home stays in Tharu villages near the Valmiki Tiger Reserve.
- **Eco-resort:** The State's first eco-resort is coming up in the Rajgir forest.
- **Bodh Gaya:** The Myanmar President visited Bodh Gaya. He went to the Mahabodhi Temple and the Sujata Temple at Bakraur.

## People in news

- **18 June 2026, Akshara Gupta:** Akshara Gupta of Raxaul scored 306 not out off 126 balls, a triple century. It came in the BCA Women's Under-19 One-Day tournament at Bhagalpur.
- **Shubham Kumar:** The student from Gaya secured All India Rank 1 in JEE Advanced 2026, with 330 marks. The exam was organised by IIT Roorkee.
- **Harsh Vardhan:** The President of the Bihar Cricket Association (BCA) received the Global Prestige Award at the House of Lords, London.
