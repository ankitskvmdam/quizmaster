## Gist
The Supreme Court was in the news all month. An ordinance raised the number of its judges, a woman lawyer was elevated straight from the Bar for only the second time, and the Court declared that walking safely on a **footpath is a fundamental right**.

The Centre changed several sets of rules: for **foreign contributions (FCRA)**, for the registration of foreigners, and for telecom companies, which now work under "authorisations" instead of the old licences. The **NITI Aayog Governing Council** met for the 11th time with all Chief Ministers present.

Election and federal questions stayed alive: a report on the criminal records of Rajya Sabha members, a paper on delimitation from the Prime Minister's economic advisers, and river disputes over the **Mekedatu** and **Tungabhadra** dams.

## Key facts
- The **Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Ordinance, 2026** raised the sanctioned strength of the Supreme Court from **34 to 38** judges (including the Chief Justice of India).
- **Five new judges** were appointed to the Supreme Court, taking its working strength to **37**.
- Senior Advocate **V. Mohana** became the **second woman** to be elevated directly from the Bar to the Supreme Court; the first was **Indu Malhotra** in 2018.
- In June 2026 the Supreme Court held that the **right to walk on safe, demarcated footpaths** is a fundamental right under **Article 19(1)(d)** read with **Article 21**.
- The footpath judgment was given by a Bench of Justices **P.S. Narasimha** and **A.S. Chandurkar**.
- The **11th Governing Council meeting of NITI Aayog**, chaired by the Prime Minister, had the theme **"Inclusive Human Development for Viksit Bharat @2047"**.
- At the NITI Aayog meeting the Prime Minister called for raising the number of **Lakhpati Didis** from **3 crore to 6 crore**.
- The Ministry of Home Affairs notified the **FCRA Amendment Rules, 2026**: an NGO must use **75 per cent** of the foreign funds already received before it gets the next instalment.
- The Home Ministry amended the **Immigration and Foreigners Rules, 2025**: foreigners on visas of **180 days** or less must now register before the 180 days end.
- The Department of Telecommunications notified a new **authorisation** framework under the **Telecommunications Act, 2023**, replacing licences under the **Indian Telegraph Act, 1885**.
- The Delhi High Court upheld the temporary blocking of **Telegram** under **Section 69A of the IT Act, 2000**, ahead of the NEET-UG 2026 re-examination.
- The Ministry of External Affairs clarified that a **passport** is a travel document and **not conclusive proof of citizenship**.
- An **ADR** report found that **31 per cent** of the 226 sitting **Rajya Sabha** members have declared criminal cases against themselves.
- The **Election Commission of India** urged the Supreme Court to impose a **lifetime ban** on convicted politicians, in place of the present **six-year** disqualification after the sentence.
- NCERT included the **1975 Emergency** for the first time in a **Class 9** textbook, "Understanding Society: India and Beyond".
- The draft **Andaman and Nicobar Islands Tribal Councils Election Rules, 2026** propose a fixed **five-year** tenure for tribal councils.
- **Assam and Meghalaya** share an **855-km** border with 12 disputed sectors, of which six were settled by an agreement in 2022.
- The **Tamil Nadu Assembly** unanimously passed a resolution against Karnataka's **Mekedatu** dam project on the **Cauvery**.
- **33 new spillway gates** of the **Tungabhadra Dam** were inaugurated, and the Centre proposed a high-level committee of Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.
- The Supreme Court used its powers under **Article 142** to set aside a conviction under the **POCSO Act**, because the victim had later married the man.
- **Section 116 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam** (presumption of legitimacy of a child) has replaced Section 112 of the Indian Evidence Act; the Court upheld a DNA test order in a paternity dispute.
- The **Viksit Bharat Shiksha Adhishthan (VBSA) Bill, 2025** proposes to merge the **UGC, AICTE and NCTE** into one body.
