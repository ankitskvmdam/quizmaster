## The Supreme Court

- **Number of judges:** The Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Ordinance, 2026 raised the sanctioned strength of the Supreme Court from 34 to 38 judges. The count includes the Chief Justice of India.
- **New judges:** Five new judges were appointed to the Supreme Court. This took its working strength to 37.
- **V. Mohana:** The Senior Advocate became the second woman to be elevated directly from the Bar to the Supreme Court. The first was Indu Malhotra, in 2018.

## Court rulings

- **June 2026, footpaths:** The Supreme Court held that the right to walk on safe, demarcated footpaths is a fundamental right. It flows from Article 19(1)(d) read with Article 21.
  - The judgment was given by a Bench of Justices P.S. Narasimha and A.S. Chandurkar.
- **Telegram:** The Delhi High Court upheld the temporary blocking of Telegram under Section 69A of the IT Act, 2000. The block came ahead of the NEET-UG 2026 re-examination.
- **POCSO case:** The Supreme Court used its powers under Article 142 to set aside a conviction under the POCSO (Protection of Children from Sexual Offences) Act. The victim had later married the man.
- **Paternity dispute:** The Court upheld an order for a DNA test in a paternity dispute.
  - Section 116 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam deals with the presumption that a child is legitimate.
  - It has replaced Section 112 of the Indian Evidence Act.

## Rules and notifications

- **FCRA Amendment Rules, 2026:** The Ministry of Home Affairs notified these rules on foreign contributions. FCRA is the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act. An NGO must now use 75 per cent of the foreign funds already received before it gets the next instalment.
- **Immigration and Foreigners Rules, 2025:** The Home Ministry amended these rules. Foreigners on visas of 180 days or less must now register before the 180 days end.
- **Telecom authorisations:** The Department of Telecommunications notified a new authorisation framework under the Telecommunications Act, 2023. Telecom companies now work under authorisations instead of the old licences under the Indian Telegraph Act, 1885.
- **Passport:** The Ministry of External Affairs clarified that a passport is a travel document. It is not conclusive proof of citizenship.

## Governance and education

- **NITI Aayog:** The 11th Governing Council meeting of NITI Aayog was chaired by the Prime Minister. All Chief Ministers were present.
  - The theme was "Inclusive Human Development for Viksit Bharat @2047".
  - The Prime Minister called for raising the number of Lakhpati Didis from 3 crore to 6 crore.
- **1975 Emergency:** NCERT included the Emergency of 1975 for the first time in a Class 9 textbook, "Understanding Society: India and Beyond".
- **VBSA Bill, 2025:** The Viksit Bharat Shiksha Adhishthan (VBSA) Bill, 2025 proposes to merge three bodies into one. They are the University Grants Commission (UGC), the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE).

## Elections and representation

- **Rajya Sabha:** A report of the Association for Democratic Reforms (ADR) found that 31 per cent of the 226 sitting Rajya Sabha members have declared criminal cases against themselves.
- **Lifetime ban:** The Election Commission of India urged the Supreme Court to impose a lifetime ban on convicted politicians. At present they are disqualified for six years after the sentence.
- **Delimitation:** The Prime Minister's economic advisers brought out a paper on delimitation.
- **Andaman and Nicobar:** The draft Andaman and Nicobar Islands Tribal Councils Election Rules, 2026 propose a fixed five-year tenure for tribal councils.

## Inter-State disputes

- **Assam and Meghalaya:** The two States share an 855-km border with 12 disputed sectors. Six of them were settled by an agreement in 2022.
- **Mekedatu:** The Tamil Nadu Assembly unanimously passed a resolution against Karnataka's Mekedatu dam project on the Cauvery.
- **Tungabhadra Dam:** 33 new spillway gates of the dam were inaugurated. The Centre proposed a high-level committee of Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.
