## Wetlands, forests and wildlife

- **5 June 2026, 100th Ramsar site:** The Jai Prakash Narayan Bird Sanctuary (Surha Tal) in Ballia, Uttar Pradesh, was announced as India's 100th Ramsar site. A Ramsar site is a wetland of international importance.
  - Surha Tal is an oxbow lake of the Ganga, a lake left behind by a bend of the river.
  - It is the 13th Ramsar site of Uttar Pradesh.
  - With 100 sites India has the most Ramsar sites in Asia.
  - It ranks third in the world, after the United Kingdom (176) and Mexico (144).
- **Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam:** Uttar Pradesh planted over 5 crore saplings under this campaign.
- **Western Ghats:** The Centre is preparing to notify over 56,000 sq km of the Western Ghats in six States as an Ecologically Sensitive Area. This comes after a wait of thirteen years.
  - The move is largely based on the Kasturirangan Committee report (2013).
- **HAWK:** Kerala added e-court integration to its HAWK software. It became the first State with a fully digitised, court-linked wildlife crime system.
- **International Big Cat Alliance:** Bangladesh became its 27th member. The alliance covers seven big cat species and is headquartered in India.

## Oceans and coasts

- **Mombasa Declaration:** It was adopted at the 11th Our Ocean Conference in Kenya. It is against illegal, unreported and unregulated fishing.
- **3D-printed reefs:** Tamil Nadu will place India's first 3D-printed artificial reefs in the sea off Ramanathapuram.
- **Dolphins:** Gujarat counted more than 680 dolphins along its coast.

## Heat, rain and water

- **Monsoon:** The India Meteorological Department (IMD) forecast the 2026 southwest monsoon at 92 per cent of the long period average. That is below normal.
- **Heat:** An Oxford University study of 205 cities found 14 Indian cities among the 50 most heat-vulnerable in the world. Ahmedabad ranked 2nd.
- **Kishau Multipurpose Dam:** Six States agreed on this dam on the Tons, a tributary of the Yamuna.
- **"5% Model":** Korea district of Chhattisgarh is known for this model. Farmers set aside 5 per cent of their land for soak pits to recharge groundwater.

## Clean energy and pollution

- **5 June 2026, E85 fuel:** The rollout of E85 fuel began in New Delhi on World Environment Day, at 48 outlets. It contains 80–85 per cent ethanol and is meant only for flex-fuel vehicles.
- **Floating solar:** The National Institute of Solar Energy estimated India's floating solar potential on reservoirs at about 102.18 GW. Maharashtra has the highest potential.
  - The Omkareshwar floating solar park in Madhya Pradesh (278 MW) is India's largest.
- **Carbon credits with Japan:** India adopted the rules for the Joint Crediting Mechanism with Japan, for trading carbon credits. The mechanism works under Article 6.2 of the Paris Agreement.
- **Old vehicles in Delhi-NCR:** The Union Cabinet approved a Rs 9,585-crore scheme to replace old, polluting trucks and buses in Delhi-NCR (National Capital Region). It is funded through the NCR Planning Board.
- **GHCI:** The Ministry of New and Renewable Energy launched the Green Hydrogen Certification Portal of India (GHCI).
