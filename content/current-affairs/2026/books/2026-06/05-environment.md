## Gist
On **World Environment Day (5 June)** India got its **100th Ramsar site**, a bird sanctuary in Ballia, Uttar Pradesh, and began selling **E85** fuel. The month's other observance, on 17 June, was about deserts and drought, with rangelands as the theme.

Heat and rain were the worry: the weather office expects a **below-normal monsoon**, and an Oxford study ranked Indian cities among the world's most exposed to heat. The Centre moved to notify the **Western Ghats** as an ecologically sensitive area after a wait of thirteen years.

On clean energy, a national study measured the scope for **floating solar** plants, a certification portal for green hydrogen was launched, and India and Japan settled the rules for trading carbon credits.

## Key facts
- **World Environment Day** was observed on **5 June 2026**; the global host was **Azerbaijan** and the theme was **"Inspired by Nature. For Climate. For Our Future."**
- On 5 June 2026 the **Jai Prakash Narayan Bird Sanctuary (Surha Tal)** in **Ballia, Uttar Pradesh**, was announced as **India's 100th Ramsar site**.
- Surha Tal is an **oxbow lake of the Ganga** and the **13th Ramsar site of Uttar Pradesh**.
- With 100 sites India has the most Ramsar sites in **Asia** and ranks **third** in the world, after the **United Kingdom (176)** and **Mexico (144)**.
- **Uttar Pradesh** planted over **5 crore** saplings under the **Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam** campaign.
- The rollout of **E85** fuel (**80–85 per cent ethanol**), meant only for flex-fuel vehicles, began in New Delhi on World Environment Day at **48 outlets**.
- The **World Day to Combat Desertification and Drought** is **17 June**; the 2026 theme was **"Rangelands: Recognize. Respect. Restore."**
- **2026** is the **International Year of Rangelands and Pastoralists**.
- The Centre is preparing to notify over **56,000 sq km** of the **Western Ghats** in **six States** as an Ecologically Sensitive Area, largely on the basis of the **Kasturirangan Committee** report (2013).
- The **India Meteorological Department** forecast the 2026 southwest monsoon at **92 per cent** of the long period average, which is **below normal**.
- An **Oxford University** study of 205 cities found **14 Indian cities** among the 50 most heat-vulnerable in the world; **Ahmedabad** ranked 2nd.
- The **National Institute of Solar Energy** estimated India's **floating solar** potential on reservoirs at about **102.18 GW**; **Maharashtra** has the highest potential.
- The **Omkareshwar** floating solar park in **Madhya Pradesh** (278 MW) is India's largest.
- **Kerala** became the first State with a fully digitised, court-linked wildlife crime system, by adding e-court integration to its **HAWK** software.
- **Bangladesh** became the **27th member** of the **International Big Cat Alliance**, which covers **seven** big cat species and is headquartered in India.
- The **Mombasa Declaration** against illegal, unreported and unregulated fishing was adopted at the **11th Our Ocean Conference** in **Kenya**.
- India adopted the rules for the **Joint Crediting Mechanism** with **Japan**, which works under **Article 6.2 of the Paris Agreement**.
- The Union Cabinet approved a **Rs 9,585-crore** scheme to replace old, polluting trucks and buses in **Delhi-NCR**, funded through the NCR Planning Board.
- The Ministry of New and Renewable Energy launched the **Green Hydrogen Certification Portal of India (GHCI)**.
- **Tamil Nadu** will place **India's first 3D-printed artificial reefs** in the sea off **Ramanathapuram**.
- Six States agreed on the **Kishau Multipurpose Dam** on the **Tons**, a tributary of the **Yamuna**.
- **Gujarat** counted more than **680 dolphins** along its coast.
- **Korea district** of **Chhattisgarh** is known for its **"5% Model"**, in which farmers set aside 5 per cent of their land for soak pits to recharge groundwater.
