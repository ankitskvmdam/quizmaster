## Gist
The Prime Minister travelled to **France** for the **52nd G7 Summit** at Évian, held talks with President Macron at Nice, made the first visit by an Indian Prime Minister to **Slovakia**, and later went to **Seychelles** for its 50th National Day. Myanmar's President came to India on his first state visit since the 2021 coup.

At the United Nations, **Bangladesh's Foreign Minister** was elected President of the next General Assembly session and **five countries** won seats on the Security Council. The United States and Iran signed an interim peace deal, the **Islamabad MoU**.

India, which chairs **BRICS in 2026**, hosted the BRICS security advisers, and was chosen for the **Vice-Presidency of the FATF** for the first time. **Uzbekistan** joined the BRICS bank.

## Key facts
- The **52nd G7 Summit** was held at **Évian, France**; the Prime Minister took part in its outreach session.
- On the sidelines of the G7, Canada's **Cameco** and India's Department of Atomic Energy signed a **CAD 2.6 billion** uranium supply deal for 2027–2035.
- At **Nice**, India and France adopted the **Innovation Roadmap 2030** and launched their first **Economic Security Dialogue**.
- The Prime Minister's visit to **Slovakia** was the first by an Indian Prime Minister since its independence in 1993; ties were raised to a **Comprehensive Partnership** and **11 agreements** were signed.
- The Prime Minister was **Guest of Honour** at the **50th National Day of Seychelles**; India extended a line of credit of **Rs 1,250 crore** and the two sides agreed to roll out **UPI** there.
- Myanmar's President **Min Aung Hlaing** made a five-day state visit to India, his first since the **2021** military coup.
- **Khalilur Rahman**, Foreign Minister of **Bangladesh**, was elected President of the **81st session of the UN General Assembly**, defeating Andreas Kakouris of **Cyprus**.
- The UN General Assembly elected **Austria, Portugal, Trinidad and Tobago, Zimbabwe and Kyrgyzstan** as non-permanent members of the Security Council from **1 January 2027**.
- **Kyrgyzstan** will sit on the UN Security Council for the first time.
- The United States and Iran signed a 14-point interim peace agreement called the **Islamabad MoU**, mediated by **Pakistan**; it opens a **60-day** window for talks and reopens the **Strait of Hormuz**.
- **Uzbekistan** became the **10th member** of the **New Development Bank** on 5 June 2026, the first from Central Asia; the bank is headquartered in **Shanghai**.
- India will hold the **Vice-Presidency of the Financial Action Task Force (FATF)** for the first time, from July 2026 to June 2027, through **Vivek Aggarwal**.
- India hosted the **16th BRICS National Security Advisers Meeting** in New Delhi, chaired by NSA **Ajit Doval**.
- India chairs BRICS for the **fourth time** in 2026; the theme is **"Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability"**.
- India and Nepal linked **UPI** with Nepal's **National Payments Interface**, and Nepal agreed to export **10,000 MW** of hydropower to India over the next decade.
- The India–UK **Critical Minerals Global Supply Chain Observatory** was launched in New Delhi by **IIT (ISM) Dhanbad's TEXMiN** and the **University of Cambridge**.
- The United States blocked a Pakistan–China proposal to list the **Balochistan Liberation Army** and its **Majeed Brigade** under the UN **1267** sanctions regime.
- The **114th International Labour Conference** was held in **Geneva**; the ILO adopted the first global treaty protecting **digital platform (gig) workers**.
- The **Indian Ocean Rim Association (IORA)**, which has **23 members**, is examining **Canada's** request to become a dialogue partner.
- The **Antarctic Treaty** completed **65 years** on 23 June 2026; India's planned fourth Antarctic station is **Maitri II**.
- A **7.8-magnitude** earthquake struck off **Mindanao** in the southern Philippines.
- The **UAE** became the first Arab country to set a legal minimum age for social media, barring children **below 15**.
