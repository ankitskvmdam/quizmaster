## At a glance: the Prime Minister's visits

| Place | Occasion | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Évian, France | 52nd G7 Summit; the Prime Minister took part in its outreach session | A uranium supply deal with Canada's Cameco, signed on the sidelines |
| Nice, France | Talks with President Macron | Innovation Roadmap 2030 adopted; first Economic Security Dialogue launched |
| Slovakia | First visit by an Indian Prime Minister since Slovakia's independence in 1993 | Ties raised to a Comprehensive Partnership; 11 agreements signed |
| Seychelles | Guest of Honour at its 50th National Day | A line of credit of Rs 1,250 crore; UPI to be rolled out there |

- **Uranium deal:** Canada's Cameco and India's Department of Atomic Energy signed it. It is worth CAD 2.6 billion (Canadian dollars) and covers 2027–2035.

## Visitors to India

- **Min Aung Hlaing:** Myanmar's President made a five-day state visit to India. It was his first since the 2021 military coup.

## The United Nations and global bodies

- **UN General Assembly:** Khalilur Rahman, Foreign Minister of Bangladesh, was elected President of its 81st session. He defeated Andreas Kakouris of Cyprus.
- **UN Security Council:** The General Assembly elected five non-permanent members from 1 January 2027: Austria, Portugal, Trinidad and Tobago, Zimbabwe and Kyrgyzstan.
  - Kyrgyzstan will sit on the Security Council for the first time.
- **FATF:** India will hold the Vice-Presidency of the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) for the first time. Vivek Aggarwal will hold it, from July 2026 to June 2027.
- **International Labour Conference:** The 114th conference was held in Geneva. The ILO (International Labour Organization) adopted the first global treaty protecting digital platform (gig) workers.
- **IORA:** The Indian Ocean Rim Association (IORA) has 23 members. It is examining Canada's request to become a dialogue partner.

## BRICS

- **5 June 2026, New Development Bank:** Uzbekistan became the bank's 10th member, and its first from Central Asia. The bank, also called the BRICS bank, is headquartered in Shanghai.
- **India's chairship:** India chairs BRICS for the fourth time in 2026. The theme is "Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability".
- **Security advisers:** India hosted the 16th BRICS National Security Advisers Meeting in New Delhi. It was chaired by National Security Adviser Ajit Doval.

## Peace, security and neighbours

- **Islamabad MoU:** The United States and Iran signed this 14-point interim peace agreement. Pakistan mediated it.
  - It opens a 60-day window for talks.
  - It reopens the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Balochistan Liberation Army:** Pakistan and China proposed listing it and its Majeed Brigade under the UN 1267 sanctions regime. The United States blocked the proposal.
- **Nepal:** India and Nepal linked UPI with Nepal's National Payments Interface. Nepal agreed to export 10,000 MW of hydropower to India over the next decade.

## Other news

- **23 June 2026, Antarctic Treaty:** The treaty completed 65 years. India's planned fourth Antarctic station is Maitri II.
- **Critical minerals:** The India–UK Critical Minerals Global Supply Chain Observatory was launched in New Delhi. It was launched by IIT (ISM) Dhanbad's TEXMiN and the University of Cambridge.
- **Mindanao earthquake:** A 7.8-magnitude earthquake struck off Mindanao in the southern Philippines.
- **UAE:** It became the first Arab country to set a legal minimum age for social media. Children below 15 are barred.
