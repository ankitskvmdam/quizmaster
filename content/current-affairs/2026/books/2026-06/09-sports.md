## Gist
Two world events began in June: the **FIFA World Cup 2026**, hosted jointly by the USA, Canada and Mexico, and the **ICC Women's T20 World Cup 2026** in England. At the **French Open**, both singles champions won their first Grand Slam title.

Indian players had a good month. **R. Praggnanandhaa** became the first Indian to win **Norway Chess**, the women's football team won a record sixth **SAFF** title, and India topped the medal table at the first **World Yogasana Championship**. The men's volleyball team won India's first medal at the **AVC Cup**.

Young players also made news: a girl from Bengaluru reached world No. 1 in the ITF Under-13 rankings, and a 15-year-old became the youngest Indian woman to win a WTT Feeder title. **Smriti Mandhana** was the only Indian in the first **TIME100 Sports** list.

## Key facts
- The **FIFA World Cup 2026**, hosted jointly by the **USA, Canada and Mexico**, opened on **11 June 2026** at the **Estadio Azteca**, where Mexico beat South Africa 2–0.
- The **ICC Women's T20 World Cup 2026** opened on **12 June 2026** at **Edgbaston**, where **England** beat Sri Lanka by **87 runs**.
- In the opening match England's **Danni Wyatt-Hodge** scored **105 not out**, and England made a record total of **219 for 1**.
- **Alexander Zverev** beat Flavio Cobolli to win the **French Open 2026** men's singles, his **first Grand Slam** title.
- Zverev is the first German man to win a Grand Slam title since **Becker** in **1996**.
- **Mirra Andreeva** won the French Open 2026 women's singles, her first Grand Slam title.
- **R. Praggnanandhaa** became the **first Indian** to win **Norway Chess**, beating **Vincent Keymer** in the last round.
- India beat **Bangladesh** in the final of the **SAFF Women's Championship 2026** at **Madgaon (Goa)** to win a record **sixth** title.
- India won **114 medals**, including **102 gold**, at the inaugural **World Yogasana Championship 2026** in **Ahmedabad**.
- India's men won **bronze** at the **AVC Men's Volleyball Cup 2026** in **Gandhinagar** by beating **Bahrain 3–1**; it was India's first medal in the event.
- After the AVC Cup, India's men's volleyball team rose from 60th to **42nd** in the **FIVB** world ranking.
- **Srishti Kiran** of **Bengaluru** became the **world No. 1** in the **ITF Under-13** rankings.
- **Divyanshi Bhowmick**, aged **15**, became the youngest Indian woman to win a **WTT Feeder** singles title, at **Prishtina, Kosovo**.
- **Smriti Mandhana** was the **only Indian** in the inaugural **TIME100 Sports** list, in its **"Titans"** category.
- **Lionel Messi** became the **first footballer** to win the **Princess of Asturias Award**.
- **International Olympic Day** is **23 June**; the 2026 theme was **"You Can Do This! Let's Move"**.
- The **International Olympic Committee**, headed by President **Kirsty Coventry**, is based at **Lausanne**.
