## Gist
The President gave away the second batch of the **Padma Awards 2026** on 23 June. The Prime Minister received two foreign honours during his visits, from **Slovakia** and **Seychelles**. Sand artist **Sudarsan Pattnaik** and three Indian school students also won international prizes.

New faces took charge of the **Intelligence Bureau** and the **National Statistical Commission**, the Solicitor General got another term, and an Indian jurist was elected to the sea-law tribunal at Hamburg.

The month's important days were the **International Day of Yoga** (21 June), **World Refugee Day** (20 June) and **Statistics Day** (29 June), each with a theme worth remembering.

## Key facts
- On **23 June 2026** the President conferred **65 Padma Awards** at the second Civil Investiture Ceremony: 2 Padma Vibhushan, 7 Padma Bhushan and 56 Padma Shri.
- The **Padma Vibhushan** went to **K.T. Thomas** (Public Affairs) and **P. Narayanan** (Literature and Education).
- **Padma Bhushan** recipients included actor **Mammootty**, singer **Alka Yagnik**, tennis player **Vijay Amritraj** and **Shibu Soren** (posthumous).
- **Padma Shri** recipients included cricketer **Rohit Sharma**, hockey player **Savita Punia** and actor **R. Madhavan**.
- Slovakia conferred its highest state honour, the **Order of the White Double Cross (1st Class)**, on the Prime Minister; President **Peter Pellegrini** presented it in **Bratislava**.
- **Seychelles** conferred the **"Guardian of the Blue Horizon"** distinction on the Prime Minister for his work on conservation and the blue economy.
- Sand artist **Sudarsan Pattnaik** became the first Indian to win the **Russia Grand Sand Master Cup 2026**, at Kaliningrad.
- Three Indian teenagers won **The Earth Prize 2026** for **"Plas-Stick"**, a tamarind-based powder that removes microplastics from water.
- **Arup Bose** of ISI Kolkata received the **Sukhatme National Award in Statistics 2026**.
- **Mahesh Dixit**, a 1993-batch IPS officer, was appointed Director of the **Intelligence Bureau**, succeeding **Tapan Kumar Deka**.
- **Saibal Chattopadhyay** was appointed Chairperson of the **National Statistical Commission**.
- **Tushar Mehta** was reappointed **Solicitor General of India** for three years from 1 July 2026.
- **Neelkanth Mishra** was named India's **Executive Director at the World Bank**.
- Diplomat **Vipul** was appointed India's Ambassador to **Saudi Arabia**.
- **Bimal N. Patel** was elected a judge of the **International Tribunal for the Law of the Sea (ITLOS)** for 2026–2035; the tribunal sits at **Hamburg**.
- According to Bloomberg, **Elon Musk** briefly became the world's first **trillionaire** after the stock market debut of **SpaceX**.
- The **12th International Day of Yoga** was observed on **21 June 2026** with the theme **"Yoga for Healthy Ageing"**; the Prime Minister led the main event at **Kolkata**.
- **World Refugee Day** is **20 June**; the 2026 theme was **"Until Everyone Is Safe"**.
- **International Sickle Cell Day** is **19 June**; India aims to eliminate sickle cell anaemia by **2047**.
- The **20th Statistics Day** was observed on **29 June 2026**, the birth anniversary of **P.C. Mahalanobis**, with the theme **"Unlocking the Potential of Administrative Data"**.
- **Telangana Statehood Day** is **2 June**; the State was formed on 2 June 2014.
- A bronze statue of the ancient Indian surgeon **Sushruta** was unveiled at the **Royal College of Surgeons of Edinburgh**.
- **Kempe Gowda**, whose birth anniversary falls on 27 June, founded **Bengaluru** in 1537.
- Astronaut **Shubhanshu Shukla** wrote **"The Second Orbit"**, a memoir of his Axiom-4 mission to the International Space Station.
