## Padma Awards 2026

- **23 June 2026:** The President conferred 65 Padma Awards at the second Civil Investiture Ceremony. They were 2 Padma Vibhushan, 7 Padma Bhushan and 56 Padma Shri.
  - **Padma Vibhushan:** K.T. Thomas (Public Affairs) and P. Narayanan (Literature and Education).
  - **Padma Bhushan:** Recipients included actor Mammootty, singer Alka Yagnik, tennis player Vijay Amritraj and Shibu Soren (posthumous).
  - **Padma Shri:** Recipients included cricketer Rohit Sharma, hockey player Savita Punia and actor R. Madhavan.

## Other honours and prizes

- **Slovakia:** It conferred its highest state honour, the Order of the White Double Cross (1st Class), on the Prime Minister. President Peter Pellegrini presented it in Bratislava.
- **Seychelles:** It conferred the "Guardian of the Blue Horizon" distinction on the Prime Minister, for his work on conservation and the blue economy.
- **Sudarsan Pattnaik:** The sand artist became the first Indian to win the Russia Grand Sand Master Cup 2026, at Kaliningrad.
- **The Earth Prize 2026:** Three Indian teenagers won it for "Plas-Stick". It is a tamarind-based powder that removes microplastics from water.
- **Arup Bose:** He received the Sukhatme National Award in Statistics 2026. He is from the Indian Statistical Institute (ISI), Kolkata.

## Important days

- **2 June, Telangana Statehood Day:** The State was formed on 2 June 2014.
- **19 June, International Sickle Cell Day:** India aims to eliminate sickle cell anaemia by 2047.
- **20 June, World Refugee Day:** The 2026 theme was "Until Everyone Is Safe".
- **21 June 2026, International Day of Yoga:** The 12th International Day of Yoga had the theme "Yoga for Healthy Ageing". The Prime Minister led the main event at Kolkata.
- **27 June, Kempe Gowda:** His birth anniversary falls on this day. He founded Bengaluru in 1537.
- **29 June 2026, Statistics Day:** The 20th Statistics Day had the theme "Unlocking the Potential of Administrative Data". The day is the birth anniversary of P.C. Mahalanobis.

## Persons in news

- **Elon Musk:** According to Bloomberg, he briefly became the world's first trillionaire after the stock market debut of SpaceX.
- **Sushruta:** A bronze statue of this ancient Indian surgeon was unveiled at the Royal College of Surgeons of Edinburgh.
- **Shubhanshu Shukla:** The astronaut wrote "The Second Orbit", a memoir of his Axiom-4 mission to the International Space Station.
