## Gist
The Army got a new chief: **Lt Gen Dhiraj Seth** took over on 30 June. The Navy commissioned **three Indian-built ships** in one day at Kolkata, and the Air Force's **Netra** early-warning aircraft received its final clearance.

DRDO had a busy month of tests, including the **RudraM-II** anti-radiation missile, a new naval anti-ship missile and a long-range cruise missile. India received its **fourth S-400** squadron and signed a **BrahMos** export deal with **Vietnam**.

The **SIPRI Yearbook 2026** ranked India fifth in military spending. Abroad, Indian troops trained in Mongolia in **Exercise Khaan Quest**, and the Air Force flew relief to earthquake-hit Venezuela in **Operation Amistad**.

## Key facts
- **Lt Gen Dhiraj Seth** was appointed Chief of the Army Staff with effect from **30 June 2026**, succeeding **General Upendra Dwivedi**.
- Lt Gen Dhiraj Seth is the first officer of the **Armoured Corps** to become Army Chief in nearly three decades.
- **Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit** was appointed **Vice Chief of the Air Staff**, succeeding Air Marshal Narmdeshwar Tiwari.
- The Prime Minister commissioned three naval ships at **Kolkata**: **INS Dunagiri**, **INS Sanshodhak** and **INS Agray**, all built by **GRSE**.
- **INS Dunagiri** is a **Project 17A** stealth frigate, **INS Sanshodhak** is a large survey vessel and **INS Agray** is an Arnala-class anti-submarine shallow-water craft.
- DRDO and the Air Force flight-tested **RudraM-II**, an indigenous air-to-surface **anti-radiation missile**.
- India received the **fourth squadron** of the Russian **S-400 Triumf** air defence system, called **"Sudarshan"** in Indian service.
- The S-400 deal of **2018**, worth **USD 5.43 billion**, is for five squadrons; the fifth is expected in 2027.
- India signed a deal to export the **BrahMos** missile to **Vietnam**; the Defence Secretary announced it at the **Shangri-La Dialogue** in Singapore.
- The **Philippines** was the first foreign buyer of BrahMos, in 2022.
- According to the **SIPRI Yearbook 2026**, India was the world's **fifth-largest military spender** in 2025, at **USD 92.1 billion**.
- SIPRI ranked India the **second-largest arms importer** for 2021–25, after **Ukraine**.
- SIPRI estimated India's nuclear arsenal at **190 warheads**, against **620** for China and **170** for Pakistan.
- The President conferred **51 gallantry awards** at the Defence Investiture Ceremony 2026 (Phase-I): **7 Kirti Chakras, 15 Vir Chakras and 29 Shaurya Chakras**.
- **Project Kusha** is India's **Extended Range Air Defence System**, with three interceptors (M1, M2 and M3).
- DRDO carried out the maiden test of the **Naval Anti-Ship Missile–Medium Range (NASM-MR)**, which has a range of up to **350 km**.
- The indigenous **Netra** airborne early warning and control (AEW&C) system, developed by DRDO's **Centre for Airborne Systems**, received **Final Operational Clearance**.
- Netra is mounted on the Brazilian **Embraer** EMB-145I aircraft.
- **Exercise Khaan Quest 2026**, a multinational peacekeeping exercise, was held in **Mongolia** from **20 June to 3 July 2026**; it was the **23rd edition**.
- The Indian Air Force will take part in **Exercise Pitch Black 2026**, hosted by **Australia** from 20 July to 7 August 2026.
- The **10th India–Thailand Defence Dialogue** was held in **Bangkok**.
- **Garudastra**, an indigenous **120 mm** vehicle-mounted mortar system, was tested at **Mhow** in Madhya Pradesh.
- The Army will raise specialised drone units called **Baaz Battalions**.
- India launched **Operation Amistad** to send relief to **Venezuela** after twin earthquakes; "Amistad" means friendship in Spanish.
