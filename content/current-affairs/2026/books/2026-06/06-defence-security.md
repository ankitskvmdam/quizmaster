## Appointments and awards

- **30 June 2026, Army Chief:** Lt Gen Dhiraj Seth took over as Chief of the Army Staff. He succeeded General Upendra Dwivedi.
  - He is the first officer of the Armoured Corps to become Army Chief in nearly three decades.
- **Vice Chief of the Air Staff:** Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit was appointed to the post. He succeeded Air Marshal Narmdeshwar Tiwari.
- **Gallantry awards:** The President conferred 51 gallantry awards at the Defence Investiture Ceremony 2026 (Phase-I). They were 7 Kirti Chakras, 15 Vir Chakras and 29 Shaurya Chakras.

## Navy and Air Force

- **Three ships at Kolkata:** The Prime Minister commissioned three Indian-built naval ships in one day. All were built by GRSE (Garden Reach Shipbuilders and Engineers).
  - INS Dunagiri is a Project 17A stealth frigate.
  - INS Sanshodhak is a large survey vessel.
  - INS Agray is an Arnala-class anti-submarine shallow-water craft.
- **Netra:** This indigenous airborne early warning and control (AEW&C) system received Final Operational Clearance.
  - It was developed by DRDO's Centre for Airborne Systems.
  - It is mounted on the Brazilian Embraer EMB-145I aircraft.

## Missiles and weapons

- **RudraM-II:** DRDO and the Air Force flight-tested this indigenous air-to-surface anti-radiation missile. An anti-radiation missile homes in on enemy radar.
- **NASM-MR:** DRDO carried out the maiden test of the Naval Anti-Ship Missile–Medium Range (NASM-MR). Its range is up to 350 km.
- **Cruise missile:** DRDO also tested a long-range cruise missile.
- **Project Kusha:** This is India's Extended Range Air Defence System. It has three interceptors: M1, M2 and M3.
- **Garudastra:** This indigenous 120 mm vehicle-mounted mortar system was tested at Mhow in Madhya Pradesh.
- **Baaz Battalions:** The Army will raise these specialised drone units.

## Deals and the SIPRI Yearbook

- **S-400:** India received the fourth squadron of the Russian S-400 Triumf air defence system. In Indian service it is called "Sudarshan".
  - The S-400 deal of 2018, worth USD 5.43 billion, is for five squadrons.
  - The fifth squadron is expected in 2027.
- **BrahMos to Vietnam:** India signed a deal to export the BrahMos missile to Vietnam. The Defence Secretary announced it at the Shangri-La Dialogue in Singapore.
  - The Philippines was the first foreign buyer of BrahMos, in 2022.
- **SIPRI Yearbook 2026:** SIPRI is the Stockholm International Peace Research Institute. Its yearbook gave three findings on India.
  - India was the world's fifth-largest military spender in 2025, at USD 92.1 billion.
  - India was the second-largest arms importer for 2021–25, after Ukraine.
  - India's nuclear arsenal was estimated at 190 warheads, against 620 for China and 170 for Pakistan.

## Exercises and operations

- **20 June to 3 July 2026, Khaan Quest 2026:** This multinational peacekeeping exercise was held in Mongolia. It was the 23rd edition.
- **20 July to 7 August 2026, Pitch Black 2026:** The Indian Air Force will take part in this exercise, hosted by Australia.
- **India–Thailand Defence Dialogue:** The 10th dialogue was held in Bangkok.
- **Operation Amistad:** India launched it to send relief to Venezuela after twin earthquakes. The Air Force flew the relief. "Amistad" means friendship in Spanish.
