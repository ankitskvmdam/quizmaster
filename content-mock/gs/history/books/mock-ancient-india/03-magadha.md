## Gist
Of the sixteen Mahajanapadas, Magadha (in present-day Bihar) rose to become the most powerful.

## Key facts
- There were **sixteen Mahajanapadas**.
- Magadha's first capital was **Rajagriha** (Rajgir).
- **Bimbisara** of the **Haryanka** dynasty expanded Magadha.
- **Vaishali** was the seat of the **Licchavi** republic.
