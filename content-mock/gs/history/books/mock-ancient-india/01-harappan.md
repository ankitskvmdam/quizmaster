## Gist
A Bronze Age urban civilisation in the north-west of the subcontinent, known for planned cities and drainage.

## Key facts
- **Harappa** was the first site excavated, by **Daya Ram Sahni** in **1921**.
- The **Great Bath** is at **Mohenjo-daro**.
- **Lothal** in Gujarat had a **dockyard**.
- The Harappan script is still **undeciphered**.
