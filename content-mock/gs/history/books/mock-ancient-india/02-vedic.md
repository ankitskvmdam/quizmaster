## Gist
The age of the Vedas: early pastoral society moving towards settled agriculture in the Ganga plains.

## Key facts
- The **Rigveda** is the oldest Veda, with **10 mandalas**.
- The **Gayatri Mantra** is in the **Rigveda**.
- **Sabha** and **Samiti** were early popular assemblies.
