The Mauryan Empire was the first empire to unite most of the Indian subcontinent.

> **Key facts**
> - Founded by **Chandragupta Maurya** around **321 BCE**, with the help of **Chanakya (Kautilya)**, author of the *Arthashastra*.
> - Capital: **Pataliputra** (present-day Patna).
> - The Greek ambassador **Megasthenes** described the Mauryan court in *Indica*.
> - **Ashoka** turned to Buddhism after the **Kalinga war**.
