In **1917** Gandhi led the **Champaran Satyagraha** in Bihar, his first satyagraha in India.

> **Key facts**
> - Against the forced cultivation of **indigo** under the **tinkathia** system.
> - **Raj Kumar Shukla** persuaded Gandhi to visit Champaran.
