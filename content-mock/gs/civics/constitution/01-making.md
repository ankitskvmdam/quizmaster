The Constitution was framed by the **Constituent Assembly**.

> **Key facts**
> - First met on **9 December 1946**; **Dr Rajendra Prasad** was its president.
> - **Dr B. R. Ambedkar** chaired the **Drafting Committee**.
> - Adopted on **26 November 1949**; in force from **26 January 1950**.
