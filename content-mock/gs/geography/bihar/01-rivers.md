The **Ganga** flows west to east through Bihar and divides it into north and south Bihar.

> **Key facts**
> - The **Kosi** is called the **"Sorrow of Bihar"** because of its floods.
> - The **Son** joins the Ganga from the south; it rises near **Amarkantak**.
