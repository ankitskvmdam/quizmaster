Bihar is a **landlocked** state where **agriculture** employs most people.

> **Key facts**
> - **Jharkhand** was carved out of Bihar on **15 November 2000**.
> - **Bihar Diwas** is celebrated on **22 March**, marking its separation from Bengal in **1912**.
